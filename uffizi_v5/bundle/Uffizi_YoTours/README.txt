YO TOURS - UFFIZI GALLERY, FLORENCE
Audio guide, edition of 24 Oct 2026 (67 tracks: 53 main stops + 14 short Extras)
English, Spanish, French, German, Italian

WHY THIS EDITION
================
The previous guide sent visitors into rooms that are now closed (A40-A42, the Lippi and Pollaiolo
rooms), described the Botticellis as they hung before the June 2026 rehang, and got about 22 facts
wrong (among them the 1737 Medici Family Pact). This edition was rebuilt from scratch:
1. Every work's room was read on its own page on uffizi.it (24 Oct 2026) and checked against the
   museum's October 2026 visitor map, the notice of closed rooms (15 Sep 2026) and the list of works
   not on display (21 Oct 2026). Where the pages and the map disagree, the map's room codes are used.
   Works on loan are not stops; where a work is away, the guide says so in one dated line.
2. The route is one walking order on two floors: the whole second floor (13th century to Raphael,
   Michelangelo and the Niobe Room), down the Lanzi staircase ONE floor (not out to the street), the
   Contini Bonacossi collection, the self-portraits, the 16th century, Venice, the Verone and the
   17th century (Caravaggio, Artemisia, Rembrandt), then out by the Magliabechi staircase.
   Closed rooms are never stops. The Botticelli rooms are found by position and the museum's signs,
   because the museum's own sources disagree on their numbers.
3. Every track ends by naming the next room and one thing to look for, following the doors drawn on
   the museum's map. Two independent reviewers re-checked every route and every fact.
4. Three lengths (one recording, three folders):
   Full tour   67 tracks, about 2 h 45 with walking: everything
   Essential   40 tracks, about 2 h:      001 002 004 006 007 009 012 014 015 017 018 020 022 023 025 029 030 031 034 035 036 037 041 042 045 046 048 049 052 055 057 059 060 061 062 063 064 065 066 067
   Highlights  19 tracks, about 1 h 15:    001 002 006 007 017 018 022 025 030 034 035 037 046 052 057 062 063 064 067
   In the shorter versions a short spoken line at the end of a track tells you the next stop.
5. The Yo Tours sound: voice finishing, a soft room tone and a short chime before each track.
   MP3s are labelled for phones (title, album, track number, Yo Tours, cover picture).

WHAT YOU GET
============
One folder per section, tracks numbered in walking order, plus Uffizi_Audio_Guide_Route.pdf: a map
for phones drawn from the museum's own plan (an overview of the two floors, then each part of the
route with every stop numbered, then the list of stops with the three lengths).

HOW TO MAKE THE AUDIO (on a Mac)
================================
English:          double-click generate_audio.command  (voice 1 = Ava)
                  option 1 = all three lengths (records once; folders _Essential and _Highlights)
                  option 2 = two voice samples (018 Birth of Venus + 062 Medusa)
                  option 3 = Highlights only (quickest; option 1 later reuses those recordings)
Other languages:  double-click generate_audio_ES_FR_DE_IT.command and pick the language
                  (1 Spanish, 2 French, 3 German, 4 Italian), then the same three options.
Needs internet, python3, ffmpeg (brew install ffmpeg) and an up-to-date edge-tts:
  python3 -m pip install --user --upgrade edge-tts
If it stops, run it again: finished tracks are skipped.
