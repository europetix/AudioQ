 Giovanni Bellini
 WHERE YOU ARE
 The room holding the collection's Venetian jewel — a small, luminous panel by Giovanni Bellini.
 WHAT YOU SEE
 A single, exquisite panel: Giovanni Bellini's Saint Jerome in the Desert — the founding master of the
 Venetian Renaissance, painting a saint at prayer in a glowing, light-filled rocky landscape, around 1480.
 LISTEN FOR
 Why Bellini matters so much, and the painting to walk up to.


 This room holds the collection's Venetian jewel, and it's worth knowing who painted it, because few
 artists were more important. Giovanni Bellini was the founding father of the Venetian Renaissance —
 the teacher of Giorgione and Titian, the man who, more than anyone, taught Venice to paint glowing
 light and atmospheric landscape in oil.

 The painting to stand in front of is his Saint Jerome in the Desert. There's a track for it. Go and find the
 small, luminous panel of a saint reading among the rocks, and play the next track.
