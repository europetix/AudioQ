 The Room of the Dynasties — Bronzino

 Cool, polished, magnificent portraits by Bronzino, court painter to Duke Cosimo I — the Medici and their

 LISTEN FOR
 What Bronzino was really painting for, and the portrait to walk up to.


 This is the room of the dynasties — the Medici exactly as they wished to be remembered, fixed forever
 — and its master is Agnolo Bronzino.

 Bronzino was the official court painter to Duke Cosimo the First, and his portraits are like nothing
 painted before them. The skin is smoothed to cool, flawless enamel; the fabrics are rendered with an
 almost hypnotic, thread-by-thread precision; and the sitters hold you at a proud, unreadable, glacial
 distance. These are not warm likenesses of individuals so much as images of power — made to project
 the permanence, wealth and absolute magnificence of a family that had only recently turned itself from
 bankers into hereditary dukes, and needed everyone to feel that the change was eternal. There's a
 deliberate chill to them that is its own strange kind of beauty, and you can see the same cool perfection
 carry over into his religious paintings nearby.

 The portrait to stand in front of is his Eleonora di Toledo with her son Giovanni — Cosimo's Spanish
 duchess in a spectacular gown. There's a track for it. Go and find it, and play the next track.
