# Uffizi V4.5 → V5 — Fact audit of the old guide (non-placement claims)

Prepared 24 Oct 2026 for the V5 rebuild. Scope: every checkable claim in old tracks 001–073 plus the title page. Not covered: **room placement** (which room, which wall, what hangs next to what). Two other researchers are checking that. Where a placement-adjacent claim is affected by a dated change I found, it is flagged "→ ROOM TEAM" and not judged.

**Verdicts**

- **OK**: supported by the source.
- **WRONG**: contradicted; use the fix.
- **HEDGE**: contested, or true only with a qualifier; use the safe wording.
- **UNVERIFIED**: hold; do not use unless sourced.
- **DATED**: true once but likely to change, or already changed.
- **REMOVE**: price, hours or ticket rules; commercial advice; invented-sounding content.

**Source grades**

- **OFF**: an official museum or institution page, read directly.
- **SEC**: a reliable secondary source, read directly.
- **SNIP**: seen only as a search-result title or snippet.
- **VASARI**: the claim is a story from Vasari's *Lives*. It is a primary source, but it should be framed as "Vasari tells…".

Uffizi pages were fetched one at a time. No curl, no browser, no CAPTCHA. Wikipedia was never used as a source.

## 1. Summary

**Claims checked:** about 330 claims across 73 tracks plus the title page. Totals: **OK ≈ 225 · WRONG 22 · HEDGE 52 · UNVERIFIED 14 · DATED 13 · REMOVE 6**. These are approximate because many claims are compound.

### Headline errors (fix before anything else)

1. **001: Family Pact.** The old guide says Anna Maria Luisa "left the entire collection to the city of Florence" when "the last of the Medici died".
   - What the 31 Oct 1737 pact did: it was agreed with the new Lorraine grand duke, Francis Stephen. The collections stayed with the grand-ducal succession, on condition that nothing could leave "the capital and State of the Grand Duchy". The stated purposes were "a ornamento dello Stato, per utilità del Pubblico e per attirare la curiosità dei Forestieri".
   - It did not leave them to the city. It says nothing about "never sold".
   - It was not signed when "the last of the Medici died": Gian Gastone died in July 1737, and Anna Maria Luisa lived until 1743.
2. **044 and 058: self-portraits in the Vasari Corridor.** The old guide says they hung there "for a century and a half" (044) and "for centuries" (058). The true dates are **1973 to 2016**. They have been in the main museum route since **July 2023**: 12 bright-pink rooms, 255 works, and a collection of more than 2,000.
3. **042: "the only sculpture by Bernini you can see anywhere in Florence".** False. The Bargello owns Bernini's bust of Costanza Bonarelli.
4. **043: Bernini's St Lawrence is a "life-scale figure".** False. It measures 66 × 108 cm.
5. **021: Dürer.** The 1504 painting was not made "just after his second journey to Italy". By 1504 he had been to Italy only once. The self-portrait is also misidentified: it is the **profile king with long blond curls**, not the kneeling king.
6. **016: Primavera has "close to two hundred identifiable species of plant".** The Uffizi says **at least 42** (14 green plants and 28 flowering plants).
7. **038: the director quote is misattributed.** The "most Florentine" remark (Eike Schmidt) was about **Bernini's St Lawrence**, not Castagno.
8. **051: the tiny figure in the Madonna of the Long Neck is St Jerome**, not "a prophet" (Uffizi).
9. **053: Pontormo and Rosso were not "both born around 1503".** They were born in **1494** and **1495**.
10. **008: Lorenzetti.** The Good and Bad Government frescoes are by **Ambrogio** Lorenzetti alone, not by both brothers.
11. **005 and 007: Maestà dates.** The Uffizi dates are Cimabue **c.1290–1300** (the old guide says c.1280) and Giotto **c.1300–1305** (the old guide says c.1310). On the museum's own dates, Duccio's 1285 panel is the **earliest** of the three. The "old master, then the pupil" framing needs a hedge.
12. **024: Piero's diptych.** The Uffizi dates it **c.1473–75**; the old guide says c.1465–72. That makes Battista's portrait most likely **posthumous**, since she died in 1472.
13. **018: Portinari Triptych.** It was not "hung among the warm Italian tempera on purpose". It was made for the Portinari chapel in Sant'Egidio, the church of the hospital of Santa Maria Nuova.
14. **070: the Bacchus carafe self-portrait is not visible to the naked eye.** It was found by cleaning in 1922 and imaged by infrared reflectography in 2009. Do not tell visitors to look for it.
15. **015: "one of the first great paintings on canvas".** The Uffizi says canvas was widely used throughout the 15th century for decorative works for noble houses.
16. **056: Eleonora and Giovanni did not die "within days".** Giovanni died on 20 Nov 1562 and Eleonora on 17 Dec 1562, so they died within weeks of each other. The burial-dress legend has been disproved, not merely "doubted".
17. **058: butchers on the Ponte Vecchio.** They were replaced by goldsmiths in **1593 under Ferdinando I**, not under "the duke" (Cosimo). The corridor is **about 750 m long** (760 m on one Uffizi page), not "nearly a kilometre".

### Must remove (ticket, price, hours or commercial advice)

- **001:** "book a timed entry slot… or the queue can swallow an hour", and "ticket check".
- **058:** "it needs its own reserved ticket and a separate visit".
- **073:** the coffee on the rooftop terrace and "the bookshop by the exit is genuinely good". These are commercial; keep at most a neutral mention of the terrace view.

### Superseded or dated by 2025–26 museum changes (→ ROOM TEAM)

- **Botticelli rooms (16 Jun 2026):** they have a **new permanent installation**. Venus and Primavera are now in airtight cases and "face each other". Primavera is in "the room facing this one". This makes 012, 014 and 016 out of date: the temporary showing in A9, "rooms ten to twelve closed", and "stand at the centre between this and the Birth of Venus".
- **Pollaiolo room:** the new Botticelli suite includes a Pollaiolo room (the *Virtues*). This may make the 046 and 048 "rehoused on the first floor" premise out of date.
- **Historic entrance (3 Mar 2026):** the Scala Lorenese has reopened as the historic entrance to the route. This affects 001's "Door 1" and "top of the staircase".
- **Vasari Corridor:** it reopened on 21 Dec 2024. Since Jul 2025, more than 50 Roman portrait busts stand in its Ponte Vecchio section, so it is no longer completely "bare".
- **Contini Bonacossi:** its rooms in the main route opened in **2018**, so "only a few years ago" is now stale.

## 7. Method notes

- About 45 Uffizi pages were fetched, one at a time.
- Secondary sources used: Finestre sull'Arte, University of Florence SMA, National Gallery London, The Art Newspaper, Britannica, Smarthistory, Gallerie dell'Accademia, Visit Tuscany, the Pisa tourism office, and *Costume* (2025).
- The Family Pact text was not read in a primary edition. The wording above comes from two independent SEC sources that agree. If V5 quotes the pact verbatim, use the Italian phrase "a ornamento dello Stato, per utilità del Pubblico e per attirare la curiosità dei Forestieri".
- Vasari anecdotes are marked VASARI. Each should be voiced as "Vasari tells…", never as fact.
