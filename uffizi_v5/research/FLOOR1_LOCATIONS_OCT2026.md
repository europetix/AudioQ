# Uffizi first floor: where the old guide's works hang now (research of 24 Oct 2026)

Method: I read each work's own page on www.uffizi.it with WebFetch, one page at a time (`/en/artworks/<slug>`; the Italian `/opere/` slugs I tried did not resolve). The room text quoted below is the page's "Location" field, verbatim. I found slugs with WebSearch restricted to uffizi.it. The room names and codes come from the museum's October 2026 visitor map (`source/mappa_uffizi_ottobre2026.pdf`, created 8 Oct 2026; `research/map/p-2.png` for the first floor, which I also rendered at 300 dpi). Official news and notice pages were used to explain disagreements. No Wikipedia, no curl, no browser.

Source grades: **OFF** = official uffizi.it page or the official Oct 2026 map, read directly. **SEC** = official uffizi.it news, event or video page that describes a hang but is older than the map (it may be out of date). **SNIP** = search-result snippet only. Every Location value in the tables is OFF unless marked otherwise.

## Caveats (read before using any row)

1. **The artwork pages use two numbering systems.** For B, C1–C3, D1–D17 and E4–E6 the page codes match the Oct 2026 map exactly (e.g. Rosso's *Moses* "D12. Pontormo - Rosso Fiorentino", Bronzino "D15. Room of the Dynasties", Honthorst "E6. Painting by Candlelight"). **From D19 on, many pages still carry the old numbering, one number lower than the map:**
   - page "D19. Venetian Small Chapel" = map **D20 Venetian Chapel**
   - page "D20. El Greco" = map **D21 El Greco**
   - page "D22. Tiziano and Venere di Urbino" = map **D23 Titian – Venus of Urbino**
   - page "D25. Veronese" = map **D26 Veronese**
   - page "D26. Veronese Corridor" = map **D27 Veronese Corridor**

   On the map, D19 is now the "Vasari Corridor entrance". The official Vasari Corridor rules notice (modified 11 Sep 2026) confirms this: "The entrance to the Vasari Corridor is at the Room D19 Meeting Point on the first floor". That inserted room explains the shift. In the tables I give the page wording and, separately, the room the map implies. Where the two differ I say so.
2. **The 17th-century pages are older still.** Artemisia's *Judith* says "D29. Caravaggio and Artemisia"; the *Bacchus* says "D32. Caravaggio - The Bacchus"; the Rembrandt *Old Rabbi* says "D34. Rembrandt - Rubens". The map has only **E4–E7**. Other pages already use the E codes (*Sacrifice of Isaac* "E4. Caravaggio (The Medusa) e Artemisia"; Reni's *David* "E4. Caravaggio (The Medusa)"; Honthorst "E6"). So for these three works I go with the map: Judith E4, Bacchus E5, Rembrandt E7.
3. **The map shows unfinished work.** On the first floor, C6–C11 (and C12, which is drawn on the plan) are "Early Renaissance painting – Work in progress", C7–C8 are "Temporarily closed", and D3, D10 and D11 are "Work in progress". On the second floor, A10–A12, A33 and A40–A42 are work in progress. Also, an official news item of **21 Oct 2026** says a "first-floor gallery—due to open soon—devoted to 17th-century painting" will receive a new Luca Giordano. So the 17th-century section (E1–E3, not shown on the map) is still incomplete.
4. **I did not read the PDF list of works not on display** ("The Uffizi", updated 21.10.26, linked from https://www.uffizi.it/en/notices/artworks-not-on-display). That belongs to the second-floor and notices researcher. `NOTICES_OCT2026.md` did not yet exist when I finished. Any row below may still be overturned by a loan on that list.
5. Two map oddities: the plan prints "D22" twice (once between D20 and D25, once beside D24), and it does not draw D28 "Verone" as a separate room (only a "VERONE" label inside the D27 strip).

Status key: SAME (the room matches the old guide) · MOVED · ON LOAN · RESTORATION · NOT ON DISPLAY (page says "Depots/Deposits") · NOT FOUND (no official page) · CHECK (unresolved conflict between the page and the map, or no evidence).

## 3. First-floor route findings (Oct 2026 map p-2, OFF)

The plan is drawn with the Arno on the **right** side (the "FIUME ARNO" band). Directions below are map directions.

- **Legend:** Classic = coral line, room groups **E + D**. Complete = orange line, room groups **B + C + D + E**. The Fast itinerary does not come to this floor (front page: "Only the 2nd floor (exit down the Lanzi staircase)").
- **Complete start: Lanzi staircase**, at the far left (west) of the plan, beside a lift, WCs and the C1 strip, with an Info Point just below. From the stair foot:
  - a **dotted** orange line runs right, past the Info Point, through **B1 to B8** (Contini Bonacossi). The dotted line marks an optional out-and-back.
  - the solid orange line goes up the C strip, **C1 → C2 → C3** (up arrow), turns right along **C4, C5**, then runs through the corridor rooms **C6, C9, C10, C11, C12** (right arrow) into **D1**.
  - C7–C8 hang below C6 off the line.
- **Classic start: Buontalenti staircase**, at the bottom middle of the D block, beside a lift, under D9 and D11. The coral line goes up through **D9** to the long corridor. There a **dotted** coral line runs left to D1 (an optional backtrack to D1–D8), and the solid coral line turns right.
- **Shared section:** both lines run right along the corridor that edges D1, D6, D13, D14, D15 and on to **D26** (right arrow). They then turn up the right-hand strip labelled **D27 / VERONE** (up arrow), and left (left arrow) along the corridor beneath the teal E block.
  - The lines enter the E block at **E4** (the room nearest the Verone) and pass **E4 → E5 → E6 → E7**.
  - From E7 they turn up to an exit symbol and the **Magliabechi staircase**, where the map says "End of Complete itinerary / End of Classic itinerary".
  - The front-page 3-D view shows both lines going down the Magliabechi stairs to the ground floor (cloakroom, audioguides, bookshop/giftshop) and out the "Exit – back way out of the building" on the Palazzo Vecchio side.
- **D rooms inside the block** (D2–D5, D7–D8, D10–D12, D16–D25) are side rooms. Neither line threads through them, so the map prescribes no internal order. The old guide's D4 → D9 → D12 → D15 → D18 → D19 → D20 → D21 → D23 → D25 → D26 sequence is a choice, not the museum's route.
- **Vasari Corridor:** a black arrow drops from the D19/D21 area to a "Vasari Corridor entrance – Reservation only" box. The official notice says the meeting point is "Room D19".
- **D23** sits at the bottom right corner, next to the "Ponte Vecchio ↓" marker. D22 is printed twice.
- **Inconsistency in the museum's own map:** the front-page text for the Classic itinerary says "Descent from 2nd to 1st floor (through the Lanzi staircase)", word for word the same as the Complete text. However, both the 3-D view and the first-floor plan draw Classic down the **Buontalenti** staircase and Complete down the **Lanzi** staircase. The second-floor plan agrees: "Buontalenti staircase – continue classic itinerary"; "Lanzi staircase – continue complete itinerary". Use Buontalenti for Classic.
- **One-way exit:** the map warns, "Once out, it is no longer possible to access the cloakroom and audioguide desk". The cafeteria and terrace are on the second floor (OFF https://www.uffizi.it/en/the-uffizi), so track 073's "ride up to the rooftop terrace" should move earlier in the visit.
- **Contini Bonacossi access:** the collection has been on the ordinary route since **2018** (SEC: "After 49 years, the entire Contini Bonacossi bequest is finally accessible for all visitors", https://www.uffizi.it/en/events/contini-bonacossi-collection-on-view-again-in-newly-inaugurated-premises-at-the-uffizi; eight rooms). The 2026 map draws it as a dotted side branch of the Complete route. I found **no official statement of restricted days or booking** for 2026 (CHECK on site).
- **Self-Portraits Collection:**
  - opened on the first floor in **July 2023** as "12 rooms for 255 works" (SEC, https://www.uffizi.it/en/news/selfportraits_uffizi, dated 10/07/2023).
  - On the Oct 2026 map it has shrunk to **C1–C5**, ending at "Early 18th century".
  - The former later rooms (C6 onwards) are now "Early Renaissance painting – work in progress".
  - The pink walls and the Foggini statue of Leopoldo are SEC 2023 claims, not re-confirmed.
