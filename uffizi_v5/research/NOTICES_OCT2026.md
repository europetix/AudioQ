Related news (OFF):
- **"The new permanent installation of the Botticelli Rooms"** (16 Jun 2026, mod. 17 Jun 2026), https://www.uffizi.it/en/news/the-new-permanent-installation-of-the-botticelli-rooms. The Venus and the Primavera "are now displayed facing one another" in two "jewel-box rooms" joined by a passage. The Magnificat and Pomegranate Madonnas flank the Venus. The Rose Garden and Cherubim Madonnas flank the Primavera. The sequence opens with the Lama Adoration and closes with the Calumny. A Pollaiolo room holds the Virtues (Fortitude). "The complete replacement of the museum's signage is also underway." No room codes.
- **"Work in progress"** (5 Aug 2026, mod. 9 Sep 2026), https://www.uffizi.it/en/news/work-in-progress. A two-year programme. At the Uffizi it covers the ground-floor introductory rooms, the Magliabechiana reception, "Reopening of the rooms of the Flemish Masters" (new layout and lighting; ongoing), the "Refurbishment of the Filippo Lippi and Andrea del Castagno room", the gallery of ancient inscriptions ("will be opened to the public"), the Verone Corridor, the Scarpa-Michelucci-Gardella rooms, and Vasari Corridor air-conditioning. No room codes or dates.
