# Independent audit: Uffizi V5 tracks 035–067 (24 Oct 2026)

Auditor: independent (did not write the tracks). Scope: `v5/tracks/035–067.perf.txt`, the bridge lines for 035–067 in `v5/bridges/*.txt`, against the Oct 2026 map (`source/map.txt`, `research/map/p-1/p-2/p-3/hi-3.png`, plus a fresh 300 dpi render of PDF page 2 to read first-floor doorways), the research files, `v5/PLAN.md` (old numbers for 059–068 read as one lower), `build/static/lengths.json`, `build/static/levels.txt` and `build/static/make_lengths.py`.

Official pages re-read with WebFetch for this audit, one at a time, 24 Oct 2026: Bramantino *Madonna and Child with eight saints*; Parmigianino *Madonna with the long neck*; Rosso *Enthroned Madonna and Child with four saints*; Titian *Venus of Urbino*; Titian *Flora*; Rembrandt *Old Rabbi*; Veronese *Count Giuseppe da Porto*; *I corridoi di Galleria*; El Greco *Saints John and Francis*; Google Arts & Culture *Medusa* (Uffizi partner text). Everything else was checked against the writers' @sources, the notes and the audit `AUDIT_OLD_GUIDE_FACTS_OCT2026.md`.

Verdicts: **OK** = matches the source · **FIX** = wrong or contradicts a source/PLAN, change it · **HEDGE** = unsourced or overstated, soften · **CHECK-ONSITE** = can only be settled in the galleries (O-number from PLAN §5).

## How bridges are played (read first)

- `make_lengths.py` **appends** the bridge after the full track (0.6 s gap), keyed `NNN E>MMM` / `NNN H>MMM`. PLAN §3 still says a bridge "replaces the track's last cue paragraph": that sentence is out of date. WRITER_BRIEF and the caller agree with the code: the bridge follows the normal cue. I audited every bridge on that basis.
- `levels.txt` has `"skip_extras_silently": true`, so a skip over Extras only (E 042→045, 046→048, 052→055, 057→059) gets no bridge. That is right, because each of those Full cues already names the next main stop.
- Every skip that needs a bridge in 035–067 has one: 035 H>037, 037 E>041, 037 H>046, 046 H>052, 049 E>052, 052 H>057, 055 E>057, 057 H>062, 064 H>067. All of them use the new numbers (lengths.json: E … 055 057 059 060 … 067; H … 057 062 063 064 067). The writer's notes still mention "057 E>060", but the bridge file no longer has it. That is correct, because 057's Full cue now goes straight to D26.
- All nine bridges are 31–40 words.
