## 0. Route walk on the map (what the doorways on `hi-3.png` actually show)

Doorways were read as gaps in the grey wall lines of `hi-3.png`. **Second floor, Eastern Corridor:**
- **A3** sits on the **far side of the vestibule from the rest of the route**, on the short dead-end stretch toward Palazzo Vecchio, with the lift block between A3 and A1. It has a corridor door.
- **A5 has no corridor door.** So the first corridor doorway after the vestibule, going the route's way, is **A4**. This supports the 003 cue.
- **A4↔A5:** a doorway near the corridor, in the staircase-side wall. **A5↔A6:** a doorway at the back of A5, in the vestibule-side corner.
- **A6↔A7:** a gap **is drawn** at the back of their shared wall. Pixel scan: the wall at x≈642 on p-3 runs only from y 121 to 203, against an outer wall at y≈109. That contradicts PLAN §1 ("No A6–A7 doorway is drawn"). 010's conditional cue covers both cases.
- **A4↔A7 and A4↔A8:** two doorways **side by side** on A4's far side, either side of the A7/A8 wall. There is no A7↔A8 doorway. **A4↔A9:** none.
- **A8↔A9:** a doorway. **A8↔A10:** none. **A9↔A10:** a doorway. **A10↔A11–12:** a doorway at the back. **A9** has a corridor door. **A10 and A11–12** have none. **A11–12↔A13:** a doorway.
  - So the **only drawn way from A8 into the Botticelli rooms is through A9**. The map shades A9 "not open", and the 15 Sep notice lists A9 as work in progress.
- **A13↔A14:** a doorway. **A14 opens only into A13.** **A13↔A15:** a doorway. **A13 and A15** each also have a corridor door. **A16 (Tribune)** has a corridor door and connects to A15. **A17–A21** are shaded closed. **A22** is open, at the end of the corridor.

**Western Corridor:** walking from A23 toward the Lanzi end, the rooms are on your **left**. The corridor doorways come in this order:
1. **A25**
2. **A34**
3. **A35**
4. **A36** (closed)
5. the **passage to the Buontalenti staircase** (A37 lies beside or below the stairs: O15)
6. **A38**
7. then A39 and the rooms beyond.

Inside the suite the doors run A25→A26→A27→A28→A29 (with A30 off A29)→A31→A32→A33 (work in progress)→A34. There is no A25↔A34 or A34↔A35 doorway. This matches PLAN §1 and every Western-Corridor cue in 023–032.

**Closed rooms:** in speech, A17–A21, A36 and A40–A42 are only ever passed, and A33 appears only in a conditional ("if the doorway … is open"). Neither speech nor bridges ever say A9, A10, A11 or A12. Both rules are respected. (But see M1/M2: the *route* into the Botticelli rooms from A8 needs A9.)

**Version lists (`build/static/lengths.json`):** for 001–034 they match PLAN §3. Essential = 001 002 004 006 007 009 012 014 015 017 018 020 022 023 025 029 030 031 034. Highlights = 001 002 006 007 017 018 022 025 030 034.
