# Uffizi Gallery: official and competitor audio guides (as of 24 Oct 2026)

Prepared for Yo Tours, Uffizi audio guide V5. All pages were read on **2026-10-24** with WebSearch and WebFetch only. Museum sites were fetched one page at a time. No curl, no browser, no CAPTCHA.

**Grades**
- **OFF**: the official page itself was read directly. For museum facts, this means uffizi.it or its own asset host. For a product, it means the seller's or app store's own listing page.
- **SEC**: a secondary source, such as press, a forum or a blog.
- **SNIP**: a search result only. In this session, search results returned **titles and URLs only, with no snippet text**, so a SNIP item proves only that a page with that title exists.

**Caveat:** WebFetch returns text pulled out by a model rather than the raw page, especially for PDFs. Quotes below are as WebFetch returned them. Anything marked "verify" should be checked by eye before we rely on it.

### Context that affects every guide: room numbering, routes, closures (OFF)
- **Numbering (Oct 2026 map).** The second floor uses A1–A42 (13th–14th c., Early and High Renaissance, Tribune). The first floor uses B1–B8, C1–C9, D1–D28 (16th–17th c., Vasari Corridor entrance) and E4–E7 (Caravaggio, Rembrandt/Rubens/Van Dyck). The map suggests three itineraries: **Fast = A; Classic = A+D+E; Complete = A+B+C+D+E**. Per the extraction, Leonardo is in A35 and Botticelli in A9 and A34. **Verify by eye**, because A9 is also on the closed list below. Self-portraits and Contini Bonacossi labels appear in the first-floor plan, but the extraction could not tie them to room numbers. https://www.datocms-assets.com/103094/1791457668-mappauffizien_ottobre2026.pdf
- **Rooms closed for works:** A9, A17/A21, A40/A42 (floor 2); C7/C12, D3, D9/D11 (floor 1). "The works regularly displayed in rooms A40/A42 (floor 2) can now be viewed in rooms C9/C10 (floor 1)." No dates are given. https://www.uffizi.it/en/notices/restoration-and-rearrangement-works-closed-rooms (modified 2026-09-15)
- **Botticelli rehang, 16 Jun 2026.** *Birth of Venus* and *Primavera* now hang in adjoining rooms on opposite walls. The museum calls it a new phase of the renovation under director Simone Verde. ## 4. What each one does well

- **Official audioguide:** cheap (€6); 8 languages, including Japanese, Polish and Russian; no phone, battery or download needed; museum-sanctioned permanent-collection content. (OFF, uffizi.it/en/the-uffizi)
- **Factories of Stories (official, free):** distinctive human storytelling by staff and migrants, celebrity voices (Paolini, Costa), and non-European languages (Arabic, Farsi, Mandarin). It works on your own phone and on Spotify. (OFF)
- **Rick Steves:** free, offline, with a strong single narrator, a tight one-hour highlights path, a printable map, and a trusted brand. (OFF app page + SEC forum)
- **ACCORD app:** widest language set (15–18), offline, interactive map, and the only option with a **Vasari Corridor** audio component. (OFF)
- **Slow Tour app:** frictionless bundle with ticket and audio on the phone; reviewers like going "at our own pace". (OFF GYG)
- **VoiceMap (Korey / Thomas):** named art-historian authors, GPS auto-play, offline, clear stop counts and durations (22 stops/120 min; 26 stops/90 min + 30 min first-floor extension). Reviewers rate depth and production highly. Korey openly flags room changes. (OFF)
- **ITCares app:** image recognition, an interactive map with artwork positions, 9 languages, and about 3 h of audio. Positions were updated in 2025. (OFF)
- **Smartify:** a large artwork database (701 records) for scan-and-learn. (OFF)

## 5. The bar to beat: V5 checklist

**Must match or exceed**
1. **Price:** at or below about €6 equivalent value, or clearly better value at a higher price. Rick Steves and the Factories of Stories tracks are free.
2. **Languages:** ≥ 8 (official) at launch, with a roadmap toward 15–18 (ACCORD).
3. **Offline, on your own phone, no ID deposit, no 17:00 pick-up cut-off, no return desk.** This removes the Lanzi-staircase trap: "ONCE OUT, IT IS NO LONGER POSSIBLE TO ACCESS THE CLOACKROOM AND AUDIOGUIDE DESK."
4. **Depth:** ≥ 26 stops (VoiceMap Thomas) and ≥ 40 works (ITCares), every track fully narrated. Avoid "25 paintings narrated… such a small number" and "Missing audio segments… too brief".
5. **Named expert authorship and strong narration** (VoiceMap, Rick Steves), with stated stop counts and durations on the listing.
6. **Interactive map with artwork positions** (ACCORD, ITCares), plus image recognition or a search-by-work option (ITCares, Smartify).
7. **Easy install:** one link or QR, small download. Avoid "a little difficult t[o] download" and "lack of information in the brochure".

**Gaps none of them covers well (where V5 can win)**
1. **Current A/B/C/D/E room numbers everywhere,** with a visible "verified on [date]" stamp. Rick Steves' audio dates from 2019 and its map covers only floor 2. No competitor shows an update date.
2. **Live closed-room handling** for A9, A17/A21, A40/A42, C7/C12, D3 and D9/D11, with redirects such as A40/A42 → C9/C10. Update within days of the museum's notices page changing.
3. **The June 2026 Botticelli rehang** reflected correctly (*Birth of Venus* and *Primavera* now in adjoining rooms).
4. **Time-based routes that mirror the museum's own Fast (A) / Classic (A+D+E) / Complete (A+B+C+D+E)**, plus 60/90/120/180-min options. Only VoiceMap states durations, and nobody maps them to the official itineraries.
5. **Full first-floor coverage** (16th–17th c. D rooms, Caravaggio and Rembrandt/Rubens E rooms). Most guides stop at the Tribune and Michelangelo/Raphael.
6. **Contini Bonacossi and self-portrait tracks.** No competitor mentions either.
7. **Vasari Corridor companion** usable *inside* the Corridor. The official guide must be handed in first, staff "do not offer guided tours", and only ACCORD offers one. Stay compliant with the Corridor rules.
8. **Exit-aware routing.** Warn before the Lanzi staircase, explain the floor-2-only exit versus going down to floor 1, and tell users where the cloakroom is.
9. **Kids/family track** and **accessibility**: transcripts, audio description, large-text mode. None was found among the competitors, and the official accessibility page lists none.
10. **English versions of story-led content.** Factories of Stories has no English track.
