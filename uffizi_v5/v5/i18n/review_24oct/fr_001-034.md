## Names
French forms for the Medici: Cosme premier, François (premier) de Médicis, Ferdinand, Laurent le Magnifique, Cosme l'Ancien, Pierre (016), Pierre le Goutteux (030). Exceptions: in 022 I wrote "François premier **de Médicis**" so French listeners don't hear the King of France. Lorenzo di Pierfrancesco (017, 028), Pierfrancesco (027) and "Lorenzo de' Medici" (028, the English's own words, then glossed as Lorenzo di Pierfrancesco) stay Italian, so he is not confused with Laurent le Magnifique.
Kept as given: Anna Maria Luisa, Pierre-Léopold, Agnolo Poliziano, Vittoria della Rovere. French spellings: Léonard, Michel-Ange, Raphaël, le Titien, Pétrarque, Savonarole, Polyclète, Cléomène.

## Things in the English worth a look (translated as written)
- 022 "the Treaty of Vienna sent her home": none of the cited sources names a treaty. The Medici Venus came back in 1815–16 after the Congress of Vienna, so "Treaty" may be loose wording. Kept.
- 003 "the oldest room in the gallery" probably means the room with the oldest works. Translated literally.
- 006 "From the late fifteen hundreds" → "Dès la fin du seizième siècle". The source gives 1591. There is no year digit to carry because the English has none.
- 003 "early twelve hundreds" and 004 "mid twelve-eighties … early thirteen hundreds" are written as digits ("années 1200 / 1280 / 1300"). The checker does not detect these years.
- 022 "5,780" is written as "5780" (one token). TTS will read it as a number.
