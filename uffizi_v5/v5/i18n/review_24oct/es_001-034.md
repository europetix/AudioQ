## Quotations
- 001: the Family Pact's words were originally Italian. As in the English, they are reported speech ("en palabras del pacto"), without quotation marks.
- 004: Dante, Purgatorio XI. The English (Longfellow) is translated faithfully and still introduced as "En la traducción de Longfellow". It is not presented as Dante's Italian.
- 007: "has the cry" becomes «tiene la fama», the same wording as in 004.
- 009: "Ave gratia plena" stays in Latin, glossed from the English. 019: "Magnificat anima mea Dominum" stays in Latin, glossed «mi alma engrandece al Señor».
- 013: Uccello's line (Vasari, originally Italian) is translated from the English: «¡Qué cosa tan dulce es esta perspectiva!».
- 023: the museum's words are translated from its English page: «a punto de derrumbarse junto con el Ponte Vecchio».
- 024: the inscription (originally Latin) is translated from the English: «Madre de Dios, ten piedad de mí».
- None of the quotations was originally Spanish.

## Choices and flags
- Highlights are called "imprescindibles"; Essential is "versión esencial"; Full is "recorrido completo".
- 012/013: "the gold starts to come down" is rendered "el oro empieza a retirarse", meaning the gold ground gives way. The same wording is used in both tracks.
- 019: "may have hung in a court of the Florentine Republic" is rendered literally as "en un tribunal". If the source means a magistracy or office, "una magistratura" would be more exact. Please check against the Italian page.
- 022: the English says the "Treaty of Vienna" sent the Venus home (1816). This is kept as written. Historically the return followed the Congress of Vienna.
- 022: "5,780" is written as the digits 5780, which the TTS reads as a number. Check that the Mexican voice says "cinco mil setecientas ochenta" (feminine, agreeing with "piezas").
- Imperatives use usted with gender-neutral wording ("vaya directamente", never "directa").
- The Botticelli rooms have no room codes, as in the English. No other room code was added or changed (checked by script against the English, track by track).
- Nothing in the English looked factually wrong apart from the two flags above (019, 022).
