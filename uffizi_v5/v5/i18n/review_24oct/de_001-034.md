- 022: "the Treaty of Vienna sent her home". The return is usually credited to the Congress of Vienna or the 1815 settlement. The phrase is kept as "der Vertrag von Wien".
- 010: The text says "around 1335", while @what says c. 1335–1340. I kept the text.
- 001: The tour lengths are given as "zwei Stunden und fünfundvierzig Minuten". The version names are rendered „Das Wesentliche“ and „Highlights“; please align them with the app's German labels if those differ.
- Decades such as "1580s" and "twelve hundreds" are written as "1580er-Jahre" and "1200er-Jahre", so they appear as digits.
