## Quotations
- 051 « sans fin amour dure »: the original is French. I checked it verbatim on https://www.uffizi.it/en/artworks/ritratto-di-lucrezia-panciatichi (WebFetch, one page, 24 Oct 2026) and kept it word for word. The English gloss "Love lasts without end" is rendered as « L'amour dure sans fin ».
- 044 "Michelangelo of the Bambocciate" (originally Italian): translated as « Michelangelo des Bambochades », bambochade being the standard French term.
- 045 Ferdinando's letter (originally Italian): translated from the English, « dessiné comme par Raphaël, achevé avec l'âme mais sans peine ».
- 046 Latin inscription: "In English it reads" became « Traduite : » followed by the translated English. The hymn « Ton cou est comme une colonne » is translated from the English.
- 057 "the naked woman" (Guidobaldo's letter, originally Italian): « la femme nue ».
- 063 Book of Judith « Le Seigneur l'a frappé par la main d'une femme » and Artemisia's testimony « J'ai dit la vérité et je la dirai toujours, parce que c'est vrai »: both translated from the English, introduced the same way.
- 056 Susanna's elders ("Give in, they say…") is not a quotation in the English either. I left it unquoted.
- 067 "in the museum's own words" is kept: « selon les mots mêmes du musée ».

## Things to know
- The @where lines of 038, 041, 043 and 060 carry internal production notes in parentheses (O19, O20, O35: map doorway, walk-round, stereo sound, pale green walls / 2026 works). I left these out of the French @where because they are not text for travellers. Nothing navigational was dropped.
- French needs more words than English. To stay within the word limit I compressed the phrasing, never the facts. A few tracks are only 0–2 words under the English (042, 045, 065 equal).
- Agreement: I avoided past participles that change with the listener's gender (vous êtes entré, etc.) by using « d'où vous venez », « Vous voici à Venise », « celle qui a arrêté vos pas ».
- I found no factual errors in the English 035–067. Spot checks: Bernini 18–19 in 1617, Raphael about 23 in 1506, Parmigianino died at 37 in 1540, Caravaggio died at 38 in 1610, Medusa unattributed 1598–1631.
