## Decisions for the lead
1. **059 Tintoretto is held: no work confirmed.** Seven WebSearch queries restricted to uffizi.it (Tintoretto / Jacopo Robusti / Leda / Sansovino portrait / Venus and Adonis / "Location D24") found **no Tintoretto artwork page**. They turned up only: Marietta Robusti's *Self portrait with madrigal* ("Deposits"); a 2018 event placing a Tintoretto self-portrait at Bagno a Ripoli; a transparency-portal record of maintenance on a Contini *Venere e Adone* (no location); the virtual tour (old "Tintoretto Gallery", no works named); and the "16th century" tag page (no Tintoretto). *Leda* is on loan in Paris until 24 Jan 2027. So, as instructed:
   - 057's Full cue now names **Room D26, Veronese** as the next main stop, through D24 and D25. The PLAN had "the next main stop is Room D25, Tintoretto".
   - 058's cue sends people through D24 and D25, "the Tintoretto room", to D26.
   - **060 has no Tintoretto line**, because there is no source. If O33 finds works on site, 059 can be written from PLAN, and the 057/058 cues revert to D25.
2. **What to look for in D26: Da Porto, not the Holy Family.** The *Holy Family with St Catherine* page says "D26. Veronese Corridor" = **map D27**. So all cues into D26 (057, 058, bridge 057 E>060) use the confirmed D26 work, the tall *Count Giuseppe da Porto with his son Adriano* (page "D25. Veronese" = map D26). 060 says, citing the page, that the Holy Family hangs in the Veronese Corridor, D27 (O34). The PLAN bridge destination text said "Veronese's Holy Family". I changed only the thing to look for, not the room.
3. **Bridge 057 E>060 is now redundant.** With 059 held, the Full cue of 057 already sends people to D26, so the Essential bridge only repeats it (it says "also"). It can be dropped.
4. **El Greco provenance.** The PLAN says "from the Contini collection". The El Greco page says only "purchased by the Uffizi in 1976" (inventory 1890 no. 9493). I did not say Contini.
5. **067's cue.** The PLAN wording "The next track is for the top of the stairs" fails lint ("next track"). The cue now says: "The last words of this guide are for the top of the stairs."
6. **Silenus material.** 061's PLAN cue said "the marble Silenus". The video page says only "a copy from a Roman bronze statue executed by Jacopo Del Duca between 1571 and 1574", which is ambiguous about the material of the copy. The track and cues give **no material** (O35).
