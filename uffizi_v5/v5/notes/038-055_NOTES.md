# Notes, tracks 038–055 (writer, 24 Oct 2026)

All uffizi.it pages below were read with WebFetch on 24 Oct 2026, one at a time. No browser, no curl. Room codes follow the Oct 2026 map (`research/map/p-2.png`, re-rendered at 300 dpi to read the doorways of B1–B8, D1–D9 and D15–D21). Lint: all 18 tracks OK. Spoken words 4,526. The plan's midpoints give about 4,160: the excess is mostly the navigation cues (042, 044, 046, 047), which I kept complete.

## Lead decisions
1. **Length.** About 370 words over the midpoints. If the guide runs long, cut first: 051's Holy Family paragraph, 055's busts sentence (DATED anyway), 049's Fontainebleau paragraph, 046's alchemy line.
2. **045 has no Correggio to look at.** The *Adoration of the Christ Child* is in Dresden until 10 Jan 2027, and the *Rest on the Flight* page still says "D8. Correggio - Parmigianino" (O22). So 045 talks about Correggio but points only at the Parmigianino. If O22 finds the *Rest on the Flight* in D4, a sentence can be added after the loan sentence.
3. **048: no Pontormo is confirmed on view in D12.** All three D12 Pontormo pages are works on the loan list. The track presents Pontormo as a person, with the loan as one sentence, and shows Rosso's *Enthroned Madonna* (page "D12"). The Parmigianino *Zachariah* is not named (O25).
4. **Bernini 041: the PLAN's "Medici tombs" link is not on the page.** The page names Michelangelo's St Peter's *Pietà*, the *River God* and the Sistine *Adam*. I used the page. I also dropped "the Uffizi's only Bernini marble", because no source confirms it.
5. **Castagno 038: the page links the figures to Donatello, not Masaccio.** I wrote "as solid as sculpture" and named neither.
6. **Bramantino 040: no date is spoken.** The page's catalogue date is c. 1512–1514, but its own text places the work in the first half of the 1520s.
7. **Correggio's birthplace.** The PLAN says "both painters are from Parma". The page says Correggio was born at Correggio, so 045 says he worked in Parma.
8. **Bridges.** There are four (046 H, 049 E, 052 H, 055 E), the only rows of PLAN §3 for these tracks. Wherever the shorter versions follow the Full cue's next main stop (041→042, 042→045, 046 E→048, 052 E→055), no bridge is needed.
