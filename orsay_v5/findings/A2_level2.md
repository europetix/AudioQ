# A2 — Level 2 block audit (tracks 12–19) — Orsay V4 → V5

Auditor: A2 · 5 Oct 2026 · Sources: v4_tracks/12–19.txt, prev.txt (route lines 40–115, 180–210).
Grades: OFF = musee-orsay.fr (or other official) snippet · SEC2 = two reputable independent · WEAK = wiki/blog/single · CONTRA = contradicted · NONE = nothing found.

## Headline (read first)

1. **Phase 2 "level 2" no longer exists as written.** Of the eight tracks, only the Rodin / Claudel / Nabi / Pavillon Amont material of T12 is on level 2.
   - **MNR room (T14)** is room **10b, level 0, at the end of the nave**, not on level 2 (OFF).
   - **Carpeaux's La Danse (T13)**: the work Orsay shows is the **original stone group** in the **level 0 central sculpture aisle** (OFF).
   - **Van Gogh (T15–T17)** has been in **level 5, room 36**, "at the end of the Impressionist gallery", since 6 Feb 2024 (OFF).
   - **Gauguin / post-Impressionist rooms (T18)** are on **level 5** (nine rooms renovated in 2019; OFF and SEC2).
   - **Proposed route:** Level 0 gains Carpeaux (stone) and MNR 10b at the end of the nave. Level 2 becomes Rodin terrace, Gates of Hell, Claudel, Nabis and Décors modernes. Level 5 runs clock (Salon de l'horloge) → Impressionists → Van Gogh rooms 36–37 → post-Impressionists and Gauguin → Café Campana.
2. **T13's main claim is false.** Orsay does not hold a "life-sized" working plaster of La Danse to stand in front of. It holds a **half-size model (RF 818)**, sketches and fragment busts. The track also says the stone on the Garnier facade today is the 1869 original. Later in the same track it says, correctly, that the original was replaced by a copy in 1964.
3. **T15 provenance is wrong.** The Orsay self-portrait came from **Dr Gachet's collection**, given by Paul and Marguerite Gachet in 1949. It is not the portrait sent to his mother. Both Van Gogh "quotes" in T15 could not be found in the letters.
4. All eight tracks break C rules:
   - **Length:** 617–1,203 words against a 180–360 limit.
   - **Jargon:** "Track N" cues; "Half A/Half B" in T18.
   - **Command openers.**
   - **Parentheses** in T12, T15, T17 and T18.
   - **Reading-guide wording:** "before you read anything" in T15.
