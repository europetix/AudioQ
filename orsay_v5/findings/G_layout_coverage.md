## 1. Current layout (2025–2026)

**Entrance / security.** From 10 Mar 2026 there is a temporary entrance on the Seine side and a separate temporary exit on the quai Valéry-Giscard-d'Estaing side, until about summer 2028 (SEC2). The forecourt's six *Continents* statues and the three animals (rhinoceros, elephant, horse) are removed during the works and shown at the Jardin des Plantes (SEC2). All collections stay open (SEC2). The ticket and information desk is the meeting point for official tours (OFF).

**Level 0 (rez-de-chaussée): nave and side rooms, about 1848–1870s**
- Central aisle of sculpture (allée centrale) holds academic sculpture and Couture's *Romains de la décadence*, which hangs in the aisle itself (OFF). At the far end is the **Opéra space**: Carpeaux's *La Danse* plaster (OFF), the Garnier Opéra section and the model of the Opéra quarter. The model lies under a glass floor at the end of the nave; its exact place is not confirmed by an official snippet (NONE).
- **Lille side** (academic painting): room 1 Ingres, *La Source* (OFF); room 3 Cabanel, *Naissance de Vénus* (OFF); Bouguereau, Chassériau, Delacroix, Gérôme, Ingres listed under "Académisme" (plan-guide spring 2026, OFF). Symbolists (Moreau, Puvis) are on this side. The name "Salle du Bord de l'eau" is unverified (NONE).
- **Seine side** (Realism): room 5 Millet, *Des glaneuses* (OFF); room 7 Courbet, *L'Atelier* and *Un enterrement* (OFF); Daumier. The plan-guide heading is "Le réalisme ou l'art vivant: Courbet, Daumier, Millet" (OFF). Room 14 Manet: *Olympia*, *Le Fifre*, *Lola de Valence*, *Émile Zola* and others (OFF).
- **Room 10b**: MNR room "À qui appartiennent ces œuvres ?", opened 5 May 2026 (OFF).
- Whistler's *Arrangement in Grey and Black No. 1* is on the "niveau nef", hung with Fantin-Latour (OFF; room number not found). It was lent to the Clark, Williamstown, until 27 Sep 2025 (SEC2) and is assumed back.
- Far end, between the two towers: Rodin's *Gates of Hell*, on axis with the aisle since 2022 (OFF).

**Level 2 (niveau médian): terraces and Lille-side rooms, about 1880–1914**
- Sculpture terraces (Seine and Lille), with sculpture 1870/1880–1914 (OFF plan-guide). The *Terrasse Rodin* is listed on the plan (OFF). Claudel's *L'Âge mûr* is on this level (position not confirmed).
- Room 62: Pompon's *Ours blanc* (OFF, artwork page 15530). A Basilico photo is captioned "sur la terrasse côté Lille", which is the historic spot.
- Rooms 61–66: Art Nouveau from Belgium, Spain, France and Italy, including Guimard and Gallé (OFF).
- Room 68: Toulouse-Lautrec, *Jane Avril dansant* (OFF).
- Rooms 70–72: Nabis (Bonnard, Denis, Vallotton, Vuillard) and the Marcie-Rivière collection (OFF).
- Restaurant du musée: level 2, in the former Hôtel d'Orsay dining room (OFF).
- **Pavillon Amont**: 5 levels of decorative arts, connected across levels; opened 20 Oct 2011 with the renovated Impressionist gallery and the Café Campana (culture.gouv.fr, OFF).
- Medals: a permanent selection has been shown since the 2012 exhibition "In the Palm of the Hand" and includes Roty's study for *La Semeuse*, 1887 (OFF node/504/medals). Its room is not found (NONE), so don't plan a track until it is located.

**Level 5 (niveau supérieur): Impressionist and Post-Impressionist galleries**
- Clock viewpoint before the gallery entrance, described as a "breathing space" with a clock window over Paris (SEC2).
- Rooms 29–35, Impressionism (OFF plan):
  - 29: Monet *Coquelicots*, Monet *Déjeuner* fragments, Manet *Déjeuner* (SEC2)
  - 31: Monet *Gare Saint-Lazare*; Caillebotte *Raboteurs de parquet*; Degas *La Classe de danse* (all OFF)
  - 32: Degas *Petite danseuse de quatorze ans* (bronze cast 1921–31); Morisot *Le Berceau* (both OFF)
  - 34: Monet *Rouen Cathedral* series (OFF)
- Rooms 36–37: Van Gogh (OFF).
- Rooms 38–47 and up: Neo- and Post-Impressionism (OFF plan). Room 38: Seurat *Le Cirque* (OFF). Pont-Aven (Bernard, Gauguin, Sérusier) and "Gauguin à Tahiti" are listed in the spring 2026 plan-guide (OFF). Sérusier's *Le Talisman* is in room 48 (OFF snippet; level not stated, presumably 5).
- **Café Campana** is at the exit of the Impressionist gallery, behind the great clock (OFF).
- Getting up: escalators from the nave and the Seine-side lifts (SEC2).

**Closures and changes 2026:**
- Forecourt and marquee works run 10 Mar 2026 – summer 2028 (SEC2).
- *Burial* restoration finished Aug 2026 (SEC2).
- Tokyo loans run 14 Nov 2026 – 28 Mar 2027 (OFF/SEC2).
- The Monet loan to the Artizon Museum, Tokyo, of about 50 works ran 7 Feb – 24 May 2026 and is now over (OFF).
- Decorative arts 1850–1890 galleries open 4 Dec 2026 (OFF/SEC2).

## 3. Recommended order (≤3 h, no backtracking)

**0 → 5 → 2 → exit** is the most efficient. Level 5's Post-Impressionist rooms run straight on from Impressionism to Van Gogh, the Neo-Impressionists, Gauguin and the Café Campana. After that, level 2 (Lautrec, Nabis, Art Nouveau, terraces) sits on the way back down to the exit.

1. **Level 0, about 55 min.** Entrance and nave overview, then:
   - Lille side: Couture in the aisle, Ingres (room 1), Cabanel (room 3), Symbolists.
   - Seine side: Millet (room 5), Courbet (room 7), Manet *Olympia* (room 14), Whistler.
   - MNR room 10b.
   - Walk the aisle to the Opéra space (*La Danse*, quarter model), then the *Gates of Hell* at the end.
2. **Escalator or lift to level 5, about 85 min.** Clock viewpoint, then:
   - rooms 29 (both *Déjeuners*, *Coquelicots*), 31 (*Saint-Lazare*, Caillebotte, Degas class), 32 (Degas dancer, Morisot), 34 (Rouen), Renoir and Cézanne;
   - rooms 36–37 Van Gogh;
   - room 38 and on: Seurat, Pont-Aven, Gauguin, *Talisman* (room 48), Redon, Rousseau;
   - **Café Campana** break at the gallery exit.
3. **Down to level 2, about 35 min.** Lautrec (room 68), Nabis (70–72), Art Nouveau (61–66), Pompon (62 / Lille terrace), Rodin and Claudel terraces. Then the escalator down to the exit.

If the producers want the V4 "Campana closing chord", use **0 → 2 → 5** instead and run level 5 in the same order, so Campana is last. The cost is the lift trip back down to the exit at the end.
