- Rooms 38 and up: Post-Impressionists. Seurat and Neo-Impressionism, with Le Cirque, in room 38. Source: musee-orsay.fr/en/artworks/le-cirque-3015 (OFF). Le Talisman is in room 48. Source: musee-orsay.fr Talisman pages (OFF). The 2025 plan-guide lists on level 5: Cézanne, Seurat, **Gauguin, Pont-Aven (Bernard, Gauguin, Sérusier), Gauguin à Tahiti, Redon and the Nabis**, and the Café Campana. Source: musee-orsay.fr/sites/default/files/2025-04/PLANGUIDE Orsay Europe hiver25.pdf and 2025-08 été25 (OFF, snippet only).
- Café Campana is "at the exit of the Impressionist gallery … behind one of the great exterior clocks". Opening hours: Tue–Sun 10:30–16:45, Thu until 20:45. Source: musee-orsay.fr/fr/visite/restauration (OFF).

**Route consequence (HIGH, cross-track):** the guide puts Van Gogh (T15–17) and Gauguin (T18) on level 2 in "Phase 2". Official 2024–2025 sources put both on level 5. Van Gogh is in rooms 36–37, between the Impressionist rooms and the Post-Impressionist rooms. So the level-5 phase walks right past rooms 36–37 between T28 and T29, and T31's recap ("you met Van Gogh … downstairs") is wrong. Phases 2 and 3 need to be re-sequenced. Expected order: T19 clock → rooms 29–35 → Van Gogh 36–37 → Seurat 38 → Cézanne → Pont-Aven/Gauguin → Nabis 48 → Café Campana.

**Order within rooms 29–35 (MED, E):** the current track order zig-zags through the rooms. It goes 29 Coquelicots → 31 Gare → 34 Rouen → back to 30/31 Bal → 31 Classe → 32 Danseuse and Berceau → back to 31 Raboteurs. Suggested order: 29 Pissarro and Monet Coquelicots → 30/31 Bal → 31 Gare Saint-Lazare, Caillebotte, Classe de danse → 32 Danseuse and Berceau → 34 Rouen.
