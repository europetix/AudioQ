# FR review log — Musée d'Orsay V5 (75 tracks)

Reviewer: independent native French editor (France, Vivienne). Source of truth: v5/tracks/NNN.perf.txt.

## Global decisions
- Every track read sentence by sentence against the English, including @where and every closing navigation paragraph and loan/option condition (007, 008, 011/012, 020, 021, 045, 046, 052, 053, 055, 056). The conditions were already accurate. One fix: 007 now says "ce mur" to match "that wall".
- Quotations (rule 12 / point 8): each of the 37 « » passages in the bodies has a source URL in notes/fr_*.md. I re-checked these against the cited pages myself (WebFetch, no WebSearch needed): 001/075 Detaille, 003 Juvénal, 005 Zola, 009 Millet, 011 Courbet ×3, 013 musée, 014 Manet, 015 Zola (the museum page attributes it to Zola), 017 musée, 018 Bazille, 023 musée, 030 Leroy, 036 musée, 037 Rivière, 042 musée, 044 lettres 800/805, 045 lettre 705, 046 lettre 691, 047 lettre 879, 048 RM23, 053 Gauguin, 064 Paul Claudel, 073 Pompon. All matched the source wording. The only differences are the modernised accents (connaître, pâleur, & → et) and the ellipses the notes already declare. Nothing had to become reported speech. The reported-speech passages the translators chose stay as they are: Champfleury in 011, the museum in 016, Monet in 039, Artaud in 044, Fénéon in 046, de Gaulle in 062, and l'incarnation de la danse in 068.
- The « » in the @where fields of 019, 025 and 068 are sign and room names, not quotations. 058 « yeux clos » and « inca » are the museum's own words, verified on its page.
- Titles: the French titles were checked against @what and the notes, and no change was needed. One naming fix: the room in 018 is now « Galerie Seine 2 », as in 014 and 015.
- Tense: the bodies use the historic present with the passé composé. I replaced the stray literary passé simple forms that broke the oral register (001, 012, 038, 039). A few remaining "fut" forms read naturally and were kept.
- Consistency: Chat noir (as in the museum's room title) in 050. The dancers of Carpeaux's La Danse are « danseuses » in 026, as in 022.
- Length: three trims (001, 005, 057) keep every track at or under the English word count after my edits. The final check passes 75/75 OK.
