**021 Degas, Bellelli**
- @where: "a mother in black… the father seated apart" → "a mother in mourning… the father turned away". "Black" and "seated" are not on R6.
- "That's him in the frame" → "His portrait hangs in a frame on the wall, close to her face". This is R6 wording and also works for a listener standing at Sémiramis.
- "Now read the family. The mother stands impressively dignified" → "Now read the family, on the canvas or in your mind's eye. The mother is impressively dignified". "Stands" is unsourced, and the new wording works at the fallback.
- "After his death the Musée du Luxembourg bought it" → "In nineteen eighteen the Musée du Luxembourg bought it". R6 gives the 1918 sale, not the timing relative to his death.
- "the way Ingres taught" → "the Ingres way" (R6 wording).

**054 Cheval blanc**: every claim matched R7 (OFF). No change.

**055 Oviri**
- "the largest piece he ever made" → "his largest piece in clay". R7 says "his largest piece" in the context of his ceramics.
- Christopher Gray quote turned into reported speech: "saw in this figure an expression of Gauguin's deep disillusionment and discouragement". The wording on R7 matches, but R7's header says its OFF extracts "may be lightly paraphrased, check against the page before printing a quote", and NEW9_NOTES flagged the same. **056 Charmeuse de serpents**
- "black Eve" quote turned into reported speech: "The museum's commentary likens the figure to a black Eve in an unsettling Garden of Eden, charming a snake every bit as frightening as the serpent of Genesis was seductive". It had the same R7 extract caveat as the Gray quote.

- @where: "It came back from loans in July 2026" → "It was on loan until July 2026". R7 says the rehang is not confirmed.

**058 Redon, Les Yeux clos**

- The "refined, savage, grandiose and delicate" quote became reported speech: "In an article that same year, he praised Gauguin as refined and savage, grandiose and delicate". R7 gives a November 1903 article, and the same extract caveat applies.

**059 Chat Noir**

**060 Cinema**: every claim matched R6 (e). The optical toys are already hedged. No change.

**062 Salle des fêtes**
