## 2) Problems only

### 052 — HIGH, rule: the Coordinator decision is not applied
- **Quote:** "According to the story, this canvas began as a commission from a pharmacist in Tahiti. And the pharmacist refused it, because the horse was too green." The story also drives the @listen card, the opening line, "So there is an irony in what comes next", "Ask yourself how white it really is", "A horse with green in its coat is in good company" and the last line, "If the pharmacist really did say no…".
- **Why:** PLAN, Coordinator decisions: "remove it in revision and rebuild the track from OFF facts only. If it can't reach 200 words, hold the track." The story is still there, hedged. That hedging matches the older PLAN row but not the later decision. The only support is SEC1 (FACTS_R4 054: "SEC1: artsandculture … A second reputable source is needed"). That gives three WEAK claims: the commission, the refusal and the green in the horse's coat.
- **Closest evidence:** FACTS_R4 054: "1898, second Tahiti stay. Acquired 1927 (OFF existence …; dimensions, inventory and room not found). Restored in 2019 (AUDIT G)."
- **Fix:** hold track 052. After the story is removed, the OFF facts left are 1898, the second Tahiti stay, acquired 1927 and restored 2019. That is far short of 200 words. Change 051's closing cue to: "Then walk on towards the Nabi rooms and find Paul Sérusier's very small painting on wood, Le Talisman. Play its track there."
- **Also NOTFOUND in 052:**
  - @what says "Acquired by the State in 1927". R4 says only "Acquired 1927". Fix: "Acquired in 1927."
  - "Gauguin was not a painter who kept to the expected colours." No line supports this. Fix: cut the sentence. It goes anyway if the track is held.
- **Also a repetition, and it fails during the loan:** "circle a woman's face like a Japanese print, blow a small Maori motif up into a giant idol, and paint a dog red" retells the anecdotes from 050 and 051. During the Tokyo loan the visitor will not have seen Arearea.

### 050 — rule: the closing cue sends the visitor to a loaned work with no fallback
- **Quote:** "Tahiti is close by. Find Arearea, two women seated near a tree with a red dog in front, and play its track there."
- **Why:** Arearea is in Tokyo from 14 Nov 2026 to 28 Mar 2027, and it may come off the wall earlier (FACTS_R4 053; G §0 row 7). A visitor facing an empty wall never reaches the fallback inside 051.
- **Fix:** "Tahiti is close by. Find Arearea, two women seated near a tree with a red dog in front, or the empty place where it hangs, and play its track there."
