WHY THIS EDITION
================
The previous guide said every claim had been verified, but an independent check found about 48 wrong facts (an "Almohad"
Lion Gate, María de Padilla in rooms finished after her death, Almohad "cisterns" that the Alcázar itself calls Gothic
vaults, swapped Game of Thrones scenes), places described in the wrong part of the palace, and price and ticket advice.
This edition was rebuilt from scratch:
1. Every space is named by its NUMBER on the Alcázar's own map (the official leaflet, 1-53) and its Spanish name, as on
   site. The route map that comes with this guide uses the same numbers.
2. Facts were checked on the Alcázar's own pages and publications (30 Oct 2026); legends are told as legends. Restorations
   and anything that may change are single, dated sentences.
3. The route follows the leaflet: Puerta del León, the first courtyards, the Casa de la Contratación, King Pedro's palace,
   the Gothic palace and the Baths, the gardens, and out by the Apeadero to the Patio de Banderas. Every track ends by naming
   the next number and one thing to look for. Two independent reviewers re-checked every route and fact.
4. Three lengths (one recording, three folders):
   Full tour   @@N_FULL@@ tracks, about 2 h with walking (the last 2 tracks are the optional Cuarto Real Alto Extras)
   Essential   @@N_ESS@@ tracks, about 1 h 15:  @@ESSENTIAL@@
   Highlights  @@N_HI@@ tracks, about 45 min:   @@HIGHLIGHTS@@
   In the shorter versions a short spoken line at the end of a track tells you the next stop.
5. The Cuarto Real Alto (the upper royal rooms) is a separate timed visit: its two optional Extras are at the end.
6. The Yo Tours sound: voice finishing, a soft room tone and a short chime before each track. MP3s are labelled for phones.

HOW TO MAKE THE AUDIO (on a Mac)
================================
English:          double-click generate_audio.command  (voice 1 = Ava)
                  option 1 = all three lengths (records once; folders _Essential and _Highlights)
                  option 2 = two voice samples (007 the palace facade + 014 the emperor's wedding)
                  option 3 = Highlights only (quickest; option 1 later reuses those recordings)
Other languages:  double-click generate_audio_ES_FR_DE.command and pick the language, then the same options.
Needs internet, python3, ffmpeg (brew install ffmpeg) and an up-to-date edge-tts:
  python3 -m pip install --user --upgrade edge-tts
If it stops, run it again: finished tracks are skipped.
