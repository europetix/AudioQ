**Headline.** The route rules held: no left/right, no "number 11", no "next track", no prices, hours, tickets or shop, every Extra announced with "the next main stop is…", order matches PLAN, exit via 53 to the Patio de Banderas, Cuarto Real Alto described as a separate timed visit. **Eight factual errors remain** (four of them contradicted by the very sources the writers cite). The worst are 030 (the Pedro-era plaster is on the pool, not the pavilion), 019 ("they do not show the sack": the series has a *Sack of Tunis* panel), 018 (the whole palace lost its vaults; only number 32 did), 028 (pavilion "open again" is unconfirmed) and 023 (the snow story is told back to front). Also: no `pronunciation.py` exists for this guide yet, although `render_edge.py` imports one, and "Carlos V" / "Felipe II" will be read as letters by an English voice.

## C. The 1526 wedding: consistent wording for 012, 014, 021, 028

**What the sources support.** GPC (V), quoting Sandoval: Charles entered Seville on 10 March; "aquella misma noche los desposó por palabras de presente el cardenal legado… en la cuadra grande, que llaman media naranja" (= number 15). After midnight "fué aderezado un altar en una cámara del alcázar… dijo allí la misa y los veló" (an altar set up in a chamber; the room is not named). That night they "se pasaron a cenar a sus aposentos". PLEG (V) names that chamber the king's oratory, today's Salón del Techo de Carlos V (number 13), "escenario de la misa de esponsales", but cites no source. ITIN puts a banquet in the Sala Gótica (33), also without a source.

So 012 and 014 do not contradict each other on the facts: the vows were in 15 and the Mass was in a chamber, which one scholar identifies as 13. What fails is the wording, which makes them *sound* contradictory, and 014 is the only wedding track heard in Essential and Highlights. Proposed:

- **014** (replace "After midnight, now 11 March, the Archbishop of Toledo said Mass in the palace and gave the couple the nuptial blessing."):
  > After midnight, now 11 March, an altar was set up in a chamber of the palace, and the Archbishop of Toledo said Mass there and gave the couple the nuptial blessing. One historian of the Alcázar places that Mass in the king's old oratory, number 13, the Salón del Techo de Carlos Quinto.
- **012** (replace "The historian Alfonso Pleguezuelo also identifies this room as the setting of the Mass for the marriage of Charles the Fifth and Isabel of Portugal."):
  > The historian Alfonso Pleguezuelo identifies this room as the place where, after midnight, the wedding Mass of Charles the Fifth and Isabel of Portugal was said. The vows themselves, the chronicler tells us, were exchanged in the great domed hall, number 15, the Salón de los Embajadores.
- **021**: hedge as in the 021 table.
- **028**: "married… in the Salón de los Embajadores": keep.
