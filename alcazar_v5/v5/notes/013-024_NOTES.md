## For the lead: decisions and conflicts
1. **Dowry (014): the PLAN wording is wrong against its own source.** García Parody & Caballero (2018), re-read, say "900.000 **doblas** de oro castellanas" (net 660,332). "Ducados" appears in the article only for a merchant's payment. The PLAN gives "900,000 ducats" and bans "doblas". I **left the dowry out**. If you want it, use "900,000 gold doblas" and lift the ban.
2. **Isabel's age.** The same article says "23". Patrimonio Nacional gives her birth as 24 Oct 1503, so she was 22. I used **22**, as the PLAN does.
3. **The name "Danza" (024).** The PLAN says no story for the name is sourced. The Alcázar's own EXPO-MERCURIO PDF says the Jardín de la Danza "data del siglo XVIII" and had "un pequeño escenario destinado a danzas y representaciones". I used it, hedged ("Hence, perhaps, the name"). The PDF also names "Pierre-César de Meaux" for the 18th-century remodelling. I could not confirm that name anywhere else, so it is **not used**. Cut the sentence if you prefer the PLAN line.
4. **Almohad origin of the Baths (022).** The Baños page (?p=10943), re-read, does **not** say Almohad. I used the leaflet's line instead: "much of the Patio del Crucero" belongs to the Islamic period. The track says "Islamic", not "Almohad".
5. **Wedding banquet in the Bóvedas (021).** The Patronato's school itineraries PDF calls the Sala Gótica the "Sala de las Fiestas" because the 1526 wedding banquet was held there. andalucia.com says the wedding "may have been held" there. The track says "may have moved in here" and gives the Alcázar's notes as the source.
6. **The Tunis honesty line (019).** The sack of Tunis is sourced from *Mediterranea ricerche storiche* 49 (2020) and Mulay Hasan's reinstatement from the Royal Collection Trust. Vermeyen as an eyewitness comes from the Mechelen museum. I found no reliable figures for deaths or enslavement, so none are given.
7. **First mentions in the shorter versions.** In Highlights, the 016 H bridge is the first mention of 38 and 34, without "on your Alcázar map", to stay within 40 words. In Essential and Highlights, number 39 gets "on your Alcázar map" in the 022 bridge.
