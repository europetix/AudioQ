# ES notes: Real Alcázar de Sevilla, tracks 001–035 and bridges (30 Oct 2026)

Self-check: `assemble.py --check … es` reports all 35 tracks OK. Word counts are 94–99% of the English, and no track is longer. Tags, pauses, paragraphs and years all pass.
Files: es/001.json … es/035.json, es/bridges.txt (10 lines, the same keys and map numbers as the English).

## Official names used (leaflet legend, research/VISIT_ROUTE_AND_SPACES_OCT2026.md)
- Every number from 1 to 53 is unchanged, and so is every Spanish space name as the English gives it.
- Spoken forms follow rule 13: "Salón del Techo de Carlos Quinto", "Salón del Techo de Felipe Segundo" and "Pabellón de Carlos Quinto". The @title/@where text keeps the leaflet forms "Carlos V" and "Felipe II".
- No. 19: the spoken text uses the leaflet name "la Escalera al Cuarto Real Alto" where the English has "the stair to the Cuarto Real Alto".
- No. 34: the leaflet prints "Baños Doña María de Padilla" (no "de"). I followed the English, "Baños de Doña María de Padilla". Please check which form you want.
- Regnal names spoken in words: Abderramán Tercero, Fernando Tercero, Alfonso Décimo, Alfonso Undécimo, Alfonso Cuarto de Portugal, Pedro Primero, Carlos Quinto, Felipe Segundo, Felipe Tercero, Felipe Quinto, Alfonso Trece.
- Pedro's nickname "the Just" is given as "el Justiciero", the usual Spanish epithet.

## Quotations
- **007, façade inscription (originally Castilian):** quoted verbatim as «El muy alto et muy noble et muy poderoso et muy conqueridor don Pedro por la gracia de Dios rey de Castilla et de León». I checked the wording in J. Marquer, Anales de Historia del Arte 23 (2013): https://revistas.ucm.es/index.php/ANHA/article/download/42850/40706/62639. "Mandó hacer esta obra" is reported speech, without quotation marks.
- **007, Nasrid motto (Arabic):** «no hay vencedor sino Dios», a faithful rendering of the English.
- **010, palace greeting (Arabic):** «Gloria a nuestro señor el sultán don Pedro, ayúdele Dios». This is Marquer's own Spanish rendering (same URL, checked).
- **013, door inscription (Arabic):** reported speech, as in the English.
- **014, Sandoval (originally Spanish):** quoted verbatim as «la cuadra grande, que llaman media naranja». Checked in García Parody & Caballero (2018), which quotes Sandoval: https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/7042473.pdf. "Los desposó por palabras de presente" uses the same wording, without quotation marks.
- **022, the teachers' note (originally Spanish):** quoted verbatim as «No hay rigor histórico». Checked in https://alcazarsevilla.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/02-Itinerarios-y-Actividades.pdf.
- **021, "Sala de las Fiestas":** checked in the same PDF ("A esta sala también se le llama la Sala de las Fiestas"). It is used as a name, not as a quotation.
- **018, leaflet line on the Gothic palace:** reported speech with no quotation marks: "escenificó la transición drástica al nuevo orden cristiano". The leaflet itself reads "del nuevo orden".
- **034, Niculoso's signature:** not a Spanish quotation. It is given in reported form with no quotation marks: "Niculoso Francisco, italiano, me hizo". I did not fetch the original ("NICULOSO FRANCISCO ITALIANO ME FECIT", per Pleguezuelo).
- **Paraphrases of official texts (no quotation marks):** 008 Atarazanas "too damp", 024 the Italianate gardens line, 027 "única fuente órgano…", 031 the "styles dominant" line, 035 the residence list.

## Choices and flags
- **Game of Thrones names:** these follow the Spanish dub. Ellaria Arena, las Serpientes de Arena, los Jardines del Agua de Dorne, la casa Martell, Jaime Lannister. Some Latin American listeners may know Ellaria as "Ellaria Sand".
- **009:** I dropped the English glosses "of the Seafarers" and "the Navigators" because they repeat themselves in Spanish. The names Mareantes and Navegantes are both kept.
- **028:** "if the pavilion is still behind screens" → "Si el pabellón sigue cubierto por vallas de obra".
- **Bridges 022 E>025 and H>025:** the English "skip the garden front" is unclear. I translated it literally as "omita el frente de los jardines". Worth a check.
- **013:** the Hijri year 767 is written in digits.
- **English facts:** I found none that looked wrong. "Great-great-grandfather" (018) checks out: Alfonso X → Sancho IV → Fernando IV → Alfonso XI → Pedro I.
