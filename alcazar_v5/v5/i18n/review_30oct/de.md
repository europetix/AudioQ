## Official titles / names used
- Map numbers and Spanish space names exactly as in the legend of the official leaflet
  (https://alcazarsevilla.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/triptico_alcazar_sevilla.pdf, via
  research/VISIT_ROUTE_AND_SPACES_OCT2026.md). Where the English speaks "Carlos Quinto / Felipe Segundo" inside a space
  name, kept as spoken (Salón del Techo de Carlos Quinto, Pabellón de Carlos Quinto, Salón del Techo de Felipe Segundo).
  @title/@where keep the printed forms "Carlos V", "Felipe II" as in the English headers.
- Formula: first mention "Nummer N auf dem Alcázar-Plan, der/die/den …" (article declined); then "Nummer N".
  Automated check: the set of map numbers in every DE track equals the English set; no "Nummer 11".
- Signs kept in Spanish: Salida (once glossed "zum Ausgang" in 032), Palacio Gótico, Casa de la Contratación.
- Persons: König Pedro / König Peter der Erste (glossary: "Pedro the First" → Peter der Erste, "King Pedro" → König
  Pedro); Karl der Fünfte; Philipp der Zweite/Dritte/Fünfte; Alfons der Zehnte/Elfte/Dreizehnte; Alfons der Vierte von
  Portugal; Ferdinand der Dritte; Isabella (die Katholische), Isabella von Portugal; Ferdinand (the Catholic Monarch);
  Johann von Gent; Blanca von Bourbon. Kept Spanish: Enrique, Juan, Constanza, María de Padilla.
- Game of Thrones names follow the German dub: "die Sandschlangen", Jaime Lannister. Film title: "Lawrence von Arabien".
- 001: tour lengths rendered "die vollständige Tour / die Kernversion / die Highlights". If the German app labels
  Essential/Highlights differently, align these two words.
- Titles of 004 and 018 use worded regnal numbers ("Alfons dem Elften", "Alfons dem Zehnten") in case titles are voiced.
