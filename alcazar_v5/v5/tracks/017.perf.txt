[conspiratorial] For decades, a passage from this corner of the palace to the gardens lay unused. Since 2025, it has been open again.

It runs from the Patio de las Muñecas to the Jardín del Príncipe, the Prince's Garden. When conservators worked on it, they recovered its tiles, its carved plaster and its floors, and they found historic details that had long been hidden.

The prince in these names is usually said to be Juan, son and heir of Fernando and Isabel, the Catholic Monarchs. He was born in Seville in 1478, and tradition places his birth in these rooms. They are said to have been the queen's bedroom until Isabel built the floor above. [quietly] Juan was heir to both his parents' crowns. He died in 1497, still in his teens, and never reigned.

[pause] You will see where the passage comes out later, at number 35 on the Alcázar map, the Jardín del Príncipe. For now, stay inside the palace. The route goes through the Gothic halls first.

Number 31, the Patio del Crucero: follow the signs for the Palacio Gótico, back through the Patio de las Doncellas to the Patio de la Montería and along its gallery.
