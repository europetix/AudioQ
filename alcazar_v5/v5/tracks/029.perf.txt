[amused] In October 2014, these gardens left Spain for a while. They became Dorne.

The crew of Game of Thrones came to film the fifth season here, and the Alcázar played the Water Gardens of Dorne, home of House Martell. The scenes are often mixed up, so here they are as the Andalucía Film Commission lists them.

In the Salón de los Embajadores, under the golden dome, Jaime Lannister is brought before Prince Doran. At the Grutesco, in front of the Mercury pool, Doran appears with his guard Areo Hotah and with Ellaria Sand. Myrcella and Prince Trystane stroll together in the Jardín de las Damas. In the Baths of María de Padilla, the guards arrest Ellaria.

[pause] And right here, beside this pavilion, the Sand Snakes fight Jaime and Bronn over Myrcella. That one scene took more than a week to shoot.

[lightly] Dorne was not the Alcázar's first screen role. Decades earlier, parts of the palace stood in for Damascus in Lawrence of Arabia.

Number 45, the Cenador del León, is the next stop, just beyond, at the end of this open space: a pavilion behind a pool presided over by a lion.
