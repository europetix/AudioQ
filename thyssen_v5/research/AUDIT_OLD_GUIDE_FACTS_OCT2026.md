# Fact audit: old English audio guide, Museo Nacional Thyssen-Bornemisza (V4.5, "Tour No. 47", 70 tracks)

Audited 23 October 2026 for the Yo Tours rebuild. Source: `/home/claude/audioq/thyssen_v5/source/old.txt` (same text as `old/NNN.txt`).

**Grades:** OK = confirmed on the work's museothyssen.org page, another official source, or two independent reputable sources. WRONG = contradicted by a source (correct fact given). UNVERIFIED = no adequate source found. HEDGE = broadly right but needs softer or more precise wording.

**Method:** WebSearch and WebFetch only, one page at a time. Most work pages are the museum's own collection pages (`museothyssen.org/en/collection/...` or `/en/node/NNNNN`).

**For the room-check agent:** many museum pages carry a display status. Any status below is quoted from the page as read on 23 Oct 2026. "Not exhibited" on a museum page may be temporary (a loan, conservation or a rehang), so confirm it on the live plan.

**Pages that could not be read:**
- britannica.com/biography/Michelangelo: the permission request timed out, and it was not retried.
- museothyssen.org artist index pages for Cassatt and Constable, and the per-work slugs for Memling, Holbein, Caravaggio, Goya, Van Gogh, Kandinsky and Lissitzky: these returned client errors. The same pages were then found and read through their `/node/` URLs, except Cassatt and Constable.

## Summary: the worst errors

1. **Works named or featured that the museum currently lists as Not exhibited (12):** Christus (006), Antonello (008), Dürer (011), Cranach (012), Rubens (019, after its special display closed on 13 Sep 2026), Zurbarán (020), Van Gogh (041), Marc (046), Kandinsky (049), Bacon (062). Two more are named in intros: Antonello in 002, and Dürer and Cranach in 010. Holbein (013), Goya (031) and Dalí (056) have no room on their pages. Ten single-work tracks point visitors at walls without the work.
2. **Track 068 (Constable):** the Carmen collection's Constable, *The Lock*, was sold in 2012. No replacement could be confirmed.
3. **Track 039 (Monet):** wrong date (painted 1899–1901, sold by Nov 1901, not 1903), and it is a Carmen Thyssen work, not permanent Room 33.
4. **Physical descriptions contradicted by the museum:**
   - Rubens' Venus is not seen from behind (019).
   - Caravaggio's light comes from the right, not the upper left (018).
   - Bacon's reflection is less distorted, not "just as broken" (062).
   - Kirchner's background is green, not orange (044).
   - Cranach shows deer, not partridges (012).
   - Hopper's woman holds a timetable, not a dropped letter (060).
   - Van Eyck's reflections are in the background, not the bases (004).
   - Pollock's picture is a Black Painting, not an all-over drip (061).
5. **Wrong media and dates:**
   - Gainsborough "panel" should be canvas (029).
   - Dalí "canvas" should be panel (056).
   - Cranach header "after 1537" should be c. 1530–34 (012).
   - Titian "very last year" contradicts the track's own next line (015).
6. **Anachronism and invention:**
   - Dalí's "atom" in 1944 (056).
   - The Kandinsky "bought in Milan" story, with no source (049).
   - The Rauschenberg centenary display, which ended in May 2026 (065).
7. **Museum history:**
   - The guide calls the Carmen collection a "deposit"; it has been a 15-year lease with a purchase option since 2022.
   - "Spain bought the whole thing" should be "775 works for $350m".
   - "the last Baron" is wrong.
   - The guide never gives the 8 Oct 1992 opening, Moneo or the Villahermosa palace. Add them.
   - Track 002 says "Ground floor" for the top-floor rooms.
8. **Room numbers and levels** have drifted for at least nine works. Examples: Carpaccio is in Room 7, Cézanne 41, Dix 45, Derain 34, Picasso 45, Pollock 46, Rauschenberg 48, Saenredam 26, and Memling 5. These are passed to the room agent.
9. **Quotes:**
   - Five checked out: Cézanne, O'Keeffe, Dix (but the remark is Liebermann's), Braque and Rauschenberg. Use exact wording and credit.
   - Hopper's is paraphrased wrongly ("sunlight on the side of a house").
   - The Picasso and Matisse "father of us all" line needs hedging.
   - "Almost shocked her" (O'Keeffe) is unsourced.

## Verdict: rebuild, with the old guide used only as a scaffold

The tour's shape still matches the 2026 museum: Old Masters on Level 2, the 19th century to Pop on Level 1, and the Carmen Thyssen Collection on Level 0. The intro tracks and about two-thirds of the work tracks are broadly sound and can be edited down. But:
- About 15% of the single-work tracks feature works that are not on display.
- One track (068) is built on a painting sold in 2012.
- Roughly 20 load-bearing claims are wrong, and many more need hedging.

The fact-level errors are spread across the text, not concentrated in a few tracks, so a light "reuse with fixes" pass is not safe.

**How to rebuild:**
1. Re-select the featured works against the live on-view list. Use the room agent's output, and swap in on-view works for 006, 008, 011, 012, 019, 020, 041, 046, 049, 062 and 068.
2. Re-source every work track from its museothyssen.org page, using the URLs above.
3. Keep the tone and the strong intro tracks, after fixing the specific lines listed here.
