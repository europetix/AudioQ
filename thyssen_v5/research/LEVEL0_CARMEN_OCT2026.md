# Thyssen-Bornemisza, Level 0: Carmen Thyssen collection, rooms A–J (research of 23 Oct 2026)

**Method.** I read each work's official museothyssen.org page with WebFetch, one page at a time. Many were reached through `/en/node/NNNNN` links; the canonical URL is given. "Room wording" is the location label printed under "Inv. no." on the English page. Room titles come from `source/map.txt` and `research/map/p-4.png` (Level 0). I did not read the Spanish pages here.

**Caveat.** The collection pages can be out of date: see `CURRENT_LOCATIONS_OCT2026.md`, where the Van Eyck page still says "Room 3" although the work is in The Hague. Re-check before recording, and preferably in the galleries.

All works below are inventoried "CTB." (Carmen Thyssen-Bornemisza). Credit line on the pages: "Carmen Thyssen Collection". The Delaunay page says "Colección Carmen Thyssen-Bornemisza en depósito en el Museo Nacional Thyssen-Bornemisza".

## 2. Status of the Carmen Thyssen collection (Oct 2026)

**The lease, as reported**
- **Type:** a lease of part of Carmen Cervera's collection by the Spanish state (Ministry of Culture).
- **Term:** 15 years.
- **Rent:** €6.5m a year. The museum page adds that it is updated each year by CPI. The total is about €97.5m.
- **Purchase option:** the state has an option to buy at the end of the term ("preferential acquisition rights" on all or part of the collection).
- **Timeline:**
  - Agreement in principle: 29 Jan 2021.
  - Royal decree / government authorisation: Jul–Aug 2021. The decree's guarantees include that the collection stays together and on view "in a stable manner", and that the state insures the works.
  - Signing: 9 Feb 2022, the same day *Mata Mua* went back on view.

**Sources**
- Official, museum: https://www.museothyssen.org/en/collection/carmen-thyssen. It gives the 15 years, a purchase option at the end of the lease, and €6.5m a year with CPI. Its footnote says "298 works out of 328 which sum up in the loan".
- Official, ministry: https://www.cultura.gob.es/actualidad/2021/01/210129-acuerdo-thyssen.html (29 Jan 2021). It gives 15 years, €6.5m a year, the state's purchase option at the end, and "unas 400 obras". *Mata Mua* had left under a 2019 export licence.
- Official, museum *Memoria 2024* (published Nov 2025): https://www.museothyssen.org/sites/default/files/document/2025-11/Memoria_2024_enfrentadas-es.pdf. It says the lease has run since 2022 and the contract covers 329 works.
- Reputable 1: Artnet News, Aug 2021, https://news.artnet.com/art-world/madrid-carmen-cervera-collection-1995611. It says the collection is in Madrid "until 2036", with preferential acquisition rights and the state guarantees above. Its meta description says "2026"; that is a typo in the article.
- Reputable 2: ¡Hola!, 9 Feb 2022, https://www.hola.com/actualidad/20220209204286/baronesa-thysse-hijo-borja-firma-convenio-museo-thyssen/. It covers the signing that day: 15 years, €6.5m a year, 329 works, a preferential purchase option, and *Mata Mua* staying at least 15 years.

**My reading for Oct 2026**
- **End date.** Counted from the 9 Feb 2022 signing, the lease runs to about **Feb 2037**. Artnet's "2036" counts from 2021. No source gives an exact contractual end date, so in the script say "on long-term lease to the Spanish state until the late 2030s".
- **Renewal or purchase.** I found no news (2024 – Oct 2026) of an early purchase, renegotiation or early end. The 2024 annual report describes the lease as current.
- **Work counts differ between sources.** They give about 400 (2021 announcement), 425 (blog), 329 (2022 signing and 2024 report) and 328 (museum web page). Use "about 330 works" if a number is needed.

**Risk 1: the baroness is selling privately held works (new, Oct 2026).**
- Sotheby's New York will auction Edward Hopper's *The "Martha McKeen" of Wellfleet* (1944) on **18 Nov 2026** in "The Modern Evening Auction", with an estimate of $20–30m. Sources:
  - Forbes España, 16 Oct 2026: https://forbes.es/?p=1046953
  - Qué!, 16 Oct 2026: https://www.que.es/2026/10/16/subasta-hopper-tita-thyssen/
  - joseantoniocobena.com, 20 Oct 2026: https://joseantoniocobena.com/2026/10/20/definitivamente-el-barco-de-hopper-seguira-buscando-islas-desconocidas/
- The painting once hung in the museum. In 2015 it was cited as on view, and in June 2020 it was one of four works missing after the reopening (El Independiente, 17 Jun 2020: https://www.elindependiente.com/?p=1055249).
- Press calls it part of her "private" collection. Its museum page URL returned an error. My reading is that it has been outside the leased group since 2020, but that is **not confirmed**.
- It is not on Level 0 now. **Do not mention it as a work to see.** Other works have also gone on the market: Constable's *The Lock* (2012), and about 20 smaller family pieces at Christie's in March 2020 (Qué!).

**Risk 2: works inside the lease can still be off the wall.**
- Five Carmen Thyssen pages say "Not exhibited" (see section 1), including the O'Keeffe highlight.
- The visitor map warns that works go out on loan without notice.

**Guidance for the script**
- Script only the works in section 1 that have a room.
- Avoid "forever" and "permanent" wording about rooms A–J.
- Keep one generic line: "This collection belongs to Baroness Carmen Thyssen and is leased to the Spanish state; individual works are sometimes lent or rotated."
- Re-check the room labels and the lease news before each release.
