**Level 2:**
- **Rooms 1 to 5.** From the corridor you walk west into Room 1. Then 1→2→3→4 through doors on the west (outer) side, then 4→5 and 5→6.
- **Gallery 6 and the side rooms.** Gallery 6 has a door into each of 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 12, and another into 13 at its east end.

  - Room 10 also opens north-east onto the mid-floor stair, lift and toilet corridor, which runs to Room 15.
- **Rooms 13 to 18.**
  - 13→14 is a door at the east side, and so are 14→15 and 15→16.
  - 17 is a side room of 16, with one door at mid-height. It has no door to 18.
  - 16→18 is a door at the east side.
- **North of 18.**
  - 18 opens west along the corridor to 19–21, the Classical rooms.
  - 18 also opens north into 22, through a door at the east end of its north wall.
  - 22→23 is open, and so is 23→24.
- **Rooms 24 to 29.**
  - Rooms 25, 26, 27 and 28 each open off the 24 gallery, through a gap at the west end of each room's south wall. They also connect in a line (25↔26↔27↔28) through doors at their north end.
  - There is NO door 28↔29. Room 29 opens only to the south, onto the east end of the 24 strip.
  - That end of the strip is a landing. The lift is immediately east of 29. The stairs go down south-east from the landing, at the east end of the 24 gallery.

**Level 1:**
- The lift and the stairs arrive on a small landing east of Room 30. It opens into Room 30 through its south-east doorway.
- The Rodin room opens off 30's north-east corner.
- Rooms 30, 31, 32, 33 and 34 connect in a line, each pair through two doors (north and south).
