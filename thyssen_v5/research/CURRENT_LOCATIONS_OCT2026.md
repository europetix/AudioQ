# Thyssen-Bornemisza: where the old guide's works hang now (research of 23 Oct 2026)

Method: I read each work's official museothyssen.org collection page with WebFetch, one page at a time. The location text is the label printed directly under "Inv. no." / "Nº INV." (it links to `#splash-artwork-location`). Where the English page said "Not exhibited" or gave no room, I also read the Spanish page. The room titles come from the uploaded map (`source/map.txt`, Dec 2025, FolletoThyssen_EN-20).

**Caveat on the collection pages:** they are not always up to date. The Van Eyck diptych page still says "Room 3", but the Mauritshuis names it among the works lent to The Hague from 15 Oct 2026. In some cases the EN and ES pages also disagree (rows 006, 031 and 041). Rows marked CHECK should be confirmed in the galleries or with the museum before recording.

Status key:
- SAME: the room matches the old guide.
- MOVED: the work is in a different room.
- ON LOAN: the work is out of the building.
- TEMP EXH: the work is in one of the museum's own temporary shows.
- NOT ON DISPLAY: the page says "Not exhibited" / "No expuesta".
- NOT FOUND: there is no official page.

A row marked * checks one sample work for a track that covers a group of works or a whole school.

## 2. Floor plan facts (official map, Dec 2025: `source/map.txt`; https://www.museothyssen.org/sites/default/files/document/2025-12/FolletoThyssen_EN-20.pdf; web map https://www.museothyssen.org/en/visit/resources-visit/map)

- **Level 2, permanent collection, rooms 1–29.**
  - The map marks "Recommended start of the visit" at Room 1.
  - Rooms 1–5 hold 14th–15th century painting and Renaissance portraiture.
  - Room 6 is the "Villahermosa Gallery".
  - Room 7 is 16th-century Italian painting.
  - Rooms 8–9 are German painting.
  - Room 10 is 16th-century Netherlandish painting.
  - Room 11 is "Tiziano, Tintoretto, Bassano and El Greco".
  - Room 12 is "Caravaggio and Baroque Painting".
  - Rooms 13–15 are 17th-century Italian, French and Spanish painting.
  - Rooms 16–18 are 18th-century Italian painting.
  - Rooms 19–21 are the "Classical rooms". These currently hold the Mauritshuis exchange show. Per articulo14, Vermeer's *View of Delft* has its own room in "las nuevas Salas Clásicas de la segunda planta".
  - Room 22 is 18th-century Italian painting.
  - Rooms 23, 26 and 28 are Dutch landscape.
  - Room 24 is 18th-century French and English painting.
  - Room 25 is Dutch scenes of daily life and interiors.
  - Room 27 is Dutch portrait.
  - Room 29 is "Goya and Romanticism".
- **Level 1, permanent collection, rooms 30–56.**
  - Room 30 is "Transatlantic relations".
  - Rooms 31–32 are American landscape.
  - Room 33 is "The Impressionist Period".
  - Rooms 34–35 are Expressionist landscapes and portraits.
  - Rooms 36–38 are the body, urban unrest and flowers.
  - Room 39 is "Pioneers of abstraction".
  - Room 40 is "Popular flavor".
  - Rooms 41–42 are "The Cubist Tradition I/II".
  - Room 43 is "Abstract Utopias".
  - Room 44 is "Dada and Surrealism".
  - Room 45 is "Interwar Realisms".
  - Rooms 46–47 are "American Abstraction I/II".
  - Room 48 is "Post-war American Art".
  - Room 49 is "Post-war European Figurative Art".
  - Room 50 is "Informalisms".
  - Room 51 is "Homo Ludens".
  - Room 52 is "Pop Art".
  - Rooms 53–56 are the "Postpop rooms".
  - The page images also show a "Rodin room" (p-3.png).
- **Level 0, Carmen Thyssen collection, rooms A–J, plus temporary exhibition rooms, the hall and the garden.**
  - A: Old Masters, 17th–18th centuries.
  - B: North American Landscape.
  - C: French Naturalist Landscape.
  - D: Impressionism.
  - E: Monet and North American Impressionism.
  - F: Gauguin and Postimpressionism.
  - G: Neo-Impressionism and its Wake.
  - H: Early Avant-gardes.
  - I: Between the Wars Painting. Cubism, Abstraction and Surrealism.
  - J: North American Painting and Others.
  - The map key shows the "Entrance" from the Paseo del Prado, plus "Access to Carmen Thyssen Collection" and "Access to Permanent Collection".
- **Level -1:** temporary exhibition rooms (the "Rooms Moneo"), the conference room and the EducaThyssen Studio.
- **Services:**
  - The map text lists the café-restaurant, shop, Friends desk and audio-guide service at Level 0. Its services legend also lists the ticket office, toilets, adapted lift, stairs, ATM and cloakroom, but the text extraction places them inconsistently between levels. Check on p-4.png / p-5.png.
- **Visit order and access:**
  - The visit page (https://museothyssen.org/en/node/24067) says "We recommend that you start on the second floor, room 1" and to "go to the end of the central hall and go up the stairs or take the lift to the second floor, room 1".
  - Re-entry is allowed, except into the ground-floor temporary exhibition.
- **Visit length:** "Estimated duration of the visit: 2 hours and 30 minutes".
- **Loans notice on the map:** "works from the collection are often out on loan … specific replacements, changes in installations and the placing of notices".
- **Rehang history:**
  - The big reinstallation was in 2021: old masters on Level 2 with salmon walls, modern art on Level 1 with white walls, and the Carmen Thyssen collection on the ground floor.
  - I found no 2025–26 rehang of the numbered rooms. The Dec 2025 map has the same numbering.
