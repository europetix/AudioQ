### 1.4 Track 053: the O'Keeffe quote

The *From the Plains II* page (https://www.museothyssen.org/en/collection/artists/okeeffe-georgia/plains-ii, read 23 Oct 2026) carries it **verbatim**. The page has O'Keeffe telling Edith Halpert that "the color is just plain color out of the tube — red and orange to lemon — It shocks me so that I’m rather struck with it — I don’t know what it will get to."

Restore it as its own sentence straight after the Halpert line. It adds no em-dash and brings the track to about 333 words, within the limit.

> [lightly] To her dealer, Edith Halpert, she wrote that the colour was "just plain color out of the tube", red and orange to lemon. "It shocks me so that I'm rather struck with it," she added.
