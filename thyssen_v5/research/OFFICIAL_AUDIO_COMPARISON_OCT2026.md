# Thyssen-Bornemisza: the museum's audio guide vs third-party audio tours (research of 23 Oct 2026)

**Method.** WebSearch and WebFetch only, one page at a time.

**Blocked pages.** Two pages were blocked by permission prompts that timed out, so I did not read them:
- https://entradas.museothyssen.org (ticket shop)
- https://www.ricksteves.com/watch-read-listen/audio/audio-tours

**Reviews.** Recent (2024–2026) reviews that mention audio are scarce. The aggregator pages I could read show star ratings but little review text. Everything below is quoted or summarised from the pages cited.

## 1. The museum's own audio guide

**Sources**
- Current page, Visit > Resources for the visit > Audioguides: https://www.museothyssen.org/en/visit/resources-visit/audioguides-webapp (= https://www.museothyssen.org/en/node/21670). It already lists the Dalí show that opened on 20 Oct 2026, so it is up to date.
- Older page "Discover our audioguides": https://www.museothyssen.org/en/node/19237. Its figures differ from the current page (see the end of this section).
- Accessibility page: https://www.museothyssen.org/en/node/19802
- Fees page: https://www.museothyssen.org/en/visit/fees
- 2025 price PDF: https://museothyssen.org/sites/default/files/document/2025-02/MNTB_Prices%202025.pdf

**Price**
- Fees page: "Price + €5.50", added to the ticket. General ticket €14, so €19.50 with the guide; reduced or student €10, so €15.50.
- The 2025 price list shows the audio guide at €5.00 (€4.50 for tourism professionals). The price has gone up €0.50 since 2025.
- Accessible guides (sign language and audio description) are **free**.
- IYTC card: 10% off ticket + audio guide.
- Where to buy: online with the ticket, at the ticket office, or at the audioguide desks.

**Device**
- Two options: "your own smartphone or a device the museum provides".
- On your own phone it is a web app: you collect headphones at the audioguide desk and "scan the QR code to access all the content". There is no app to install, and the page names none.
- The page does not mention offline use. A web app is presumably online-only and the map advertises free Wi-Fi, but this is not confirmed.
- Desks: the Level 0 map shows two audio-guide points, one by the main hall and information desk, and one by the "Access to Permanent Collection". The Level -1 legend also lists "Audio guides (Adapted audio guides)".
- Older app: "Selected Works" (2013, iPad, then Android; 143 works; Fundación BBVA), https://www.museothyssen.org/sites/default/files/document/notas_prensa/NP_App_INGLES.pdf. This was a catalogue app, not an audio guide. I found no current official Thyssen audio-guide app in the stores, and the museum points only to its web app.

- Magnetic induction loops are free and work with all guides.
- One purchase gives access to all the content.
- The pages publish no track list, so which 15/30/50 works are covered is not stated. The guide is sold by duration and number of works.
- The older page (node 19237) gives different figures: "Masterpieces" 50 works in about 2.5 h, a "Highlights" set of 39 works in about 45 min, a 39-work multimedia guide with maps in about 3 h, and a children's tablet guide of 14 works in about 40 min. Resellers repeat the same older wording: "2.5 hours or a shorter 30-minute version" (Musement/TUI).
- TUI also lists shows with **no** audio guide: "Guardi and Venice, Marina Vargas, and Tarek Atoui".

**What the official guide does not show on the web:**
- a track list
- directions or wayfinding between rooms (the 2013-era multimedia guide had "interactive maps", but the current page says nothing about this)
- any loan notices inside the guide
- its narrator or voice
- offline use

**Risk for loans.** The Hague loan is running (15 Oct 2026 – 17 Jan 2027). It includes the Van Eyck diptych, the Dürer and an El Greco (see `CURRENT_LOCATIONS_OCT2026.md`), all works a 50-work "masterpieces" guide would normally include. I found nothing on how the official guide handles the gaps. It is probably by signs in the galleries: the map says "the placing of notices with information regarding the loan".

## 2. Reviews (what visitors like or miss)

There is very little dated review text on readable pages.

**Official guide**
- Headout, Jan 2024, 5/5: "Da fare con audioguida!" (https://headout.com/reviews/7985).
- Tripadvisor product page (WeGoTrip ticket), Sep 2022, 4/5: "The audio tour is necessary to save time and enhance the experience" (the reviewer paid an extra €5 for the official guide). Also Sep 2022, 1/5: the WeGoTrip guide was "good, [but] you can get the same thing for €5 in the museum".
- Older: Tripadvisor, Mar 2015, "Beware the audioguide": "set up in an odd way and if your first language is not Spanish" (https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g187514-d198867-r257407908-Museo_Nacional_Thyssen_Bornemisza-Madrid.html). Also Mar 2015: "Do take the audio guide; it really does make you look at paintings".

**Aggregate scores (no text)**
- Tiqets, "Thyssen with Themed Audio Tours", supplied by the museum: 4.5 from 33 reviews; the museum as provider scores 4.6 from 3,872.
- Headout, Spanish themed-audio ticket: 4.3 from 1,644 (now "not available").
- Musement "skip-the-line with audio guide": no reviews on the page.

**Third-party apps (Tripadvisor, WeGoTrip ticket + self-guided audio, 3.4/5 from 18, product now "unavailable")**
https://www.tripadvisor.com/AttractionProductReview-g187514-d23499028-Thyssen_Bornemisza_Museum_Ticket_with_Self_Guided_Audio-Madrid.html
- Jul 2022, 1/5: "the museum re labeled all the room numbers so they no longer correspond with the audio tour". This is the **rehang problem**, and Yo Tours must avoid it.
- Oct 2022: "We gave up on the app and paid for the official Guided Tour".
- Nov 2022: "hard to use and a waste of money".
- Sep 2022: "I wasn't able to download the file".
- Dec 2022: "helpful, but also overwhelming as there were so many choices".
- Positives (Sep–Oct 2022): "Easy to navigate"; "easy to follow".

**Themes (inferred from thin evidence)**
- *Likes:* the guide makes you look harder; good value at about €5; many languages.
- *Misses:* the structure is confusing (2015); third-party apps fall out of sync with room numbers after rehangs (2022); download and usability problems with apps.
- I found **no** 2024–26 review that discusses depth, voice or loans. This is a gap. The Yo Tours team should sample the official web app in person.

## 5. Gaps a Yo Tours guide could fill (based on the evidence above)

1. **Level 0 in more languages.** The official Carmen Thyssen tour is ES/EN only, and no third-party product mentions rooms A–J.
2. **Directions.** No current product clearly gives turn-by-turn directions room to room. One third-party app broke when the rooms were renumbered, so give directions by room title and landmark, not just by number.
3. **Honesty about loans.** No product says which works are away. In Oct 2026 that means The Hague (until 17 Jan 2027), Dalí in the ticketed show (until 24 Jan 2027), the "Not exhibited" works, and the Hopper sale (18 Nov 2026).
4. **Sourced, current facts.** Each third-party product I checked has stale or wrong items.
5. **Price.** The official guide is cheap (€5.50) and comprehensive. A paid Yo Tours guide has to compete on story depth, directions and accuracy, not on coverage alone.
