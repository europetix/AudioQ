# Thyssen-Bornemisza, Level 1: what is on the walls, room by room (research of 23 Oct 2026)

**Purpose:** a menu for the lead to pick 2–4 stops per room for the new Yo Tours audio guide.

**Read with:** `CURRENT_LOCATIONS_OCT2026.md`. Works marked "prev" were checked there and are not re-checked here.

## Method

- **Room numbers and titles** come from `source/map.txt` and `research/map/p-3.png`. This is the official Level 1 plan, Dec 2025.
- **How I found candidates:** from the museum's own thematic-tour pages, whose stops print the live location label, plus "related works" links on artwork pages.
- **How I checked them:** every work in a table was then read on its own museothyssen.org artwork page (EN). The location text is the label under "Inv. no.". The "Room wording" column quotes it.
- **Spanish pages:** I spot-checked the ES page where something looked surprising or conflicting.
- **Fetching:** one page at a time with WebFetch. Five pages (Macke *Circus*, Beckmann *Self-Portrait with Raised Hand*, Nolde *Glowing Sunflowers*, and two tour pages) showed a JavaScript "verify" redirect, so I skipped them.
- **Tour-only works:** works seen only on a tour page, not checked on their own artwork page, are listed under "Also listed (tour pages only)". Treat them as likely, not confirmed.
- **Pages can be stale.** The tour pages disagree with the artwork pages in four cases, flagged below. Re-check every chosen stop shortly before recording, and if possible in the galleries.

## Corrections to the earlier file (important)

1. **Van Gogh is on display.** *The Stevedores in Arles* (1888) is in **Room 34**: EN "Room 34 / Level 1", ES "Sala 34 / Planta primera". Only *Les Vessenots* is off the wall.
2. **Kandinsky is on display.** There are two works:
   - *Murnau: Top of the Johannisstrasse* (1908), **Room 37**.
   - *In the Bright Oval* (1925), **Room 39**.
   - Only *Picture with Three Spots, No. 196* is "Not exhibited".
3. **Balthus, *The Card Game*, is in Room 45** (Interwar Realisms), not in the post-war room.
4. **Rooms 53–56 (Postpop rooms) hold the Kenny Scharf exhibition** until 17 Jan 2027. The page gives the place as "Postpop rooms, Level 1, Permanent Collection".
5. **The museum's web map has no Room 47.** It goes from 46 to 48, but the PDF map shows 47. Artwork pages do use "Room 47" (Rothko, Albers, Stella).

## Rodin room (next to Room 30, beside the lift)

The museum's own pages give no works for this room.

**Museum pages**

- The museum's Rodin artist page lists four marbles. All are Carmen Thyssen works hung on **Level 0**:
  - *Christ and Magdalen* (ca. 1905): "Room A".
  - *The Death of Athens* (1904–1906): "Room E".
  - *The Dream (The Angel's Kiss)* (ca. 1905): "Room F".
  - *The Birth of Venus (The Aurore)* (1906–1907): "Room B".
- The web map has a "Rodin room" entry (#sala-rodin) but describes nothing.

**Press reports (Time Out Madrid, 15 Nov 2023; Bonart, 20 Nov 2023)**

- The room shows two Rodin marbles on long-term loan from Francesca Thyssen-Bornemisza:
  - *The Death of Alcestis* (c. 1889).
  - *Young Girl Confiding Her Secret to Isis* (1906).
- **Origin:** August Thyssen commissioned them for the winter garden of Schloss Landsberg, which he bought in 1903. They were part of a set of seven. Bonart calls this the start of the family's collecting.
- The room loosely recreates the Landsberg setting.
- **Sources:** https://www.timeout.es/madrid/es/noticias/el-museo-thyssen-incorpora-dos-nuevas-esculturas-de-rodin-a-su-coleccion-y-ya-se-pueden-ver-111523 · https://www.bonart.cat/en/n/44217/two-new-rodin-sculptures-at-the-thyssen-bornemisza-national-museum

**Status:** CHECK that both loans are still there in Oct 2026. Otherwise this is a strong opening for Level 1, since the collection literally began with these Rodins.

**Best for the main route:** an **intro stop** for Level 1, "where the Thyssen collection began", if the loans are confirmed.
