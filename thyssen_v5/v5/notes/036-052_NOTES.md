# Notes, tracks 036–052 (Level 1, Rooms 33–46), written 23 Oct 2026

All official pages below were read with WebFetch, one at a time, on 23 Oct 2026. Every work named as present in the speech has its own museothyssen.org page giving that room. Lint: `python3 v5/lint.py 036 … 052`. All 17 print OK.

## Across all tracks
- **Cues follow PLAN.** Directions are as the map shows: 33 → 34 next door, 34 → 35 round the corner, 35 → 36 → 37 down the east side, 40 → 41 round the corner, then 41–46 along the south row. Not checked on site: the 36 → 37 door and where the opening into Room 56 is (PLAN E).
- **Short versions.** In the Essential and Highlights versions, some cues name a room the listener will not hear next. For example, 039 says "next main stop Room 37", but in Essential the next track played is 043 in Room 39. 043 → 045 and 045 → 049 work the same way, as does 038 → 050 in Highlights. The cues follow PLAN. The lead must decide whether the short versions need their own cues.
- **Callbacks to other writers' rooms.** These link to other writers' tracks: Room 24 Watteau (040, 050), Room C Van Gogh (038), Room G Kandinsky (043) and Goncharova on Level 0 (040). None names the other track's content beyond what PLAN states.
- **Spanish titles.** I put a Spanish title in @what only where the page showed it: 036, 042, 043, 044, 045 (Braque), 046, 047 (Popova) and 050. I removed the titles I had guessed.
