# Notes: tracks 001–017 (writer 1), 23 Oct 2026

Every work page below was read on museothyssen.org with WebFetch, one page at a time, on 23 Oct 2026. The room label quoted is the text under "Inv. no.". All 17 tracks pass `python3 v5/lint.py 001 … 017`.

## For the lead: decisions and flags

1. **Ribera, *Saint Jerome in penitence* (inv. 335) is NOT on display.** The EN page says "Not exhibited" and the ES page says "No expuesta". This contradicts LEVEL2_ROOMS_OCT2026.md and PLAN 016 ("Room 12"). So 016 names only the Ribera *Pietà*, and 015 drops the Titian-and-Ribera Jerome comparison the PLAN asked for. Please correct the research file and PLAN.
2. **Spanish titles in @what.** I captured the ES title only where a page showed it: Daret, *La Adoración del Niño*, and Giordano, *El juicio de Salomón*. Every other @what gives the official EN title. If the house style needs ES titles everywhere, that needs one ES page fetch per work.
3. **Van Eyck (004), Dürer (012) and the later El Greco *Annunciation* (014)** each get one removable sentence: on loan to The Hague "until January 2027". The Van Eyck page still says "Room 3". The loan comes from CURRENT_LOCATIONS (Mauritshuis show, 15 Oct 2026 – 17 Jan 2027). I did not re-check it on mauritshuis.nl.
4. **Tintoretto, *Paradise* (001, 011).** The page says "Central hall", ca. 1588. It calls the work "Tintoretto's new proposal for the decoration of the main wall after Veronese's death", NOT the competition sketch. The PLAN's "sketch for the Doge's Palace competition" is therefore wrong. The script says "his proposal for the great council hall … after a fire". 001 hedges whether the work is on the wall ("look out for").
5. **The Memling back (008)** is hedged: "If the panel is shown so that you can see its back…". This still needs the on-site check.
6. **Cue wording I polished, without changing where any cue sends people:**
   - 004 says "pale, red-eyed Risen Christ". The page mentions no night sky.
   - 011 drops "small" for the Cranach *Virgin*, which is 71.5 cm tall.
   - 012 says "wide landscape". The Patinir is a small panel, 31.5 × 57.5 cm.
   - 013 says "an early Annunciation … chequered floor and a pink curtain". The painting is 117 × 98 cm, so it is not small.
   - 016 says "Make your way to Room 13, at the end of the long gallery … Then turn into Room 14". This works whether or not Room 12 opens straight into Room 13 (PLAN E).
