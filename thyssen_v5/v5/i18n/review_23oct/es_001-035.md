## Things to check or flag in the English and my choices
- 034: the English says "A scholar". Spanish forces a gender, so I wrote "Una especialista" (Katherine Manthorne, per the museum page).
- 033: I kept "rara avis" and dropped the spoken gloss "a rare bird", which would be redundant in Spanish.
- 031: "stroke" is rendered as "derrame cerebral", which is neutral for Latin America and Spain. "ictus" would sound Spain-only.
- Other vocabulary choices: "fósforos" (not cerillas/cerillos), "tomados de la mano" (not cogidos), "magulladura" (not moratón/moretón), "teléfono" (not móvil/celular).
