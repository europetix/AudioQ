## Possible problems in the English (translated as written)
- 035: "Stafford House … across from Buckingham Palace". Stafford House (now Lancaster House) stands beside St James's Palace, near Buckingham Palace rather than facing it.
- 031: "a stroke in 1835 ended his work in oils". After 1835 Friedrich worked mainly in sepia and watercolour, but some small oils are usually dated after the stroke.
- 034: "neither of them appears" in Cole's Expulsion. This is correct for the Thyssen version, but it differs from Cole's better-known Boston Expulsion, where the figures are visible.
