## Official texts read (verbatim excerpts usable as OFF quotes)
- Maman: "Almost 9 meters tall, Maman is one of the most ambitious of a series of sculptures by Bourgeois that take as their subject the spider … Intended as a tribute to her mother, who was a weaver … they suggest both protector and predator—the silk of a spider is used both to construct cocoons and to bind prey … legs like Gothic arches that act at once as a cage and as a protective lair to a sac full of eggs perilously attached to her undercarriage."
- The Matter of Time: "from the relative simplicity of a double ellipse to the complexity of a spiral. The last two pieces of this sculpture are created from sections of toruses and spheres … The entire room is part of the sculptural field … corridors with different, always unexpected proportions (wide, narrow, long, compressed, high, low). The installation also includes a progression in time …"
- Holzer: Truisms from 1977 ("A LITTLE KNOWLEDGE GOES A LONG WAY" … "YOUR OLDEST FEARS ARE YOUR WORST ONES"); Times Square Spectacolor board 1982 ("ABUSE OF POWER COMES AS NO SURPRISE", "MONEY CREATES TASTE").
- Kapoor: "consists of 73 reflective spheres anchored around three axes … Images of the surrounding city, including the Nervión river, Buren's sculptural intervention on La Salve Bridge (Arcos rojos/Arku gorriak, 2007), and the museum itself, are cast into dynamic suspension."
- Puppy: "A behemoth West Highland terrier carpeted in bedding plants … references the 18th-century formal European garden … to instill, in his own words, 'confidence and security.' … stands guard at the museum."
