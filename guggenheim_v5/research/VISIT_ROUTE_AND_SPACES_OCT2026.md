# Guggenheim Museum Bilbao: Visit Route, Spaces, Placement and Status
Research for the Yo Tours audio guide (v5). Compiled 30 Oct 2026.

## How to read this file

**Method.** Research used only WebSearch and WebFetch. Pages were fetched one at a time, with the official site first; curl, a browser and CAPTCHAs were not used. Every page cited below was fetched on **30 Oct 2026**. Almost no official page prints its own publication or update date; most show only the footer "© FMGB Guggenheim Bilbao Museoa, 2026". "Fetched 30 Oct 2026" is therefore the reliable date.

**Grading.**
- **OFF**: an official guggenheim-bilbao.eus page (or its map subdomain) was fetched.
- **SEC**: a secondary page was fetched (press, euskadi.eus, a sponsor, a reputable outlet).
- **SNIP**: a search snippet only. Treat it as a lead, not a fact.
- **INF**: my own inference from graded facts, labelled as such.

**Date order on the official site.** English pages print dates as **MM.DD.YYYY** and Spanish pages as **DD.MM.YYYY**. I confirmed this against the ES page `/exposiciones` (for example, Steve McQueen is "09.10.2026 - 21.02.2027" in ES and "10.09.2026 - 02.21.2027" in EN). All dates below are written out in words to avoid mistakes.

**Caveat on quotes.** WebFetch returns text through a summarising model. Quotes marked verbatim were requested "exact characters" and look clean, but the client should spot-check the few that carry the most weight. They are marked ★.

**Scope (from the coordinator).** The guide covers the building and the permanent works. Temporary exhibitions are still recorded here because they decide which galleries a visitor walks through, and whether a gallery is open.

## 1. Arrival, entrances, ticket hall, atrium, lifts, floors, exit

### 1.1 Approach and entrances
**Source:** /en/the-building/outside-the-museum (OFF, fetched 30 Oct 2026). Verbatim:
- "The Museum plaza and main entrance lie in a direct line with Calle Iparragirre"
- "visitors access the Hall by making their way down a broad stairway," / "an unusual feature that successfully overcomes the height difference"
- "It is possible to walk all the way around the Museum,"

**Source:** /en/useful-information and /en/useful-information/getting-here (OFF, both 30 Oct 2026). ★ Verbatim:
- "There are two accessible entrances:"
- "Main facade: Access by elevator for wheelchair users (entrance through the Bar)."
- "Riverside façade: Ramp access for wheelchair users."
- "There is also the entrance down the broad stairway on the main façade."
- Address: "Abandoibarra Etorbidea, 2. 48009 Bilbao, Bizkaia". The getting-here page has "Avenida Abandoibarra, 2 - 48009 Bilbao, Spain".
- The getting-here page also says the signs lead to "Avenida Abandoibarra, which follows the river and leads straight to the Museum."

**Plaza facilities** (OFF, /en/routes/a-day-in-the-museum): "If you need a break or a snack, go to the Bar Guggenheim Bilbao, next to the Bistró, in the Museum plaza."

**Not found:**
- A separate named group or coach entrance.
- Any official statement about which door is the exit. The museum never says "exit via …". No official page describes an exit route.

### 1.2 Hall (ticket hall) and lobby
- The Hall is the first interior space: "Once inside the Hall, visitors access the Atrium, the real heart of the Museum" (OFF, /en/the-building/inside-the-museum).
- Admission desk and screens (OFF, /en/useful-information/resources): "Located over the admission desk, these screens show the exhibitions on view…". Information is given "in Spanish, Basque, English, and French."
- Information desk (same page): "Personalized information service located in the Hall…"
- Coatroom (OFF, /en/useful-information/accessibility): wheelchairs are borrowed there ("Ask at our coatroom"), and "Ultra-light chairs to rest at any point on your tour" are also offered.
- **ZERO** is in the lobby; see 1.6. The admission desk, information desk and coatroom are named, but **no page gives their exact positions relative to each other**. The interactive map has a "Services (8)" layer on floor 1, but its labels did not come through the fetch.

### 1.3 Atrium
- (OFF, /en/the-building/inside-the-museum) ★ verbatim: "With curved volumes and large glass curtain walls that connect the inside and the outside, the Atrium is an ample space flooded with light and covered by a great skylight." Also: "The three levels of the building are organized around the Atrium and are connected by means of curved walkways, titanium and glass elevators, and staircases." And: "Also an exhibition space, the Atrium functions as an axis for the 20 galleries, some orthogonally shaped and with classical proportions and others with organic, irregular lines."
- (OFF, /en/the-building/outside-the-museum) "The highest part of the Museum is crowned by a large skylight in the shape of a metal flower covering the Atrium"
- (OFF, /en/useful-information/tips) "The Atrium is the heart and soul of the Museum and serves as a point of reference for visitors." It continues: "The three floors of galleries arranged around this central area are interconnected by catwalks, glass elevators, and stairs." And: "The exhibition space comprises a total of 20 galleries." And: "Non-exhibition areas include a 300-seat Auditorium, two restaurants, a café, and a store/bookstore."
- (OFF, /en/routes/architecture) "The galleries on the third floor are crowned by skylights that let in natural light, which reaches the spaces on the floor below as well."
- The atrium height is **not stated** on any official page I fetched.

### 1.4 Lifts and stairs
- OFF: "titanium and glass elevators" and "glass elevators, and stairs". The accessibility page adds: "All elevators are large enough to provide convenient service between floors."
- The interactive map (screenshot from the user, floor 1, saved at /home/claude/audioq/guggenheim_v5/research/map/floor1_on_this_floor.png) shows **four lift-and-accessible-WC icons** on floor 1:
  - one near the west end by 103;
  - one between 101 and the centre;
  - one at the centre-south;
  - one at the west end of 104, where it meets the atrium.
- The museum does **not** name or number the stair towers. "Stair towers" is not official wording.

### 1.5 Floors and gallery numbering

**Museum wording** (OFF, route pages):
- "1st floor, Atrium"
- "1st floor, gallery 104"
- "1st floor, terrace"
- "Balcony, 2nd floor … the balcony opposite gallery 204"
- "the third floor … the balcony opposite gallery 304"
- "The third floor houses Works from the Guggenheim Museum Bilbao Collection"

The museum calls the Atrium/Hall level **floor 1**, not floor 0.

**Floor 0.** The accessibility page says: "There are also baby-changing facilities on floors 1 and 0." (OFF). So a floor 0 exists for services. It is **not** on the visitor map, which offers only floors 1, 2 and 3, and I found no description of it. INF: it is probably a lower service or auditorium level. Unconfirmed.

**Gallery numbers confirmed on official pages (30 Oct 2026):**
- **Floor 1:** 101, 103, 104, 105, plus ZERO, which the map labels "0".
  - **Gallery 102:** never mentioned anywhere I looked, and not drawn on the floor-1 map screenshot.
  - INF: the confirmed numbers (101, 103, 104, 105 + 201–209 + 301–307) total exactly the official "20 galleries", which suggests no gallery 102 is in public use. This is inference only.
- **Floor 2:** 201, 202, 203, 204, 205, 206, 207, 208, 209. All nine appear on official exhibition pages.
- **Floor 3:** 301, 302, 303, 304, 305, 306, 307. The Collection page mentions no 308 or 309; neither appears anywhere official that I fetched.
- **The brief assumed 301–309.** I found no evidence of 308 or 309.

**Names of galleries.**
- Gallery 104: I found no "ArcelorMittal gallery" name or "fish gallery" nickname on any official page fetched. The inside-the-museum page shows only the caption "Gallery 104 | The building", and the Matter of Time page says nothing either. ArcelorMittal naming is a lead only, not verified. **Do not use it in scripts unless verified.**
- Gallery 104 description (OFF, outside-the-museum) ★: "Stretching under the bridge, gallery 104—an enormous, column-free space". This is the La Salve Bridge.
- On the floor-1 map screenshot (OFF), 104 is the long boat-shaped gallery running east from the atrium.
- Gallery 103 houses the "Film & Video" project (OFF, Express route): "Gallery 103 in the Museum houses Film & Video, a project dedicated to video art and moving images."
- Gallery 208 is the home of the **in situ** project (OFF, A day at the Museum route): "the Museum's project inviting artists to create specific works of art for gallery 208". The current and upcoming in situ shows use 204 + 208.

**La Salve Bridge** (OFF, outside-the-museum): "The Museum site is crossed at one end by La Salve Bridge that, since 2007, supports the sculpture commissioned from Daniel Buren". The building "ends in a tower, a sculpture gesture that brings the architectural design to a crescendo".

**Outdoor artists named officially** (outside-the-museum): "Louise Bourgeois, Eduardo Chillida, Yves Klein, Jeff Koons, or Fujiko Nakaya".

**Reflecting pool / pond** (OFF, Architecture route): "The pond pays tribute to the Nervión estuary". Its status as a work in itself: not found.

**Puppy's flowers** (OFF press release, **16 May 2024**, ES):
- The planting is changed "dos veces al año", "en los meses de mayo y octubre".
- Each change "tiene una duración de nueve días", using "las cerca de 40.000 plantas".
- INF: an October change may be under way or just finished around 30 Oct 2026, which could put scaffolding or netting on Puppy. **No 2026-specific notice was found.**

**Atrium and other site-specific works: older page, probably not current.** /en/exhibition/atrio-y-exteriores (OFF, undated; images from 2021–22) lists:
- Chillida ×2 (now confirmed on the Atrium Terrace, as above)
- Sergio Prego, *Sequence of Dihedrals* (2007), "the Atrium and several scattered places of the second floor"
- Marcius Galan, *Diagonal Section* (2008)
- Asier Mendizabal, *Agoramaquia* (2014), "Gallery 201"

Gallery 201 is now Johns/Flavin space, so treat that page as **archived**. The current status of Prego, Galan and Mendizabal was **not verified**.

**Is the Collection display on now?** Yes. "Works from the Guggenheim Museum Bilbao Collection" is on floor 3 (301–307), with 101 and 104 grouped under it on the map. It runs to 1 Oct 2027 per the exhibition page, or 31 Dec 2026 per the map.

## 4. Changes 2025–2026, accessibility, audio guide

- **Urdaibai expansion (elsewhere; note only).** On 16 Dec 2025: "El Patronato del Museo Guggenheim ha decidido suspender definitivamente el proyecto de Urdaibai." The decision was "por unanimidad" (SEC, orain.eus, 16/12/2025). There is no effect on the Bilbao building.
- **Renovation or closure at Bilbao 2025–26:**
  - No official notice was found.
  - The press-release list for June–Oct 2026 (OFF) shows no closures. Its items include: "El Museo Guggenheim Bilbao recibe a su visitante 30 millones" (30 Sep 2026); a joint ticket with the Museo de Bellas Artes (11 Sep 2026); the Art&Music Summer Edition on the terrace (23 Jun 2026).
  - Floor 2 is between exhibitions (see §2).
- **Works not on view:** Tulips (Koons); Twombly *Nine Discourses on Commodus*; Beuys *Lightning with Stag in Its Glare*.
- **Accessibility** (OFF, /en/useful-information/accessibility):
  - Accessible entrances: the main façade via the Bar lift, and the riverside ramp.
  - "All elevators are large enough…"
  - Wheelchairs and ultra-light chairs from the coatroom.
  - "All restrooms have individual wheelchair accessible toilets"; baby-changing on floors 1 and 0.
  - Magnetic loop in the auditorium and at desks.
  - Sign-language guided tours; video guides "In Spanish sign-language or with subtitles".
  - A sensory guide for blind and low-vision visitors.
  - A companion goes free.
  - Accessible parking in the Plaza Euskadi public car park.
  - Educational spaces: "All of the spaces are fully accessible". Some works may "not provide optimum visibility".
  - The page also says "An audio guide app will be available for download" (future tense, undated).
- **Audio guide pickup point:** **none.** The official model is QR-on-phone. Resources page ★: "Admission fee includes an audio guide containing information about the building and the exhibitions for a self-guided tour." And: "Once inside the Museum, you will be able to scan a QR code with your phone." Outdoor works, Holzer's Installation for Bilbao and Serra's The Matter of Time have QR labels. Headphones, languages and a device desk are **not mentioned**. Exhibition-specific audio guides on the website are Community-member exclusives.
- **Labels and wall texts:** Basque, Spanish and English.
- **Visitor rules relevant to scripts** (OFF, tips):
  - Photography: "Photographs without flash are permitted for private use only."
  - "tripods and selfie sticks are prohibited inside the Museum galleries."
  - Bags must be "under 35 x 35 cm".
  - No food or drink in the galleries.
  - "the temperature inside the Museum is quite cool" in summer.

## 8. OPERATIONS ONLY (not for scripts)
- Hours (OFF, /en/hours-and-admission): "Tuesday through Sunday, from 10 am to 7 pm."
- Open on these Mondays: "March 30, April 6, Mondays from June 15 to September 7, October 12, December 7 and 28."
- 8 pm closing at Easter week and in summer (15 Jun – 13 Sep). Closed 25 Dec and 1 Jan. Closes at 5 pm on 24 and 31 Dec.
- "Admissions desks close 30 min before closing"; galleries are cleared 15 min before closing.
- Prices as returned by the fetch (verify before use): adults €12; students 18–26 and over-65s €6; under-18s free. "All tickets include audio guide." On the last day of each exhibition there is 50% off from 4 to 7 pm, which applies to Igshaan Adams on 1 Nov 2026.
- Fog Sculpture: "every hour on the hour … eight minutes". October–March 9:00 am – 8:00 pm; April 9:00 am – 9:00 pm; May–August 9:00 am – 10:00 pm; September 9:00 am – 9:00 pm.
- Fire Fountain: "active every fifteen minutes for one minute" (ES: "se activa durante un minuto cada cuarto de hora"). November–February 6:30–7:30 pm; March 7:30–8:30 pm; April 8:30–9:30 pm; May–July 9:00–10:00 pm; August–September 8:30–9:30 pm; October 7:30–8:30 pm. **These times are after closing**, so the fire is seen from outside.

  - Metro: Moyua, exit "Ercilla-Guggenheim".

  - Buses: "Museo 1 / Henao" etc.
