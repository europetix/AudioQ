Prepared for: Yo Tours audio guide, guggenheim_v5

Method: WebSearch + WebFetch only. Official site (guggenheim-bilbao.eus) first, one page per call. No curl, no browser, no CAPTCHA.

Grades:
- **OFF**: official museum page, fetched
- **SEC**: secondary page (reseller, blog, app listing, press), fetched
- **SNIP**: search-result title or snippet only, not fetched or fetch failed

Caveat: WebFetch returns a model summary of each page, not raw HTML. Quotes marked "verbatim" are what that summary reported as verbatim. Re-check them on the live page before using them in client-facing copy.

### 1.3 ZERO and Didaktika (official interpretation spaces)

- **ZERO**: "ZERO is a new introductory gallery and immersive optical device" in the lobby. It "provides insight into the urban history of the Bilbao region", offers "a new approach to Frank O. Gehry's building" and shows "the most iconic works in the Permanent Collection" (Basquiat, Kiefer, Warhol). "An original audio composition brings ZERO beyond the visual level", which is a soundscape, not narration. Grade: OFF, /en/zero. ZERO is the museum's own building-story product and our main overlap risk on architecture.
- **Educational spaces / Didaktika**: "The educational areas designed in conjunction with virtually every new exhibition … are designed to complement the exhibits and bring them to life through a variety of educational tools"; "The spaces are always integrated within the itinerary of the exhibition"; "All of the spaces are fully accessible". The word "Didaktika" does not appear on the resources pages. It does appear in the McQueen press dossier: "As part of the Didaktika program, the Guggenheim Museum Bilbao develops educational resources and …". Grade: OFF (resources page); SEC (showonshow.com press dossier, 2026).

## 3. What visitors say about the official guide

Direct reviews of the current QR/phone guide were **not retrievable**: Tripadvisor and Google text did not surface, and GetYourGuide failed to fetch. What exists:

- **Praise** (Civitatis, possibly for the reseller's own audio): "easy to use and very precise in their explanations". Grade: SEC.
- **Praise for the old device (2018)**: "an almost perfect audio guide!"; auto-triggering, "you do not have to punch in numbers!" The current QR system loses that hands-free triggering. Grade: SEC.
- **Navigation**: "Navigation is in general easy, but sometimes the overwhelming visual inputs from the amazing architecture might be confusing" (2018). Grade: SEC.
- **Building is the hero in reviews**: "The building itself is a piece of art"; "El edificio es una obra de arte en sí mismo"; "The architecture of the museum is in itself a work of art worth admiring". Grade: SEC.
- **Collection feels thin**: "la colección algo limitada"; "esperaba más obras"; "I expected something more". A guide that adds meaning to the building and Serra answers this directly. Grade: SEC.
- **Practical friction**: you must bring headphones and scan per-gallery QR codes. There is no published track count or length, and no language list on the official site. Grade: inferred from OFF plus SEC.

**Inferred weaknesses of the official offer** (from official pages, not reviews):
1. No published length, stop count or language list on the official site.
2. Phone QR model: scanning gallery by gallery, own headphones, no auto-trigger.
3. Outdoor works and Serra QR codes point to "additional information … on the Museum's website", which is web text, not a sequenced audio walk.
4. Written routes give gallery numbers only, with no landmark directions.
5. Express route views Serra from a balcony only and lists a show closing 1 Nov 2026.
6. Family route is unmaintained (COVID-19 text).
7. Public live tours are in Spanish and Basque only, so there is no affordable English live option.
8. Building story is split between ZERO (visual and sound, no narration) and a 4-stop architecture page.
9. Narrators are anonymous. There are no Gehry or artist voices, and curator content sits behind membership.
