# Guggenheim Museum Bilbao: graded facts on the building and the permanent works (research for the V5 audio guide)

Researched 30–31 October 2026 for Yo Tours. **Scope:** the building, its history, the "Bilbao effect", and the permanent and long-term works (Serra, Holzer, the outdoor works). Temporary exhibitions are out of scope. The dated third-floor Collection display appears in section H only so the writer knows what is on the walls.

**Method:** WebSearch and WebFetch only. Official sources come first. One page at a time per site. No curl, no browser, no CAPTCHA. Wikipedia is used only as a pointer, never as the sole source. The lead's two files are treated as inputs: `research/map/OFFICIAL_MAP_NOTES_30OCT2026.md` (the official interactive map, OFF) and `research/VISIT_ROUTE_AND_SPACES_OCT2026.md` (route and on-view status). Where a row relies on them, it says "(map file)" or "(route file)".

**Grades**
- **OFF**: read on an official page. That means guggenheim-bilbao.eus (including its pro., prensa. and teachers'-guide pages), guggenheim.org, the artist's own studio site (jeffkoons.com), or a public institution (Art Gallery of NSW archive, National Gallery of Canada).
- **SEC**: read in full on a reputable secondary source: a newspaper, the architecture or art press, an academic case study or journal, or the builder's own blog (Ferrovial). The source is named on each row.
- **SNIP**: seen only in a search-result snippet or headline, in a partial reprint, or in Wikipedia alone. **Confirm before scripting.**
- **UNVERIFIED / CONFLICT**: no adequate source, or sources disagree. The row says what to do.

**Dates:** "pub." is the publication date shown by the page. "undated" means the page shows none (all were read 30–31 Oct 2026).
**Quotes** are verbatim from the page cited. Each one notes its language. "EN as printed" means an English text whose original language the page does not state.
