[warmly] On Saturday 18 October 1997, a king opened a museum by flipping a light switch.

A choir sang in Basque as King Juan Carlos and Queen Sofía came in. The King declared, as AP reported it in English: "The Guggenheim Museum is inaugurated." Then he threw the master switch that lit up the Atrium. The Basque president was there too. Security was tight, with marksmen watching from the rooftops.

[quietly] At the ceremony, the Basque president remembered Txema Agirre, the officer whose name is on the plaza.

[pause] Not everyone came to celebrate. That same day, AP reported, about two thousand Basque artists and others marched on the other side of town, denouncing the museum.

The doors opened to the public the next day, Sunday 19 October.

And the verdict of the architects? The American architect Philip Johnson is reported to have called it "the greatest building of our time".

[curious] Before you go out, picture the Atrium you stood in earlier, dark, and then that one switch bringing all of it to light at once.
