## Work titles and official names
- The museum's German page (https://www.guggenheim-bilbao.eus/de, checked 31 Oct 2026) only has visitor information. It names no works,
  galleries or floors; the only space term it uses is "Ausstellungssäle". So every English work title is kept: Puppy, Tulips, The Matter of Time,
  Snake, Torqued Spiral / Torqued Ellipse / Double Torqued Ellipse, Between the Torus and the Sphere, Blind Spot Reversed,
  Installation for Bilbao, Arno, Like Beauty in Flames, Tall Tree and the Eye, Maman, Equal-Parallel: Guernica-Bengasi,
  Arcos rojos / Arku gorriak. Chillida: the brief's model was followed, giving „Rat an den Raum fünf“ and „Umarmung elf“, with numbers in words.
  These are descriptive German renderings, not official German titles. Besarkada is kept.
- "Tall Tree & the Eye" is written "Tall Tree and the Eye" everywhere, in titles and in speech, because of the TTS rule against "&".
- "Gallery" is "Saal" (the museum's "Ausstellungssäle"). In @title/@where it is written as digits, "Saal 104". In speech it is always
  in words: „Saal hunderteins / hundertvier / zweihundertvier / dreihundertvier“.
- FLOORS (navigation): the English "second/third floor" means the museum's floors 2 and 3, where galleries 2xx and 3xx are, and gallery 104 is on floor 1.
  In German, „zweiter Stock“ would mean one level higher. So I used **„Ebene 2 / Ebene 3“** (spoken „Ebene zwei / drei“) everywhere,
  including inside the museum quote in 016. Please keep this.
- Glossary terms used consistently: Vorplatz, Eingangshalle (Hall), Foyer (lobby), Atrium, Terrasse/Atriumterrasse, Uferpromenade,
  Wasserbecken, La-Salve-Brücke, große Straßenbrücke, Fußgängerbrücke, breite Treppe, Glasaufzug, geschwungene Stege,
  wetterfester Stahl, verdreht (torqued), Riesenspinne an der Fußgängerbrücke, hoher Baum aus Silberkugeln, roter Rahmen auf der Brücke,
  „vollständige Tour“, „kurzer Extra-Titel“, „die nächste Hauptstation ist …“.
- 018's title is „Ebene 3 — Die Metallblume“, which echoes the museum's „Oberlicht in Form einer Metallblume“. The spoken references in 017 and in bridge 009 H>018 match it.
- 019's place is „Auf dem Weg nach unten“ ("Going down"). Spoken stop names keep the English "Ort · Titel" pattern with "·", as the English does.
