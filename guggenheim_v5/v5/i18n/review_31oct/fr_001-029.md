## Terms and choices (used the same way throughout)
- Floors: "second/third floor" is rendered **niveau deux / niveau trois** (titles: "Niveau 2", "Niveau 3"), never "deuxième étage". A French listener reads "deuxième étage" as two floors above the ground, which is the museum's level 3. The gallery numbers 204/304 confirm the level. Inside the museum's own quote in 016 I also wrote "niveau deux".
- Galleries are digits in @title/@where and words in speech: « la salle cent un / cent quatre / deux cent quatre / trois cent quatre ».
- Other terms: river walk = **la promenade le long du fleuve** (shortened to "la promenade" when you are already on it). Footbridge = **la passerelle piétonne**, to keep it apart from the interior "passerelles". Plaza = le parvis. Pond = le bassin. Big road bridge = le grand pont routier. Glass lift = ascenseur de verre.
- Spoken next-stop names: the "·" is replaced by a comma ("Salle cent quatre, Tore et sphère"), with no « » around titles, as in the English bodies. "·" stays only in @title.
- Listening aids: in 010 and 012 the English sub-titles of the Torqued Spirals get a French gloss ("fermé, ouvert…", "droite, gauche…"). These are not new facts. In 025, "Maman, French for 'Mum'" becomes « Son nom, en français dans le texte : Maman ».
