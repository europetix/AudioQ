## Points in the English that may be wrong
1. **012 Serra:** the EN has "It is not linear; it is narrative." The museum's ES teachers' guide reads «No es lineal; no es narrativo.» The two say opposite things. I translated the English ("No es lineal. Es narrativo.") and flag it for checking against the original.
2. **002:** the EN calls Lucía Agirre "a museum spokeswoman". The article calls her «subdirectora Curatorial y de Archivo». I kept «una portavoz del museo», as the English has it.
3. **025:** "Almost 9 metres" is the museum's figure (ES «casi 9 metros»), while the work record gives 927 × 891 × 1023 cm. No change made.

## Adaptation choices worth a look
- Some names are left unglossed or written in full for the voice:
  - "lehendakari" is glossed as «el presidente vasco» in 003 and 020.
  - AP is written out as «la Associated Press».
  - "Bilbao Ría 2000" keeps its digits.
- 010 and 012: the Torqued Spiral subtitles are given in their Spanish meaning ("cerrado, abierto…", "derecha, izquierda…"), so the "walkable title" point still works. The titles themselves stay English.
- 013: "toro" is glossed as «en geometría… como un salvavidas», so it isn't heard as a bull.
- Bridge 009 H>018 was compressed to fit the length limit. It says «puede entrar en la escultura cercana» and «en el de la trescientos cuatro», meaning the balcony opposite 304; «frente a» is said only once, for the 204 balcony.
- Bridge 018 H>025 says «salga» without «del museo», also for length.
- "evening" is rendered «al caer la tarde» throughout.
- The footbridge is «la pasarela» outside the building. Inside, the walkways are «pasarelas curvas».
