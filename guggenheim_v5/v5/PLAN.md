### The route (reasoning; binding order, provisional geometry)
**Fixed points (sourced).**
- **The plaza and entrance.** "The Museum plaza and main entrance lie in a direct line with Calle Iparragirre." Then "visitors access the Hall by making their way down a broad stairway, … an unusual feature that successfully overcomes the height difference" (OUTSIDE, OFF). The square is at floor-2 level and the Hall/Atrium at floor 1 (MAP: Puppy is drawn on the floor-2 plan, "outside, south of 207").
- **Puppy** is in the "Museum Square" (WP-PUPPY). **The Txema Agirre plaque**: "Una placa le recuerda en la pequeña plaza que precede al Puppy" (EI17, re-read by the planner 31 Oct 2026: the small square in front of Puppy), with a monolith (ORAIN22) [O2].
- **Inside.** The Hall leads to the Atrium (INSIDE). ZERO is "in the Museum's lobby" (ZERO); the map draws it as "0" beside the 105 block, near the Atrium (MAP).
  - The Atrium "has access to all galleries and to the outside terrace" (ATRIUM).
  - Holzer is in 101, visible "from the Atrium" (R-COLL).
  - 104 is the long gallery running from the Atrium "under the bridge", with a lift at its Atrium end (MAP) and an educational space with a video "at the far end" (R-COLL).
  - **104 appears to be a dead end** (MAP draws no other door): you walk back to the Atrium [O6].
- **Upstairs.** The floors are linked by "curved walkways, titanium and glass elevators, and staircases" (INSIDE). The Serra view is from "the balcony opposite gallery 204" (floor 2) and the Atrium view from "the balcony opposite gallery 304" (floor 3) (R-EXP).
  - **Floor 2 is between exhibitions on 2–10 Nov 2026** (ROUTE §2). The balconies are not galleries, but O9 must confirm they stay open.
- **Exit.** **No official page describes the exit** (ROUTE §1.1, §7). There are two accessible entrances: the main facade by a lift, and a "Riverside façade" ramp (ACCESS). So a door on the river side exists, but whether visitors leave by it is unknown → **O11 CRITICAL, ALT-X**.
- **Outside.** "It is possible to walk all the way around the Museum" (OUTSIDE). The routes say: "Walk under the La Salve Bridge" (R-ARCH).
  - **Placements** (MAP + work pages):
    - Kapoor in the pond, drawn by the western galleries (101/103);
    - Maman "Exterior next to the footbridge", drawn north of the west end of 104, i.e. on the river side, between the pond and the bridge;
    - La Salve crossing over 104, with the tower at the building's end (OUTSIDE).
  - Fog: "the pool next to the riverfront facade" (WP-FOG). Fire: "a row of five fire fountains … in a pool outside" (WP-FIRE).
  - A travel blog (ABC-BLOG, low grade) says the pond "separates the Bilbao Riverwalk from the Guggenheim", that stairs lead down "to the left of the Guggenheim" [seen from the plaza] "towards the park and the River walk", and that Maman is "near the La Salve Bridge". These are **ONSITE hints only** (O12–O14).
- **What can be seen from the terrace (sourced only):** the pond, which "makes the perfect setting for artwork by Anish Kapoor and Yves Klein" (R-ARCH, "1st floor, terrace"); the building's "materials (glass, titanium, limestone)" (R-ARCH); and the Chillidas on it (WP-ADV, WP-EMB). The official day route says: "walk into the terrace and discover the works by Eduardo Chillida and also by Louise Bourgeois" (R-DAY), so **Maman is probably in view** [O7]. **Whether the terrace connects to the public river walk is unknown** → O7 CRITICAL, ALT-T. Until then the route goes back inside from the terrace.

**Chosen order (one loop, no backtracking except the 104 return walk):**
Plaza: welcome (001), Puppy (002), the plaque (003), the building and the city (004) → down the broad stairway (005) → ZERO in the lobby (006) → the Atrium (007) → Holzer seen from the Atrium (008) → gallery 104 (009–014, back to the Atrium) → the terrace (015) → glass lift to the balcony opposite 204 (016) → lifts and walkways (017) → balcony opposite 304 (018) → down to the Hall (019, 020) → out and round to the river walk (021) → the pond: Kapoor, fog, fire (022–024) → Maman (025) → the titanium up close (026) → La Salve Bridge, the tower and Buren (027, 028) → close by the bridge (029).

**Why this order:**
1. **The plaza first, the river last.** Both are public, and the plaza is where everyone arrives. Doing the outdoor works after the visit means the fog has had more chances to come round on the hour, and the loop ends at the bridge, the strongest last view.
2. **Serra on foot before Serra from above.** The official Express route only looks down from the balcony (COMP §4: our gap). Walking it first makes the balcony pay off.
3. **The terrace straight after 104.** Both are off the Atrium (ATRIUM), so this costs no extra lift ride.
4. **The pond before Maman.** This follows the map placements (Kapoor by the western galleries; Maman toward the bridge) for a listener coming down to the river walk on the side away from the bridge [O12–O14]. The order flips if O14 finds otherwise (ALT-M).

**Rejected alternatives:**
- **The outdoor loop first.** The fog and the fire are timed; doing them before the visit gives the least chance of seeing the fog. It also splits the building story from the inside.
- **The terrace as the way out to the river.** It is attractive, but unconfirmed (ALT-T).
- **Holzer after Serra.** 101 and 104 are both off the Atrium, but 101 is seen from the Atrium itself (R-COLL), so it fits naturally right after 007.
