Route idea (planner to refine, mark every unconfirmed step ONSITE):
A. The plaza (public): Puppy; the Txema Agirre plaque; the building's story; down the broad stairway into the Hall.
B. Inside (paid): ZERO → Atrium → Holzer in 101 (seen from the Atrium) → Gallery 104, walking Serra's eight works →
   the Atrium terrace (Chillidas; the view to the pond, the river, Artxanda) → floor 2 balcony opposite 204 (Serra from
   above) → walkways/glass lifts → floor 3 balcony opposite 304 (the Atrium and its flower skylight) → down and out via
   the Hall.
C. Outside loop (public; "It is possible to walk all the way around the Museum"): to the river side → Maman by the
   footbridge → the pond: Kapoor's Tall Tree & the Eye, Nakaya's F.O.G. (on the hour, 8 min), Klein's Fire Fountain
   (evening only, after closing) → the titanium skin up close → La Salve bridge, the tower, Buren's Red Arches → view
   across the river (Zubizuri, Isozaki towers, Artxanda) → close.
