A Serra really did disappear once. Thirty-eight tonnes of steel, gone. But it wasn't this one, and it wasn't in Bilbao.

The lost one was called Equal-Parallel: Guernica-Bengasi. Serra made it specifically for the Museo Reina Sofía in Madrid, for the exhibition that opened that museum in 1986.

As the Catalan paper Ara told it when Serra died, the work weighed thirty-eight tonnes, and it was lost. It was put into a warehouse, and from there it vanished, without anyone being able to explain what happened.

Thirty-eight tonnes. Mislaid.

The story has a better ending than you might expect. In 2009 Serra made a copy, without charging his fees, so that the work could go back into the museum's galleries.

So if you ever read that Bilbao lost a Serra, or that Snake went missing, you can smile. Snake is here in this gallery, the oldest of the eight pieces around you.
