YO TOURS - GUGGENHEIM MUSEUM BILBAO: THE BUILDING AND ITS PERMANENT WORKS
Audio guide, edition of 31 Oct 2026 (29 tracks: 25 main stops + 4 short Extras)
English, Spanish, French, German

WHY THIS EDITION
================
Built from scratch to the Yo Tours standard. The museum's own audio is reached by scanning QR codes with your phone; its
outdoor works and Serra's sculptures are covered by web text rather than narration, and its self-guided routes give gallery
numbers without directions. This guide:
1. Covers the building and the works that belong to it, inside and out: Puppy and the plaza, the stairway down, ZERO, the
   Atrium, Jenny Holzer's columns of light, a walk through all eight of Richard Serra's sculptures in The Matter of Time and
   a look down on them from above, the Chillidas on the terrace, the metal-flower skylight, and then the river walk:
   Kapoor's tree of mirrors, Nakaya's fog, Klein's fire fountains, Bourgeois's Maman, the titanium skin up close, and
   La Salve Bridge with the tower and Buren's red arches. The changing exhibitions are not part of this guide.
2. Tells the whole story: the 1991 deal ("commerce, pure and simple"), the competition, how it was built on time and on
   budget, the officer Txema Agirre, killed by ETA days before the opening, the Bilbao effect and its critics, and the
   shipyards that became a promenade. Every quote is attributed; popular errors (the crumpled paper, "Puppy kept by
   popular demand", Holzer's "Truisms", the 50-metre Atrium, Soviet-submarine titanium) are corrected.
3. Checks every fact on the museum's own pages and reliable sources (30-31 Oct 2026). Two independent audits (facts;
   route and listening). Gehry (d. 2025) and Serra (d. 2024) in the past tense. No prices, hours or ticket advice.
4. Three lengths (one recording, three folders):
   Full tour   29 tracks, about 1 h 40 with the outdoor loop
   Essential   19 tracks, about 1 h 15:  001 002 003 004 007 009 010 013 015 017 018 019 021 022 023 025 026 027 029
   Highlights  9 tracks, about 45 min:   001 002 004 007 009 018 025 027 029
   The times include walking and the lifts. The fog runs on the hour; the fire fountains only in the evening.
   In the shorter versions a short spoken line at the end of a track tells you the next stop.
5. The Yo Tours sound: voice finishing, a soft room tone and a short chime before each track. MP3s are labelled for phones.

HOW TO MAKE THE AUDIO (on a Mac)
================================
English:          double-click generate_audio.command  (voice 1 = Ava)
                  option 1 = all three lengths (records once; folders _Essential and _Highlights)
                  option 2 = two voice samples (009 the gallery under the bridge + 025 Maman)
                  option 3 = Highlights only (quickest; option 1 later reuses those recordings)
Other languages:  double-click generate_audio_ES_FR_DE.command and pick the language, then the same options.
Needs internet, python3, ffmpeg (brew install ffmpeg) and an up-to-date edge-tts:
  python3 -m pip install --user --upgrade edge-tts
If it stops, run it again: finished tracks are skipped.
