## 0. Method and keys

- **Tools.** WebSearch and WebFetch only. One page at a time per site, no bursts. No curl, no browser, no CAPTCHA. All reads were done on 31 Oct 2026.
- **WebFetch caveat.** WebFetch returns a model summary, so "verbatim" quotes are as relayed. **Re-check exact wording against the page before quoting on air.** The original language is marked: (EN), (ES), (CA).
- **Grades.**
  - **OFF**: official. That means the operator (poble-espanyol.com, Poble Espanyol de Montjuïc S.A.U.) or the Ajuntament de Barcelona (barcelona.cat, the Arxiu Fotogràfic, the Observatori del Turisme, museum sites). **OFF-op** marks the operator's own marketing pages. They are official but promotional, and several contain errors (see §9).
  - **SEC**: a named secondary source read directly. That covers peer-reviewed papers, university theses, Enciclopèdia Catalana, national and specialist press, and the BIE.
  - **SNIP**: a search-result snippet only.
  - **WIKI**: used as a lead only, never as sole support.
- **Verdicts.**
  - **OK**: safe to state as written.
  - **HEDGE**: true in substance; use the safe wording.
  - **LEGEND**: tradition or marketing lore; script only as "the story goes…".
  - **UNVERIFIED**: do not script.
  - **ERROR**: a claim found in circulation, or on an official page, that is wrong or contradicted.
- **Sources** are listed in §11 as [O…] (operator), [A…] (Ajuntament / public bodies) and [S…] (secondary).
- **Blocked or failed this session.** None was retried.
  - books.google.com (Mendelson, *Documenting Spain*, p. 35): permission not answered.
