# AUDIT V5: facts (Poble Espanyol, 25 tracks), 31 Oct 2026

Independent fact audit of `v5/tracks/001–025.perf.txt` (spoken bodies), checked against FACTS_OCT2026, VISIT_ROUTE_AND_SPACES, the official plan notes and images, PLAN §2 / §4 NEVER / Held, and the three writers' notes. Tracks were not edited.

**Web method.** WebFetch and WebSearch only, sequential, one page per site at a time, sites alternated. No curl, no browser, no CAPTCHA. WebFetch returns a model summary, so quotes below are "as relayed". About 30 pages were checked; the URLs are listed in each finding and in §4.

**Counts.** 25 tracks: **9 OK, 15 FIX, 1 HOLD.** Findings: **3 MUST, 12 SHOULD, 16 NICE.** 15 claims are unverified either way (§3).

**NEVER / Held check.** No NEVER item or held item slipped into speech. There are no prices, hours, shop names, events, Stresemann, Kadokawa, Guinovart, Franco in 1968, Amaya's birth year, "exact replicas", "miniature", "1,600 villages", "Sevillian", "concentration camp", the King's visit date, or present tense for the dead. One near-miss is 011, "the man who … came from the business": this is past tense about Daurella, but it is an event, not a claim that he is alive or dead, so it is acceptable.

## 2. Findings

### MUST

**M1. 023: wrong year implied.**
- **Track text:** "Work began in January 1928. More than a thousand workers from across the country built it, the Poble says, and it opened the following May, about sixteen months later."
- **Problem:** "The following May" after January 1928 is May 1928. HIST: "inaugurated on the 20th of May 1929". The sentence contradicts itself, and the writer's notes call it intentional ("'the following May' for 1929").
- **Source:** https://poble-espanyol.com/en/el-poble/the-history/ (via FACTS 3.1; the writers re-read it 31 Oct).
- **Replace with:** "…built it, the Poble says, and it opened in May 1929, about sixteen months later."

**M2. 007 (and 004): the clutch story overreaches its source.**
- **007 track text:** "And this is Navarre, the region of the burnt clutch. Tudela, where the Hispano-Suiza sat in the repair shop, is a Navarrese town."
- **004 track text:** "It wasn't all smooth. On the road to Zaragoza the car broke down, and Utrillo recorded the moment in a caricature. A letter from Borja says, in Catalan, 'we have the car in Tudela being repaired, because the clutch burned out'."
- **Problem:** POST25, as relayed today, quotes "esperant l'Expres de Zaragoza perque tenim l'auto a Tudela en reparació per haverse cremat l'embrague", dated "(FP) LP26-28 (15/11/27)".
  - The authors do **not** say it was written from Borja. It sits in their Borja section, and the letter says "el nostre cuartel general es a Zaragoza".
  - They do **not** say the car was the Hispano-Suiza ("l'auto").
  - 15 Nov 1927 falls inside the **second** trip (9–20 Nov, ACE19 p. 237).
  - ACE19's breakdown ("llegando a Zaragoza", Fig. 6, caricature) is undated, so it may be a different incident.
  - 004, heard straight after the September trip, puts the clutch on the Hispano-Suiza journey, and 007 states it outright.
- **Sources:** https://upcommons.upc.edu/bitstreams/a65af596-4fe6-4b4f-94dd-8406ce2b35d7/download ; https://revistes.upc.edu/index.php/ACE/article/view/5460/807
- **Replace 007 with:** "And this is Navarre. On the second trip, that November, the car ended up in a repair shop in Tudela, a Navarrese town."
- **Replace 004 with:** "It wasn't all smooth. Near Zaragoza the car broke down, and Utrillo recorded the moment in a caricature. On the second trip a letter, written while they waited for the Zaragoza express, says, in Catalan, 'we have the car in Tudela being repaired, because the clutch burned out'."

**M3. 004: the notebooks' drawer.**
- **Track text:** "More than a hundred photographs and sketches, in five travel notebooks, stayed in Reventós's desk for about ninety years."
- **Problem:** ACE19 p. 239 n. 11, as relayed: the notebooks "han permanecido ocultas en el cajón del escritorio del despacho de **J.M Reventós**". That is the office of a J. M. Reventós, not the architect Ramon Reventós (d. 1976); EGA24's references cite the "Private archive of the Reventós family". **No source gives a duration**: ACE19 says only "hasta hoy", and EGA24 says "remained hidden until today". FACTS 2.14 already wrote "ninety years" into its safe wording without a source, and PLAN repeated it. Five notebooks were "shared by Nogués and Reventós" (ARQ20 p. 83), so all five being in one desk is the papers' account, but not the timing.
- **Sources:** https://revistes.upc.edu/index.php/ACE/article/view/5460/807 ; https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-57579-2_26 ; https://www.redalyc.org/journal/1936/193662824005/193662824005.pdf
- **Replace with:** "…in five travel notebooks, lay hidden in a desk drawer in a Reventós family office until the researchers found them."
- **Related 025 text:** "Remember the notebooks that lay hidden in a drawer?" This line in 025 is fine as it stands.
