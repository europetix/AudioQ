I checked each cue's last line against the next @title with a script, played the Full, Essential and Highlights orders with the bridges, and counted words and sentence lengths (tags stripped, the same way as `v5_direction.clean`).

**Counts.** MUST 3 findings (5 track instances) · SHOULD 22 · NICE 13.

## 2. Findings

### MUST

**M1. Extras offered to versions that don't contain them (002, 015, 018).**
- 002 (heard in H and E; 003 is Full only): *"There's a short Extra you can play here, on what these towers became about sixty years later."*
- 015 (heard in H; 016 is E and Full): *"There's a short Extra in the Sculpture Garden, below the viewpoint, where the giant slides are."*
- 018 (heard in H; 019 is E and Full): *"There's a short Extra on Calle Arcos, at the flamenco house, the Tablao de Carmen, house 61, number 9 on the street: go through Plaza Peñaflor and down Calle Arcos."*
- **Problem:** the listener is told to play a track that isn't in their playlist. It happens at the second stop, right after 001 promised "every stop tells you where to go next". In 018 the H bridge then has to contradict the line ("skip Calle Arcos").
- **Replacements** (word-neutral within ±6; 002 is at 358/360, so cut "about sixty years later" → "in 1990"):
  - 002: *"If you're on the full tour, there's a short Extra here, on what these towers became in 1990. The next main stop is the start of Calle Caballeros…"*
  - 015: *"On the Essential and full tours, there's a short Extra in the Sculpture Garden, where the giant slides are. The next main stop…"*
  - 018: *"On the Essential and full tours, there's a short Extra at the flamenco house on Calle Arcos, the Tablao de Carmen: go through Plaza Peñaflor and down Calle Arcos. The next main stop…"* (this also removes the 61/9 problem, S10).

**M2. 008 cue sends people to the wrong square.**
- **Exact words:** *"Walk round to the Plaza de la Iglesia, the small square on the other side of the tower and the church, below the long grey front of the Fran Daurel Museum."*
- **Problem (plan, NE zoom):**
  - The tower stands between Plaza Aragonesa (below it) and Plaza del Carmen (above it). The grey museum block borders Plaza del Carmen at 62.
  - The Plaza de la Iglesia is reached from Plaza Aragonesa along the foot of 59/58/57 (the church). The blue accessible line runs exactly there. Its fixed feature is the top of the wide steps (the "Santiago Stands" hatching between 33 and 35/36).
  - So "the other side of the tower… below the grey museum" describes the Plaza del Carmen. A visitor following it will stop there.
  - "Grey" is the plan's colour code for non-region buildings, not necessarily the building's real colour (S13).
  - The same picture is in 008 E>011 ("walk round to the Plaza de la Iglesia") and in the @where of 009 and 011.
- **Replacement:** *"Walk on along the front of the church to the Plaza de la Iglesia, the small square where a wide flight of steps goes down [O5, O14]: Plaza de la Iglesia · A church from three towns."* (33 words, under the old 41.)
- **008 E>011:** *"On this shorter tour, skip the church: walk on along its front to the Plaza de la Iglesia and stop by the door of the Fran Daurel Museum: Plaza de la Iglesia · A bottler's art collection."*
- **ONSITE:** add to O5: "From Plaza Aragonesa, is the Plaza de la Iglesia reached along the church front? Is the museum's front actually grey?"

**M3. 023: "the following May" is heard as May 1928.**
- **Exact words:** *"Work began in January 1928. More than a thousand workers from across the country built it, the Poble says, and it opened the following May, about sixteen months later."*
- **Problem:** after "January 1928", "the following May" means May 1928. The next phrase, "sixteen months", contradicts it, so the listener is left with two dates.
- **Replacement:** *"Work began in January 1928, and the village opened in May 1929, about sixteen months later."* This also drops the workers clause, which fixes the pile-up (S15).

### SHOULD (route and placement)

**S1. 001: tell people to take the plan.** All the numbers (115, 2, 102/103, 41, 83, 81, 69, 21, 26) only work if they are posted on the houses (O2) or the visitor holds the plan. 001 says *"Many stops name a house by its number on that plan"* but never says to pick it up.

**S2. 005: the two purple houses are probably not on the street.** *"Near the start of the street the plan shows two purple houses…"*

**S3. 012/011 cue: where 83 is.** *"Stop at house 83, just below where Carrer Mercaders comes in."*

- **Replacement:** *"Stop at house 83, a few doors up, before Carrer Mercaders comes in: the house with an outside stair up its front [O6]."* Use the same in 008 H>012.

**S4. 015/016: the way into the Sculpture Garden is unverified, and the plan points to the steps by 89.**

- O10 checks only "open? dead end? labels". It doesn't ask where the entrance is.
- **Action:**

  - if it's by 89, hold an ALT-G2: play 016 from the 012 stop (house 83 is next to the junction at 89), with 012's E cue ending *"There's a short Extra in the Sculpture Garden, down the steps by house 89…"*, and 016's cue *"Come back up the steps and walk up Carrer Mercaders to house 81"*.
