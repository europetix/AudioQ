# Poble Espanyol de Montjuïc: visitor route, spaces and status (research as of 31 Oct 2026)

Prepared for: Yo Tours audio guide (poble_v5). Researcher: subagent, 31 Oct 2026.
Method: WebSearch + WebFetch only. Official site first (EN/ES/CA pages, plus the site's own public WordPress data feeds at `poble-espanyol.com/wp-json/...`, which are official data), fetched one page at a time. I did not use curl or a browser, and I hit no CAPTCHA.

Grades:
- **OFF**: an official poble-espanyol.com page or official data feed, fetched.
- **SEC**: a secondary page, fetched (Ajuntament/Turisme de Barcelona, Barcelona Film Commission, press, reference works, academic theses, accessibility platforms). The type is shown in brackets.
- **SNIP**: a search snippet only. Treat it as a lead, not a fact.
- **INF**: my own inference from the graded facts listed with it. It is not a source fact, so verify on site before it goes into a script.

Unless noted, every page was fetched on 31 Oct 2026. Where a page carries a modified date, it is shown as "(mod. YYYY-MM-DD)".

Quote limits: the fetch tool only returns short excerpts (under about 30 words), so the verbatim quotes below are short by necessity.

## 0. Key facts for the script (summary)

1. **There is one public day entrance: the gate copied from the Puerta de San Vicente in Ávila (the "towers of Ávila").** OFF, CA building page: *"L'entrada principal del Poble Espanyol és la representació d'aquest monument."* The postal address used everywhere is Av. de Francesc Ferrer i Guàrdia, 13. The building record for the gate itself gives "Avinguda Marquès de Comillas, 27" (OFF data). A 2011 UPC thesis (SEC-acad) calls it the lowest point of the site: *"Nuestra visita obliga a que entremos por la puerta de San Vicente, hecho que la dota de entrada principal."*
2. **The site rises from west to east.** The gate is at the west, low end. The Romanesque **Monastery of Sant Miquel** stands at the east, high end: *"situado en uno de los extremos del recinto"* (OFF, ES space page). The thesis (SEC-acad) also puts the monastery at the highest point.
3. **The only official sequencing I found is the family treasure-hunt ("gimcana"/Pouch Game) answer sheet** (OFF PDF, July 2025). Its stops run in this order:
   1. *Paraguas – Calle Caballeros*
   2. *Caballeros y Príncipe de Viana*
   3. *Plaza Aragonesa (Fiesta y Museo Fran Daurel)*
   4. *Catalunya hasta Plaza de la Font*
   5. *Jardín Escultórico y Monasterio de Sant Miquel*
   6. *Andalucía y Tablao de Carmen*
   7. *Gradas de Santiago – Camino de Santiago*
   8. *Plaza Mayor – Rincón del Triste*

   That is a loop: out along the north side, back along the south side, finishing in Plaza Mayor.
4. **Plaza Mayor** is *"El espacio más emblemático y grande del recinto"*, 2,043.65 m², *"Réplica de la plaza castellana de Riaza, en Segovia"* (OFF). The **Valderrobres Town Hall** is *"ubicado en la Plaza Mayor"* (OFF). The photographic exhibition about the site's construction is in that Town Hall (OFF).
5. **Fran Daurel Museum: still open and included in the ticket** (OFF, mod. 2025-09-25). Its official location is **"Placeta de la Iglesia"**, beside Plaza Aragonesa. The **FIESTA** 10-minute audiovisual is in the **Utebo Tower** (OFF, mod. 2026-03-03).
6. **Andalusian quarter: "Arcos Street, which belongs to the Andalusian quarter"** (SEC, Barcelona Film Commission). Tablao de Carmen is at "C/ Arcos, 9 (Poble Espanyol)" (SEC), which is official "Casa 61" (OFF).
7. **Official map**: a 2026 plan exists only as PNG images (uploaded 19 to 23 Feb 2026), in 6 languages. I could not read the image, so its numbering is not transcribed here (section 2). The "accessible route" map and "visual guide" downloads are announced, but I could not find their files.
8. **Changes 2024 to 2026:**
   - The former glassblowing workshop became the **Vidre Art Gallery**, opened 21 Feb 2024 (SEC, Time Out). It is open Wednesday to Saturday only (OFF).
   - The live glassblower continues at **Vitromarce, Casa 81** (OFF, mod. 2026-03-18).
   - **Today (31 Oct 2026)** there are Halloween events: LA ZONA (night, separate ticket, access via *Camí de la Foixarda*), Mascarada, and Creepy Halloween Family.
   - The Christmas season starts 28 Nov: Natalis shows *"at the Plaza Mayor"*, and Olivia in the Espai Guinovart theatre.
   - Plaza Mayor can be covered by the "Hivernacle" structure of more than 1,350 m² for events.

### 3.2 Zones (official)

The official site files buildings into 4 zones (`categories-edifici-poble`). Verbatim from the ES architecture page:

- Mediterráneo: *"En la zona mediterránea del recinto conocerás la arquitectura de Cataluña y Baleares."*
- Sur: *"Pasea por las típicas calles con casitas blancas y macetas azules de los pueblos de Andalucía…"*
- Centro: *"una región repleta de construcciones medievales que en el Poble Espanyol de Barcelona no podía faltar."*
- Norte: *"En el Poble te harás una idea del encanto y funcionalidad de la arquitectura de la franja cantábrica."*

The site claims **117 buildings** (OFF, several pages). The official building database holds **102 records** (CA listing: Mediterrani 26, Nord 35, Centre 32, Sud 9). The other 15 are not individually documented online.

**Area figures disagree:**
- "more than 40,000 m²" (OFF, home page)
- 49,000 m² (SEC, Film Commission)
- 20,000 m² (SEC, Enciclopèdia Catalana)

Avoid quoting an area in scripts, or use the official "more than 40,000 m²".

**Data errors in the official records** (do not copy them into scripts):
- "Arcos de la Lonja" is filed under "Castilla y León / Zaragoza", but Zaragoza is in Aragón. Its title gives "Sos del Rey Católico".
- The "Arcos de Sos" page describes Jérica.
- Casa Consistorial (La Fresneda, Teruel), Casa Esquinera (Albarracín) and Convent de les Clarisas (Medinaceli) are filed under Centre.
- Some Castilian and Cantabrian items are filed under Nord.

### 3.3 Where every documented building sits (official map pins)

The official building database gives each building a hand-placed map pin. I converted the pins to **metres east (E) and north (N) of the Ávila gate pin**.

How reliable this is:
- The pins are **approximate**. At least one pair is identical: Graus and Caldas de Reyes share a pin, so one of them is wrong.
- Use the numbers for **cluster and order, not exact door positions**.
- Grade: OFF data (wp-json, fetched 31 Oct 2026); the interpretation is INF.
- Scale: the site spans about 320 m east–west (gate at 0, monastery at +296 m) and about 110 m north–south.

The resulting layout (INF from the pins, consistent with the thesis and the treasure-hunt order):

1. **Gate and Extremadura/Castile vestibule** (−25 to +45 E). Just inside the gate are the Cáceres and Plasencia palaces:
   - Casa del Sol, Golfines de Abajo, Carvajal, Mayoralgo, Almaraz, Hernando de Ovando, and Palacio del Obispo (Miajadas)
   - Escudo de un Atienza
   - This is the "Plaza Castellana" area.
   - Casa del Sol is Casa 3 (Casa del Sol Bar Bistro, OFF).
2. **North street eastwards = Calle Caballeros** (y = 0 to +9 N). It is lined by Castilian houses with coats of arms:
   - Casa del Doncel (Sigüenza), Casa de la Torre and Palacio de los Contreras (Ayllón), Casa del Segle XV (Segovia)
   - Casa del Marqués de Santillana (Cantabria), Palacio Episcopal (El Burgo de Osma), Casa del Francès (Molinos de Duero)
   - Benavides and Toro (Toro), Casa Ramos (Vinuesa), Peñafiel
3. **It continues as Príncipe de Viana (Navarre/Basque)** (+107 to +148 E, about 0 N):
   - Escut de López de Goicoechea (Olazagutía), Relieves prerrománicos (Villatuerta), Caserío Arteche (Erandio, Vizcaya), Valle del Roncal
   - Palacio de San Cristóbal (Estella), Vergara (Guipúzcoa), Palacio Álava-Esquivel, Casa de las Cadenas (Corella)
   - These match the treasure-hunt stop *"Caballeros y Príncipe de Viana"*.
4. **Plaza Mayor** (about +30 to +80 E, −10 to −70 N):
   - North side: Ajuntament de Vallderoures/Valderrobres (+47, −13), Ajuntament de Sigüenza (+44, −11), Casa de los Moreno (Segovia), Casa de Manuel Jiménez
   - South side: El Mirador (Sigüenza), Aranda de Duero, El Burgo de Osma, Alquézar
   - West approach: Las Arcadas (Sangüesa), Casa Consistorial and Casa de Encomienda (La Fresneda), Ajuntament de Graus
   - Plaza Mayor bars are Casa 27–28 (Abrelatas *"Main Square bar"*, Boca Loco), and Cookiteca's location is "Plaza Mayor" (OFF).
5. **South-west block** (−15 to +35 E, −55 to −93 N):
   - Antiguo Ayuntamiento de Sigüenza, Navalcarnero (Madrid), Casa del Cabildo (Sigüenza), Casa de Águila and Casa Barreda (Santillana del Mar), Casa de los Ángulos (Borja), La Casona (Riaza)
   - INF: the Foixarda-side houses (Casa 9–18: Albí, La Llavor, Upload, La Foixarda) are probably here. **Not verified.**
6. **Maestrazgo / Aragonese–Valencian block east of Plaza Mayor** (+76 to +116 E, 0 to −30 N):
   - Arcos de Sos, Casa Miralles (Catí), Morella, Casa Jassa and Casa Moix (Calaceite)
   - Albarracín, Fraga, Casa de las Conchas (Borja), Sariñena, Maluenda, Casa Esquinera (Albarracín)
7. **Galicia and Asturias, south of that block** (+76 to +101 E, −35 to −68 N):
   - Cambados, Pazo de Fefiñáns (Cambados), Villanueva de Lourenzá, Betanzos, Cangas de Onís, Arcos de la Lonja
   - INF: the **Gradas de Santiago** are probably here, because the Galician cluster lies between Andalusia and Plaza Mayor, as treasure-hunt stops 6→7→8 require. **Verify on the plan.**
   - The Galician-named restaurants Faro do Norte (Casa 33) and MexGal (Casa 34–35) support the idea that houses 30–35 are in this area (INF).
8. **Plaza Aragonesa / Placeta de la Iglesia** (about +125 to +160 E, −40 N):
   - Església de les Carmelitas (Alcañiz) at +127/−40, Convent de les Clarisas (Medinaceli) at +129/−45
   - Torralba de Ribota (Calatayud) at +152/−43, and the **Utebo tower** at +158/−43
   - The Fran Daurel Museum (Placeta de la Iglesia) and FIESTA (Utebo Tower) are here.
9. **Catalan / Mediterranean zone (north-east)** (+150 to +231 E, about 0 to −25 N):
   - Portal d'en Bover, Montblanc and Casa Desclergue (Montblanc); Besalú; Camprodon; Isona; Santa Pau
   - The Rupit houses: Can Lo, Can Vázquez, Can Rovira, Can Betes, Can Banús
   - Hospital de Santa Magdalena, Casa Ciurana (Morella), Casa dels Balbs, Casa Plandiura (La Garriga), Cornudella, Casa del Marqués de la Floresta (Tàrrega), La Jana
   - This is the Plaça de la Font and Mercaders area, with the artisan concentration (Casa 64–114).
10. **Andalusian quarter (south-east)** (+159 to +213 E, −17 to −49 N):
    - Posada de las Ánimas (Ronda), Córdoba, Palacio del Marqués de Peñaflor (Écija), Calle Arcos (Arcos de la Frontera)
    - Pati (Córdoba), Casa de Nínca (Úbeda), Casa de los Celdrán (Murcia), Casa de García de Veas (Arcos de la Frontera), Iglesia del Santo Ángel (Córdoba)

    - Tablao de Carmen is Casa 61. The flamenco shops Olé que Arte and Dolores Montero are also Casa 61, and La Bodeguita is Casa 60.
11. **Monastery of Sant Miquel** (+296 E), with the **Sculpture Garden**, the **viewpoint** *"just davant"* the monastery, and the **Baluard**. This is the eastern end, the highest point, with views over Barcelona.

Note on Casa Desclergue (Montblanc): its pin (+37, −14) lands at Plaza Mayor, which is odd for a Catalan building. The Jérica pin (+30, −17) is similar. Treat both as **suspect pins**. Check them on the plan.
