[curious] In 1929 this village was meant to be one long party.

The Poble's own history lists what went on during the Exhibition: "folk festivals with dances, regional dances, open-air dances, pageants and even horse-riding competitions." A whole country's fiestas, staged in a village that was itself a kind of stage set.

Today the fiesta comes in ten minutes. The Poble places its show, called Fiesta, "inside the iconic Utebo Tower", and makes a simple promise: "For 10 minutes, let yourself be carried away by a journey of light and sound."

[lightly] Among the festivals it takes in are the human towers of the Castellers, La Patum of Berga, the Sanfermines of Pamplona, and Holy Week in Andalusia.

The show was made in 2018 by Mediapro, the audiovisual group. The room is seven metres high, and the images fill two great walls, each about eight metres by seven.

Before you go in, take one more look up at the tower that holds the show. It's the cast they made in plaster moulds, the one they said they liked better than the original.

Once the lights go down, this guide has nothing to add.

When you come out, the next main stop is the door of the Fran Daurel Museum, on the Plaza de la Iglesia: Plaza de la Iglesia · A bottler's art collection.
