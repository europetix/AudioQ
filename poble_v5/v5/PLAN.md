### The route (reasoning; binding order, provisional geometry)
**Fixed points (sourced).**
- **One public entrance and exit: the Ávila gate** (ROUTE §1.1–1.2: "L'entrada principal del Poble Espanyol és la representació d'aquest monument", OFF; no daytime exit by any other gate found). Just inside: **Plaza Castellana** (PLAN; RAH11 "espacio vestíbulo"), the information point beside house 26 (PLAN "i"), and the arcade at 26 into the Plaza Mayor (PLAN; RAH11 "two porticoed crujías").
- **The site climbs** from the gate, its lowest point, to the monastery, its highest (RAH11; SPACE-MON). On the plan the gate is at the bottom right and the monastery at the top left.
- **The official loop** is the treasure-hunt answer sheet (GIMCANA, July 2025): 1 Paraguas – Calle Caballeros → 2 Caballeros y Príncipe de Viana → 3 Plaza Aragonesa (Fiesta y Museo Fran Daurel) → 4 Catalunya hasta Plaza de la Font → 5 Jardín Escultórico y Monasterio de Sant Miquel → 6 Andalucía y Tablao de Carmen → 7 Gradas de Santiago – Camino de Santiago → 8 Plaza Mayor – Rincón del Triste.
- **The plan** confirms the streets in that order (PLAN images):
  - Calle Caballeros leaves Plaza Castellana between houses 115 and 2, climbs past 113, 112, 110, 109, 108, and becomes Calle Príncipe de Viana at 102/103, where Travesía del Maestrazgo goes off beside 41 (accessible-route marker);
  - Calle Príncipe de Viana runs past 101, 100, 99, 97 (a round shape on the street edge), 96, 95, 94 into Plaza Aragonesa (accessible-route marker by 92–94);
  - the brick tower (3D drawing) stands between Plaza Aragonesa and Plaza del Carmen, with 57–59 at its foot, the grey Fran Daurel block and the Guinovart Space beyond, and Plaza de la Iglesia below them;
  - Carrer de la Davallada climbs from Plaza Aragonesa past 89–93 and 83/88 to Carrer Mercaders; Calle Arcos comes down into it at the yellow 61/93 corner;
  - Carrer Mercaders runs up to Plaça de la Font between 75–80, 85, 82 on one side and 84, 81, 86, 87 on the other;
  - Plaça de la Font opens onto the Baluard and Barcelona Panorama; the Sculpture Garden and the Giant Slides lie below; steps lead from the Baluard up to the monastery path; the accessible path goes round behind 73/74 to a junction by 69;
  - the Monastery of Sant Miquel (3D drawing, 116) sits at the top, with a loop path; from the junction by 69 a path runs between 69 and the Vidre art gallery (67/68) into Plaza de la Hermandad;
  - the yellow Andalusian quarter: Plaza de la Hermandad (66, 65, 67, 68), Plaza Peñaflor (between 64 and 65), Calle Arcos (the many 61s), Calle Bulas (63, 64) down to Plaza del Carmen and 62;
  - the Gradas de Santiago (steps) lead down from the Plaza de la Iglesia between 33 and 35–37 to Calle Alcalde Zalamea; Rincón del Triste is the corner by 30, 31 and 20 at the top of the Plaza Mayor;
  - the Plaza Mayor: the town hall 21 on its Calle Rius i Taulet side, the bandstand (3D drawing) at the opposite corner, houses 13–20 along Calle Alcalde Zalamea, 8–12 along Calle Tercio, 25–28 and 4–5 on the Plaza Castellana side; grey lines inside mark the steps of its level change (RAH11: about 2 m, handled with a platform and steps).
- **The accessible route** (blue dashed line on the plan) follows Calle Caballeros and Calle Príncipe de Viana, Carrer de la Davallada and Carrer Mercaders, goes round behind 73/74 to the monastery, back along the top path, through the Andalusian quarter by Calle Bulas, and into the Plaza Mayor from Plaza Castellana and by Calle Alcalde de Móstoles. **It avoids** the Gradas de Santiago, the Bajada de Cervantes, the Baluard steps and the square's inner steps [O18]. The monastery and the interior of the Andalusian patio are not wheelchair-accessible (TUR4ALL; APROPA).
- **What can be seen from the viewpoint (sourced only):** "Right in front of the Romanesque monastery of Sant Miquel is a viewpoint"; "Take some time out there to marvel at what is in front of you: La Sagrada Familia, the Mapfre towers, Tibidabo… and Barcelona at your feet" (EN; VIEW, OFF-op, page mod. 29 Jan 2026, planner re-read 31 Oct 2026). The Sculpture Garden page: "a unique view over the city of Barcelona" from "the vantage point at the end of the garden" (GARDEN). The Baluard page: "unas espectaculares vistas sobre Barcelona" (SPACE-BAL). **Nothing else.** O9 confirms each of the three named landmarks from where the listener stands; any landmark not ticked is cut.

**Chosen order (one loop, no backtracking except the Sculpture Garden dead end and one re-crossing of Plaza Aragonesa):**
Plaza Castellana: welcome (001), the gate (002, Extra 003) → Calle Caballeros (004 at its start, 005 walking) → the junction at 102/103 (006) → up Calle Príncipe de Viana (007 walking) → Plaza Aragonesa and the tower (008) → Plaza de la Iglesia (009, Extra 010 FIESTA, 011 Fran Daurel) → up Carrer de la Davallada to 83 (012) → Carrer Mercaders, 81 (013) → Plaça de la Font (014) → the Baluard and Barcelona Panorama (015) → Sculpture Garden (Extra 016, in and back) → up the steps to the monastery (017) → down the path past 69 to Plaza de la Hermandad (018) → Plaza Peñaflor and down Calle Arcos to the Tablao (Extra 019) → Plaza Aragonesa (re-crossed) to Plaza del Carmen (020, heard crossing to the Plaza de la Iglesia) → the top of the Gradas de Santiago (021) → down the steps, past Rincón del Triste, into the Plaza Mayor (022) → the town hall, 21 (023) → the square's steps (024) → the arcade at 26 by Plaza Castellana (025) → out through the gate.

**Why this order:**
1. **It is the operator's own loop** (GIMCANA), so signs, staff and the family trail all agree with us, and it is a true circuit ending by the gate.
2. **The Plaza Mayor last, not first** (the skeleton had it both first and last). The square is the natural place to sit at the end, the town hall's photographs of the building site recap the whole story, and the CLOSE stands by the exit. The trips story, which frames every house, moves to the start of Calle Caballeros (004), so the listener carries it up the hill: Navarre's burnt clutch on Príncipe de Viana (callback 007), "we like ours better" at the Utebo tower (008), the Rupit stair (012), Andalusia added for the King (018), La Casona at Riaza (022).
3. **Uphill first, downhill back.** The steady climb is done while the listener is fresh; the walk back goes down through Andalusia and the Santiago Steps.
4. **The viewpoint before the monastery**, because the official viewpoint is "right in front of the monastery" and the treasure hunt does "Jardín Escultórico y Monasterio" in that order; the monastery then leads straight down the path into the Andalusian quarter (PLAN).
5. **Politics at the viewpoint** (015): its sourced content is one sentence of landmarks, while the 1929 Exhibition and its dictatorship were all round this hill (F3.17). The Franco years (020) come late, in time order, on the walk back.

**Rejected alternatives:**
- **The Plaza Mayor first** (skeleton 003–005): sends people into the big square, back out to Calle Caballeros (a re-walk of Plaza Castellana), and crowds the ending with nothing new. Kept as ALT-P only.
- **The monastery before the viewpoint and the garden**: the garden is a dead end below the viewpoint; doing it after the monastery means climbing twice.
- **Andalusia from Plaza Aragonesa on the way up** (via Calle Bulas): splits Catalonia from the monastery and leaves the walk down with nothing to see.
- **The Bajada de Cervantes as a route**: steps, and off the loop. Mentioned only as an optional look (007).

- **PIN**: weak (pin far off, or colour mismatch). Town name only, never placed.
