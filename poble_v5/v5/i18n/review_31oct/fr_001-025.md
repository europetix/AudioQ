- Kings: « Alphonse treize », « Victoire-Eugénie » (French usage, no Roman numerals). Please flag if the voice should keep "Alfonso".

- 018: "Exactly when he said it, the sources don't agree" becomes « Sur la date exacte, les sources divergent. »
- 020: "the sources we have read" becomes « Nos sources ».
- 024: "A night bar opened in the gate you came in by" becomes « dans la porte d'entrée ». This avoids a gendered past participle.
