# DE notes, Poble Espanyol 001-025 (Katja, formal Sie)

Self-check: `assemble.py --check … de` gives all 25 tracks OK, exit 0. German word counts run 1–8% under the English. Bridges: 8/8 keys, each ≤ the English word count, every one starts "In dieser Kurzfassung".

## Names and titles
- No official German pages from the Poble were used. Every street, square and building name is kept exactly as the English gives it.
- Glossary applied: das Poble Espanyol / das Dorf, der offizielle Plan, das Ávila-Tor, Mudéjar-Stil, die Weltausstellung von 1929, Kernversion / vollständige Tour / Highlights, "ein kurzer Extra-Titel".
- Monastery of Sant Miquel: in titles and cues it is "Monestir de Sant Miquel" (the brief's option, since there is no German official name). In speech it is "das Kloster Sant Miquel". 017 keeps "auf Katalanisch das Monestir de Sant Miquel".
- Fran Daurel Museum becomes "das Museum Fran Daurel", with only the generic word translated. Vidre art gallery becomes "die Kunstgalerie Vidre". Sculpture Garden becomes "Skulpturengarten". Town hall becomes "Rathaus". Utebo tower becomes "Utebo-Turm".
- Santiago Steps (001) is "die Santiago-Stufen". 021 keeps "Gradas de Santiago, die Santiago-Stufen".
- Cue titles in speech use the German track titles, in the format "Ort · Titel".

## Quotations
- No quote in the guide was originally German. Every quote is a faithful German translation of the English, in „…“.
- The English framing "in Spanish" / "in Catalan" is kept as "auf Spanisch" / "auf Katalanisch" everywhere it appears: 001, 002, 003, 004, 005, 006, 007, 008, 009, 011, 012, 013, 014, 015, 016, 018, 022, 023, 024, 025.
- Spanish or Catalan words that the English keeps in the original stay in the original: "Castilla, Navarra y País Vasco" (007), una simbiosis (009), leves pendientes (016), Bailaora (019).
- Digits inside English quotes are spoken in words: hundertsiebzehn, achthunderttausend, fünfundvierzig, fünfzehn, zehn Minuten. "20th century" (016) becomes "des zwanzigsten Jahrhunderts".
- 007: the school guide's „elf kleine Skulpturen warten auf euch“ keeps the informal plural of the original, which speaks to children. The listener is still addressed as Sie.
- 015 (dictatorship) and 020 (war, camp, museum) are translated level, with every attribution and hedge kept.
