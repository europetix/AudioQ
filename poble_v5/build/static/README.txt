WHY THIS EDITION
================
Built from scratch to the Yo Tours standard. The village's own audio guide is a paid tablet that reviewers call hard to
follow; its guided tours run on weekends only. This guide:
1. Walks one loop from the Ávila gate uphill to the Monastery of Sant Miquel and back down to the Plaza Mayor, naming
   every street and square as the official plan does, and pointing out houses by their number on that plan.
2. Tells you, house by house, where the original stands and what the makers changed: the Utebo tower cast in plaster
   moulds and a little smaller, the Rupit stair with seven steps instead of fourteen, the town hall of Valderrobres made
   larger, the monastery assembled from four Catalan Romanesque churches, a Plaza Mayor laid out like Riaza's.
3. Tells the story of the four men who made it (Utrillo, Nogués, Folguera, Reventós), their journeys across Spain in 1927
   and 1928, marks out of ten and a burnt-out clutch; Andalusia left out and then added for the King; the dictatorship it
   was born under, the war and the Franco years, the critics, the revival, and the village today. The difficult history
   is told evenly, with every view attributed.
4. Checks every fact against the village's own pages, academic studies of the 1927 journeys and reliable sources
   (31 Oct 2026). Where the documentation is thin (the northern houses), the guide says so. Errors on the village's own
   pages (for example, that Puig i Cadafalch designed the monastery) are corrected. Two independent audits.
5. Three lengths (one recording, three folders):
   Full tour   @@N_FULL@@ tracks, about 1 h 30; nearer 2 h if you also go round the Fran Daurel art museum
   Essential   @@N_ESS@@ tracks, about 1 h 15:  @@ESSENTIAL@@
   Highlights  @@N_HI@@ tracks, about 45 min:   @@HIGHLIGHTS@@
   The times include walking. In the shorter versions a short spoken line at the end of a track tells you the next stop.
6. The Yo Tours sound: voice finishing, a soft room tone and a short chime before each track. MP3s are labelled for phones.

HOW TO MAKE THE AUDIO (on a Mac)
================================
English:          double-click generate_audio.command  (voice 1 = Ava)
                  option 1 = all three lengths (records once; folders _Essential and _Highlights)
                  option 2 = two voice samples (008 the Utebo tower + 012 seven steps)
                  option 3 = Highlights only (quickest; option 1 later reuses those recordings)
Other languages:  double-click generate_audio_ES_FR_DE.command and pick the language, then the same options.
Needs internet, python3, ffmpeg (brew install ffmpeg) and an up-to-date edge-tts:
  python3 -m pip install --user --upgrade edge-tts
If it stops, run it again: finished tracks are skipped.
