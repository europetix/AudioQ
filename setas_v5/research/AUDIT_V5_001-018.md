## Per-track findings

### 001 Welcome (ANCHOR, 350 w)

1. **SHOULD, route.** "If it's closed when you're here, go to the lifts beside its entrance and play A plaza with many lives."
   - Problem: 001 plays in all three versions. Highlights doesn't contain "A plaza with many lives" (lengths.json H = 001 008 012 018), so an H listener is pointed to a stop they can't find. The H bridge then sends them somewhere else.
   - Source: build/static/lengths.json; PLAN §3, never-skip rule.
   - Replace with: "If you're on the full tour and it's closed when you're here, go to the lifts beside its entrance and play A plaza with many lives."
2. **SHOULD, standard.** "Whichever is on your phone, play it in order: the numbers may jump, and every stop tells you where to go next."
   - Problem: PLAN §4 (NEVER) bans "stop numbers, any numbering". The writer flagged this as a lead decision.
   - Replace with: "Whichever version is on your phone, play the stops in order. The shorter versions skip some, and every stop tells you where to go next."
3. **SHOULD, unverified.** "The full tour, with the Antiquarium, takes just under an hour. The essential version takes about thirty-five minutes and the highlights about twenty-five."
   - Problem: these are PLAN §3 desk estimates (55, 36 and 23 minutes) that depend on the O17 stopwatch walk. The walking time is a guess with no map.
   - Fix: keep it marked provisional. Re-record after O17, or cut to "The shorter versions take less time" if O17 hasn't been done.
4. **NICE, version.** "This guide takes you from one to the other."
   - Problem: the "one" is the Medusa mosaic in the Antiquarium. Essential and Highlights listeners never go in.
   - Replace with: "This guide connects the two."
5. **NICE, route.** "If you came in from the north side, you'll reach the same underground level."
   - Problem: this plays before anyone has gone down, so the past tense doesn't fit.
   - Replace with: "If you're at the north entrance instead, the way down there leads to the same underground level."
6. **NICE, standard.** "the taquilla, the box office" and "its own admission"
   - Problem: PLAN's gloss is "ticket office" and its exception allows "its own ticket". The writer changed both only because lint bans "ticket".
   - Fix: relax lint to allow "ticket office" and the one 001 line, then use PLAN's wording.

### 002 Found under a car park (W, 299 w)

Verified:
- 1990s car-park plan abandoned; opened 27 March 2011; 4,879 m²; "most important archaeological site that remains from the Roman period in Seville" (https://visitasevilla.es/en/?p=1576).
- Tiberius (30 AD) to the 13th century (https://andalucia.org/en/sevilla-visitas-antiquarium).

1. **NICE, listening.** "A car park that was never built became a window into two thousand years of the city."
   - Problem: the same track says just before that the remains cover "some 1,200 years". Two different spans, close together.
   - Replace with: "A car park that was never built became a window into Roman and Moorish Seville."
2. **NICE, unverified.** "It is run by Seville City Council."
   - Problem: no page read today names an operator. VS-ANT is silent. Research route §3.1 marks it CHECK. The comparison file cites the Patronato del Real Alcázar, which is also a city body, so the line is probably true. It adds nothing for the listener.
   - Fix: cut it, or keep it only after the O4 signage check.

### 003 Seville stacked up (R, 254 w)

Verified:
- The 1991 trader-commissioned dig, the 3 × 3 m pit and the depths (AAA91, AAA99 PDF https://juntadeandalucia.es/cultura/tabula/bitstream/20.500.11947/20301/1/AAA_1999_111_pozoblazquez_antiguomercadoencarnacion_sevilla.pdf).
- Mayer's "a structure that sits on the Roman ruins" (https://inhabitat.com/video-inhabitat-interviews-metropol-parasol-architect-juergen-mayer-h/2/, in the page transcript).

1. **SHOULD, listening.** "The first proper look under this square was paid for by the market traders."
   - Problem: 002, heard just before, opens with "In the 1990s, Seville wanted somewhere to park. It dug under this square, and it found the Romans." Heard back to back, the two tracks disagree about who dug first.
   - The sources agree on the order: the traders' test dig came in 1991 (AAA91), and the car-park rescue excavation came in 1998 (JMR2018).
   - Replace the opening with: "Before the big digs, the very first look under this square was paid for by the market traders."

### 004 The House of the Column and its Medusa (W, 277 w)

Verified:

2. **NICE, listening.** "There is the Casa del Sigma"

   - Fix: add "the House of the Sigma, the Greek letter", with no claim about why it has that name, or drop the name.

### 009 The Cathedral and the Giralda (W, 289 w; dates 1184, 1248, 1401)

Verified:
- UNESCO: "With its five naves it is the largest Gothic building in Europe"; the Cathedral, Alcázar and Archivo de Indias as one series; the Giralda "was the former minaret" (https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/383/).
- 1248 mosque turned into the church of Santa María; 1401 canons decide on a new cathedral (catedraldesevilla.es its-history).
- MoMA 1:200 model, 2004–07, 442.2006 (https://www.moma.org/collection/works/100411).

1. **SHOULD, version.** "the same Moorish Seville as the house found under this square"
   - Problem: "the house" assumes the listener knows it. The Almohad house is only in 006 (Full). Essential plays 009 (lengths.json) after 001, where "Moorish remains" is all they heard.
   - Replace with: "the same Moorish Seville as a house found under this square".
2. **SHOULD, TTS and PLAN.** "When Fernando III took the city in 1248"
   - Problem: a TTS engine may read "III" as "three" or "I I I". The name is also not one of the seven CAT-FACTS rows PLAN allows for 009. It is true (Ferdinand III of Castile took Seville in November 1248).
   - Replace with: "When Christian Castile took the city in 1248", or, to keep the king, write "King Fernando the Third".
3. Note for the lead (no change needed): MoMA presents "cathedral without walls" as the *architect's aspiration* ("the architect's aspiration to create a 'cathedral without walls' that would be 'democratic'"), not as its own phrase, as FACTS §10 says. The script's "it describes the design as a democratic 'cathedral without walls'" is still accurate.

### 010 The river and the Aljarafe (W, 292 w)

Verified against GA2010 (https://www.ancientportsantiques.com/wp-content/uploads/Documents/PLACES/Spain-Portugal/Hispalis-Gonzalez2010.pdf):
- "datada en la 1ª mitad del s. I d. C."
- "un largo pasillo distribuidor pavimentado de laterculi … ca. 15 m", so "long corridor" is fine
- "cinco piletas de las cuales se han excavado cuatro"
- "3 m de ancho por 3 m de largo"
- "grandes vertidos de abandono donde predominan los restos de pescado apelmazados"
- amortización "época flavia o antonina temprana"
- author at the Universidad de Sevilla

1. **SHOULD, accuracy.** "led to five tanks, each about three metres across. When they were dug out, the tanks were still full of compacted fish remains." The opening also says "Roman tanks, still packed with fish."
   - Problem 1: only four of the five tanks were excavated.
   - Problem 2: the fill is waste dumped when the works was abandoned, mostly fish remains. It is not stock left in the tanks.
   - Replace the opening with: "Some of the oldest things found under this square were Roman tanks, filled with fish remains."
   - Replace the find with: "…led to five tanks, each about three metres across. Four have been excavated. When the works was abandoned, they were filled with rubbish, mostly compacted fish remains. Judging by that fill, they went out of use somewhere between 70 and 120 AD."
2. **NICE, listening.** Figures in the find paragraph: "first century AD", "five tanks", "three metres", "70 and 120 AD".
   - Problem: four figures in four sentences.
   - Fix: acceptable as is. If trimming, drop "each about three metres across".

### 011 The glue that had to be baked (R, 239 w)

All verified:
- *Architect* magazine: "the world's largest building to be held together primarily by glue" (https://www.architectmagazine.com/technology/architectural-detail/metropol-parasol_o).
- Arup: 3000 connection nodes, glued-in steel bars.
- Metsä: "more than 3,000 joints"; "many tasks were also carried out manually, for example drilling 35,000 holes"; largest panel "16.5 metres high"; Kallio, "It is perhaps the most complex timber structure ever built" (https://www.metsagroup.com/metsawood/news-and-publications/news/2011/metropol-parasol---an-icon-of-world-class-timber-engineering/).
- Fraunhofer/ScienceDaily: Kruse's "almost 60 degrees", glue rated "up to 60 degrees", reheating after gluing, bond stable to 70 (https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2010/01/100118091909.htm).

1. **NICE, attribution.** "In 2007 the engineers warned it could not be built as drawn, and it was largely re-engineered."
   - Problem: only one secondary source (Dezeen 2026: "Arup informed the city that the project was unfeasible in 2007", https://www.dezeen.com/?p=2314624). Arup's own project page doesn't mention it. The wording is FACTS's approved HEDGE wording, so this is acceptable.
   - Fix (optional): soften to "By one account, in 2007…".

### 012 The highest point (W, 262 w)

Verified:

   - Problem: O7 hasn't confirmed that the body of the cathedral, not just the Giralda, is clearly visible from the Mirador. The 2011 SEC source says "easily spot the Giralda and the Cathedral".

### 013 Love it or hate it (W, 303 w)

Verified:
- Público 28 Mar 2011: protests at the opening over money for neighbourhood rehabilitation; "como a un Cristo dos pistolas"; "Una moderna catedral urbana…" (https://www.publico.es/actualidad/legado-monteseirin.html).
- Dezeen: 2007 → four years late; Rowan Moore's quote (Dezeen relays it as "published in the Guardian").
- Defensor del Pueblo, 2 Jul 2013: "coste presupuestado de 51.206.918,25 euros", "coste final de 102.349.992,07 euros", museum "supuso un incremento de 8.499.531,64 euros" (https://www.defensordelpueblo.es/noticias/el-defensor-del-pueblo-suspende-su-investigacion-sobre-las-setas-de-la-encarnacion-tras-la-informacion-remitida-por-el-ayuntamiento-de-sevilla-2/).
- Red Dot: "Best of the Best", Product Design 2012, "Urban design and public spaces" (https://www.red-dot.org/project/metropol-parasol-29871).
- Mies van der Rohe 2013: one of five finalists (https://architecturefoundation.org.uk/news/2013/jan/mies-van-der-rohe-award-2013-finalists-announced).
- "More than a million people went up to the lookout the first year" (https://publicspace.org/works/-/project/g315-metropol-parasol).

1. **SHOULD, accuracy and listening.** "from a budget of about 51 million euros to a final bill of about 102 million euros. The museum for the Roman remains alone added 8 and a half million."
   - Problem 1: the Ombudsman notice introduces the museum figure with "Además" and doesn't say whether it is inside the €102.3 m or on top of it. "Alone" implies it is part of the doubling.
   - Problem 2: three large figures in two sentences.
   - Best fix: cut the museum sentence and keep "…roughly doubled, from about 51 million euros to about 102 million."
   - Alternative: "It also said the museum for the Roman remains had added about eight and a half million."
2. **NICE, unverified.** "The British critic Rowan Moore, writing in *The Observer*"
   - Problem: Dezeen says Guardian. Wikipedia cites an Observer/Guardian review of 27 Mar 2011 (theguardian.com/artanddesign/2011/mar/27/…), which is a Sunday and so probably The Observer. The Guardian was blocked, so I couldn't confirm.
   - Fix: keep it if the lead accepts that. The safe version is "The British critic Rowan Moore".
3. **NICE, listening.** "and Feeling Sevilla, the film room, which you should play before you go in."
   - Problem: grammatically this tells you to play the film room.
   - Replace with: "and Feeling Sevilla, about the film room. Play that one before you go in."
