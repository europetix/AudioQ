# Fact audit of the OLD V4 English audio guide (Setas de Sevilla), claim by claim

Auditor: independent. Read 30 Oct 2026. Source checked: `source/old_v4.txt` (20 tracks plus the "About this tour" front matter). I checked it against `research/FACTS_OCT2026.md`, `VISIT_ROUTE_AND_SPACES_OCT2026.md`, `AUDIT_V5_001-018.md` and `OFFICIAL_AUDIO_COMPARISON_OCT2026.md`, and against new web checks (WebSearch and WebFetch only, one page at a time per site). I compared everything with the V5 scripts (`v5/tracks/001–018.perf.txt`) to find what V5 lacks. No other file was edited.

**Verdicts**
- **TRUE**: the source supports it as worded, or close enough to keep with light rewording.
- **PARTLY**: the core is right but a detail is wrong or overstated.
- **FALSE**: contradicted by a better source.
- **UNVERIFIED**: no adequate source found. Do not use it.
- **OPINION**: a judgement or a rhetorical line, not a checkable fact.
- **PRACTICAL**: prices, hours or tips. Never usable in V5.
- **TRUE\***: general knowledge on topics outside the Setas (other monuments, bars). It was not fetched in this audit, so re-check it before use.

**Grades:** OFF = official or primary · SEC = academic, press, trade or reputable secondary · SNIP = blog, Wikipedia-derived or a search title. Research-file IDs (S-HIST, VS-ANT, DEFP, FG2009, AAA91 and so on) are as defined in `FACTS_OCT2026.md`. New sources are listed in §5.

**Overall:** the old guide's **architecture and Antiquarium-object material is mostly real**. Andalucia.com's Antiquarium page (6 Dec 2011, modified 2 Apr 2026) is its visible source for the kissing birds, the Medusa on the floor, the "5th-century" House of the Column, the Bacchus drinking vessels, and the "11 areas / 7 houses". Its **history is badly wrong**:
- Mendizábal (1836) instead of Napoleon's 1810 decree.
- An 1842 market instead of the 1830s.
- An "underground car park 1973–2003" that was never built.
- A "2003 discovery" when the first dig was in 1991.

It also contains several **apparent inventions**:
- a jury with Toyo Ito and Herzog
- EMVISESA as organiser
- the 2013 trademark name change
- a 2015 demolition debate
- polls by age
- a University of Seville "€154 m" study
- "AI-driven" Aurora with temperature and humidity sensors
- moving seats in Feeling Sevilla
- glass floor panels on the walkway

## 4. FALSE list with corrections (worst first)

1. **"Discovered in 2003 when diggers hit Roman masonry while clearing an underground car park" (Intro, 01, 02, 03, 15).**
   - Correct: a traders' test dig in 1991 found Roman Seville 7–9 m down.
   - A 1990s underground car-park plan led to a 1998 rescue excavation and was abandoned.
   - The big excavation ran 2003–2009.
   - There was never an underground car park, only a surface one. (AAA91 OFF; VS-ANT OFF; KRAMER SEC)
2. **"Competition brief rewritten to include the Antiquarium" (03).** Correct: the 2004 brief already required conserving and displaying the remains. (IAPH-PH50 OFF)
3. **"Mendizábal disentailment of 1836" (02).** Correct: demolished after José I Bonaparte's decree of 20 Apr 1810. (FG2009, AAA99)
4. **"In 1842 Melchor Cano designed the market" (Intro, 02).** Correct: approved 1832, built 1833–37, with later works into the 1840s. (FG2009)
5. **"Convent stood almost three hundred years" (02).** Correct: founded 1591, gone by 1810. (AAA99)
6. **"Market for seventy years, car park for thirty, building site for seven" (01).** Correct: market hall about 1837–1973; car park and dig 1973–2000s; construction 2005/06–2011.
7. **"Earliest layers 1st century BC, Republican Roman" (Intro, 03, 04).** Correct: 1st century AD (Tiberius); mosaics from the mid-1st century AD. (JA-ORG, KRAMER)
8. **"Polyurethane resin connections / custom polyurethane resin glue" (Intro, 10).** Correct: epoxy-glued steel rods, heat-tempered. Polyurethane is the 2–3 mm coating. (METSA, FRAUN)
9. **"Supported on only six concrete columns" (01 S&L).** Correct: two concrete columns house the lifts (and V4 contradicts itself in Track 10). (VS-SET)
10. **"Almohad empire… to Senegal" (04).** Correct: the Maghrib plus Islamic Spain; Marrakech was the capital. (BRIT)
11. **"28 metres above the plaza" / "23 m above the elevated plaza… 28" (13).** Correct: heights are above street level. The plaza is at +5 m and the top at about 28.5 m. (S-PM, S-HIST)
12. **"Elevated plaza 3,000 m²" (12).** Correct: almost 4,000 m². (S-PM OFF)
13. **"Feeling Sevilla room on the lower levels" (16).** Correct: inside the parasols at 21 m. (SACYR2021)
14. **"Aurora… projection screen" (12, 17).** Correct: LEDs embedded in the timber. Not a projection. (JMH-AUR)
15. **"Most visited structure after the cathedral" (15).** Correct: the operator said "third most visited" (2021). (SACYR10)
16. **"Alameda de Hércules to the east" (14).** Correct: roughly north (about 345°), and not confirmed as visible.
17. **"Plaza Cristo de Burgos three minutes north" (19).** Correct: east and south-east.
18. **"Triana slightly elevated" (14).** Correct: the elevated ground west of the river is the Aljarafe.
19. **"Wood silvered" (18).** Correct: the timber is polyurethane-coated. Don't promise weathered wood.
20. **"Take the lift to level two" / "up to level 1 market" (10, 11).** Correct: the lift runs from −1 to the walkways. The market is at street level.
21. **"Alameda nightlife since the 16th century" (14).** Correct: a promenade from 1574; nightlife is modern.
