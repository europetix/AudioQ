Prepared for: Yo Tours, Setas V5 audio guide
Method: WebSearch and WebFetch only. Official sites were fetched one page at a time. No curl, no browser, no CAPTCHA. Every page was read on **2026-10-30**.

**Evidence grades**
- **OFF**: official source (the operator, the Sacyr concession site, Junta de Andalucía or Ayuntamiento tourism), fetched.
- **SEC**: secondary source (reseller, app store, review aggregator, press, blog), fetched.
- **SNIP**: seen only as a search-result title or snippet, not fetched. Treat as a lead, not a fact.

**Blocked or not found**
- No page returned a hard block.
- No GetYourGuide URL for the Setas came up in search. GYG data was read through the Expedia listing ("By Setas de Sevilla on GetYourGuide") and a GYG affiliate mirror instead.
- I could not find a public App Store or Google Play listing for the official Setas app.
- I found no Smartify content for Seville.
- setasdesevilla.com does not publish an audio-guide FAQ that search could find.

## 4. What each one does well

- **Official QR audio guide:** It is included free with the ticket and needs no download (free Wi-Fi on site). It probably has the widest language range (8, per two resellers).
- **Official app:** It is the richest media mix in the set: 3D, VR/AR, video, a Giraldillo close-up, a children's game strand and favourites. Nobody else does kids.
- **Feeling Sevilla / Aurora:** These official set-pieces give the visit a strong ending and are often praised in reviews. Our guide should cue them, not compete with them.
- **Antiquarium panels:** Reviewers say the narrative of the city's evolution is clear and accessible in English. The ticket is cheap or free with the Alcázar.
- **WeGoTrip:** It sells the ticket and the audio in one purchase and links the Setas to a full city walk.
- **Tiqets / Lokalee:** Broad coverage (100+ POIs) and browser access with no install (Lokalee).
- **GPSmyCity:** Offline maps, a clear route length and time, and a Level 0 Antiquarium pointer.
- **Audiala:** It is free, works offline, has a QR entry point and lets you ask questions. Its text covers the architect, the competition, the overruns and the Antiquarium, and it was refreshed in April 2026.
- **Rick Steves:** It sets the bar for voice, storytelling and trust, at no cost. It just does not go to the Setas.
- **PastView (guided):** It shows Roman Hispalis visually, which is exactly what Antiquarium visitors say they miss.

## 5. The bar to beat

### Match or exceed (parity checklist)
- [ ] **Languages: at least 8** (ES, EN, FR, DE, IT, PT, KO, ZH), matching the official claim. Add NL, PL and JA if cost allows.
- [ ] **Free-feeling access:** QR start on the spot, no forced install, playable in a browser, and works offline once loaded.
- [ ] **Correct, current practical data:** €16+ ticket, 09:30–01:00, last entry 00:15, *Aurora* 21:30–01:00 (April to October), ticket office at −1, 48 h re-entry, Antiquarium Tue–Sun hours and €2 / Alcázar-ticket note. Audiala's "€3–5" error shows how quickly rivals go stale. Show a "verified on" date.
- [ ] **Setas coverage** deeper than 2 minutes or 240 words. The rivals' ceiling is about 10 minutes (Lokalee).
- [ ] **Cue the official set-pieces:** when *Feeling Sevilla* screens (every ~15 minutes), what *Aurora* is and when it runs. This fixes the "not quite knowing what the show was" complaint.
- [ ] **Offline map** with a visible route length and time (GPSmyCity parity).
- [ ] **Something for kids,** matching the official app's goblin strand.
- [ ] **Good voice and storytelling** at Rick Steves level, with human narration. WeGoTrip's AI-generated audio is a weak point to exploit.
- [ ] **Accessibility:** step-free route through the lift, seating, and a transcript for every track.

### Gaps nobody covers (Yo Tours' chance to lead)
1. **A real Antiquarium audio guide.** Nobody offers one. Go house by house: Casa de la Columna (Medusa), Baco, Ninfa, Noria, Sigma, Basas, the Hospitium of the Dolphins, the Patio del Océano mural, and the Almohad house. Add reconstruction sketches or "what it looked like" images, which reviewers asked for. Say which mosaics are originals, which are replicas and which are shown only in photos.
2. **Walkway wayfinding.** Turn-by-turn from the −1 ticket office → lift → 21 m level (*Feeling Sevilla* room) → 28.5 m walkway → viewpoint → exit, with where to stand. No rival gives directions.
3. **"What you're looking at" from each viewpoint.** Point-by-point skyline identification: Giralda and Cathedral, El Salvador, Alcázar, the Torre Sevilla tower, Triana, the churches of the San Pedro and Santa Catalina districts, and the market roofs below. The official app only zooms on the Giraldillo.
4. **The design story and the controversy, told straight:**
   - Jürgen Mayer H. won the 2004 competition.
   - The Roman finds made during the abandoned car-park dig changed the brief.
   - Construction took about six years, until 2011.
   - Cost rose to a reported ~€123 m (andalucia.com) or more.
   - The "mushrooms" nickname, local opinion then and now, and the claim to be the world's largest timber structure.
   - None of the audio rivals verifiably covers this. Audiala covers it in text only.
5. **One story from the Roman street to the rooftop.** The visit physically goes from 1st-century Hispalis at −5 m to 21st-century Seville at 28.5 m. No product uses that vertical timeline.
6. **Night versus day versions.** A *Aurora* and sunset track, plus heat and shade tips for midday.
7. **The tie-in with the Alcázar ticket** (free Antiquarium entry, per 2026 reviewers; bring a printed QR) and the 48 h re-entry. Nobody explains how to use them.
8. **Clear information in one place.** The official audio guide's languages, length and access differ across resellers (8 vs 3 vs 1 language). Yo Tours can publish exact track count, length, languages and update date.
