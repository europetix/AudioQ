# Setas de Sevilla (Metropol Parasol): visit route and spaces, as of 30 Oct 2026

Prepared for the Yo Tours / AudioQ Setas v5 audio guide. Purpose: get route, placement and open/closed status right. Prices and hours are for operations only. Do not put them in scripts.

## 0. Method and grading

- Tools: WebSearch and WebFetch only. Official site (setasdesevilla.com) pages were fetched one at a time with gaps. No curl, no browser, no CAPTCHA.
- Grades:
  - **OFF**: official page read directly. This means setasdesevilla.com (the operator's site), sacyr.com (the operator's parent company), visitasevilla.es (Seville city tourism) and andalucia.org (Junta de Andalucía tourism).
  - **SEC**: secondary page read directly (blogs, resellers, press).
  - **SNIP**: search snippet only.
- WebFetch returns a model summary of each page, not the raw HTML. Wherever it mattered, I asked for verbatim Spanish. The quotes below come from those verbatim requests.
- The `los-niveles` page draws its level tabs with JavaScript. Only the "Nivel Subterráneo" panel was readable, so the other level panels are not captured.
- The official images could not be read. The level diagram (niveles.jpg) returned a server error through WebFetch, so the images are not described here.
- No official walkway map or plan exists online (see §2).
- I found no 2025–2026 press notices of closures or works (see §5). Treat that as "not found", not as "none happened".

## 1. Official route, step by step

### 1.1 Operator
- Operated by **Sacyr Concesiones**: "built by Sacyr Engineering and Infrastructures, and operated by Sacyr Concesiones". OFF, https://sacyr.com/en/-/setas-de-sevilla (published 16/09/2021, modified 30/03/2026).
- Sacyr invested about €14M in the 2021 renewal. OFF, https://sacyr.com/-/10-aniversario-setas-sevilla (16/07/2021).
- The current official site is **setasdesevilla.com**. Its homepage promotes a "Campaña 15 Aniversario" (2026 is 15 years since the 2011 opening). OFF, https://setasdesevilla.com/

### 1.2 Arriving and where you enter (level −1, below street level)
- There are two street accesses, both leading down to the same underground level. OFF, https://setasdesevilla.com/planifica-tu-visita (verbatim):
  - **Acceso Sur (main):** "El acceso principal se encuentra en la zona Sur de la Plaza de la Encarnación, en su confluencia con Calle Imagen." It "conecta directamente con el mercado, el museo Antiquarium y el acceso Mirador."
  - **Acceso Norte:** "a él se llega desde las calles Regina y José Gestoso". It "conecta también con el mercado, el museo Antiquarium y el acceso Mirador."
- The ticket office (taquilla) is on **planta −1**. OFF, FAQ https://setasdesevilla.com/preguntas-frecuentes and `/experiencia-combinada-setas-tablao`: "directamente en la taquilla en la planta -1 del edificio".
- The Mirador access is **next to the Antiquarium entrance** on the underground level. OFF, FAQ (paraphrase): go down to the underground level, next to the Antiquarium entrance; after buying your ticket there, take the lift to the roof of the building ("cubierta"). Also OFF, `/los-niveles`: on the Nivel Subterráneo, "Acceso Mirador y Antiquarium".
- SEC, https://www.headout.com/setas-de-sevilla/official-tickets-to-setas-de-sevilla-e-41691/: "Head to the entrance on Level -1 at Plaza de la Encarnación, next to the escalators connecting to Plaza Mayor."
- SEC, https://www.travelchoreography.com/las-setas/ (updated 21 Aug 2026): "Access is through Calle Imagen. Stand in front of the escalators and go down the ramp to the left … The entrance is on floor -1, where tickets are also sold."
- **Conflict:** https://www.seville-tickets.com/setas-de-sevilla/plan-your-visit/ (SEC, reseller) says there is a "single public entrance on the north side" at ground floor. This contradicts the official site. **Do not use it.**

### 1.3 Going up
- Lifts: "Contamos con dos ascensores accesibles". OFF, `/planifica-tu-visita`.
- The two concrete columns (trunks) house the lifts that serve the viewing point. OFF, https://andalucia.org/en/sevilla-cultural-tourism-metropol-parasol
- Where the lift stops: the official FAQ says the lift goes to the building's roof ("cubierta"). SEC https://viajesglobetrotter.com/visitar-setas-sevilla-informacion-consejos/ agrees: "un ascensor nos llevará directamente a la cubierta del edificio". Whether the lift also stops at the 21 m "Experiencias" level is not confirmed (**CHECK**, see 1.5).
- Before going up, tickets are validated and a QR code opens the official multilingual audio guide. SEC, Headout.

### 1.4 The walkways (pasarelas) and the Mirador 360º
- The official site sells the visit as "Mirador 360º", described as views "desde el punto más alto del monumento". OFF, `/planifica-tu-visita`.
- Length and form (SEC only): Headout says "Stroll 250 metres along a serpentine elevated walkway that winds across the structure". andalucia.org (OFF) says the path "takes visitors around most of the structure".
- **One-way or marked circuit: not stated by any official source.** The only claim found is a reseller saying there is "no mandatory direction" (seville-tickets.com, SEC, low reliability). **CHECK on site.** Until then, do not tell listeners to "turn left" or "continue clockwise".
- Steps and access. OFF, FAQ: about 90% of the route is accessible with ramps and lifts. The exception is the walkways: their ramps are too steep to use, and the Mirador itself can only be reached by a stepped route. Headout (SEC) words it as: "The footbridge ramps have an incline; the Viewing Platform is only reachable via steps".
- Other things on the walkways (SEC):
  - The railing is not glass, so you feel the wind: seville-trip.com.
  - There is no shade on the walkways: viajesglobetrotter.
  - In 2011 and 2015 accounts there were information boards naming the skyline landmarks, plus coin binoculars: andalucia.com 2011 https://www.andalucia.com/blog/new-view-sevilla and devourtours.com https://devourtours.com/blog/best-views-in-seville. **Whether these boards are still there in 2026 is a CHECK.**
- Dimensions (OFF, andalucia.org): 150 × 70 m, about 29 m high, six parasols, central ones rising 21 m.
- Pets (OFF, FAQ): on the walkways and Mirador, only guide dogs and toy or mini dogs under 5 kg, carried throughout.

### 1.5 Experiences inside the structure: Feeling Sevilla, rest area, shop, exhibition
- The official site lists the visit's stops in this order: Mirador 360º → Aurora → Sala Feeling → Tienda → Exposición → Plaza Mayor. OFF, `/planifica-tu-visita`.
  - Intro: "Desde el Mirador 360º hasta el Antiquarium, te guiamos por una experiencia completa…". This is marketing; the Antiquarium is a separate site.
  - Last stop: "**Finaliza tu recorrido en la emblemática Plaza Mayor**".
- The official site names five levels (OFF, https://setasdesevilla.com/descubre-setas):
  1. Acceso, Mirador y Antiquarium
  2. Mercado y Restauración
  3. Plaza Mayor
  4. **Nuestras Experiencias**
  5. Pasarelas y Mirador
- **Feeling Sevilla** ("Sala Feeling", sala multisensorial):
  - What it is: an immersive multisensory film. Room "a 21 m de altura", over 200 m², with a curved LED screen 21 m × 3 m, 360º sound, floor projection, air and scent effects. OFF, Sacyr 2021 and Sacyr 2026-modified page.
  - Sessions run every 15 minutes: "Las sesiones de Feeling Sevilla se realizan cada 15 minutos". OFF, `/planifica-tu-visita`. About 15 minutes long: SEC (Headout, viajarasevillabarato).
  - Its 21 m height places it at level 4, "Nuestras Experiencias", inside the parasols. This is an inference from "21 m" and the level list.
  - **Order is a CHECK.** The official stop list puts it after the Mirador. One SEC blog (travelchoreography, 2026) says the film came first on arrival.
- **Zona de descanso** (rest area): "Un espacio para hacer una pausa, consultar los tótems interactivos o disfrutar de un tentempié durante la visita." OFF, `/experiencia-general`. Location not stated (**CHECK**).
- **Tienda** (shop): "productos locales y recuerdos exclusivos". OFF, `/planifica-tu-visita`. SEC (travelchoreography): "take the stairs beside the gift shop instead of the lift when you're done."
- **Exposición** (exhibition on the way down):
  - "Explora una exposición única **mientras desciendes**, con obras que capturan la historia de la ciudad." OFF, `/experiencia-general` and `/experiencia-combinada-setas-tablao`.
  - "…que muestra la evolución de la Plaza de la Encarnación hasta la actualidad." OFF, `/planifica-tu-visita`.
  - SEC (travelchoreography): going down the stairs "You'll see some interesting facts about the building", with information panels.
- **Restaurant/bar:**
  - andalucia.org (OFF) says "inside the central parasols: an area for tapas and an area for events", and lists "bar" among the services.
  - The official site lists only the rest area with snacks ("tentempié"). Level 2 is named "Mercado y Restauración", meaning food at market level.
  - seville-trip.com (SEC) mentions a "Setas Bar at the top". This is not corroborated.
  - **Do not script a named rooftop restaurant or bar. CHECK.**
- **Tablao Flamenco Las Setas** (combined ticket): inside Metropol Parasol per Forbes 23 Jan 2023 (SEC, https://forbes.es/ultima-hora/222886/…). Level not stated (**CHECK**). It is not part of the general visit.

### 1.6 Coming down and exit
- Best-supported reading: lift down **or** stairs, passing the shop and the descending exhibition, and ending on **Plaza Mayor**, the raised plaza on top of the market.
  - Exhibition "mientras desciendes" and "Finaliza tu recorrido en … Plaza Mayor": OFF.
  - Lift or stairs, stairs beside the gift shop: SEC, travelchoreography 2026.
- From Plaza Mayor, visitors reach the street by stairs or escalators. The escalators by the Calle Imagen (south) access are SEC (Headout, travelchoreography).
- **CHECK:** whether everyone must exit via Plaza Mayor, and whether the lift also returns to −1.
- **Script implication:** do not say "you'll come back out where you started at the ticket office." Say the visit ends up on the Plaza Mayor level, the raised plaza.

### 1.7 Aurora (night light show): what it is
- What it is: a light-and-sound show on the walkways and Mirador. It reacts to the building's occupancy and visitors' movement, weather, wind and time of year. OFF, Sacyr 2021 and Sacyr page modified 2026. Official site wording: "Déjate llevar por las pasarelas y el Mirador con el cálido abrazo de la luz de Aurora." OFF, `/experiencia-general`.
- When:
  - It starts at sunset: "comienza a la puesta de sol: aproximadamente a las 18:00 h en invierno y en torno a las 21:00 h en verano." OFF, `/planifica-tu-visita`.
  - The official homepage shows Aurora "21:30 to 01:00, April to October only". OFF, homepage.
  - **These two official statements conflict on whether Aurora runs in winter. CHECK.**
  - **For scripts:** "after sunset", with no times and no months.
- It is included in the general ticket. A return visit within 48 h is allowed, subject to capacity, but not in the sunset slot. OFF, FAQ.

### 1.8 Typical duration
- 60–90 min: OFF, `/experiencia-general`, `/planifica-tu-visita`. The FAQ says about 60 min.
- Resellers say 40–45 min (Headout, Civitatis). SEC.

### 1.9 Other official services (operations)
- Free audio guide via the WebApp, in 8 languages. This competes with our product.
- Wi-Fi via the QR code on the ticket.
- Lockers, subject to availability.
- Refillable water point.
- OFF, FAQ and `/planifica-tu-visita`.

## 3. Levels and what is on each

The official numbering on `/descubre-setas` is 1–5. Physical description: OFF, andalucia.org https://andalucia.org/en/sevilla-cultural-tourism-metropol-parasol

| Official level | Physical position | What is there | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Acceso, Mirador y Antiquarium | Underground, **planta −1** | Ticket office; Mirador access and lifts; Antiquarium entrance next to it | OFF FAQ, /los-niveles |
| 2. Mercado y Restauración | Street level | Mercado de la Encarnación (market), shops, cafés and restaurants | OFF descubre-setas; andalucia.org: "The La Encarnación market and shops and cafes are to be found at ground level." |
| 3. Plaza Mayor | Raised plaza on the roof of the market, under the parasols | Shaded public plaza, "open to the sky and used for events"; **end point of the visit** | OFF andalucia.org; OFF planifica ("Finaliza tu recorrido en … Plaza Mayor") |
| 4. Nuestras Experiencias | Inside the central parasols (~21 m) | Feeling Sevilla room (21 m); per andalucia.org, also tapas and events areas | OFF Sacyr; OFF andalucia.org |
| 5. Pasarelas y Mirador | Top of the parasols (~26–29 m) | Walkways and Mirador 360º; Aurora after dark | OFF |

### 3.1 Antiquarium (level −1)
- **Separate site with its own entrance and ticket.** Its entrance is on −1, next to the Mirador access. OFF, FAQ. SEC viajesglobetrotter: "next to the ticket offices and lifts", about €2.
- Visit via glass walkways and partitions over the remains. Ruins are visible through plate glass from outside. Screens and VR reconstructions. SEC, https://andalucia.com/cities/seville/museum-antiquarium.htm (modified 2 Apr 2026).
- Opened 27 March 2011. Remains found in the 1990s during excavations for a planned underground car park, which was abandoned. Open-plan area of 4,879 m². OFF, https://visitasevilla.es/en/?p=1576
- What is on show:
  - **Roman (1st–6th c.):** mosaics from the Roman House, the House of the Nymph and the House of Bacchus (Casa de Baco); a 3 m mural in the Ocean Courtyard (Patio de Océano). OFF, visitasevilla.
  - **Casa de la Columna**, with a **Medusa** mosaic. OFF, visitasevilla.
  - House of the Basas, House of the Sigma, House of the Noria, and the Hospitium of the Dolphins (Hospitium de los Delfines). OFF, visitasevilla.
  - **Andalusí (12th–13th c.): an Islamic Almohad house.** OFF, visitasevilla. andalucia.org (OFF) says "a Moorish house from the XII and XIII centuries".
  - **Fish-salting vats (1st c. AD)**, 11 areas of remains, seven houses, streets, and the "kissing birds" mosaic as the museum's emblem. SEC, andalucia.com.
  - andalucia.com (2011) also says the Carambolo treasure "will be housed here". **Do not use.** It is an old plan and unconfirmed.
- **Operator: not confirmed.** visitasevilla and andalucia.org name no operator. Historically it was municipal (ICAS, the Ayuntamiento's culture institute), but no source read today confirms that. **CHECK.**
- Admission linked to the Alcázar: andalucia.org (OFF, hours "from 09-04-2024") and travelchoreography (SEC) both say entry is free with a Reales Alcázares general ticket. **CHECK**; operations only.
- Hours differ from the Mirador (operations only):
  - Monday closed per andalucia.org (OFF): Tue–Sat 10:00–19:30, Sun and holidays 10:00–13:30. Price €2.
  - andalucia.com (SEC) gives Tue–Sat 10–20, Sun 10–14.
  - **Script implication:** if the Antiquarium is mentioned, do not imply it is open whenever the Mirador is (closed Mondays, shuts early on Sundays, no evenings).
