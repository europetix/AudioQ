(edition 5.1 of 31 Oct 2026, 21 tracks; track numbers renumbered to 5.1. O-numbers and track numbers are as in PLAN.md sections 2 and 5.)

HOW TO USE THIS LIST
- Walk the Full route once with the guide before recording, including the Antiquarium. Go on a day
  it is open: not Monday, and not a Sunday afternoon.
- Tick each item. Note anything different, with its track number, and send it to us. We fix only
  that track.
- Items marked CRITICAL decide which cue, or which route order (ALT-A to ALT-G), gets recorded.
- There is NO official walkway map yet. So:
  - left/right, "straight ahead", "clockwise" and distances are BANNED from the scripts;
  - the only compass words allowed are "south, toward the Giralda" and "west, toward the river";
  - cues name the place plus one fixed feature: the ticket office, the Antiquarium entrance, the
    lifts, the steps up to the Mirador, the way down, the Plaza Mayor.
- Photograph every official information panel and label you pass, front and any English side.
  Labels are how the Antiquarium tracks get their details, and how held items come back.
- Views: photograph each direction from the walkway and from the Mirador. We name a landmark only if
  you can name it for sure.
