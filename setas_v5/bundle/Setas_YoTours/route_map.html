YO TOURS · SETAS DE SEVILLA · THE ANTIQUARIUM

The Antiquarium · 2–6

Below the square, on level −1: the remains found when the city dug for a car park that was never built. Walkways run over the excavation, from the entrance to the dig, the Roman houses and the Almohad house. Order as in the guide; the positions inside are still to be checked on site.

Separate museum, Full tour only

The Antiquarium has its own entrance and its own admission, beside the taquilla on level −1. Essential and Highlights skip it and go straight to the lifts. If it is closed when you are there, go to the lifts beside its entrance and play 7.

Find the label

The way through has not been checked on site yet, so the drawing shows only the order. Each track names the label to look for: the Casa de la Columna, the Casa de Baco, the casa almohade.

After 6

Follow the exit signs, then go to the lifts beside the Antiquarium entrance, the Mirador access. Play 7 there, before you go up.

YO TOURS · SETAS DE SEVILLA · THE WALKWAYS

The walkways and the Mirador · 7 to 14

Play 7 by the lifts on level −1, and start 8 as the lift doors close. On top, walk on until the Giralda is in clear view (9), face west toward the river (10), walk on toward the steps (11), climb to the Mirador 360º (12), then back down the steps and on toward the way down (13; Extra 14 anywhere).

Only the order is fixed

There is no official map of the walkway yet, and nobody has confirmed whether it runs one way. Every spot drawn here is a guess at the order, not the place. Play each track where it says.

Two views to find

9: wherever the Giralda and the cathedral are in clear view, to the south. 10: stand with the Giralda on your left, facing west, toward the river and the Aljarafe.

Steps to the top

The Mirador 360º, the highest point, is reached only by steps. Some walkway ramps are steep. After 12, go back down the steps and on toward the way down.

YO TOURS · SETAS DE SEVILLA · COMING DOWN

Coming down and the Plaza Mayor · 15 to 18

From the walkway, toward the way down. If you watch the Feeling Sevilla film, play Extra 15 before you go in. Then the way down past the exhibition (16), and the Plaza Mayor, the raised square on the market's roof, where the visit ends (17, 18).

Feeling Sevilla (15)

An optional Extra about the film room, on the experiences level inside the parasols. Play it before you go in. Where the room is, and when you reach it, is still to be confirmed on site.

The way down (16)

Play 16 as you walk down past the exhibition. At the bottom you come out on the Plaza Mayor, the big raised square under the parasols. Whether there is also a lift down is still to be confirmed.

The end (17, 18)

Both play on the Plaza Mayor, with the market under your feet. To leave, follow the signs down to the street, by the stairs or the escalators. You do not go back to level −1.
