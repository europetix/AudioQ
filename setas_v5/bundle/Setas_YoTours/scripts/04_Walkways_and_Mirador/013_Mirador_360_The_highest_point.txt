Three directions are worth fixing, even if you found some of them on the walkway. Find the Giralda, the cathedral's tall bell tower, with the great bulk of the cathedral beside it. That way is south, less than a kilometre off.

Now put the Giralda on your left. You're facing west, toward the river, the Guadalquivir, and the rooftops of Triana, the old neighbourhood across the water. Beyond them rises the Aljarafe, the higher ground on that side of the river.

Last, put the Giralda at your back. You're looking north now, toward the Macarena district, about a kilometre away.

The rest of the skyline is yours to explore, at your own pace. Take a slow turn around the rail before you go.
