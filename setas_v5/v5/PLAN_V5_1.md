## Why V5.1
The user compared V5 with their old V4 guide (source/old_v4.txt) and found V5 degraded: about 4,700 words against ~8,000,
the Antiquarium reduced to "read the labels", no raised-plaza stop, a thin panorama. V4 was rich but often wrong
(research/AUDIT_OLD_V4_FACTS_OCT2026.md: 74 TRUE, 73 PARTLY, 32 FALSE, 57 UNVERIFIED rows). V5.1 keeps V5's accuracy and
restores every TRUE, rich item, aiming at ≈ 6,500 spoken words. **Never** reintroduce a FALSE / UNVERIFIED / PRACTICAL row
of that audit (its §3–§4), and never a NOT item of PLAN.md.

## Rules that change from V5
- Antiquarium objects may now be described in the words of the academic and SEC sources (KRAMER, ACOM, ACOM-B), always
  hedged to work whatever the layout: "look for…", "somewhere along the walkway…", "if the label says…". Never "in front of
  you", never a position, never a century for a single mosaic. Each such line is tagged [O4] in @where/@what.
- The house-name conflict stays unresolved: do not say which house the noria belongs to (audit row 25).
- Views (research §4.1 + audit row 33): south, the Giralda and the cathedral; west, toward the river and Triana's rooftops,
  with the Aljarafe as the high ground beyond; north, "toward the Macarena district, about a kilometre away" (direction is
  geography; do not claim the basilica is visible). Nothing else.
- Track lengths as the standard (R 180–280, W 250–360, X 150–230, ANCHOR/CLOSE 250–360). Use the upper half of the
  range when the material is real; never pad.
