**Binding for writers.** Navigation and placement come first: in earlier guides, customers complained that things were described in the wrong place. Facts come second, and only in the wording of `research/FACTS_OCT2026.md`, using its verdicts (OK / HEDGE / LEGEND). Anything marked UNVERIFIED or WRONG is never scripted. **There is no official map of the walkway** (none online; a printed "plano guía" is handed out at the ticket office, and the user has been asked for a photo). The destinations and the order below are therefore binding. But every left/right, "straight ahead", "clockwise" and distance is **forbidden** until MAP/ONSITE. Every compass word except the two sourced views is also forbidden, and so is any position of the Feeling Sevilla room. Cues name the destination plus **one fixed feature**: the ticket office, the Antiquarium entrance beside it, the lifts, the steps up to the Mirador, the way down past the exhibition, or the Plaza Mayor. The Giralda serves as a visual landmark. Wherever geometry matters there is an ONSITE or MAP item (section 5 and `v5/ONSITE_CHECKLIST.txt`), tagged [Oxx] in the table. ### The route (reasoning; binding order, provisional geometry)
**Fixed points (sourced).**
- **Entrances.** Both accesses lead down to the same underground level, and both connect "con el mercado, el museo Antiquarium y el acceso Mirador" (PLAN, OFF):
  - the main **Acceso Sur** is on the south side of the Plaza de la Encarnación, where it meets **Calle Imagen**;
  - the **Acceso Norte** is reached from Calle Regina and José Gestoso.
  - From Calle Imagen: "stand in front of the escalators and go down the ramp" (TRAVCH, SEC). The escalators connect to the Plaza Mayor (HEADOUT, SEC).
  - **Do not use** the reseller's "single entrance on the north side" (research §1.2, conflict).
- **Level −1.**
  - The ticket office is on planta −1 (FAQ).
  - The **Mirador access** (the lifts) is **next to the Antiquarium entrance** (FAQ; NIVELES: "Acceso Mirador y Antiquarium").
  - There are two accessible lifts (PLAN), inside the concrete trunks (JA-SET). The lift goes up to the roof, the "cubierta" (FAQ).
  - **Whether it also stops at the 21 m "Experiencias" level is unknown** → O5. An official image is named "Ascensor Experiencias".
- **Antiquarium.**
  - It is a separate site with its own entrance and ticket, on −1 beside the Mirador access (FAQ).
  - Visitors go along glass walkways over the remains (ANDCOM-ANT, SEC).
  - **No plan of its interior exists online** → O4 CRITICAL, ALT-A.
  - Its hours differ from the Mirador's (closed Mondays, shuts early on Sundays, no evenings; ops only): 001's "if it's closed" line.
- **Walkways and Mirador.**
  - A walkway about 250 m long winds across the top (S-HIST, HEADOUT).
  - The Mirador 360º is the highest point, "reachable only via steps" (FAQ, HEADOUT).
  - **One-way or free circulation is not stated** → O6 CRITICAL.
- **Experiences level (≈ 21 m).** Feeling Sevilla (SACYR). The official stop list runs Mirador → Aurora → Sala Feeling → Tienda → Exposición → Plaza Mayor (PLAN), but one 2026 blog had the film first → O11 CRITICAL, ALT-B.
- **Way down and end.**
  - "Explora una exposición única **mientras desciendes**" (EXPGEN), showing "la evolución de la Plaza de la Encarnación hasta la actualidad" (PLAN).
  - "**Finaliza tu recorrido en la emblemática Plaza Mayor**" (PLAN).
  - Lift or stairs, with the stairs beside the shop (TRAVCH, SEC; the shop is never named).
  - **Whether everyone must end on the Plaza Mayor, and whether the lift returns to −1, is unknown** → O12 CRITICAL, ALT-C/ALT-D.
  - From the Plaza Mayor, stairs or escalators lead to the street (HEADOUT, TRAVCH, SEC) [O14].

**Chosen order (one line from bottom to top to middle, no backtracking):**
Calle Imagen entrance (001) → down to level −1, ticket office → **Antiquarium** (Full: entrance 002 → the dig 003 → Roman houses 004–005 → Almohad house 006) → **the lifts beside it** (007) → lift up (008 starts as the doors close) → **first stretch of walkway** (008) → where the **Giralda** is in view (009) → the side facing **west** (010) → on along the walkway (011) → **steps up to the Mirador 360º** (012) → back down the steps toward the way down (013; Extras 014 Aurora and 015 Feeling Sevilla) → **the way down past the exhibition** (016) → **Plaza Mayor** (017, 018) → stairs or escalators to the street.

**Why the Antiquarium comes first (the default, confirmed):**
1. **Geometry.** Its entrance is beside the ticket office and beside the lifts (FAQ). The official visit ends on the Plaza Mayor, not back at −1 (PLAN). Doing it last would mean going from the Plaza Mayor down to the street and down again to −1. That is a backtrack the official route doesn't support (O12).
2. **Story.** The route becomes chronological and vertical: Roman Hispalis (1st c.) → the Almohad house (12th–13th c.) → convent, market and car park (007) → the 2004 design (008) → the rooftop → the plaza today. Nobody else tells this "from the Roman street to the rooftop" story (comparison §5.5).
3. **Operations (planning reason only, never spoken).** The Antiquarium closes earlier than the walkways, all day Monday and early on Sunday. Doing it first keeps the walkway for the later light and for Aurora after sunset.

**Rejected alternatives:**
- **Antiquarium last.** It backtracks via the Plaza Mayor to −1 (≈ +4 min), and the museum may already be closed.
- **Antiquarium between the walkway and the Plaza Mayor.** It isn't on the way down.
- **Two separate playlists.** Fewer cues, but it breaks the single route the user asked for.

**Within the walkway:**
- The two view tracks (009, 010) are **position-free**. Each one plays "where the Giralda is in clear view" or "facing west, with the Giralda on your left", on the walkway or at the Mirador. Their order and their place before the Mirador hold whatever the circulation turns out to be.
- If the lift delivers you straight to the Mirador steps, ALT-E reorders the playlist.

**Non-Full listeners and a closed Antiquarium:**
- **E and H** skip 002–006 with the 001 bridge (section 3).
- **If the Antiquarium is closed**, Full listeners are told in 001 to go to the lifts and carry on at 007 (Full minus the Antiquarium ≈ 39 min).
