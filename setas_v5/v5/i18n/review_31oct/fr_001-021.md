## How the reviewed 5.0 translations were reused
- I compared every 5.1 track sentence by sentence with its 5.0 English, using the brief's mapping.
  - Where a sentence was unchanged, I kept the approved FR wording, including the 30 Oct reviewer fixes.
  - Where a sentence was new or changed, I translated it fresh.
  - Where the 5.1 English dropped a sentence, I dropped it too. Examples: « Il est géré par la mairie de Séville » (002), « Vous verrez les deux » (002), the Casa del Sigma in 004 (now in 005), « Vous pourrez le visiter ensuite, depuis la rue » (019), « Mayer a cité les voûtes de pierre… » and « Attardez-vous un instant… » (010), and the 1830s and 1973 reasons in 019.
