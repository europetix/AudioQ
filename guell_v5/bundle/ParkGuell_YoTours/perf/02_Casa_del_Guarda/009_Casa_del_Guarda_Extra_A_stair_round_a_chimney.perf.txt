[curious] In the middle of this floor, a spiral stair winds up to the lookout. Its secret is in its core. Inside the round brick wall of the stair runs a second brick cylinder. It's the flue that carries the smoke out from the chimneys, and together with the outer wall it holds up the ceramic steps.

So the chimney holds the stair up. Look at it from here, and follow the steps with your eyes as they turn round the flue.

Now look over your head. This is a Catalan dome, three layers of thin brick, tied round with a twisted metal strap. The city's history museum singles out Gaudí's use of the Catalan vault as a sign of the refinement in this small house.

Look at the two attic windows: each forms a cross, worked in porcelain.

The exhibition's theme up here is the city: how the park fits into Barcelona, and its bond with the city below. One panel is a portrait of monumental Barcelona in 1917.

When you come out, walk along the inside of the wall to the Marianao entrance, without going out. From just inside it, steps and ramps climb to the Low Viaduct, the Pont de Baix. Stop on it: The Low Viaduct · Roads before houses.
