Gaudí did not come to this house alone. "Gaudí himself moved there in 1906," the park's history says, "to live with his father and niece."

His father was Francesc Gaudí i Serra. His niece was Rosa, known in the family as Roseta. She was the daughter of Gaudí's sister, also Rosa, and since she was a small child her grandfather and her uncle had brought her up. So the house held three of them: an old man, his son, and the niece the two of them had raised.

It didn't last. His father died that October, within months of moving in. His niece died in 1912.

Gaudí stayed on alone. The park's historians count it, in Catalan, as "almost twenty years, from 1906 until a few months before his death". In November 1925 he left for good, for a small study at the Sagrada Família. Partly, it is said, he went to be nearer an ailing friend, the sculptor Llorenç Matamala.

Look at the house once more. For most of those years, one man lived in it.
