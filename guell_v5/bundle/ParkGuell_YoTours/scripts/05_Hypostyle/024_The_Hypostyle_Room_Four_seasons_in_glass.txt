The four biggest discs in this ceiling are suns. When the city restored them, it described them, in Catalan, as "the medallions of the four Suns, one for each season of the year". That's how the city reads them. They're the work of Gaudí's young assistant, Josep Maria Jujol, who dressed this ceiling in recycled glass and tile.

Now pick one sun and look closely. Give your eyes a moment. The city's restoration notice lists what went into them: "feet of wine glasses, cut-glass serving dishes, liqueur-bottle stoppers, little perfume bottles". Tableware and bottles, broken up and pressed into a ceiling.

They nearly didn't survive. In 2013 pieces began to fall, and the city had to step in urgently. Later, each of the four Suns was cut into twenty-four pieces, taken down, restored and put back. The work was done by the restorers Gamarra and García, with the firm Eurocatalana.

Four seasons, made of somebody's wine glasses.
