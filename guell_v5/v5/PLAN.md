### The route (reasoning; binding order, provisional geometry)
**Fixed points (sourced).**
- **Gates.** Nine gates: four for neighbours only, four shared with visitors, **"one exit at carrer Olot"** (ACCESS; GOV20 "Exit only: one gate on carrer d'Olot"). The three visitor gates the operator recommends: **Carretera del Carmel, 23**; **"Carrer de Larrard (entry from the Carrer d'Olot / Carrer Marianao side lane)"**, which the works notice calls **"the Marianao entrance"**; and **Avinguda del Santuari de Sant Josep de la Muntanya, "entry by escalator"** (NODE268; DISRUPT). The Baixada de la Glòria gate is unclear in 2026 and the Vallcarca escalators are "not recommended" (ROUTE §1.1; FAQ): never named.
- **No re-entry:** "once you have exited, you cannot re-enter" (FAQ); "Once the ticket has been validated at an access control point it will no longer be possible to leave and then re-enter" (ACCREG 2.11).
- **The inner section.** The high-concentration spots, "Pl. Natura, the Hypostyle Room, the Laundry Room Portico, the Dragon Stairway and the Austria Gardens", "are found within the section that has a second controlled access point, where visitors follow a one-way route" and "are likely to have to wait" (RESTRICT). **Where the second control is and which way the flow runs are not published (ROUTE §1.2): O1.** Best evidence (INF, ROUTE §1.2): controlled mouths at the foot of the stairway, at the Sant Josep de la Muntanya side and at the Nature Square from the Palm Trees Path.
- **The official recommended route, A–I** (ROUTES): A Entrance and concierge · B The guardhouse · C Monumental staircase · D The hypostyle room, outdoor · E The hypostyle room, inside · F The Austria Gardens · G Nature square · H The roadways, paths and viaducts · I Ramp and house Larrard. "Follow the recommended route in order to visit all the points of interest signposted within the restricted area." It climbs the stairway, does the hall and the gardens, reaches the square, then goes along the paths and down the ramp to Casa Larrard, near the Olot exit (ROUTE §1.2 INF): **a loop that ends near the only exit.**
- **The Portico path.** From the square "an original iron door… leads to where there used to be the gardens of Casa Larrard"; a path above the house through a pine grove; the Portico; "the spiral ramp which takes visitors down to the house" (op10). The Portico is itself a colonnaded road on arches, "a double colonnade" holding up the path (op10, F10.13), so on our route **the Portico path is the official H** (with, for E and Full, the Low Viaduct before the control). MUHBA's itineraries list a stop called the "Viaducte de la Bugadera" (MUHBA-IT), but **which structure that name means is unclear** (G71: perhaps the Low Viaduct): **never spoken** until O19/O5 settle it.
- **The viaducts.** Three, five metres wide, climbing from the Carrer d'Olot entrance to the top of the estate; the Low Viaduct is "the first viaduct, near the central area, heading east toward the Carretera del Carmel exit" (op29; "east" never spoken); a **steps-ramp links the Marianao entrance to the Low Viaduct** (DISRUPT: maintenance since Nov 2024 "will not affect the visit"). **Closed:** "from Casa Trias to the Upper Viaduct (Viaducte de Dalt)" and Coll del Portell – Forat del Vent, for about 18 months from Nov 2026 (DISRUPT). The viaducts are outside the inner section (ROUTE §2 rows 9–10, INF).
- **The two houses of Güell's time** (Casa Trias, the show house that is now the Gaudí House Museum) "can be seen" from the centre of the Austria Gardens (op14).
- **Casa Larrard** is reached by the spiral ramp from the Portico path (op10); its address point is on Carrer d'Olot (ROUTE §2 row 8). A working school: never cue into it.

**Chosen order (default; one loop; no doubling back through the inner section):**
Inside your gate: welcome (001, any gate) → the entrance esplanade inside the Carrer d'Olot gate: the gate and wall (002), the two pavilions (003, Extra 004 at the Casa del Guarda) → **E/F only:** from the Marianao entrance up the steps-ramp to the Low Viaduct (005; Extra 006 where the Middle Viaduct is in view) and back down to the esplanade → **the second control at the foot of the stairway: the queue (007, standing; the one-way warning)** → up the Dragon Stairway (008 at the foot, 009 by the dragon, 010 at the odeon bench and tripod) → the hall's front from outside (011, official D) → inside (012, 013; Extra 014 at the Font Sarva, official E) → the Austria Gardens (015, 016, official F) → the Nature Square (017, 018 at the bench with Extra 019, 020 at the parapet, official G) → through the iron door and along the path through the pines to the Portico (021, official H) → down the spiral ramp to Casa Larrard (022, official I) → walking down to the Carrer d'Olot gate (023) → by the gate (024, CLOSE 025) → out.

**Why this order:**
1. **It is the operator's own A–I order** for everything inside the inner section (decision 2): signs, staff and the official map agree with us, and the loop ends at the only exit.
2. **The viaducts before the second control, not after the square** (the skeleton had them between the Portico and Casa Larrard). Reasons: (a) **no re-entry and a one-way flow** mean that if the end of the inner section funnels people from Casa Larrard to the exit (likely: the official loop "ends near the Olot exit"), a viaduct stop placed after it could not be reached at all, and the cue would send people somewhere they cannot go, the exact complaint we are fixing; placed before, it is always reachable from the esplanade by the Marianao steps-ramp (O5); (b) the story order is better: **"Before any house, Gaudí built the roads"** (F3.3), so the roads come before the estate story (007) and the market hall; (c) the official H is kept: on our route it is the Portico path, a road carried on a colonnade (op10). **Cost:** the steps-ramp is walked up and down (≈ 9 min, E and F only), like the Poble's Sculpture Garden dead end. ALT-L restores "viaducts after the square" if O11 shows the way from Casa Larrard leads back to the steps-ramp without leaving the park. **Lead's call (§8).**
3. **The queue is the estate story** (decision 3): 007 is heard standing in the queue, in **all three versions**, so its listening costs no extra visit time when there is a queue (§3), and it is the track that every version hears just before the second control, so it carries the one-way warning.
4. **The Gaudí House Museum is seen from the Austria Gardens** (op14: "the two houses that were built in Eusebi Güell's time can be seen"), so its one outside stop (016) needs no extra walk and stays inside the flow.
5. **The view comes last in the square** (020, at the parapet), then the iron door is close by; the stories of failure and afterlife (022–025) come on the way down, in time order, ending at the gate where everyone leaves.

**Rejected alternatives:**
- **The viaducts between the Portico and Casa Larrard** (skeleton 018 → 019 → 020 → 021): needs the Portico path to join the Low Viaduct and come back to the spiral ramp; nothing read says it does, and the Low Viaduct "heads toward the Carretera del Carmel exit" (op29), while the ramp goes down to Casa Larrard (op10). Kept as part of ALT-L only if O3/O11 show it works.
