## Wording that differs from PLAN, and why
1. **014.** "La Salut, which means health" is a gloss of the Catalan word, added to tie in with "barrio de la salud". "Roads, walls and gatehouses were already up" before the permit comes from Andruet p. 4 (Jan 1903: "la cerca, los pabellones de entrada, todos los viaductos salvo detalles"; G43). E69 was not used: the page actually says the estate was for well-to-do families "in a more healthy setting that in the centre of the city, but without losing sight of their businesses". That is about the families, not Güell, and repeats G37. G86 is told without "cholera" and without mentioning the visit itself. I treated it as a DATED framing sentence group that can be cut (paragraph 4).
2. **015.** PLAN's "every buyer paid a **yearly** fee" was changed to "a fee for the upkeep of the shared park", because the page says "un cànon", with no period. The FA N7 wording is kept: the services list is "the city's history museum lists them", and the 50-peseta and Güell-approval lines are said to come from "the museum's story for schoolchildren". The one language tag is on E67 ("the museum's notes for older pupils… in Catalan").
3. **016.** "Merlons are the square teeth along the top of a castle wall" is our gloss. The shelter is identified by sight ("held up by a thick, cone-shaped column"), never as right or left (O14).
4. **017.** "in 1900, the year the works began on this hill" links s1 with F3.1. G25's "full of shields" is not used (O14).
5. **018.** The film line (E78) is spoken without its year, to keep three dates (2007, 2025, 2026). "The one that came back from the sculptor's workshop" refers to op25's workshop study. "The hall that was meant to be Güell's market" is the 015 callback.
6. **019.** Bassegoda's line now rests on today's direct read, which confirms it is a quotation. It is introduced as "An English press note quotes him". The page's own continuation "about the Renaissance" is not spoken. It probably means the Catalan Renaixença, and the lead may want it.
7. **020.** **The Delphi quote is longer than edition 1's.** The page's full sentence ends "…wanted the columns of the park he commissioned from Gaudí to be of the Doric order." Edition 1 stopped at "the columns of the park", which changes the sense, so the full sentence is used. FA N6 is applied. The one language tag is on the flyer.
8. **021.** "The tilt is real, and it's deliberate" became "it does a job" (consistent with FA N8). E77 is one sentence: "Its rescue came in a long restoration in the late 1980s and early 1990s."
9. **022, the conduit (FA S5).** Two relays of op8 disagree on whether "it" in "A conduit running inside it" means the architrave (FA) or the bench (today). The track says "Along the front, where the bench sits on top of the colonnade, the park describes a conduit that…", which is true either way. **Lead: confirm, or restore "inside the architrave under the bench".** The DATED sentence follows the official B:SM release of May 2026 ("Gaudí's old rain tanks, this one included, would be restored, so that the park's rainwater can be collected and used again"), not El Debate. The release says the Hypostyle tank will act as a channel, so the track doesn't say it will store water. García Ávila's white-trencadís quote was cut for length, and the look line stays.
10. **024.** "Then, in 2016, each … cut into twenty-four pieces" follows FACTS F7.18's safe wording. The restorers' line (F4.15) is used.
11. **Bassegoda forms.** Full form in 022 only (PLAN). 019 and 020 use "Bassegoda" (short form): under ALT-V, Full listeners would then meet him first in 019 without an introduction.
