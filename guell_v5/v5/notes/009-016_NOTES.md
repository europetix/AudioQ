## 2. What I could not confirm, and what I did instead
- **2007 dragon repair** (009): archiweb does not say it was repaired. I don't claim a 2007 repair. The track says the foot "has since been" taken away, studied and restored (op25).
- **op8's overflow sentence** is garbled English ("which has as the stairway dragon's mouth as its overflow"). I paraphrased it ("the park's own text names the dragon's mouth as the tank's overflow") and did not quote it.
- **Q11 wording** (013): the relay did not give "quatre plafons de ceràmica i vidre que representen quatre Sols". It gave "els medallons dels quatre Sols (un per a cada estació de l'any)", and I quoted that.
- **op4's "Gaudí himself moved there in 1906 to live with his father and niece"** was relayed only as a paraphrase, so 016 has no quotation marks for it.

## 3. Wording that differs from PLAN, and why
- **009, Brattstrom:** "a garden lizard, or a North African rainbow lizard" (the page's words) instead of PLAN's "an agama".
- **009 hook:** the opening "This is the creature everybody queues to photograph" is our framing. The park's own quotes follow it.
- **011, settlement:** FACTS 7.20 says "part on rock, part on unstable ground". Today's relay says "part over the cistern area". I scripted the neutral "Part of it stands on rock, and part of it does not". I don't link the settlement to the original design ("has had troubles of its own").
- **011, the 100 error:** the lint bans the phrase "hundred columns", so the track says "Guidebooks love to round that up to a hundred. The real figure is eighty-six".
- **012:** "It isn't open to visitors" (s4, NEVER "you can visit the cistern"). Cut it if the cistern ever opens.
- **015, works closure:** PLAN says "the path up to Casa Trias is closed". op24 says the closure runs **"from Casa Trias to the Upper Viaduct"**, so the track says "the path from Casa Trias up to the top viaduct is closed for works" (DATED, cuttable). The lead should check PLAN §1's "Casa Trias approach closed" against op24.
- **015, dated sentences:** the park's "still owned by the same family" is followed by a separate, cuttable sentence: "The city, though, has moved to buy it compulsorily." (G29.) Trias is called "the Güell family's lawyer" (op17 "the lawyer for the Güell family"), never "Dr".
- **016, length of stay:** Freixa & Leniz say "until a few months before his death" and op16 says "until 1925, a year before his death". The track uses op16's words plus "nearly twenty years… as the park's historians count it". It does not quote the Catalan line, to avoid the clash.
- **016, who bought it:** Bassegoda says "l'adquirí Gaudí" and op16 says Gaudí's father. Per F2.24, the track says "the Gaudí family bought it themselves". "No other buyer ever came" is PLAN's "nobody else did".
- **016, Berenguer's dates:** Reus 1866 – 1914 is in @what only. The track has no years for him, to keep to three dates (1906, 1925, 1963).
- **Bridge 013 H>017:** to stay within 45 words it says "walk on" instead of "skip…". The destination and features are as the row gives them.
- **010** opens its last reading paragraph with "As you heard beside the dragon…". This is safe: 010 is Full only, and 009 is in Full.
