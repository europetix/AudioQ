## Changed from PLAN, or found different on the page

Scripted: "The one put up after the war looked quite different. Today's is a copy, made from photographs of the original." (No 1996 on air, to keep dates at three.) s6 also says the 1936 destruction was "probably due to its religious symbolism": not used (PLAN: never who). O2 should confirm the cross on the tower now is that 1996 copy.
2. **003, trencadís.** s24 does not say Gaudí "made it famous"; it says fragmented ceramics "have become an identifying element of Gaudí's work", and that the medieval tradition was coloured tile cladding, with ceramic fragments characteristic of Mudéjar work. Scripted accordingly ("made it his signature").
