## For the lead to decide
1. **017, the first garden party (G75).** Re-reading the MUHBA *Butlletí* 20 sentence in full gives more than FACTS_GAPS relayed: "S'ha pogut documentar un primer acte públic el 20 d'octubre de 1906: la celebració d'una Garden Party (anomenada així, en anglès) en el marc del Primer Congrés de la Llengua Catalana." I used both new details (held during the first congress of the Catalan language; "called that, in English", which pays off the *Park*-with-a-k motif). Same OFF sentence, but not in FACTS: cut the congress clause if you want FACTS-only content.
2. **019, the "92" mark.** The PLAN row allows "92" if O16 finds one, but §4 NEVER says no "92" before O16. I followed NEVER: 019 says only that each new piece "got a small engraved mark". After O16, the lead can add the number and a "look for it" line.
3. **021, Jachin and Boaz.** PLAN's line ("reads the two end columns as Jachin and Boaz") is **not what the paper says**. Rius Santamaria (s21) p. 172 says the two end columns "can hardly represent the entrance to a Solomon's Temple". On p. 193 the paper places Jachin and Boaz in relation to a column of the Portico's first section. The script now says: the study argued the park hides a picture of the Temple of Jerusalem, worked out in light and geometry, and "even looks for Solomon's two pillars, Jachin and Boaz, in this portico. It's a theory, not a fact."
4. **017, the centenary dinner (O20).** Time Out (s9) gives the dinner as announced "in the Park Güell", not in the square, and I read nothing confirming that it took place. The script says "the neighbours were invited to a community dinner in the park". Confirm before recording.
5. **023, George R. Collins dropped.** On s33 the sentence "This author had described Gaudí's personality in a famous conference in Park Güell in 1956" does not say clearly who "this author" is, so Collins is not scripted. Dalí's "1956 conference in Park Güell" is spoken as "gave a talk here", with nothing about what he said.
