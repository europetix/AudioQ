PARK GÜELL V5, EDITION 2: ON-SITE CHECKLIST (from v5/PLAN_V2.md, 7 Nov 2026; 45 tracks)
==========================================================================================
Edition 1's list (25 tracks) is kept as v5/ONSITE_CHECKLIST_ED1.txt. Item numbers O1–O24 keep their meaning;
O25–O28 and O-map are new. Track numbers below are EDITION 2's.

One walk of the FULL route, in PLAN_V2 order, before recording, with the official "Imprescindibles" flyer map
(parkguell.barcelona/sites/default/files/mapa/ParkGuell_Flyer_Imprescindibles_en_red_c.pdf, printed), a printout of
the official recommended-routes drawing (research/map/official_routes_carrer_olot.png) and a phone camera.
- Photograph every sign, plaque, map board and arrow you are asked about. Save them to research/onsite/ as Oxx_<what>.jpg.
- Note times with a stopwatch: WALKING AND QUEUING ONLY, not listening.
- Report each item as CONFIRMED / DIFFERENT (what you saw) / NOT FOUND.
- Never complete a CAPTCHA or create an account. Never go into Casa Larrard (a school), Casa Trias (a private home)
  or the Gaudí House Museum. The tower pavilion is the shop: note only where to stand outside it.
- Nothing about prices, hours, slots or tickets goes into the guide; note them only if they change the route.
- Never note directions as compass points. Use left/right AS DEFINED ON THE DRAWING (standing on the esplanade inside
  the main gate, facing up the stairway) and also as you walk, plus landmarks, so the lead can rewrite cues.
- Once through the second control the route is ONE WAY, and once out of the park you CANNOT come back in.
  Walk the outer parts (gate, Casa del Guarda, viaducts) first, then the inner section, exactly as PLAN_V2 orders them.
  A second ticketed visit is needed to test ALT-G (other gates), ALT-L or ALT-Q2 (things at the end).
