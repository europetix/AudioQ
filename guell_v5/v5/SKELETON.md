## Binding design decisions (lead)
1. **Any gate works.** 001 is a welcome heard anywhere once inside (no "look at"), with a short spoken "how to find
   the start" for each of the three visitor gates (Olot side lane / Marianao; Carretera del Carmel; Sant Josep de la
   Muntanya), each in one or two sentences, by landmarks only. The numbered loop starts on the entrance esplanade
   inside the Olot gate, between the two pavilions, facing the stairway. If the on-site check (O9) finds that Carmel
   or Sant Josep entrants cannot reach the esplanade without crossing the inner section, ALT-G: those listeners start
   at the stop nearest their gate and the guide says which track to start from.
2. **The one-way section.** Default order = the operator's published recommended route (stops A–I): entrance and
   lodge → guardhouse → stairway → Hypostyle outside → inside → Austria Gardens → Nature Square → roads/paths/viaducts
   → ramp and Casa Larrard. Things outside the inner section are done before it or after it, never by doubling back.
   Before the second control, one sentence warns: "past this point the route runs one way; you can't come back".
   ALT-B (if O1 finds the inner section is entered from the square and leaves by the stairway): reverse the inner
   section; PLAN carries both cue sets.
3. **Waiting is content.** There is likely a queue at the second control (O10). The estate story (sixty plots, the
   rules, two houses) is a track that works standing still while waiting, with no "look at" until its last paragraph.
4. **Honest length.** Expected Full ≈ 1 h 40 incl. walking and queues (route research: content runs out at ≈ 95–105
   min). Say "about an hour and three quarters" if the PLAN arithmetic says so; never pad to 120. Held items listed
   at the end of PLAN (High Viaduct and Casa Trias approach, closed; the Calvary; the Gaudí House interior; the Casa del
   Guarda interior room by room).
5. **Legends told as legends** (workman in wet plaster, Hansel and Gretel, the mason and the washerwoman, the Delphi
   and Masonic readings): one track each at most, with who says it. Nothing in Gaudí's mouth without a verified source.
6. **Credit Jujol** wherever the colour is his (bench skin, ceiling suns), using the FACTS safe wording (4.3, 4.4).
7. **Directions:** landmarks and official names only (the plan's EN names with the CA name once). No compass points
   until O3 settles east/west of the Portico. No GPS assumptions; tracks are downloaded.
