PARK GÜELL V5: ON-SITE CHECKLIST (from v5/PLAN.md, 6 Nov 2026)
==================================================================
One walk of the FULL route, in PLAN order, before recording, with the official "Imprescindibles" flyer map
(parkguell.barcelona/sites/default/files/mapa/ParkGuell_Flyer_Imprescindibles_en_red_c.pdf, printed) and a phone camera.
- Photograph every sign, plaque, map board and arrow you are asked about. Save them to research/onsite/ as Oxx_<what>.jpg.
- Note times with a stopwatch: WALKING AND QUEUING ONLY, not listening.
- Report each item as CONFIRMED / DIFFERENT (what you saw) / NOT FOUND.
- Never complete a CAPTCHA or create an account. Never go into Casa Larrard (a school) or Casa Trias (a private home).
- Nothing about prices, hours, slots or tickets goes into the guide; note them only if they change the route.
- [O#] tags in PLAN refer to the items below. Never note directions as compass points (the operator's "south side",
  "eastern side" and "heading east" are exactly what we must check); use left/right AS YOU WALK THE ROUTE, plus
  landmarks, so the lead can rewrite cues.
- Remember: once through the second control the route is ONE WAY, and once out of the park you CANNOT come back in.
  Walk the outer parts (gate, viaducts) first, then the inner section, exactly as PLAN orders them. Do a second
  ticketed visit if you need to test ALT-B or ALT-G.
