### The route (binding order; geometry from MAP, confirmed on site)

**What MAP does not settle:** the viaducts (not drawn: O5), the second control's exact spot (most likely the stairway's foot: O1), the Font Sarva annex (O13), the hilltop (O25), the Austria Gardens' inner paths (O26).
