[curious] In the summer of 1899, the Marquis of Marianao sold Eusebi Güell the top of a bald hill, the Muntanya Pelada. The Catalan government says it had "almost no vegetation". While you wait, here's what he meant to build.

His application to the city in 1904 covered two old farms, Can Muntaner de Dalt and Can Coll i Pujol. With it went a memorandum signed by Güell. In Spanish, it praised the land's "excellent hygienic conditions", which had earned it the name "barrio de la salud", the healthy quarter. It offered "villas where the people of Barcelona can live comfortably and independently, without losing sight of their usual business".

The park's history counts "some 60 triangular-shaped plots", each to have, in a Spanish study's words, "sewerage, water, gas, electricity and telephone". The city's history museum lists the shared services in Catalan: "a porter's lodge, a market, a central square, a chapel". You've seen the lodge. The market and the square are up the stairway.

[pause] Then the rules. You could build on only a sixth of your plot. The museum's story for schoolchildren adds more, in Catalan. A buyer "will have to pay a fine of 50 pesetas if he cuts down or uproots a tree". [amused] He "may not block the view of the sea". His plans had to be "supervised and approved by Güell himself".

The first plot went to a friend of Güell's, the lawyer Martí Trias. A show house went up to tempt others. Only two houses were ever built for the estate. The park's historians, in a reviewer's Catalan summary, blame poor public transport, and rules "designed so that the architecture would blend into nature", which "worked against the project". The park's history is blunt: "A lack of buyers led to the works being abandoned in 1914."

Ahead of you is the great stairway, with its dragon halfway up. One thing before the check: past this point the route runs one way, and you can't come back this way. Once you're through, stop at the foot of the first flight, out of the way of the steps: The Dragon Stairway · Goblins and a flag.
