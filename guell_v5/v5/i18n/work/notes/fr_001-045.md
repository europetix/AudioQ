# FR notes, Park Güell 001–045 (7 Nov 2026 edition), voice Vivienne

Self-check: `assemble.py --check … fr` passes for all 45 tracks. Body total is 11,390 words against 11,549 in the English, so 1.4% shorter. Every track is at or under its English count. A few tracks only reach that with no margin: 006, 012, 023, 024, 028, 035, 037, 044.
Bridges: 21 lines with the same keys. Each starts "Dans cette version courte," and none is longer than its English line. Every cue, in bodies and bridges, matches the destination's FR @title character for character (checked by script).
Typography: quotes are written «…» with no inner spaces, because the check splits on whitespace and NBSP counts as whitespace. There is no space before : ? !. The voice does not need them.

## Quotations
All quotes were translated faithfully from the English. I verified no original wording, because FR does not allow it. Attributions and hedges are kept. The language framing is kept as in the English: "en espagnol" in 004, 006, 010, 014, 017, 020, 021, 022 and 027, and "en catalan" in 002, 005, 012, 013, 015, 023, 024, 025, 028, 029, 031, 035, 036, 037, 040, 041, 042 and 044.
- Original-language words that the English itself keeps were left as they are: «barrio de la salud» (014), «molt tensa» (041), «Capelya» (037), «Eusebio Güell», the Spanish form inside Bassegoda's reported words (020).
- Quotes originally in English were translated: the MUHBA English page (001), Kopsida (011), Brattstrom and Filler in the NYRB (019), Bassegoda via an English press note (019), Freixa via an English press note (008, kept as "selon une note de presse en anglais, elle a dit"), and the park's English pages and the water museum throughout.
- Reported speech is kept as reported: 020 "He was reported as saying" became "On rapporte qu'il a dit". The 034 workman legend keeps "dit-on" and "aurait".
- Gaudí is never quoted. The English has no Gaudí quote, and the FR has none either. 045 "L'idée de Gaudí était…" is the museum's sentence, not his.

## Ambiguities in the English and how I rendered them
- 039: "though the park's pages call it the Laundry Room Portico". The park's FR pages call it "Portique de la Lavandière", so a literal FR version would be false for French listeners. I rendered it as "même si les pages en anglais du parc l'appellent le portique de la buanderie". Please confirm.
- 035: "crosses, Latin phrases, prayers and psalms run all through it". "It" could be Jujol's work or the bench. I kept the ambiguity: "y sont partout".
- 029: "His father died that October" became "dès octobre", meaning October of the 1906 move.
- 045: "The builders were an uncle and his nephew." No body track introduces these builders, so the listener has no referent (029 mentions only Gaudí as Roseta's uncle). I translated it as it stands. It may be worth adding the builders' names in the English, or checking the line.
- 001: the tour names "the essential" and "the highlights" have no fixed FR names in the app. I used "l'essentielle" and "celle des temps forts".
- 015 says "One more rule, about the view, waits…" and 036 calls back to it. I used "Encore une règle" / "une autre règle" rather than "dernière".
- 004: "Gaudí first used broken tile in the 1880s" became "a employé le carreau brisé dès les années 1880", meaning from the 1880s. The year is kept.

## Possible errors in the English (not fixed)
- I noticed no factual error while translating, and I did not re-audit the English against the FACTS files: it is final. The only items to raise are the 039 naming point and the unexplained 045 "uncle and his nephew" above.
