## Ambiguities in the English, and my choices
- 001: "the essential" is the name of a tour length. I used "das Wesentliche" because I don't know the app's German tier names. Please align it with the app UI if those names exist.
- 008: "promoter" (Freixa) became "Förderer" (sponsor). If the original Spanish is "promotor" in the developer sense, "Bauträger" would be the alternative.
- 017: "A scholar… wrote in 2023". The source (s20) is María-Belén García Ávila, so I used "Eine Forscherin". This makes her gender explicit but adds no name.
- 023: "original tile-shard mosaics" could mean the mosaics are original or that they are inventive. I rendered it "originalen" (original).
- 025 and 038: "mines in the hill" and "water mine" are rendered literally as "Minen" and "Wassermine". These are presumably the Catalan "mines d'aigua", meaning water galleries.
- 039: "though the park's pages call it the Laundry Room Portico". I wrote "die englischen Seiten des Parks" so that the English name makes sense to a German listener. This is one added word.
- 040: "One researcher" became generic masculine "Ein Forscher". Per the sources this is Rius Santamaria, whose gender I did not check.
- 043: "on an early June evening" became "an einem Abend Anfang Juni".
- 020: the Bassegoda quote has "Eusebio Güell" (Spanish form) while the rest of the guide says Eusebi. I kept "Eusebio" because it is inside the quote.
- 012 and 037: Joan Bassegoda (012 and elsewhere) and Bonaventura Bassegoda (037) are different people. I kept each exactly as the English has it.
