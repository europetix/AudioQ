## Ambiguous, or possibly wrong, in the English (reported, not fixed)
1. **039:** "though the park's pages call it the Laundry Room Portico" is true only of the park's English pages. The ES pages say "Pórtico de la Lavandera". To keep the line true, the ES says "las páginas del parque en inglés lo llaman Laundry Room Portico". Please review: this is the only added word in the guide.
2. **018:** "some writers, among them… Joan Bassegoda, see in this creature…" puts a dead man in the present tense. It is kept present ("ven") as in the English. 020 "Bassegoda took it seriously" is past.
3. **027:** Time Out ES spells the architect "Batllever". The EN's "Batllevell" (the usual spelling) is kept.
4. **017:** the EN's "A scholar" has no gender. The ES uses "Una estudiosa", since the source is García Ávila (named in 020–022). **040:** "One researcher" = "Un investigador" (gender unknown, generic masculine).
5. **013:** "For now, the path higher up… is closed for works" is a dated sentence. It is kept as "Por ahora" (PLAN bans "for now" only about the square and the view).
6. **044:** speaks of "paying visitors" and residents coming in "free". This is translated as written, though PLAN's practical limits ban tickets and prices in speech. The lead should confirm it is approved.
7. **012:** "Ice Age rhinoceros, elephants and deer" leaves the scope of "Ice Age" ambiguous. The ES keeps the same ambiguity ("de la Edad de Hielo" at the end).
8. **014:** "the old town of Gràcia" = "la antigua villa de Gràcia". It is not "a separate town" (NEVER list); 036 keeps "se había unido a Barcelona en 1897".
