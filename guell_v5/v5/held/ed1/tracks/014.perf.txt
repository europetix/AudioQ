[curious] This spring is older than everything Gaudí built around it. Barcelona's local broadcaster, betevé, called it, in Catalan, "this feature, older than the modernista complex". Its water comes from underground mines in the old Bald Mountain. A city works notice places it "in the enclosing wall of the Hypostyle Room".

Long before the park, people from La Salut, the neighbourhood around the hill, came up with clay jugs to fetch its water. It was also piped to the well of the old Larrard farmhouse, the house Güell would later make his home.

[amused] Then Güell had an idea. In 1913 he had the water analysed, and found it contained magnesium. He decided to bottle it and sell it as medicinal water. For about a decade, betevé says, you could find it in the chemists' shops of the day.

For years the spring lay hidden in the basement of the hall. The restoration works that ended in 2020 opened up its little room, a space that, in the park's words in Catalan, "until now had not been accessible". Visitors have been able to see it here since September 2020.

As you look in, picture the neighbours with their jugs, and Güell's bottles on a chemist's shelf.
