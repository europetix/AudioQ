## 2. Findings

### MUST

**M1. 020: a paraphrase spoken as a Catalan quotation of a Spanish-language newspaper.**
- **Track text:** "That same year the architect Bonaventura Bassegoda wrote in a Barcelona newspaper, in Catalan, that the chapel "would protect the whole colony, looking down on it"."
- **Source:** s3, Bassegoda 2008, https://raco.cat/index.php/ButlletiRACBASJ/article/download/219547/329537/330057. The relay says the sentence "la capella la qual protegirà, dominant-la, tota la colònia" "is **not** a direct quotation. It appears in indirect speech after 'Aquest autor diu que', with no quotation marks". The words are Joan Bassegoda's Catalan summary.
- **Language of the paper:** Enciclopèdia Catalana on the *Diario de Barcelona* says "Era redactat en castellà", with Catalan only from 22 Mar 1810 under the French (https://www.enciclopedia.cat/gran-enciclopedia-catalana/diari-de-barcelona). So "wrote … in Catalan" is unsupported, and almost certainly wrong for 1903.
- **Replace with:** "That same year the architect Bonaventura Bassegoda wrote in a Barcelona newspaper, the Diario de Barcelona, that the chapel would protect the whole colony, looking down on it." Use no quotation marks. Fix @sources ("indirect speech, CA paraphrase of an ES article").

**M2. 005: Sellés's "words" are Bassegoda's paraphrase.**
- **Track text:** "Sellés described the caves on the hill as "shady openings in the ground, as if dug as burrows by some creature of troglodyte times"."
- **Source:** s3 (above), re-read twice today. "The article doesn't use quotation marks there. Bassegoda reports Sellés's 1903 account in his own words ('en un cert moment descriu com…'), so the phrase is his paraphrase of Sellés's description." The bibliography lists Sellés's text under a Spanish title ("El Parque Güell", *Anuario de la Asociación de Arquitectos de Cataluña*, 1903). G51 had already warned that the original may be Spanish.
- **Replace with:** "In Bassegoda's Catalan retelling of Sellés's account, the caves were "shady openings in the ground, as if dug as burrows by some creature of troglodyte times"." This quotes Bassegoda's own words, attributed to him, in the past tense. The next sentences stay: "The party went in by torchlight" is confirmed ("A la llum d'algunes torxes seguiren amb passes cautes els laberíntics verals", with the excursionists as subject). Change "Bassegoda adds" to "Bassegoda added".

**M3. 017: the garden party was not "during" the congress.**
- **Track text:** "It was a Garden Party, during the first congress of the Catalan language."
- **Source, MUHBA Butlletí 20 (2010):** Freixa & Leniz, re-read today: "S'ha pogut documentar un primer acte públic el 20 d'octubre de 1906" / "la celebració d'una Garden Party (anomenada així, en anglès) en el marc del Primer Congrés de la Llengua Catalana" (https://www.barcelona.cat/museuhistoria/sites/default/files/muhbabutllet20.pdf).
- **Source, congress dates:** *El Temps* (2006): "Entre el 13 i el 17 d'octubre … se celebrà a Barcelona el I Congrés Internacional de la Llengua Catalana" (https://www.eltemps.cat/documents/el-temps_2006_10_1165_0068.pdf). "En el marc del" means "as part of".
- **Problem:** 20 October is after the sessions ended. "During" is wrong, and "the first congress" also drops "International".
- **Replace with:** "It was a Garden Party, held as part of the first international congress of the Catalan language."
- The rest of the paragraph is confirmed: "Called that, they note, in English", and "promocionar la seva urbanització" for Güell's aim.

**S1. 003: "the mushroom-shaped hat" is quoted for the wrong pavilion.**
- **Track text:** "The other house is oval, and its flue ends in what the restoration study calls "the mushroom-shaped hat"."
- **Source:** s6, Aguado, Ribas & Hosta, *Informes de la Construcción* 54 (2002), https://core.ac.uk/download/230705175.pdf. The phrase came back attached to the *conserjería* pavilion on both reads today, the second asked specifically.
  - Relay: "On p. 23, the text describes the smoke duct of the first-floor room of the conserjería pavilion, which exits through the roof at a mushroom-shaped cap: 'a través del sombrero en forma de seta que se observa en la cubierta'."
  - "Planta elíptica" belongs to the *vivienda*: "Este pabellón tiene una planta elíptica…"
- **Other sources:** La Pedrera puts a "mushroom-shaped dome" on the pavilion "intended as the caretaker's residence" (https://www.lapedrera.com/en/work-antoni-gaudi/park-guell/). Andruet speaks of "ventiladores con forma de setas" in the plural. Both pavilions may carry mushroom-like elements.
- **Replace with:** "The other house is oval, and its roof is crowned by what the La Pedrera foundation calls a "mushroom-shaped dome"."
- **Also:** re-read s6 p. 23 directly. O2 should confirm that "the gatehouse with the mushroom on its roof" (cues in 002, 003, 004) picks out only the Casa del Guarda.

**S4. 012: "gathers in the hollows of the little domes".**

It says the water "flowed to the **pendentives**", which are the corners between domes over the columns, not the domes' hollows. G3 even drafted the "hollows" sentence itself.
- **Replace with:** "The rain soaks through the square and runs down inside the columns."

**S5. 012: the op8 conduit quote is placed in the columns.**
- **Track text:** "…and runs down inside the columns. In the park's words, a conduit "collects the rainwater that filters down from the square, sending it on to an underground tank"."
- **Source:** op8 (https://parkguell.barcelona/en/park-guell/emblematic-features/hypostyle-room), re-read with a direct question. The preceding sentence is "The colonnade is crowned by an architrave on which the undulating bench is placed". The conduit "runs inside the architrave". The column-core drain is García Ávila's (confirmed: "servía como conducto de desagüe hacia la cisterna").
- **Replace with:** "Along the front, in the park's words, a conduit inside the architrave under the bench "collects the rainwater that filters down from the square, sending it on to an underground tank"."

**S6. 020: "written into the contract", from a children's story.**
- **Track text:** "The rules for buyers, as the city's history museum sums them up, said in Catalan that a buyer "may not block the view of the sea". This view was written into the contract."
- **Source:** MUHBA dossier, re-read today (https://www.barcelona.cat/museuhistoria/sites/default/files/dossier_park_guell_primaria.pdf). The rules "appear **inside the fictional story**", told about the character Josep Fortuny, and only that story says they "s'hauran de posar en el contracte". 007 discloses the register; 020 presents it as a museum summary and a contract fact. op4 does say that house height and placement were restricted to protect sea views (relayed paraphrase).
- **Replace with:** "The park's history says the houses' height and placing were limited to protect the view of the sea. In the city history museum's story for schoolchildren, a buyer "may not block the view of the sea"." Drop "This view was written into the contract."

**S7. 023: "pushed hard".**
- **Track text:** "One man pushed hard for it: Nicolau Maria Rubió i Tudurí, the city's director of parks, whom one newspaper has called "the inventor of green Barcelona"."
- **Source:** *Ara*, opinion piece by Ignasi Aragay, 1 Apr 2026 (https://en.ara.cat/culture/the-inventor-of-green-barcelona_129_5696088.html). It says only: "He promoted the acquisition of land (including Park Güell and Plaça de la Sagrada Família)". It gives no role in the purchase itself. The "inventor" phrase is the column's headline.
- **Replace with:** "The city's director of parks, Nicolau Maria Rubió i Tudurí, whom a columnist in the newspaper Ara has called "the inventor of green Barcelona", promoted the city's purchase of land for green space, and Park Güell was on his list." This merges with the next sentence.

**S8. 002: the cement overclaim.**
- **Track text:** "Güell made the very material modern Barcelona was built from."
- **Source:** s1 (https://www.enciclopedia.cat/gran-enciclopedia-catalana/eusebi-guell-i-bacigalupi). Asland's plants at La Pobla de Lillet (1901) and Montcada (1916) were "les primeres de pòrtland a Catalunya". Nineteenth-century Barcelona (the Eixample) was built of stone and brick before them.
- **Replace with:** cut it, or "Güell's cement helped build the twentieth-century city."

**S9. 016: the niece's death.**
- **Track text:** "His father died that October. His niece died six years later."
- **Source:** op22 (W): "El padre murió en octubre de 1906 y la sobrina, en enero de 1912". That is about five years and three months.
- **Replace with:** "His niece died in 1912." or "five years later".

**S10. 013: "the floors" at Casa Milà.**
- **Track text:** "At Casa Milà he gave Jujol the floors, the interior decoration and the running of the building site."
- **Source:** UPC, Fons Jujol (Roura & Suñer 2023; https://upcommons.upc.edu/bitstreams/92566456-85cc-4edd-9aa6-95f2856c4014/download), re-read: "s'encarregà dels forjats, la decoració interior i en dirigí la construcció".
- **Problem:** "Forjats" can mean floor slabs or forged ironwork. The page doesn't define it, and Jujol is popularly credited with the balcony ironwork. La Pedrera credits the railings to Gaudí and the Badia workshop (https://www.lapedrera.com/en/blog/32-unique-and-different-balconies). Either translation risks being wrong or contested.
- **Replace with:** "At Casa Milà he trusted Jujol with the interior decoration and, the archive says, the running of the building site."

**S11. 021: "carries a path on its back".**
- **Track text:** "The portico carries a path on its back, raised on leaning columns, the same trick Gaudí used for the park's viaducts."
- **Source:** op10 (https://parkguell.barcelona/en/park-guell/emblematic-features/laundry-room-portico), re-read: "The page doesn't say so… The 'atop slanting columns' phrase refers to the wave-shaped roof, not a walkway." None of the track's @sources supports it.
- **Replace with:** "It stands on leaning columns, the same trick Gaudí used for the park's viaducts." Or keep the original only after a source is cited.

**S12. 009: the foot restoration tied to the 2007 attack.**
- **Track text:** "In February 2007 vandals with an iron rod seriously damaged it. Its front right foot has since been taken away, studied by the sculptor Abel Vallhonesta, and restored."
- **Source:** op25 (https://parkguell.barcelona/en/estat-de-les-obres-de-restauracio/16-Restitucio-ma-dreta-salamandra), re-read: "The page doesn't say what damage prompted the restoration". The work began in Feb 2025 as "ongoing study and preservation". The foot was studied "for over a year" and the original was reinstalled. As written, "since" makes the repair sound like the answer to 2007.
- **Replace with:** "In February 2007 vandals with an iron rod seriously damaged it. Separately, in 2025, its front right foot was taken away and studied for over a year by the sculptor Abel Vallhonesta, then put back." Keep the 22 April 2026 sentence.
