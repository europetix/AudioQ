# AUDIT V5, EDITION 2: facts (Park Güell, 45 tracks + 4 bridge files), 7 Nov 2026

This is an independent fact audit of `v5/tracks/001–045.perf.txt` (the spoken body after `---`, plus @what and @sources) and of `v5/bridges/*.txt`. I did not write these tracks and I did not edit any of them.

**Method**
- **Baseline.** I re-checked every MUST and SHOULD from the edition-1 audit (`AUDIT_V5_FACTS.md`, "FA"), using the Audit resolution table in PLAN_V2 §1. I then compared edition 2 with `v5/held/ed1/tracks/` and concentrated on what is new.
- **Web.** I used WebFetch and WebSearch only, one page at a time and in sequence: 31 fetches and 8 searches. I used no curl, no browser and no CAPTCHA, and I never relied on Wikipedia.
- **Quotes are "as relayed".** WebFetch returns a model summary and caps verbatim excerpts at about 125 characters, so every quote below is as relayed.
- **Blocked.** One page could not be read: dialnet (Duch 1999, https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/2420342.pdf). The permission was not answered, so I did not retry it.
- **Reached this time.** Four pages blocked in edition 1 are now read: Kopsida (core.ac.uk/41804329), Rius Santamaria (diposit.ub.edu), gaudiclub 10_04_23, 10_05_26 and rest5, and sagradafamilia.org.

**Counts: 2 MUST, 8 SHOULD, 22 NICE.**
- All three edition-1 MUSTs and all twelve SHOULDs are applied.
- One edition-1 NICE (FA N9, the "well") is **not** applied, although the 025–035 writer notes say it is. It is graded SHOULD below (S6).
- Bridges: all 4 files are OK.
- NEVER list and Lead's decisions: no breach found. In detail:
  - No prices, tickets or capacity.
  - No present tense for the dead. The past-tense names (Bassegoda ×2, Sellés, Matamala, Ballell, Reixac, Rubió, Gaudí, Güell, Jujol, Berenguer, Trias) are all in the past.
  - Gràcia is never called a separate town (014 and 036 say it was absorbed in 1897).
  - None of the banned phrases: "free access", "contract", "during the congress", "violent", "hungry", "newsreels", "Hotel Arts", "Alfonso XIII", "210 m", "19 ha".
  - The garden party is given no day.
  - Nobody is named as the destroyer of any cross.
  - Nothing sends listeners into the tower pavilion, Casa Larrard, Casa Trias, the museum, the cistern, the mine, the caves or the hilltop.
  - Legends are told as legends: Hansel and Gretel, wet plaster, Delphi, the Temple of Jerusalem, the rain-machine thesis ("argues").
  - The gate cues no longer rely on "the mushroom" (lead's correction). The two spoken mushroom lines (004, 006) are attributed or refer to the 1904 plans.

## 1. Findings

### MUST

**M1. 002: "the first modest building on this hillside" is not what the source says, and the source itself contradicts it.**
- **Track text:** "Next to this gate, outside the park, is an old spring, the Font del Carbó. Around it, in 1875, the first modest building on this hillside began, a quarter of a century before Güell."
- **Source:** Bassegoda 2008 (s3), re-read today: https://raco.cat/index.php/ButlletiRACBASJ/article/download/219547/329537/330057. It reads: "El 1875 començà una tímida urbanització a la zona de la font de Carbó, dessota la casa Larrard."
- **Problem.** The source says a tentative start of urban development in the area of the spring. It does not say "first", and it does not say "building on this hillside". The same sentence places the area *below Casa Larrard*, the old farmhouse that 041 rightly calls "older than the park". FACTS_EXPANSION E47 also has the Carretera del Carmel built in 1874–81. "The first … building on this hillside" is therefore wrong.
- **The spring.** op24 (https://parkguell.barcelona/en/disruptions, last updated 2/11/2026) names the *Jardins* de la Font del Carbó, "outside the park, next to the Carrer d'Olot entrance". Nothing I read says the spring itself can still be seen.
- **Replace with:** "Next to this gate, outside the park, are gardens named after an old spring, the Font del Carbó. Around that spring, below the old farmhouse you'll pass at the end, a few houses had begun to go up by 1875, a quarter of a century before Güell."
  - Shorter: "…Around it, in 1875, a modest start was made at building on this corner of the hill, a quarter of a century before Güell."

**M2. 011: Kopsida did not "measure fifty metres of stone columns".**
- **Track text:** "She measured about fifty metres of stone columns, up to six metres high."
- **Source:** Kopsida, UPC 2011 (G-S9), read today; it was blocked for FA: https://core.ac.uk/download/41804329.pdf.
  - "a length of approximately 50 m": this is the **viaduct's** length.
  - "columns of 80cm in diameter with a maximum height of 6.00m".
  - The thesis also says the main difficulty was the lack of "any officially measured and verified structural drawing available". She built her model from drawings supplied by the Ajuntament and did not survey the structure herself.
- **Problem.** The sentence makes the bridge's length a length of columns, and it says she measured something she modelled from existing drawings.
- **Replace with:** "She studied this viaduct, about fifty metres long, on stone columns up to six metres high."
- **Also.** The programme was the Erasmus Mundus Advanced Master in Structural Analysis of Monuments and Historical Constructions, so "an engineering student" is acceptable. A more exact form is "a student of the structural analysis of monuments at Barcelona's technical university". Her supervisor was Pere Roca.

### SHOULD

**S1. 011: "all the way to the ground" goes beyond the thesis.**
- **Track text:** "So each column is tilted along the path the load wants to take, and the thrust stays inside the stone, all the way to the ground."
- **Source:** Kopsida (above). The relevant figure captions say: "the vault is stable since the thrust line is contained within the limits of the vault geometry". The thesis does not say the thrust line runs down the columns to the ground.
- **Replace with:** "So each column is tilted along the path the load wants to take, and her analysis keeps the thrust inside the vault."
  - Then cut the following "Her analysis found the vault stable", or keep it in place of the second half.

**S2. 035: the study does not call the inscriptions' meaning an open question; it claims to solve it.**
- **Track text:** "Josep Maria Jujol engraved prayers to the Virgin Mary into its tiles. One study calls their meaning an open question."
- **Source:** Rius Santamaria, "Park Güell: Reconstructing the Temple of Jerusalem through Light and Aesthetic Geometry", *Matèria* 16–17 (2020), pp. 171–198, read today; it was blocked for FA and for the writer: https://diposit.ub.edu/dspace/bitstream/2445/179272/1/705626.pdf.
  - The introduction lists the inscriptions among unresolved questions.
  - Later the paper says "we can also solve another enigma of this work: the meaning of the legends dedicated to the Virgin Mary", and it reads them as the Temple's Inner Room. G49's "treated as an open question" is therefore wrong.
- **Replace with:** "Josep Maria Jujol engraved prayers to the Virgin Mary into its tiles. What they mean is still argued over."
  - Or attribute the theory: "One researcher calls their meaning an enigma, and offers his own answer." That researcher is the same one as in 040, so prefer the first form.

**S3. 040: the paper does not say "the whole park" is a picture of the Temple.**
- **Track text:** "One researcher, writing in 2020, argued that the whole park hides a picture of the Temple of Jerusalem, worked out in light and geometry."
- **Source:** Rius Santamaria (above). The relay says the paper locates "a representation of the Temple of Jerusalem" in the park, in the central area along a line from the Doric temple (the hall) to the top of the Nature Square. It is "the Temple of Solomon that Antoni Gaudí devised at Park Güell", not the whole park.
  - On Jachin and Boaz: the paper first links them to the two equal columns at the end of the portico and then rejects that reading. Later it places "the two columns Jachin and Boaz" at the level of column nine of the portico's first section. So "in this portico" is correct.
  - The year (2020) and the title are now confirmed, which resolves the RC in PLAN_V2 §7b.
- **Replace with:** "One researcher, writing in 2020, argued that the park hides a picture of the Temple of Jerusalem, worked out in light and geometry."

**S4. 044: the manifesto is undated, but the paragraph sets it in 2013.**
- **Track text:** "The neighbours were part of the story from the start. When charging began that October, a Catalan news agency reported that neighbours had been complaining about the crowds. Neighbourhood associations also signed a manifesto against closing the park off."
- **Source:** Ajuntament, *Mirades del verd* timeline, re-read today: https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/ecologiaurbana/sites/default/files/mirades_del_verd_timeline.pdf. The entry reads: "…i la FAVB signen un manifest contra el tancament del Park Güell". The year labels are scrambled in the extraction.
  - The neighbouring entries are "Primera Biennal de Paisatge" (the first Barcelona landscape biennial, held in 1999), "Concurs Portes de Collserola" and a CCCB exhibition. This suggests the manifesto may be years older than 2013.
  - A search found no dated report.
  - "Also", straight after "that October", tells the listener it happened in 2013.
- **Replace with:** cut the sentence. Or untie it from 2013: "Long before the fee, neighbourhood associations had signed a manifesto against closing the park off." Use that only if a date confirms "long before". Otherwise: "At one point, neighbourhood associations even signed a manifesto against closing the park off."

**S5. 024: "in 2016" is not on the cited page.**
- **Track text:** "Then, in 2016, each of the four Suns was cut into twenty-four pieces, taken down, restored and put back."
- **Source:** aj8, re-read today: https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/turisme/sites/default/files/5._barcelona_restaura_els_medallons_del_sostre_1.pdf. The page gives no dates for the work. It has a 2013 emergency ("actuació d'urgència fruit d'uns despreniments"), visitor figures for 2015, and a footer "Copyright © 2016 Servei de Premsa". F7.18's "(2016)" was the year of the cost, which is itself inferred from the footer. A search found no second dated source.
- **Replace with:** "Later, each of the four Suns was cut into twenty-four pieces, taken down, restored and put back." Keep "2016" only after a dated source confirms it (RC).

**S6. 025: FA N9 ("well" should be "spring") was not applied, although the writer notes say it was.**
- **Track text:** "It was also piped to the well of the old Larrard farmhouse, which Güell later made his home."
- **Source:** betevé, 6 Apr 2021 (s19, V by FA): "també es canalitza fins a la **deu** de l'antiga masia Larrard". A "deu" is a spring.
- **Replace with:** "It was also piped to the spring of the old Larrard farmhouse, which Güell later made his home."

**S7. 037: "It was never begun" is not in the source.**
- **Track text:** "It was never begun: the plots didn't sell."
- **Source:** s3, re-read today with this question: "Després es va veure que la venda de solars no anava endavant i s'hagué d'abandonar la construcció de la capella." The relay adds: "The text does not say that construction had started… but it never confirms that building work began." "Abandonar la construcció" could mean the plan or work already started. E38's "never begun" was the research file's gloss.
- **Replace with:** "It was never built: the plots didn't sell."

**S8. 007: a parabolic arch is not "the shape of a hanging chain turned upside down".**
- **Track text:** "The same study describes them as two parabolic arches, the shape of a hanging chain turned upside down."
- **Source:** s6, re-read today: https://core.ac.uk/download/230705175.pdf. It says: "las aberturas en las paredes de carga también se realizan a través de dos arcos parabólicos" (the vivienda, ground floor).
- **Problem.** A hanging chain makes a catenary, not a parabola. The gloss is ours, and in strict terms it is wrong. Scholars argue over whether Gaudí's "parabolic" arches are catenaries, which is all the more reason not to assert either.
- **Replace with:** "The same study describes them as two parabolic arches, a curve Gaudí loved." Or end the sentence at "two parabolic arches."
