## 1. The official offer (Park Güell, run by BSM for the Ajuntament de Barcelona)

No official ticket type includes or sells an audio guide (OFF, https://parkguell.barcelona/en/buy-tickets).

### 1.3 Official rules a guide must get right (DATED)
- **Timed slot:** "Reservations are held open for half an hour after the reserved time"; "we cannot guarantee entry outside the reserved time slot." Tickets are online only ("Tickets online only" on the map). Show the QR on your phone. There is no need to print or visit a ticket office. (OFF, FAQs; maps-and-services)
- **No re-entry:** "you can stay as long as you like, but once you have exited, you cannot re-enter." (OFF, FAQs)
- **Second control point and one-way section:** the high-concentration spots (Pl. Natura, Hypostyle Room, Laundry Room/Washerwoman Portico, Dragon Stairway, Austria Gardens) "are found within the section that has a second controlled access point". Past it, visitors follow a one-way route, and waiting is likely there. (OFF, https://parkguell.barcelona/en/planning-your-visit/the-restricted-area ; https://parkguell.barcelona/en/a-park-for-everone/why-are-the-access-points-regulated)
- **Capacity and hours:** 1,400 visitors/hour; 9 entries (4 for neighbours only, 4 for visitors and citizens, 1 exit on Carrer d'Olot); citizens-only bands early morning and evening. (OFF, why-are-the-access-points-regulated)
- **Recommended entrances:** Carretera del Carmel (coach park; best for reduced mobility), Carrer de Larrard, escalator from Av. Santuari de Sant Josep de la Muntanya. **Vallcarca (L3) "not recommended"** because its mechanical stairs are out of service. (OFF, FAQs)
- **Works (updated 2 Nov 2026):** "The route between Coll del Portell and the Forat del Vent Stairway is closed"; **the route from Casa Trias to the Upper Viaduct (Viaducte de Dalt) is affected**; works last about 18 months. (OFF, https://parkguell.barcelona/en/disruptions)
- **Toilets:** Carretera del Carmel gate; Passeig de les Palmeres by Plaça de la Natura; by the Carrer d'Olot entrance. (OFF, maps-and-services)

### 1.4 The "free zone" question: CONFLICT, to resolve in FACTS before scripting
- Official pages say "The ticket provides access to the 12 hectares of the Park" and describe no unticketed area for ordinary visitors. A further **8 ha of woodland** is adjacent, but the page does not state its access status. (OFF, the-restricted-area; why-are-the-access-points-regulated)
- The official Plus tour still says it runs "through the unregulated area" (OFF, guided-tours).
- artvisitguide (Apr 2026): "The 7-hectare forest area north of the Monumental Zone is always free" (SEC). Barcelona Hacks (Oct 2026) still describes a "Free Zone" about 20 m from the benches (SEC). barcelona-tickets/Headout still uses the 2013 "just 7.9%" Monumental Zone model (SEC, probably outdated).
- **Implication for our scope:** USER_DECISIONS places "the nearest viaducts and the Portico of the Washerwoman in the free park". Official pages now list the Washerwoman (Laundry Room) Portico **inside the second-control section**, and the Upper Viaduct route is affected by works. **The lead must re-verify zone boundaries and viaduct access before route design.**

**Bottom line on the official offer:** a free, official, accessible planning app in only 4 languages. Its audio content (number of stops, length, narrator) is undocumented, and its reviews say it sometimes does not load and lacks a facing-direction arrow. A separate easy-read app has 23 short tracks. QR panels on site need the park Wi-Fi. Live tours run in up to 4 languages and last about 50 or 90 minutes. **No official product tells the story in depth, in German or Italian, offline, in a fixed walking order.**

## 4. Common gaps and mistakes, and what our guide must do better

### 4.1 Evidence from reviews (paraphrased; exact quotes and URLs in Appendix A)
1. **The track order breaks if you use a different gate.** "we entered through the wrong entrance and the sequence of explanations was not correct"; "We who entered through the topmost park door started listening about the pavilions, but they were located elsewhere" (Headout reviews 18014, Aug–Oct 2025, SEC, https://www.headout.com/reviews/18014/spain/barcelona/combo-park-g-ell-casa-batll-skip-the-line-tickets).
2. **GPS and geolocation fail.** "doesn't geolocalize us well in the park"; "After about two thirds of the tour, I lost the audio guide (or it lost me)" (Headout 18014, 2025); "gps showed an arrow in the direction you are facing" wish (official app, App Store); VoiceMap Navigation 57%.
3. **There is no signal, so the download fails.** "the internet never works there" (Viator 2024); "my phone didn't have a signal so the audio guide wasn't working" (Headout 7563, Apr 2024, SEC, https://www.headout.com/reviews/7563/spain/barcelona/park-guell-timed-entry-ticket); "data roaming restrictions" (Headout 18014). The official QR content needs the park Wi-Fi.
4. **Visitors get lost and the signs and map are poor.** "the park is immense, it's easy to get lost"; "difficult to know where something are by the provided map"; "better signage on site or a better interactive map" (Headout 18014, 2025).
5. **The one-way section catches people out.** "once you go down to the terrace (serpentine bench) you have "left" the park" (Headout 18014, Jun 2025). This matches the official second control point and one-way route.
6. **Names on the audio do not match the names on the signs.** "the audio guide shows names of points of interest different from the names given on the park signs" (Headout 7563, Apr 2024). Sources variously say dragon, salamander, lizard and "El Drac"; Washerwoman, Laundry Room and Bugadera; Greek Theatre, Nature Square and Plaça de la Natura; Carrer d'Olot vs Carrer de Larrard for the main gate (Headout entrances blog, SEC).
7. **Languages are missing.** "THERE WAS NO AUDIO GUIDE IN ITALIAN" (2024) and "there is no Italian version" (Nov 2025). The official app has no German or Italian.
8. **Content is thin or vague.** The free sample was "vague general not informative or descriptive" (Navibration). Most products cover 5–6 highlights and state no length. A reseller FAQ leaves "How long does the audio-guided tour take?" unanswered.
9. **Legends and superlatives are stated as fact.** "world's longest mosaic bench" (Waytale); the portico as a "housekeeper's shortcut" (barcelona-tickets); character narration by "historical figures" (Navibration) blurs fact and fiction. Official texts make **no** length or "longest" claim.
10. **Jujol is missing.** The official Greek Theatre text credits "Josep M. Jujol" for the bench mosaic, but WeGoTrip, Waytale, the Headout museum blog and the VoiceMap description do not mention him (SEC). The Guardian: his contribution has "often been overlooked" (SEC, https://www.inkl.com/news/gaudi-s-partner-in-iconic-barcelona-design-finally-gets-the-limelight).
11. **Facts are wrong or stale.** "tickets can be bought on site" (Booking.com); "The Park Guell is free to visit" (Vox City FAQ); old admission figures (Musement); the Sagrada Família "closed temporarily" banner after the 2024 reopening.
12. **Nothing beyond the terrace.** Most products stop at the Monumental Zone highlights. There is little help with the viaducts, Casa Trias, Tres Creus, or the current works that close or affect the Upper Viaduct route (OFF, disruptions).
13. **Practical gaps.** "too few toilets"; "no food/drink facilities"; "the hill is very challenging"; "be prepared for steps"; evening slots: "entry at 7 pm if then the park is not lit" (Headout 18014; Viator).

### 4.2 Concrete list: what the Yo Tours guide must do (no prices)
1. **Make it entrance-aware.** Give three short start tracks (Carrer d'Olot main gate, Carretera del Carmel, Av. Santuari de Sant Josep de la Muntanya escalator / upper gate). Each one joins the same numbered loop at the right point, so no track ever describes something "behind you".
2. **Follow the official one-way logic.** Tell listeners before the second control point that the Nature Square / Hypostyle / Dragon / Washerwoman section is one-way and that they cannot come back. Order the tracks so nothing is missed: see the things that sit before the control first, and do the terrace photos before going down.
3. **Make it offline-first, with no reliance on GPS.** Downloading the whole guide before arrival is mandatory and the guide says so on the booking page and in track 0. Use numbered tracks with landmark cues ("with the dragon at your back…"), not geofencing. One sentence explains that signal and Wi-Fi in the park are unreliable.
4. **Use the park's own names first,** and mention the alternatives once each: "Plaça de la Natura, the old Greek Theatre"; "the dragon, or salamander, as the park itself calls it"; the official A–I letters where they help; "the Laundry Room or Washerwoman's Portico".
5. **Separate fact from legend in plain words.** No "longest bench in the world". If we mention the length, give the verified figure as a fact without a superlative. If we use popular tales (the naked-worker bench story, the Hansel-and-Gretel pavilions, the dragon-as-Python reading, the "housekeeper's shortcut"; only the last was found in a competitor product in this research), treat them as stories, using the V5 hedge ("It's a wonderful story. And it may not be true.").
6. **Give Jujol his due.** Credit him at the bench and at the Hypostyle ceiling medallions (if FACTS verifies the medallions), citing the official wording "planned by Josep M. Jujol".
7. **Tell the full story,** which no competitor does well: Güell's English-style garden-city plan, the number of planned homes (Waytale says sixty; verify in FACTS), why it failed, the 1926 municipal park, Gaudí living there, the 2013 regulation and the 1,400/hour cap, and the UNESCO listing.
8. **Cover the near viaducts and the Washerwoman Portico** with clear route cues, and with **live works caveats** (the Upper Viaduct route is affected and Coll del Portell to Forat del Vent is closed). Hold any stop that the works make unreliable, and say so honestly.
9. **Include an outside stop for the Gaudí House Museum:** what is inside, about 30 min, the combined ticket, and the uphill path past the Austria Gardens. Mention the Porter's Lodge exhibition, the Casa del Guarda, as an option.
10. **Offer honest lengths** (45 / 75 / 120 as decided) and state each one up front, as nobody else does. Give a "short on time" path that still reaches the terrace, the Hypostyle and the dragon.
11. **Include a practical layer:** slot grace of 30 minutes, no re-entry, online-only tickets, unofficial look-alike ticket sites, wind-closure days, toilets (the three official locations), water and shade, the climb, the step-free route via Carretera del Carmel, no large luggage, and a warning that late slots near closing time may be dark.
12. **Languages:** EN, ES, FR and DE all from day one (the official app lacks DE). Note the repeated **Italian** demand for a later wave.

## 5. What visitors most want to know, and the common confusions on site

### 5.1 What they want (from FAQs, reviews and guides)
- **Is it free, and which part is paid?** Sources contradict each other (§1.4). Visitors are told different things by different sellers (Vox City FAQ "free to visit"; Barcelona Hacks "Free Zone"; official "12 hectares").
- **How long to spend:** "90 minutes for icons, 2 hours for Gaudí details, or 3+ hours for every terrace and trail" (SEC, https://guidebooks.headout.com/guides/park-guell-barcelona); the official FAQ gives no length.
- **The icons:** the bench terrace and its views ("All the best city views are concentrated at the square", SEC, https://barcelonahacks.com/is-park-guell-worth-it/), the dragon, the Hypostyle, the pavilions, and the Gaudí House Museum.
- **When to go:** the 9:30 slot and late slots are quieter. "by 10:30 the terrace rail is shoulder-to-shoulder" (Headout guidebook). The 10:00–13:00 slots sell out first (SEC, https://www.artvisitguide.com/park-guell-tickets-2026/).
- **How to get there without the climb:** bus 24 or V19 to the Carmel side is the least steep. The metro is 10–20 min uphill (SEC, https://eternalarrival.com/visiting-park-guell/; Headout entrances blog).
