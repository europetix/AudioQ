# AUDIT V5 EDITION 2: ROUTE and LISTENING, Park Güell EN (45 tracks + 21 bridges), 7 Nov 2026

I'm an independent auditor. I did not write this guide and I edited no track, bridge or plan. Navigation comes first, then how the guide sounds through a synthetic voice, then lengths. Facts are the facts audit's job. Where I noticed a fact as it is *heard*, I pass it on (N6).

**Read:** all 45 `v5/tracks/*.perf.txt`; the 4 bridge files; `v5/PLAN_V2.md` in full (§1 sections, route, ALT cues, conventions, audit resolution; §2 table; §3 aims, walking, counts, playing orders, bridges, version-neutral sentences, safe callbacks, one home per story; §4 NEVER; §6 DATED; §7 pronunciation; Lead's decisions); `v5/ONSITE_CHECKLIST.txt`; `v5/WRITER_BRIEF.md` (the @where rule); the writers' notes (`v5/notes/*`); `research/map/OFFICIAL_ROUTE_MAP_NOTES_7NOV2026.md` and the two route drawings (`official_routes_carrer_olot.png`, `official_routes_sant_josep.png`), which I looked at myself; `research/VISIT_ROUTE_AND_SPACES_NOV2026.md` (gates, viaducts); `research/AUDIT_V5_ROUTE_LISTENING.md` (edition-1 audit, "RA"). I also applied the lead's note: the "mushroom" roof is unresolved (E103), so no cue may use it to pick out a gatehouse.

**Method.**
- A script checked every cue's destination against the next track's @title, and every bridge's opening, key, word count and closing title.
- I walked Full, Essential and Highlights on the drawing, from each visitor gate, with every bridge in place.
- I counted words, sentence lengths, tags and repeated phrases on the clean text (tags stripped as `lint.py` does). `lint.py` passes all 45 tracks.

**Counts.** **MUST: 3** · **SHOULD: 21** (8 route, 13 listening) · **NICE: 18**.

**Verdict in one paragraph.** The route is sound. The A–I order matches the official drawing. The one-way warning (015) comes before the second check in all three versions. Extras are offered only with "If you're on the full tour". The 21 bridges are complete: every skip that changes the walk has a bridge, each one opens "On this shorter tour,", carries the right key and ends on the right title. No shorter version is told to play, or recall, a track it doesn't have. There is no compass word or distance in any cue, and every left/right is one PLAN tags [O-map]. Three things still need fixing before recording. The mushroom is still used to pick out the Casa del Guarda in on-screen fields and in a held ALT text (M1). The "no queue" case can put the one-way warning after the check (M2). 036 points at view landmarks that O7 hasn't ticked (M3). For listening, the guide has become monotonous in two ways: about 60 attributions to "the park" or "the museum", and a closing one-line aphorism in about 40 of the 44 tracks. Full now runs about 131 minutes against "about two hours".
