# Park Güell (Barcelona): verified fact base (as of 6 Nov 2026)

Yo Tours English audio guide v5. Scope per `USER_DECISIONS.md` (6 Nov 2026): the ticketed Monumental Zone, the nearest viaducts and the Portico of the Washerwoman, Casa Larrard and the Gaudí House Museum from outside, the Trias house, the Carmel hill context. The Turó de les Tres Creus calvary is a mention, not a stop. This file holds facts, stories, quotes and things to look at. **No prices or hours belong in scripts.** Route and on-site placement still need a route file (`VISIT_ROUTE_AND_SPACES_NOV2026.md`, not yet written).

## 0. Method and keys

- **Tools.** WebSearch and WebFetch only. One page at a time per site, no bursts. No curl, no browser, no CAPTCHA. All reads were done on 6 Nov 2026.
- **WebFetch caveat.** WebFetch returns a model summary, and this session capped verbatim excerpts at about 125 characters. So every "verbatim" quote is **as relayed**. **Re-check the exact wording against the page before quoting on air.** The original language is marked: (EN), (ES), (CA).
- **Grades.**
  - **OFF**: official. That means the park operator (parkguell.barcelona, run by Barcelona de Serveis Municipals, B:SM), the Ajuntament de Barcelona (barcelona.cat, incl. **MUHBA**, the city history museum that runs the Casa del Guarda), UNESCO, the Gaudí House Museum's own pages (sagradafamilia.org, which ran it until 2024), and the Càtedra Gaudí / UPC. **OFF-op** marks the operator's own visitor pages. They are official, and their texts are credited to Joan Roca i Albert (MUHBA director), Mireia Freixa (University of Barcelona) and Mar Leniz (architect) [O10], but they are short and contain slips (see §14).
  - **SEC**: a named secondary source read directly. That covers peer-reviewed papers (e.g. *Informes de la Construcción*), academy bulletins (Bassegoda in the RACBASJ *Butlletí*), Enciclopèdia Catalana, and reputable press.
  - **SNIP**: a search-result snippet only.
  - **WIKI**: used as a lead only, never as sole support.
- **Verdicts.**
  - **OK**: safe to state as written.
  - **HEDGE**: true in substance; use the safe wording.
  - **LEGEND**: tradition or tour lore; script only as "the story goes…".
  - **UNVERIFIED**: do not script.
  - **ERROR**: a claim found in circulation, or on an official page, that is wrong or contradicted.
  - **DATED**: a 2024–2026 fact that may change. Re-check before release.
- **Sources** are listed in §17 as [O…] (operator), [A…] (Ajuntament / MUHBA / public bodies) and [S…] (secondary).
- **Blocked or failed this session.** None was retried.
  - parkguell.barcelona/en and parkguell.barcelona/ (home pages): permission not answered. Deeper operator pages reached later through search-result links worked.
  - whc.unesco.org/en/list/320 (UNESCO): permission not answered. UNESCO facts below rest on the operator's World Heritage page [O1] and WIKI.
  - historia-hispanica.rah.es (DBE biography of Eusebi Güell): permission not answered.
  - barcelonaturisme.com Park Güell PDF: redirect to thisisbarcelona.com not followed.
  - apdnoticies.com ("Barcelona invests 4 million in renovating Park Güell… completion in 2027"): permission not answered. **That story is therefore UNVERIFIED** (see 3.30).
  - MUHBA PDF `positParckGuell_Angles_novembre12J.pdf` (English edition of Freixa & Leniz, *Park Güell and its origins*): permission not answered. **The key monograph was read only through two reviews and the publisher's blurb.**
