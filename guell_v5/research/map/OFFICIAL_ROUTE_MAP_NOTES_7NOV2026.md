The map is drawn looking up the hill from Carrer d'Olot (the street runs along the bottom right). "Left/right" below
= as seen standing on the esplanade inside the main gate, facing up the stairway. Lead's reading of the drawing (INF
from an official drawing; confirm on site):

1. **The pavilions (O2).** A "Entrance and concierge" sits at the pavilion with the tall blue-and-white chequered
   tower, beside the gate. B "The guardhouse" is the other pavilion, with the bulbous white roof cap (the "mushroom"),
   to the RIGHT of A facing the stairway. So the Casa del Guarda = the mushroom pavilion, on the right facing up the
   stairway; the tower pavilion on the left. (Matches the operator's "the one to the right… porter's lodge… today
   part of the Barcelona History Museum" if read from inside.)
2. **The flow (O1).** Both drawn routes, once at the gate, run: A/B → C up the stairway → D the hall's front (at the
   top of the stairway, on its left side) → E inside the hall → up the RIGHT side to F, the Austria Gardens (terraced
   gardens to the right of the stairway, above the guardhouse), looping up through the terraces → back left along an
   upper path to the square's right-hand corner → G round the Nature Square → out at the square's LEFT-hand corner →
   H along a path that runs above the long pink building (Casa Larrard, with the school playground in front) to the
   long stone colonnade along the left edge (the Portico of the Washerwoman) → I the ramp at the bottom-left →
   down past a small round bandstand → along the inside of the wall back to the gate (an exit marker beside A).
   **So the operator's own order is ours (A–I), the Portico is on the LEFT (west) side of the square, reached from the
   square's left-hand corner by the path above Casa Larrard, and the loop ends at the main gate.** The second control's
   exact spot is not drawn: most likely at the foot of the stairway (C), since the stairway, the hall, the gardens, the
   square and the Portico are the controlled spots.
3. **Entrances on the drawing.** Carrer Olot route: a blue entrance marker on the RIGHT, along the inside of the
   Carrer d'Olot wall beyond the guardhouse; visitors walk along the wall to B then A. Sant Josep route: entrance and
   exit markers at the BOTTOM-LEFT, by the round bandstand and the building with the slate spire; the path climbs past
   the bandstand to the esplanade, then A → B → C… and finally I → down to the same bottom-left corner.
4. **Not on the drawing:** the viaducts (Pont de Baix / del Mig / de Dalt), the Gaudí House Museum, Casa Trias, the
   Palm Trees Path beyond the square (the arcade along the top of the square is visible), the Font Sarva annex.
   Stop "H The roadways, paths and viaducts" is drawn on the Portico path.
5. **Consequences for PLAN:** O1, O2, O3 and O11 are now answered by the official drawing (subject to on-site
   confirmation): default order = A–I with the Portico reached from the square's left-hand corner; ALT-B, ALT-R, ALT-P
   can be retired to "on-site only"; "left/right" may be used where this note gives it, tagged [O-map]. The viaducts'
   place is still O5 (not drawn).
