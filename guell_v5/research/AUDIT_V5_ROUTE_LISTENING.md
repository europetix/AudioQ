# AUDIT V5: ROUTE and LISTENING, Park Güell EN (25 tracks + 8 bridges), 7 Nov 2026

I'm an independent auditor and did not write this guide. This audit covers four things:
- whether the guide can be walked as cued, given the official access rules;
- the three playing orders with their bridges;
- how each track sounds on one hearing through a synthetic voice;
- lengths and the times 001 speaks.

The facts audit is separate. Where I noticed a fact as it is *heard* (a misleading pronoun, a hedge that went missing), I flag it here for that audit. I edited no track.

**Method.**
- I read PLAN §1–§8 and the Lead's decisions, ONSITE_CHECKLIST, ROUTE (§0–§6), COMP §4–§5, and the Poble audit for format.
- I then read the lead's new note `research/map/OFFICIAL_ROUTE_MAP_NOTES_7NOV2026.md` (7 Nov). I checked its reading against the two route drawings myself (`official_routes_carrer_olot.png`, `official_routes_sant_josep.png`). I agree with it: the A–I flow is stairway → hall front (D, at the top on the left) → inside (E) → up the right-hand side to the Austria Gardens (F, terraces above the guardhouse) → back along an upper path to the square's right-hand corner → round the square (G) → out at its left-hand corner → the path above Casa Larrard (H) → the colonnade on the left edge → the ramp (I) → down past a small round bandstand → along the inside of the wall to the main gate.
- On the drawing the Casa del Guarda is the mushroom pavilion, on the right as you face the stairway. "Left/right" below is as the note defines it: standing on the esplanade, facing up the stairway. Cue texts that use it are tagged **[O-map]**.
- I checked every cue's last line against the next @title, and every bridge against its key and target, with a script. I played Full, Essential and Highlights from each of the three visitor gates.
- I counted words, sentence lengths, dates, tags and repeated phrases on the clean text (tags stripped by `v5_direction.validate`, the same way as `lint.py`). `lint.py` passes all 25.

**Counts.** MUST: 3 findings (6 track or bridge instances) · SHOULD: 25 (9 route, 16 listening) · NICE: 18. Section 6 answers the lead's new request: it lists the crowded tracks for the 45-track, 120-minute expansion.

## 2. Findings

### MUST

**M1. Extras offered to versions that don't contain them (003, 005, 013, 018).**

*Exact words:*
- 003 (H, E): *"If you go inside the Casa del Guarda, the caretaker's house with the mushroom on its roof, there's a short Extra to play at its door."*
- 005 (E): *"There's a short Extra a little higher up, where the Middle Viaduct comes into view, if the path is open."*
- 013 (H, E): *"There's a short Extra beside this hall, if its little room is open: the Font Sarva, an old spring Güell once bottled."*
- 018 (H, E): *"There's a short Extra you can play here, sitting on the bench: the words hidden in it, and how much of it is original."*

*Problem:*
- 004, 006, 014 and 019 are Full only (PLAN §3). H and E listeners are told to play a track that isn't on their phone.
- In 005, an E listener may climb "a little higher up" looking for it. That is a real detour on a steep path, toward the works closure.
- 018 then teases content ("how much of it is original") that they will never hear.
- 001 promised "every stop tells you where to go next". These lines are the first place that promise breaks.

*Replacements (word counts checked):*
- 003: *"On the full tour, if you go inside the Casa del Guarda, the house with the mushroom on its roof, play the short Extra at its door."* (27 words, was 26. 003 is at 359. S1 saves 1, so the track stays at 359.)
- 005: *"On the full tour, there's a short Extra a little higher up, where the Middle Viaduct comes into view, if the path is open."* (+4 words; 005 is at 359.) To pay for it, cut *"as the Gaudí scholar Joan Bassegoda tells it in Catalan"* to *"as Joan Bassegoda tells it"* (−5). This also helps L6.
- 013: *"On the full tour, there's a short Extra beside this hall, if its little room is open: the Font Sarva, a spring Güell bottled."* (+2 words; 013 ends at 351, or 359 with S3.)
- 018: *"On the full tour, there's a short Extra to play here on the bench, about the words hidden in it."* (20 words, was 24.)
- *Alternative, if the app can serve per-version last paragraphs:* move each Extra sentence into a **Full-only bridge** ("003 F>004" and so on), so H and E never hear it. Either way, ONSITE's tick list needs the new lines.

**M2. 001: listeners from Carmel and Sant Josep are sent toward the exit-only gate with no "stay inside"; the Marianao line has no landmark.**

*Exact words:* *"From the Marianao entrance, on the Carrer d'Olot side, the start is close by. From the Carretera del Carmel, or the escalators at Sant Josep de la Muntanya, follow the signs down to the main gate on Carrer d'Olot, without going through the second ticket check."*

*Problem:*
- The Carrer d'Olot gate is **exit only** (ACCESS, GOV20). Earlier in this same track the listener has heard it called "the way out". On site it will be signed as the exit.
- "Follow the signs down to the main gate" sends someone 5–10 minutes downhill toward a gate that lets them out, and there is no re-entry (FAQ, ACCREG 2.11). This is the exact "sent to the wrong place" failure we are fixing. The next paragraph's "just inside the main gate" comes too late to save someone who is walking and following exit signs.
- "Close by" is a distance word with no source (PLAN: distances are not allowed until ONSITE). It gives the Marianao listener nothing to walk by.
- The official drawing now supplies the landmark: the Carrer Olot route enters on the right and walks along the inside of the wall, past the mushroom pavilion, to the gate. On the Sant Josep route, the path comes down past a small round bandstand to the esplanade.

*Replacement* (52 words, was 46):
- *"From the Marianao entrance, on the Carrer d'Olot side, walk along the inside of the wall, past the mushroom-roofed gatehouse [O-map, O9]. From the Carretera del Carmel, or the Sant Josep de la Muntanya escalators, follow the signs down toward the main gate. Stay inside the park, and don't pass the second ticket check [O9]."*
- To pay for it, cut the first paragraph's *k* clause (see L5): *"Eusebi Güell wanted to recreate the British residential parks, and that's why it's Park, with a k."* → *"Eusebi Güell wanted to recreate the British residential parks."* (−8 words).
- 001 then runs to 356 words.
- Optional for Sant Josep, after O9: *"…down past a small round bandstand…"* [O-map].

**M3. 018 H>021 bridge: "skip the view for now" in a one-way section.**

*Exact words:* *"On this shorter tour, skip the view for now. Find the old iron door at the edge of the square and follow the path through the pines…"*

*Problem:*
- "For now" promises a return. On both official route drawings, the flow leaves the square at its far corner for the Portico and never comes back. Highlights listeners who wait for the view will miss it.
- The competitor research names exactly this trap: "once you go down to the terrace… you have 'left' the park" (COMP §4.1.5). Its fix is "do the terrace photos before going down" (COMP §4.2.2).

*Replacement* (40 words, was 42): *"On this shorter tour, look out over the bench now: you can't come back to the square. Then find the old iron door at its edge and follow the path through the pines: The Portico of the Washerwoman · The wave."* [O1, O3, O-map]
