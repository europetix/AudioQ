WHY THIS EDITION
================
Built from scratch to the Yo Tours standard. This guide:
1. Walks one loop in the park's own official order, stops A to I, through the one-way section: the gate and its two
   gatehouses, the Dragon Stairway, the Hypostyle Room outside and in, the Austria Gardens, the Nature Square, the path
   above Casa Larrard, the Portico of the Washerwoman, the ramp, and back along the wall to the Carrer d'Olot gate.
   It works whichever gate you come in by: the first track is heard anywhere just inside, and the guide tells you
   where the loop starts. Everything outside the one-way section comes first, because past the second ticket check
   there is no way back.
2. Takes in the viaducts (the Low Viaduct, its shelter and its leaning columns, and the Middle Viaduct in view)
   before the second check, and the Casa del Guarda floor by floor, inside, as three short Extras.
3. Credits Josep Maria Jujol wherever the colour is his: the suns on the hall's ceiling and the tiles of the bench.
   Legends are told as legends, once each, with who tells them.
4. Corrects the popular errors: the park was not a public park at first but a private housing estate that failed;
   Gaudí didn't design the pink house he lived in; the hall has 86 columns, not a hundred; the bench is about a hundred
   metres of broken tile, and no "longest bench" claim is made; Gaudí didn't invent trencadís; the cross on the
   gatehouse tower is a 1996 copy; the iron gates came from Casa Vicens. Gaudí, who died in 1926, is always in the past.
5. Checks every fact against official and scholarly sources (the park's own pages, the city history museum, the
   Gaudí scholarship; Nov 2026), with independent audits of the facts and of the route and listening.
6. No prices, hours or tickets. Headphones only: the park does not allow speakers.
7. Three lengths (one recording, three folders):
   Full tour   @@N_FULL@@ tracks, about 2 hours
   Essential   @@N_ESS@@ tracks, about 1 h 15:  @@ESSENTIAL@@
   Highlights  @@N_HI@@ tracks, about 45 min:   @@HIGHLIGHTS@@
   The times include walking and a short wait at the second ticket check. In the shorter versions a short spoken
   line at the end of a track tells you the next stop.
8. The Yo Tours sound: voice finishing, a soft room tone and a short chime before each track. MP3s are labelled for phones.

HOW TO MAKE THE AUDIO (on a Mac)
================================
English:          double-click generate_audio.command  (voice 1 = Ava)
                  option 1 = all three lengths (records once; folders _Essential and _Highlights)
                  option 2 = two voice samples (018 the dragon is a drain + 034 a hundred metres of broken tile)
                  option 3 = Highlights only (quickest; option 1 later reuses those recordings)
Other languages:  double-click generate_audio_ES_FR_DE.command and pick the language, then the same options.
Needs internet, python3, ffmpeg (brew install ffmpeg) and an up-to-date edge-tts:
  python3 -m pip install --user --upgrade edge-tts
If it stops, run it again: finished tracks are skipped.
