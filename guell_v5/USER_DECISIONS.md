# Park Güell (Barcelona) V5 — user decisions (6 Nov 2026)

- **Scope:** "Monumental + viaducts (Recommended)": the ticketed Monumental Zone plus the nearest viaducts and the
  Portico of the Washerwoman in the free park; the Gaudí House Museum gets one short outside stop (not its interior).
  Not the hilltop calvary (Turó de les Tres Creus) as a stop.
- **Lengths:** 45 / 75 / 120 min visit time including walking. Within this scope 120 may not be reachable honestly;
  if so, say so with an honest Full and the held items (Poble precedent: never pad).
- **Old guide / map:** "No, start fresh". No user map: read the official park plan ourselves.
- **Languages:** EN, ES, FR, DE, each with its own translator and an independent reviewer.
- **Standard (user, Guggenheim brief):** "kindly do not compromise on research and quality". Research ≥ 150 graded
  facts (official first; Wikipedia never alone), lead reads every track, two independent audits, fixer, honest lengths.
