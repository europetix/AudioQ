On Christmas Day 1970, a team of women played a charity match at the Camp Nou, against UE Centelles. They were not allowed to wear the club's colours. The Catalan encyclopedia explains why: the club did not recognise them. So they played as the Selecció Ciutat de Barcelona, a Barcelona city team.

Then a supporters' team in 1971. A club of its own in 1988. Part of FC Barcelona only in 2002. And fully professional only in 2015.

[warmly] Then the titles came. In 2021, in Gothenburg, they won the Champions League, four–nil against Chelsea. They won it again in 2023. In 2024, in Bilbao, they finally beat Lyon, two–nil, after losing to them four times before.

In May 2026, in Oslo, they won a fourth, four–nil against OL Lyonnes.

The crowds came too. On the thirtieth of March, 2022, more than ninety-one thousand people watched them play Real Madrid at the Camp Nou. Twenty-three days later, against Wolfsburg, even more came: ninety-one thousand, six hundred and forty-eight. Each was a world record for women's football at the time, in the club's words.

Alexia Putellas arrived at eighteen, in 2012, and left in 2026 as the club's record scorer. Aitana Bonmatí came up through the club's youth teams.

Between them, Alexia and Aitana won the Ballon d'Or five times as Barça players. In 2026, after leaving the club, Alexia won her third.

Now look at the four European trophies. [pause] The first of them came fifty years after that Christmas match.

[warmly] In Alexia's own words, as the club translates them: "All I want is for any girl who wants to play football always to have the chance to do so."

Now find the wall of shirts, a whole wall of them side by side: The wall of shirts · The shirt that paid to be worn.
