[curious] For more than a century, nobody's name went across the front of Barça's shirt. No company, no logo, nothing.

[pause] When a name finally went on, in September 2006, it belonged to a charity. It was UNICEF's, the United Nations children's agency. The UN called it the first time in the club's hundred-and-seven-year history that a logo had been featured on the shirt.

[wry] And here's the turn. It was Barça that paid UNICEF, not the other way round. The UN's news service reported it at the time: the club would give the charity money every year, for five years, for the right to carry its name on the chest. Most clubs sell that space. Barça gave it away, and paid on top.

In later years, commercial sponsors followed. The music company Spotify went on the men's and the women's shirts in 2022, and that summer its name went on the stadium too. Since then the stadium has been called Spotify Camp Nou, a deal the members' assembly approved.

The shirt deal now runs to 2030. The stadium's name is secured until 2034.

Now look along the wall of shirts. Find the oldest ones you can, with nothing at all on the front. Then look for the first with UNICEF's name across it, if it's here.

[warmly] A shirt kept clean for a century, then lent to a charity, at the club's own expense.
