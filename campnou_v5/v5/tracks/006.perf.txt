The club first thought of a museum in 1928. It opened fifty-six years later.

That first idea was the board's, in the club's bulletin: to put the trophies in order, and in one place. Nothing came of it. The museum finally opened on the twenty-fourth of September, 1984, the stadium's twenty-seventh birthday, in nine hundred and fifty square metres under the main stand.

It kept growing, to three thousand six hundred square metres by 2010. In its record year, 2016, nearly two million people came. For years, by the club's count, it was one of the most visited museums in Catalonia.

In 2023 the museum moved into the club's old ice rink, while the stadium is rebuilt. Over a million visitors came here in 2025.

A new museum is being finished inside the stadium.

[pause] The club itself is much older than any of this. It was founded in 1899, by a young Swiss called Joan Gamper.

From this hall, the trophies come first, then the club's history, then Messi's space, and finally the round room, where the exhibition ends. If there's a map in this hall, it shows the way.

[wry] So the museum you're standing in is itself waiting to move house.

Now follow the way into the exhibition. When you reach the wall of trophies, stop where you can see it whole: The wall of trophies · Where to start.
