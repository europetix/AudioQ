[warmly] Welcome. Before anything else, one honest word about today. This ticket doesn't take you inside the stadium, Spotify Camp Nou. There's no pitch, no stands and no dressing room on it. You'll see the stadium from outside, and from a viewpoint over the works.

What you do get is the club's museum, in a temporary home. The long building with the ridged roof, beside the Palau Blaugrana, the round arena, was the club's old ice rink. The museum moved in while the stadium is being rebuilt.

Inside are the trophies, the men's European Cups among them. There's the club's history, from a classified ad in a newspaper to the women's team that filled the stadium. There are Messi's golden balls, and a round room where the walls become a stadium. Then come the models of the stadium as it will be when it's finished, and the viewpoint.

The route runs like this. A short stop outside first. Then in, to the trophies, the history, Messi's space, the round room and its show, the models, the viewpoint, and out. On the shorter tours, some of these stops are skipped.

[pause] A few practical things, once each. Headphones, please. The club counts the visit at about an hour, and this guide stretches it, for those who want the stories. The show in the round room is not advised for people with epilepsy or photosensitivity, and the guide will say so again at its door. And the guide works offline.

The full tour takes about an hour and a half, the essential about an hour, and the highlights about thirty-five minutes, walking and the show in the round room included. Play the stops in order. The numbers on your phone may jump, but every stop tells you where to go next.

[lightly] One thought to carry in with you. This is a club that has moved house more than once.

Now walk onto the campus, away from the avenue, until you can see the stadium over the lower buildings: The campus · 909 days away.
