The club gives one warning for the room ahead. It says the show in this room is not recommended for people with epilepsy or photosensitivity. If that applies to you, the club asks you to tell the tour staff before you go in, so that they can guide you.

The show has its own sound, so this guide will step aside while it plays. Stop it before you go in, and start it again when the show ends.

[curious] So what is in there? A round room whose walls work as one single screen. That screen runs for seventy-eight metres all the way around the room, and it stands six metres tall. The club calls it the largest immersive room in a sports museum anywhere in Europe.

[lightly] The show is called Spotify Camp Nou Live. In the club's description, it puts you at the centre of the stadium.

When the show ends, press play again before you leave the room. Now go in when the staff let you, and stop the guide here: The round room · After the show: the first full house.
