[curious] The stadium in this model was never knocked down. Its bottom two tiers, the ones poured in the 1950s, were kept and renovated, with new seats in every row. Only the third tier, the top ring, was taken down and built again.

That is how the contractor, Limak, from Turkey, describes the job. The design comes from a Japanese firm, Nikken Sekkei, working with a Barcelona studio, b720 Fermín Vázquez.

When it's finished, the club expects about a hundred and five thousand seats.

[pause] The biggest change is overhead. The new roof is meant to cover every fan in the stadium, and it hangs from cables. There will be close to fifty thousand square metres of it, and the club's stadium site calls it one of the largest tensile structures in the world. On top, about eighteen thousand square metres of solar panels.

Then there's the skin. The new outside is inspired by the canopy the original architects designed for the stadium of 1957. The old canopy itself is going. Between the second and third tiers runs a double ring of boxes for guests, and around the whole thing, up to twenty-five hectares of gardens with no cars.

The museum's next home is planned inside it too, in the main stand, facing the campus.

On the model, find three things: the roof on its cables, the new top ring, and the skin wrapped round the outside.
