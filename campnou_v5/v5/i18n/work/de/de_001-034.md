Claret = "granatrot". Under-14s / under-19s = "U14 / U19". Scores are words ("vier zu null", "elf zu eins"). Dates use word ordinals plus digit years ("am zweiundzwanzigsten November 2025"), so Katja can't misread "22.". Big numbers are words in speech. ## Possible problems in the English (reported, not fixed)
1. 030 "Just over sixty-eight per cent chose to make the nickname official": PLAN §4 NEVER lists "'80.8 per cent' or any referendum percentage (F18.22)". This may only target the 2021 vote, but the wording says "any". Lead to confirm. Translated as written ("Etwas mehr als achtundsechzig Prozent", with no % symbol).
2. 011 "Between them, Alexia and Aitana won the Ballon d'Or five times as Barça players. In 2026, Alexia won her third." The two sentences sit side by side, and she left the club in 2026. PLAN §4 bans implying her 2026 award was "won as a Barça player" (F18.10). The English doesn't say it, but it can be heard that way. Translated as written, in separate sentences.
3. 001 says "a little over an hour and a half" for the full tour, while the lead's decision 1 wording is "about an hour and a half". Translated as the final English ("etwas über anderthalb Stunden").
4. 010 has the ambiguous "His last game…" referent (see above).
5. 007 "more than a hundred football trophies on display, twenty-five from the women's game, and about fifty from the club's other sports": it is unclear whether the 25 are inside the 100. Kept just as ambiguous.
