## Choices and ambiguities in the English
- 022: "The Ballon d'Or, the golden ball," has no gloss in FR, because the French name already means golden ball. Nothing else was dropped.
- 007: "more than a hundred football trophies on display, twenty-five from the women's game…": it is unclear whether the 25 are part of the 100. The FR keeps the same ambiguity.
- 027: "Nobody owns Barça. Its socis, the members, do." is rendered "Personne ne possède le Barça. Ses socis, les membres, si."
- 026: "never knocked down" is rendered "n'a jamais été rasé", to keep away from "démoli" (PLAN bans "demolition").
- 029: "Pope John Paul the Second" is written "Jean-Paul II". Check that Vivienne says "deux".
- 024 bridges are conditional ("quand le spectacle est terminé"). The exit's fixed name "La sortie" is never used for the way out of the round room ("le chemin qui sort de la salle").
- "When you're ready" (028 bridge, 029, 033) is rendered "quand vous le souhaitez", to avoid the masculine "prêt".
- Narrative tense is passé simple and passé composé. Dead people are always in the past. Living people and the club are in the present.
- The word limit (≤ English) made French very tight. 003, 007, 020, 025, 029, 030 and 032 are exactly at the English word count. All meaning, hedges and attributions are kept. The cuts were made in syntax only.
