FC BARCELONA MUSEUM AT SPOTIFY CAMP NOU V5: ON-SITE CHECKLIST (from v5/PLAN.md, 14 Nov 2026; 34 tracks)
==========================================================================================================
Item numbers O1-O13 keep the meaning given in research/VISIT_ROUTE_AND_SPACES_NOV2026.md §5; O14-O22 are new
(PLAN.md §5). Track numbers below are PLAN.md's. ALT names (ALT-O1 ... ALT-O16/O19) are PLAN.md §1 "ALT cues".

One walk of the FULL route, in PLAN.md order, on the Museum ticket ("Museum + Digital Audio Guide" + "Construction
Viewpoint"), before recording, with a phone camera and a stopwatch.
- Photograph every sign, label, map board, arrow and display you are asked about. Save them to research/onsite/ as
  Oxx_<what>.jpg (e.g. O19_golden_balls_label.jpg).
- Note times with the stopwatch: WALKING, QUEUING AND THE SHOW ONLY, not listening.
- Report each item as CONFIRMED / DIFFERENT (what you saw) / NOT FOUND.
- Never note directions as compass points. Use LANDMARKS: Av. de Joan XXIII, the round Palau Blaugrana, the long
  building with the ridged roof (the museum), the museum's door, the displays by what is on them (the wall of
  trophies, the European Cups, the women's trophies, the wall of shirts, Cruyff, La Masia, Messi's golden balls),
  the round room with the screens, the models, the viewpoint's rail, the old benches, the exit. Left/right only as
  "as you come in through that door", and say which door.
- Never step behind a barrier, into a closed area or onto the works; never photograph inside the 360° show if a
  sign forbids it; follow staff instructions. Do not try the RoboKeeper or VR (not on this ticket; not in the guide).
- Never complete a CAPTCHA or create an account. Nothing about prices, hours, slots or tickets goes into the guide;
  note them only if they change the route.
- Do not leave and re-enter unless staff say re-entry is allowed: walk the outside items first, then go in.
- Note gate numbers and door numbers you see (O1), but remember: no gate number is spoken until the lead approves
  (PLAN §8.9).
- If you see the stadium works (cranes, screens, fences), note them, but never write them into a cue.
