## Binding design decisions
1. **Honesty first (001).** This ticket does not take you into the stadium: no pitch, no stands, no dressing room. You
   see the stadium from outside and from the Construction Viewpoint. The museum is a temporary one, in the club's old
   ice rink, while the stadium is rebuilt. Said warmly, once, in the first minute, with what the visit gives instead.
2. **Topic-keyed, not room-keyed (the museum may move).** The club aims to open a new museum inside the stadium (Nov
   2026 target, not confirmed on 14 Nov). Every story track must survive a move: its body talks about the story and
   the object (the trophy, the shirt, the Ballons d'Or), never "this room", "the ice rink around you" except in the
   tracks that are about the building (001, the entrance track, the viewpoint). Only @where, the first look-line and
   the cue paragraph depend on the current layout. The planner marks each track ROUTE-LAYER lines so a move means
   rewriting cues, not stories. PLAN states the move as O2: if the new museum opens before recording, stop and re-plan
   the route layer.
3. **Room order unknown (O3).** The official list order is Trophies → Barça History → Messi Zone → 360° room (the
   exhibition "finishes" there) → Espai Barça models → Construction Viewpoint (last). Press 2023 differs. Cues use
   **displays, not rooms**: "the wall of trophies", "the big silver European Cups", "the wall of shirts", "the golden
   balls in Messi's space", "the round room with the screens". Where order is uncertain, the cue says "when you reach
   …" and the listener plays the track then. Tracks inside the history and trophy sections must work in any order
   among themselves (no "as we just heard" between them). Callbacks only to 001 and the entrance track.
4. **"More stories inside" (user decision).** The extra ≈ 30 minutes are story tracks played inside, standing or
   sitting, that need **no view until their last paragraph**: the founding, the colours, culers, 1925, Sunyol, 1943,
   Kubala, the anthem, Maradona, the members and the shirt, Ronaldinho, the women's crowds. They are announced only as
   "If you're on the full tour, …" and placed where there is room: a bench, a quieter corner, the old stadium benches
   at the viewpoint. Each opens by saying it can be heard anywhere ("you can listen to this one standing anywhere
   quiet"), never more than once per track and never as a formula repeated word for word.
5. **The 360° room.** A track before it gives the club's own warning (not advised for epilepsy or photosensitivity;
   tell staff) and says the show has its own sound: stop the guide during the show. A short track after it. Never
   describe what the show contains beyond the official description (it may have changed; O11).
6. **The stadium as it is (Nov 2026, DATED).** Back in use since 22 Nov 2025; ≈ 62,000 seats since March 2026; third
   tier and roof unfinished; the compression ring closed Oct 2026; roof works from summer 2027 with one more exile to
   Montjuïc; 105,000 when finished. Every one of these is a separate cuttable sentence. Never "demolition site".
   Never "the biggest stadium in the world". "Camp Nou", never "Nou Camp".
7. **Legacy thread.** Each era track ends on what it left behind (FACTS §22), not on a motto: Gamper's "cannot die";
   Sunyol's "sport and citizenship"; Kubala's crowds and the stadium; Cruyff's way of playing; La Masia; the women's
   team; the members. Payoff lines in at most a third of tracks; no two tracks end on the same idea.
8. **Even-handed politics.** 1925, Sunyol (1936), 1943 (11–1), Di Stéfano (only if GAPS gives both clubs' accounts),
   "Més que un club", 1 Oct 2017: facts first, both readings attributed, no side taken, no independence comment, no
   "regime's club" as fact. Real Madrid named with respect (our Bernabéu guide sells to the same people). The 1943
   threat is "never proven" per the historian; Madrid's press read it differently: say both.
9. **Legends as legends, with who tells them:** the colours (Basel vs Merchant Taylors'), culers (the club's own
   story, doubted), the napkin (real, but not in the museum: sold at auction in 2024), "Go out and have fun" (the
   statue's words; the Wembley scene as told, attributed).
10. **Old-guide errors never repeated** (AUDIT §3): napkin in the museum; eight Ballons d'Or won at Barça; No 10 unworn;
    Messi debut at Camp Nou; 2017 hat-trick; Sunyol exhumed or a monument here; eleven founders / Gamper president;
    five lost finals; Cruyff's volley or the Ronaldinho and Messi Bernabéu moments placed at Camp Nou; Maradona's
    career ended by illness; "two hours"; "stadium closed"; Vázquez Montalbán's "un sentiment" line; statues or a
    Messi statue (none exists).
11. **Statues** (Kubala, Cruyff): position unknown in Nov 2026 (O9). A statue may be mentioned only as "a statue of
    him stood at the main stand's entrance from 2019" (DATED, cuttable); never cue to it.
12. **Directions:** landmarks only: Av. Joan XXIII, the round Palau Blaugrana, the long low building beside it (the old
    ice rink), the museum signs, the displays named above, "the doors marked for the viewpoint" (O4), the old benches.
    No gate number spoken until O1 (the campus map and the Oct 2026 access page both put the museum at Access 9; the
    2023 practical page says 15: keep "follow the signs for the Museum" and name the street + the round arena).
    Never "the Masia is over there" (it is on the far side of the stadium).
13. **Practical lines once, in 001:** headphones; the visit is about an hour on the club's own count, this guide
    stretches it for those who want stories; the 360° warning is repeated at the room; offline. No prices, hours,
    ticket types, RoboKeeper/VR (not on this ticket: never describe), the shop, toilets, Wi-Fi.
14. **Numbers and records** are DATED where they can change (trophy totals, Ballons d'Or count, Lamine Yamal, the women's
    totals, attendance records). Messi: 672 goals; six Ballons d'Or as a Barça player, eight in all.
15. **Lengths:** Full ≈ 90 min honestly (≈ 34 tracks × ~1.9 min ≈ 65 min of listening + the 360° show + walking +
    viewpoint time), Essential ≈ 60 (≈ 20 tracks), Highlights ≈ 30 (≈ 10). If Full can't be 90 honestly, say so.
