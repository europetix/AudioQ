FIRST, PLAINLY: THIS TICKET DOES NOT INCLUDE THE STADIUM
========================================================
This guide is for the FC Barcelona Museum ticket. It does not take you into the stadium: no pitch, no stands, no
dressing room. You see the stadium from outside and from the museum's viewpoint over the works. The first track
says so too.

WHY THIS EDITION
================
Built from scratch to the Yo Tours standard. This guide:
1. Is about the FC Barcelona Museum, in its temporary home in the club's old ice rink (the long building with the
   ridged roof beside the round Palau Blaugrana, on Avinguda de Joan XXIII), while the stadium is rebuilt.
2. Walks one route, with directions at every step: outside by the museum and onto the campus with the stadium in
   view, in through the entrance hall, the trophies (the wall of trophies, the five European Cups, the women's
   trophies, the wall of shirts), the club's history (Cruyff, La Masia, Més que un club), a quiet corner, Messi's
   space, the round room and its show, the models of the finished stadium, the viewpoint and the old benches, and
   the way out.
3. Follows the displays, not room numbers: the order of the rooms may change, so every track says what to look for
   ("when you reach ...", "find ..."), and the trophy and history stops work in any order among themselves.
4. Stops for the show: in the round room the guide asks you to stop it while the show plays, repeats the club's own
   warning for people with epilepsy or photosensitivity, and tells you what to do when the show is over.
5. Covers the club's legacy and its best stories: Gamper and the ad, the colours, the European Cups from Wembley to
   Berlin, the women's team, the shirt that paid to be worn, Cruyff, La Masia, Kubala, Messi and the napkin, the
   members and why nobody owns Barça, and the stadium's great nights.
6. Tells legends as legends, with who tells them (the colours, culers, the napkin, "go out and enjoy it"), and the
   contested history even-handedly, with both sides attributed.
7. Checks every fact against official and press sources (the club's own pages first; Nov 2026), with independent
   audits of the facts and of the route and listening. Numbers that change are dated in the scripts.
8. Works offline once the MP3s are on your phone. Headphones, please. No prices, hours or tickets in the guide.
9. Three lengths (one recording, three folders):
   Full tour   @@N_FULL@@ tracks, a little over 1 h 30
   Essential   @@N_ESS@@ tracks, about 1 h:      @@ESSENTIAL@@
   Highlights  @@N_HI@@ tracks, about 35 min:    @@HIGHLIGHTS@@
   The times include walking and the show in the round room; a wait for the next show adds more. The ten Extras
   (Full tour only) are heard standing or sitting, by the museum's door, in a quiet corner and on the old benches.
   In the shorter versions a short spoken line at the end of a track tells you the next stop.
10. The Yo Tours sound: voice finishing, a soft room tone and a short chime before each track. MP3s are labelled for
    phones.

HOW TO MAKE THE AUDIO (on a Mac)
================================
English:          double-click generate_audio.command  (voice 1 = Ava)
                  option 1 = all three lengths (records once; folders _Essential and _Highlights)
                  option 2 = two voice samples (013 A name the registrar refused + 029 Where the players sat)
                  option 3 = Highlights only (quickest; option 1 later reuses those recordings)
Other languages:  generate_audio_ES_FR_DE.command (not yet: the translations are not in this bundle).
Needs internet, python3, ffmpeg (brew install ffmpeg) and an up-to-date edge-tts:
  python3 -m pip install --user --upgrade edge-tts
If it stops, run it again: finished tracks are skipped.

ON-SITE CHECKS BEFORE RECORDING
===============================
See ONSITE_CHECKLIST.txt: whether the museum is still in the old ice rink (the club plans a new museum inside the
stadium), the way in and the museum's door, the order and names of the displays, the European Cups and the golden
balls, a quiet corner inside, the round room and its show, the models, the viewpoint and its old benches, the way
out, a stopwatch walk for the times, a tick list for every track's directions and every bridge, and the listening
checks for names.
