[curious] In 2009, Barça played for six trophies and won all six. No team had done that before, the club points out.

The story starts three years earlier, in Paris. In the 2006 final, against Arsenal, Barça were a goal down with a quarter of an hour to go. Samuel Eto'o equalised. Then a full-back, Juliano Belletti, scored from Henrik Larsson's pass. Two–one, and the club's second European Cup, under the coach Frank Rijkaard.

In 2008 the club gave the first team to a former player who was coaching its reserves, Pep Guardiola. [wry] His first season began with a defeat at Numancia and a draw with Racing Santander. It didn't stay that way.

[pause] The night that saved the year came in May 2009, at Stamford Bridge, in the semi-final against Chelsea. Barça needed a goal to reach the final. In the ninety-third minute, Andrés Iniesta scored it.

Then the trophies came one after another. The Spanish cup. The league, with a six–two win at the Bernabéu along the way. Then the final in Rome, two–nil against Manchester United, with goals from Eto'o and Messi, and the first treble by a Spanish club. That summer came the two Super Cups, Spain's and Europe's. And in December, in Abu Dhabi, the Club World Cup, won in extra time by a goal Messi scored with his chest.

Now look at the cups from Paris and Rome. The one from Rome was won by a coach who, a year before, had been running the club's reserves.
