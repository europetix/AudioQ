The team has been playing here again since November 2025, and the builders never left.

Start low. The two lower tiers in front of you are the 1957 bowl, kept and renovated. Above them is the rebuilt third tier: built, but not yet open to fans.

Now look up to the very top, at the ring that runs all the way round. That ring will hold the roof's cables. Its last piece was fitted in October 2026. Ara, the Catalan paper, puts its length at eight hundred and thirty metres.

Here's the part nobody planned for. When the builders opened up the old second tier, its concrete needed more than three thousand reinforcement jobs. The plan had allowed for about eight hundred. That's nearly four times as many.

Today the stadium holds about sixty-two thousand people.

From the summer of 2027, the roof goes on. The club's treasurer said, in Catalan, that when the last match ends in May, they will start installing the roof. It is to be put together on the pitch, then lifted into place on cables. So the team will leave home again for some months, for Montjuïc, the Olympic stadium on the hill. When they come back for good, the club expects it to hold about a hundred and five thousand.

Take one more look along the tiers: the old bowl low down, the new ring above it, and open sky where the roof will be.
