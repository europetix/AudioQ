Counting the trophies at this club takes a while. In 2024 the museum listed more than a hundred football trophies on display, twenty-five from the women's game, and about fifty from the club's other sports.

Here are the big totals, as they stand today. The men's team has won the Spanish league twenty-nine times. It has won the Spanish cup thirty-two times, the first in 1910. It has won Europe's biggest club prize five times.

The newest arrivals came in May 2026: the trophies for that season's league and Super Cup. It was the second league title in a row. That league was won with a two–nil home win over Real Madrid, the first league, the club says, that it had ever clinched against them. See if you can spot the pair.

Before you go in close, stand back and work out how the wall is laid out, by competition or by year. It makes the rest easier to read. And not everything that shines in this collection was won with a football.

Now find the big silver European Cups, the tall trophies with the big handles, and stop where you can see them: The European Cups · Third time lucky.
