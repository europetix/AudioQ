Messi's first official game for Barça wasn't at the Camp Nou at all. It was a city derby against Espanyol, at the Olympic stadium on Montjuïc, on the sixteenth of October 2004. Barça won one–nil, and he was seventeen. The year before, at sixteen, he had already played a friendly against Porto, on the night they opened their new stadium.

Two and a half years later came the goal people still compare to Maradona's. It was April 2007, at the Camp Nou, in a cup semi-final against Getafe. He took a pass from Xavi in his own half, ran past five players and scored. The club's report called it Maradona-esque.

In the 2011–12 season alone, he scored forty-six goals at the Camp Nou.

He stayed until 2021. That summer, club and player had agreed a new contract. Then, on the fifth of August, the club said the deal "cannot be formalised due to economic and structural obstacles". Three days later, at his farewell, he was in tears before he could begin. Soon afterwards he joined Paris Saint-Germain.

He left his shirt number behind. The number ten passed first to Ansu Fati.

Since July 2025 it has belonged to Lamine Yamal.

If his shirts are on show here, look for the ten on the back. It was handed on, and it is still being worn.
