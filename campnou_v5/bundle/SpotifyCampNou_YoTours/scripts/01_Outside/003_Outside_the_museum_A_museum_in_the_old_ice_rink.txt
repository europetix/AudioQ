Before this building held trophies, it held ice. The long building with the ridged roof is the Palau de Gel, the club's old ice rink. It opened in 1971, for figure skating, ice hockey and anyone in the city who fancied a turn on the ice. So you're about to walk in where people skated for half a century.

When the rink closed, the museum moved in. Its old home, under the stadium's main stand, shut on the old stadium's very last day, in May 2023. Ten days later this one opened, with about two thousand four hundred square metres to fill. It was meant to be here for a season or two. Temporary is a flexible word.

Beside the museum is the Palau Blaugrana, the round arena. It opened in the same year as the rink, and it's the home of the club's professional basketball, handball, roller hockey and futsal teams.

So the trophies inside are mostly football's, but football is not the only sport this club plays.
