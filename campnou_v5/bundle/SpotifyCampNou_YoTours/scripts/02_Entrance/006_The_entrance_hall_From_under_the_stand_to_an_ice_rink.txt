The club first thought of a museum in 1928. It opened fifty-six years later.

That first idea was the board's, in the club's bulletin: to put the trophies in order, and in one place. Nothing came of it. The museum finally opened on the twenty-fourth of September, 1984, the stadium's twenty-seventh birthday, in a space under the main stand.

It kept growing, to almost four times its first size by 2010. In its record year, 2016, nearly two million people came. For years, by the club's count, it was one of the most visited museums in Catalonia.

Since June 2023 it has been here. Over a million visitors came in 2025.
