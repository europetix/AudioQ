# Spotify Camp Nou / Barça Museum V5 — user decisions (14 Nov 2026)

- **Scope:** "Museum ticket (Recommended)": the FC Barcelona Museum, the construction viewpoint and the outside of the
  stadium as reached on that ticket. Not the pricier stadium-panorama, pitch-walk or match-day products. Research
  confirms exactly what the Museum ticket covers in Nov 2026 (the stadium is still partly a building site).
- **Lengths:** 30 / 60 / 90 min visit time including walking; ≈ 32–36 tracks for the 90-minute full tour (the user's
  expectation of many real stories), never padded; honest length stated if different.
- **Old guide / map:** "No, start fresh". Find the official museum plan ourselves. The Bernabéu guide
  (bernabeu_v5) is the structural model for a stadium guide.
- **Languages:** EN, ES, FR, DE, each with its own translator and an independent reviewer.
- **Standard:** "do not compromise on research and quality": ≥ 200 graded facts (official first, Wikipedia never
  alone), official plan read, lead reads every track, two independent audits, fixer, honest lengths.
- **Context (14 Nov check):** FC Barcelona's ticket page lists every Museum ticket as "Museum + Digital Audio Guide"
  with a "Construction Viewpoint"; ours must add what that lacks. Football club history is partly contested ground
  (politics, Franco era, Catalan identity, rivalries): even-handed, attributed, no club partisanship.
