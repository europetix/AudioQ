**M1. 008: Alexanko came on after Koeman's goal, not before it.**
- **Track text:** "Ninety minutes went by without a goal. Pep Guardiola was in midfield. The captain, Alexanko, came on for extra time, and still nothing. Then, with penalties not far off, Barça won a free kick. Ronald Koeman hit it past the goalkeeper, Pagliuca…"
- **Sources:**
  - The club's player feature on Alexanko (https://www.fcbarcelona.es/es/noticias/763941/uno-de-los-nuestros-jose-ramon-alexanko, read today): "El capitán de aquel equipo disputó el encuentro ante la Sampdoria al sustituir Guardiola en el minuto 113."
  - The club's Wembley card (https://www.fcbarcelona.es/es/ficha/2982808/la-primera-champions-del-barca-wembley-1992, read today): "Koeman, en la segunda parte de la prórroga (minuto 112), envió la pelota al fondo de la portería"; "ya a la prórroga, el capitán Alexanko" (no minute).
  - The club's "Moments in history" page (https://fcbarcelona.com/en/first-champions-league, read today): "Koeman's 112th minute free kick decided the game"; "in extra time captain Alexanko also came on."
  - The Dream Team card ([GO13]) gives 111 for the goal; either way the goal comes before 113.
- **Problem.** The writer read "ya a la prórroga" as "at the start of extra time" and built a sequence on it: Alexanko on, "still nothing", then the free kick. The club's own pages put him on in the 113th minute, after the goal, replacing Guardiola. The sentence is wrong as spoken, and it is in the Highlights version.
- **Replace with:** "Ninety minutes went by without a goal, and most of extra time too. Then, with penalties not far off, Barça won a free kick. Ronald Koeman hit it past the goalkeeper, Pagliuca. Pep Guardiola, in midfield that night, made way soon after for the captain, Alexanko. And the club had its first European Cup, in its ninety-third year."
  - Shorter: delete "The captain, Alexanko, came on for extra time, and still nothing." and write "Ninety minutes went by without a goal, and extra time looked like ending the same way. Pep Guardiola was in midfield. Then, with penalties not far off…". The track is 270 words against an aim of 290, so either fits.
  - No minute is spoken either way (111 vs 112 conflict, G44).

**S1. 004: "Swiss, English, German and Catalan" leaves out one founder.**
- **Track text:** "On the 29th of November 1899, twelve men met in the Solé gymnasium, just off the Rambla. They were Swiss, English, German and Catalan."
- **Source:** The club's 125-years page for the ad (https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/club/news/4153324/125-years-since-the-ad-in-los-deportes, read today) lists the twelve: "Catalans Lluís d'Ossó, Bartomeu Terrades, Pere Cabot, Carles Pujol, and Josep Llobet; **Enric Ducay from Aragon**; Otto Kunzle from Switzerland; John and William Parsons from England; and Otto Maier from Germany", with Gamper and Wild (Swiss).
- **Problem.** The brief singled out the founders' nationalities. FACTS 1.8's safe wording omits Ducay, and the club's own list does not. "Five nationalities" stays banned (AU §3.7), so the fix should not count nations.
- **Replace with:** "They were Swiss, English and German, and the rest were from Spain, most of them Catalan."
  - Or: "Three were Swiss, two English and one German. The rest were local, most of them Catalan."

**S2. 011: "fifty-one years after that Christmas match" is fifty.**
- **Track text:** "The first of them came fifty-one years after that Christmas match."
- **Sources:** Christmas Day 1970 (GEC, F13.1); Gothenburg, 16 May 2021 (club, https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/news/2146016, via the writer; F13.4).
- **Problem.** 2021 − 1970 = 51 is a subtraction of years, but 25 Dec 1970 → 16 May 2021 is 50 years and 5 months. The writer's note gives the arithmetic as "1970 → 2021".
- **Replace with:** "The first of them came fifty years after that Christmas match."
  - Or: "…half a century after that Christmas match."

**S3. 020: Barau's "nobody has ever been able to prove" is about a different moment.**
- **Track text:** "Before kick-off, they said, Spain's director of state security came into their dressing room and reminded them who allowed them to play. A historian, Joan Barau, told Goal that Barcelona 'were overcome by military pressure'. But he also says that the dressing-room story is one nobody has ever been able to prove."
- **Source:** Goal (https://www.goal.com/en/news/they-were-overcome-by-military-pressure-did-general-franco/6hicxub57kla1nn29zr34jiqe, read today). Barau: "That's a story that nobody has ever been able to prove and there is not a single member of the Barcelona team left now." In the article it refers to the legend that the director of state security **threatened the players into going back out for the second half**, not to the pre-kick-off visit the track has just described.
  - The pre-kick-off visit and its words are the Guardian writer's (https://www.inkl.com/news/the-joy-of-six-real-madrid-v-barcelona-el-clasico-classics, read today: he "visited their dressing room just before kick-off" and said "you are only playing because of the generosity of the regime…"). So "reminded them who allowed them to play" is a fair paraphrase.
- **Problem.** The track attaches Barau's caveat to the wrong scene. Writer D caught the same kind of slip in PLAN's Guardian line ("some Madridistas question…", which is about the gun) and then made a version of it here.
- **Replace with** (G3, Catalan News, SEC, already in GAPS): "…'were overcome by military pressure'. But a later account in Catalan News says it is impossible to prove whether explicit threats were made. Barça finished the game with ten men."
  - Or keep Barau with the right scene: "But he also says that one part of the legend, that the players were threatened at half time into going back out, is a story nobody has ever been able to prove."

**S4. 012: "No company, no logo, nothing" for "more than a century" is too absolute.**
- **Track text:** "For more than a century, nobody's name went across the front of Barça's shirt. No company, no logo, nothing."
- **Sources:**
  - UN News (F14.7 [P3]): "the first time in the club's 107-year history that a logo has been featured". The track attributes that sentence to the UN, correctly.
  - FACTS 14.7 itself says "no **commercial** logo". The club's kit makers had their marks on the shirt for years before 2006: Ara's kit history (https://interactius.ara.cat/shirts-kits-fcbarcelona-history, read today) lists Meyba (1979–92), Kappa (1992–98) and Nike (from 1998). Ara does not say where their marks sat, and the one page that might have (ESPN Deportes, 125 years of kits) came back empty, so the placement is the auditor's knowledge, **not verified on a page today**.
- **Problem.** The second sentence is ours, not the UN's. Kit-maker marks on the chest from the 1980s onwards will be on the wall of shirts the listener is looking at (O18), so the line can be contradicted in front of them.
- **Replace with:** "For more than a century, no sponsor's name went across the front of Barça's shirt."
  - Delete "No company, no logo, nothing." (−5 words). The look line "Find the oldest ones you can, with nothing at all on the front" stays: it is true of the oldest shirts.

**S5. 007: "Twenty-five of them" reads the source as a subset, but it lists three separate groups.**
- **Track text:** "In 2024 the museum listed more than a hundred football trophies on display. Twenty-five of them came from the women's game. And about fifty more came from the club's other sports."
- **Source:** The museum's 40th-anniversary page (https://www.fcbarcelona.es/es/noticias/4128584/cuarenta-anos-de-historia-del-museo-del-fc-barcelona/amp, Sept 2024, via the writer; F16.9): "trofeos expuestos (más de 100 de fútbol, 25 de fútbol femenino y 50 de secciones)".
- **Problem.** The three figures are parallel categories (football, women's football, the sections), so the 25 are most likely in addition to the 100, as the 50 are. The track makes the 25 part of the 100 but the 50 extra.
- **Replace with:** "In 2024 the museum listed more than a hundred football trophies on display, twenty-five from the women's game, and about fifty from the club's other sports."
