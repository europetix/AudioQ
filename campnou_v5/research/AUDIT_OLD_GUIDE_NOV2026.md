## Grading key

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **OK** | Confirmed by official fcbarcelona.com, or by two independent reputable sources. |
| **WRONG** | Contradicted by sources. The correct fact and its source are given. |
| **UNVERIFIED** | Could not be confirmed (invented-sounding quotes, insider colour, single-source numbers). Do not narrate as fact. |
| **DATED** | True when written (or once true) but stale or about to go stale by Nov 2026. Narrate with a date or keep out of the audio. |
| **OPINION** | A judgement or rhetorical line, not a checkable fact. Usable only as clearly framed opinion. |
| **PRACTICAL** | Route, hours, rules, tips. Never reuse without a fresh official check. |

"Use" column: **keep** (as is), **fix** (keep the story, correct the fact), **drop**.
Source IDs [S#] point to the source list at the end.
