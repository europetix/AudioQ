## Grading key

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **OK** | Confirmed by official fcbarcelona.com, or by two independent reputable sources. |
| **WRONG** | Contradicted by sources. The correct fact and its source are given. |
| **UNVERIFIED** | Could not be confirmed (invented-sounding quotes, insider colour, single-source numbers). Do not narrate as fact. |
| **DATED** | True when written (or once true) but stale or about to go stale by Nov 2026. Narrate with a date or keep out of the audio. |
| **OPINION** | A judgement or rhetorical line, not a checkable fact. Usable only as clearly framed opinion. |
| **PRACTICAL** | Route, hours, rules, tips. Never reuse without a fresh official check. |
| **PARTLY** | Part right, part wrong or imprecise. Keep the story and fix the detail given. |

"Use" column: **keep** (as is), **fix** (keep the story, correct the fact), **drop**.
Source IDs [S#] point to the source list at the end.
Grades marked "(not re-fetched)" are standard football records (finals, scores, honours) not separately fetched in this audit. Spot-check them before recording. "(title)" means a search-result headline only, not a full fetch.

**Verdict.** The old guide holds many of the right stories: Gamper, the motto, Sunyol, Wembley, Cruyff and Jordi, La Masia, the napkin, Messi and the Cant del Barça. Their details are often wrong, though, and its 2026 layer is out of date. It cannot be republished with light fixes:

- About 50 outright errors, several of them repeated three times (the napkin in the museum, the Sunyol monument).
- Route promises that are now false: "stadium closed", "two hours", "viewpoint gone in a year".
- One invented-sounding epigraph (the Vázquez Montalbán line) used as the cover line and the closing line.

Rebuild it on the official Museum route (exhibition → SPOTIFY CAMP NOU LIVE 360º → Espai Barça exhibition → Construction Viewpoint). Reuse the verified stories listed in section 3, and put every date-sensitive line in a separately versioned "current season / works status" track.

## 1. Route reality check (as of 14 November 2026, DATED)

### What the Museum ticket covers now (official)

- **Product.** "Museum" (from €28): "Museum + Digital Audio Guide" plus "Construction Viewpoint". "Museum Flexible" (€38) and "Total Experience Museum" (€52, adds Robokeeper and extras) cover the same spaces [S1].
- **Building.** The museum is still the **Barça Immersive Tour**, a temporary museum opened on 7 June 2023 in the building that formerly housed the **Palau de Gel ice rink**, next to the Palau Blaugrana [S12][S3]. It covers "all 2,400 m2 of the old ice rink" [S2].
  - It was meant to close when the renovated stadium opened in 2024/25 [S4][S12]. It is still running two seasons later.
- **Spaces** [S13][S2][S5]:
  1. The exhibition in the former ice rink (history, players, trophies, values)
  2. **SPOTIFY CAMP NOU LIVE**, "a large-format, immersive show that offers a 360º experience". This is where "the exhibition ends" [S2].
  3. The **Espai Barça architectural exhibition**, "featuring models and audiovisual components"
  4. The **Construction Viewpoint**, "the final stop", reached "from what used to be the entrance to the old museum". It is a balcony over the works, with screens showing the inside of the stadium, **original benches from the old stadium** and a photo booth [S5].
- **Access and timing.** Main Access 15, C/ Arístides Maillol s/n, and Boulevard Access 9 on Av. de Joan XXIII. "The duration of the visit is 1h." Last access is 45 minutes before closing [S88].
- **Warning.** The immersive room is "not recommended for people with epilepsy and/or photosensitive people" [S2].
- **Contents in 2023, still to be checked on site** [S3]:
  - holograms of Ronaldinho, Messi, Iniesta, Cruyff, Alexia and Pedri in the circular immersive space (a 10-minute show)
  - 11 interactive columns
  - a mosaic of 200 jerseys
  - the five men's Champions League trophies and the women's trophies
  - Messi "with his several Ballon d'Or"
  - a VR area

### What changed since the old guide was written (about May 2026)

- **The stadium is partly open again.**
  - Return on 22 Nov 2025: 4-0 v Athletic, 45,157 fans under the Phase 1B licence (maximum 45,401), after 909 days away [S7].
  - Phase 1C from 10 Mar 2026: **62,652**, including Gol Nord and the new "Gol 1957" supporters' area [S9].
  - Roof: the 830 m compression ring was finished in October 2026. The roof "big lift" starts in summer 2027. The third tier returns about January 2028, and the stadium is "finished" for 2028-29 [S11].
- **Stadium tours are back, but not on the Museum ticket.**
  - The **Spotify Camp Nou Experience** (€69) adds a panoramic view of the stadium. It has many stairs and is not wheelchair accessible [S6][S1].
  - The **Match Day Tour** (€249) adds a pitch walk, the press room and the presidential box [S6].
- **The museum will move.** The next stadium sub-phases put "the museum and first-team dressing room first" [S11]. The ice-rink museum is therefore temporary, with no end date published.

## 3. What to carry over, and the biggest errors

### Best verified stories and legacy items to keep (with the fix built in)

1. **Gamper's small ad.** *Los Deportes*, 22 Oct 1899, misspelled him "Kans Kamper".
   - Twelve founders of five nationalities met in the Gimnàs Solé on 29 Nov 1899.
   - **Walter Wild** was the first president. Gamper was founder and captain, then president five times from 1908 and saved the club in 1908. [S17][S19][S20][S84]
2. **The colours mystery.** The club's own "most likely" theory is the Merchant Taylors' School rugby kit. The Basel theory has "little evidence". [S18][S21]
3. **"Més que un club."** Narcís de Carreras said it on 17 Jan 1968. A 1973 ad campaign made it stick. Its double meaning under Franco is the point. [S22]
4. **Josep Sunyol, the "martyr president".**
   - His slogan was "sport and citizenship". He was shot without trial near the Alto del León on 6 Aug 1936.
   - His remains have **never been found**. The plaque in Guadarrama dates from 1996.
   - His name was on the Camp Nou presidential box from 2015 and is missing since the reopening. This is a strong, current legacy story. [S26][S27][S28][S29]
5. **The empty Camp Nou, 1 Oct 2017.** LaLiga refused to postpone, and Barça won 3-0 behind closed doors. Messi scored **two**. Tell it neutrally and attribute it. [S30]
6. **Wembley 1992.**
   - Koeman scored in the 112th minute, after Cruyff's "go out and enjoy it".
   - It healed the **two** lost finals (1961, 1986).
   - Laporta jumped into the Thames as promised, and over a million fans welcomed the team home. [S31][S32]
7. **Cruyff and Jordi.**
   - Jordi was born in Amsterdam on 9 Feb 1974 by a planned caesarean, timed so Cruyff could play at the Bernabéu.
   - Eight days later came the **0-5** (17 Feb 1974).
   - A Franco-era registrar refused "Jordi". Catalan names became legal with **Ley 17/1977**.
   - Add the **phantom goal** v Atlético at Camp Nou (22 Dec 1973), the 14th-minute **applause** in 2016, and the 2019 statue. [S33][S34][S36][S39][S38][S82]
8. **La Masia.**
   - A 1702 farmhouse that served as the workshop for the stadium's model, then club HQ (1966).
   - It became the residence on 20 Oct 1979, with 12 boys in the farmhouse and more under the Gol Nord stand. It closed on 30 Jun 2011.
   - Now it is becoming the club's social headquarters.
   - Seven Barça starters in the 2010 World Cup final, six of them from La Masia. [S40][S41][S43]
9. **The napkin.**
   - Rexach's "3-minute walk that took 15 minutes"; "Sign him. Don't even think about it."
   - Written at the Pompeia tennis club on 14 Dec 2000, with Minguella and Gaggioli as witnesses.
   - Sold at Bonhams in 2024 for £762,400. The club stayed out of the auction. [S75][S74][S15][S14][S16]
10. **Messi's numbers.**
    - 672 goals in 778 games, a one-club record that passed Pelé's 643.
    - Six Ballons d'Or as a Barça player; six trophies in 2009.
    - The Getafe goal (18 Apr 2007), the tearful exit (8 Aug 2021) and the No 10 passed to Lamine Yamal (2025). [S44][S48][S46][S45]
11. **The Cant del Barça** (1974, 75th anniversary; Espinàs, Picas, Valls): "tant se val d'on venim, si del sud o del nord". [S52][S53]
12. **Enemy applause.** Maradona (June 1983) and Ronaldinho (19 Nov 2005) were both applauded **at the Bernabéu**. Pair the two. [S50][S49]
13. **The women's legacy.**
    - Four Champions Leagues (2021, 2023, 2024, 2026) and five Ballons d'Or Féminins between Alexia (2021, 2022, 2026) and Aitana (2023–25).
    - Women's Club of the Year 2026.
    - Alexia's 2026 departure after 14 seasons. [S63][S64][S65]
14. **The stadium itself.**
    - Opened 24 Sep 1957 for 93,053; third tier added in 1982; 99,354 at the end.
    - 909 days away, then home on 22 Nov 2025. 62,652 from March 2026. Roof lift in 2027, finished for 2028-29.
    - 18,000 m² of solar panels. [S54][S55][S7][S9][S11][S61]
15. **Add, missing from the old guide:**
    - **culers**: backsides on the wall at Carrer Indústria [S84][S85]
    - **Gamper's 1925 expulsion** after the anthem was booed [S21]
    - the contested **11-1 of 1943**, told even-handedly with the historian's caveat that the dressing-room threat is unproven [S86]

### Biggest errors in the old guide

1. **The napkin is "in this museum"** (said three times). It never was. It was sold at auction in 2024. [S14][S16]
2. **"Eight golden footballs"** in the Messi room. He won six as a Barça player. [S44]
3. **The No 10 "has not been given to anyone"**. Ansu Fati wore it, and Lamine Yamal has it now. The guide also gives "Yamal's 19". [S45]
4. **Messi's debut "in a friendly at the Camp Nou"**. It was a league derby at Montjuïc. [S44]
5. **The 2017 "hat-trick"** was a brace. The match was not "the only" one played behind closed doors. [S30]
6. **Sunyol "exhumed in the 1990s" / "roadside grave"**, plus a "2003 monument" at the complex. His remains were never found, and no such monument is documented. [S26][S29]
7. **"Eleven" founders who "voted Gamper president"**. There were twelve founders, and Walter Wild was president. [S17][S19]
8. **"Five lost European finals" before 1992**. There were two. [S31]
9. **The Cruyff chain:**
   - the "debut included" the 0-5
   - the "impossible goal" placed at the Bernabéu (it was v Atlético at Camp Nou)
   - a lollipop on the statue
   - "within a year Franco was dead"
   - the 14th minute "in silence" (it was applause)
   [S33][S36][S38][S39][S82]
10. **Messi's 2011 solo goal and Ronaldinho's ovation placed "down there" in Camp Nou**. Both happened at the Bernabéu. [S51][S49]
11. **Maradona's career "ended" by hepatitis and the leg-break**. He left after the 1984 brawl. [S50]
12. **Anthem "1974, the year Cruyff signed"**. He signed in 1973. The "last line" quoted is not the last line. [S37][S53]
13. **Stadium tech:**
    - The old roof covered "two-thirds" of the seats. Only the main stand was covered.
    - "30,000 m²" of panels, "self-sufficient with surplus". The figure is 18,000 m², covering a large share of needs.
    [S56][S61][S62]
14. **La Masia:**
    - moved "stone by stone" (no source)
    - "seven Masia graduates" in the 2010 final (six; seven were Barça players)
    - Guardiola "captain of Spain" fourteen years later
    [S40][S41][S43][S77]
15. **The 2026 layer:**
    - "five matches" left at the Clásico (three)
    - women "three" UWCL (four)
    - the men "sometimes play" at Estadi Johan Cruyff (they used Montjuïc)
    - "two hours" (1 h)
    - "stadium closed", "viewpoint gone in a year"
    [S67][S63][S7][S88][S1]
16. **Unsourced quotes:**
    - the cover epigraph credited to Vázquez Montalbán, 1972
    - Casillas "like being underwater"
    - Laporta "the future engine"
    MVM died returning from Australia and New Zealand, not "on his way to a literary festival". [S23][S25]
