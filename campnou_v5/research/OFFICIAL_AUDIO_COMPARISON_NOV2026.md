# Spotify Camp Nou / FC Barcelona Museum: official audio offer vs competitors (research as of Nov 2026, DATED)

**Status:** COMPLETE, 14 Nov 2026. **DATED:** every finding is as of 14 Nov 2026 (access date of all pages unless noted). The museum is due to move, the stadium is still a building site, and tickets, hours and match-day rules change week to week. **Re-check before launch.**
**Purpose:** what the official "Museum + Digital Audio Guide" ticket (Barça Immersive Tour, the club's temporary museum in the former ice rink) and the other official products show during the works, what commercial competitors offer, and where a Yo Tours audio guide for the **Museum ticket** (scope per USER_DECISIONS.md) can beat them.
**Method:** WebSearch and WebFetch only, one page at a time per site, no bursts. No curl, no browser, no CAPTCHA, no sign-in. Tripadvisor, Google Maps and GetYourGuide product pages were not fetched (bot-protection risk); Google ratings come from Wanderlog, GetYourGuide data from a summary site. Review content comes from Headout, TUI Musement, Klook and Civitatis review pages. Two pages failed or were empty and were not retried (thisisbarcelona Barça page; lokalee). The official ticket shop's checkout pages were not opened.
**Grades:** **OFF** = official page (fcbarcelona.com / .es and its cards and news; Ajuntament de Barcelona). **SEC+** = reseller page whose named provider is the club (Tiqets "Provider FCBarcelona"). **SEC** = other secondary page fetched and read (reseller, press, blog, aggregator). **SNIP** = search-result snippet or a negative search result.
**Recommendations contain no prices** (binding). Prices in the source log are facts as found, for context only.
**Full quotes and URLs for every point are in Appendix A (source log).** The sections below give the URL in-line the first time and the short source afterwards.

## 0. The one thing to know first (DATED, critical)
- **The Museum ticket today is the temporary "Barça Immersive Tour" in the former ice rink (Palau de Gel), open since May/June 2023**, about 2,400 m², ending in the 360º "Spotify Camp Nou Live" room and the construction viewpoint (OFF, https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/card/3044593/immersive-tour-basic ; https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/club/news/4440134/barca-museum-reaches-one-million-visitors-again-in-year-of-return-to-spotify-camp-nou).
- **A new 2,800 m² museum inside the stadium was due "during 2026"**, the club "hopes to open it in November" 2026, "The final decision is in the hands of the technicians", and the move needs **about 15 days' closure** (SEC press, Ara 7 Oct 2026, https://en.ara.cat/sports/the-tourists-who-visit-the-camp-nou-will-have-joy-in-november_1_5872327.html ; Crónica Global 19 Jun 2026, "sujeta a imponderables").
- **As of 14 Nov 2026 no report was found that the new museum has opened or that the temporary one has closed**, and every official ticket page still sells the ice-rink exhibition (SNIP, negative result). Klook shows winter hours "19 Oct to 24 Dec 2026" (SEC).
- **Consequence for us:** the route script may change within weeks of launch. The guide needs a route-agnostic core (stories keyed to objects and themes) plus a thin, swappable "where you are" layer. **The lead must confirm which museum is open before recording the route tracks.**

## 1. The official offer: "Museum + Digital Audio Guide"

### 1.1 Is an audio guide included in the Museum ticket?
**Yes (OFF).** Every Museum-family ticket lists "Museum + Digital Audio Guide" and "Construction Viewpoint" (OFF, https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/tickets/museum ; ES card "Museo + Audioguía*", https://www.fcbarcelona.es/es/ficha/3044593/museo). The Spotify Camp Nou Experience, BarçaBus and Sky Tour bundle a "Flexible Museum Ticket + Audio-guide" (OFF, https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/tickets/camp-nou-experience/barca-tours).
- **Stale claims that it is a paid extra still rank:** Barcelona Hacks (marked updated May 2026) says the guide "is not included in the standard ticket and must be added during the booking" (SEC, https://barcelonahacks.com/visit-camp-nou-barcelona/). That was true of the old pre-2023 Camp Nou Experience, where it cost extra with a card deposit (SEC, Barcelona Lowdown, https://barcelonalowdown.com/?p=9047). A 2022 TUI review complains of no audio without paying extra (SEC).

### 1.4 What the Museum ticket covers (official) and what sellers get wrong
**Official contents:** a "100% interactive" exhibition of history, players, trophies and values in "all 2,400 m2 of the old ice rink"; it "ends in Spotify Camp Nou Live", a 360º show; an exhibition on the Espai Barça project "with models and audiovisual pieces"; the "Construction Viewpoint" (OFF, Museum card). RoboKeeper and the "Barça Virtual Dream" VR are paid add-ons or in the Total Experience (OFF). City listing: "Due to the works at the Spotify Camp Nou the tickets do not include a tour of the interior of the stadium." (OFF, https://www.barcelona.cat/museusbarcelona/en/museum/museu-del-fc-barcelona-92163150849).
**Seller errors and stale copy:**
- Headout e-3390 still sells a "panoramic view of the stadium from the press box, the players' tunnel, and the players' dugout" and "walk onto the turf" (marked unavailable) (SEC).
- TUI/VoxPopGuide: stadium access "is not possible until November 2024" (SEC). The Tour Guy: "The stadium itself is closed and cannot be visited" and renovation complete "August 2026" (SEC). artvisitguide: full tour back "August 2026" (SEC).
- Klook suggests "2-3 hours"; Barcelona Hacks "90-180 minutes" (SEC). Official: "The duration of the visit is 1h."
- Civitatis: "Not wheelchair accessible" vs official "fully adapted for people with reduced mobility and wheelchair users" (SEC; OFF).
- A reseller domain, fcbarcelona.barcelona.com, headlines "Official FC Barcelona Tickets" with no affiliation notice; it is Barcelona.com, Inc. selling via Tiqets (SEC).

### 1.5 Official rules a guide must get right (DATED)
- **Hours:** 19 Oct–19 Mar 10:00–18:00; 20 Mar–17 Oct 9:30–19:00; closed 1 Jan and 25 Dec. **"Last access 45 minutes before closing."** Event-day exceptions in Nov–Dec 2026: 12 Nov until 18:45, 17 Nov until 17:00, 22 Nov until 14:00, 2 Dec until 21:30, 8 Dec until 15:00 (OFF, timetable). Third-party hours conflict (Tiqets, travel-buddies, Ajuntament Sunday 10–15, Barcelona Hacks last entry 90 min).
- **Entrances:** "Main Access 15: C/ Arístides Mallol s/n" and "Boulevard Access 9" on Av. de Joan XXIII (OFF, timetable; https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/tickets/camp-nou-experience/practical-information/getting-to-the-camp-nou). The tour entrance "is between Barça Café and Barça Store Camp Nou" (SEC, https://thebettervacation.com/barcelona/camp-nou-tour/). Bags may be scanned; "a brief security check" (SEC, Headout).
- **Quiet Hours:** Saturdays 15:00–17:00 with reduced light and sound, not on men's first-team match days nor 28 Apr–1 Sep (OFF, Accessibility).
- **Health warning:** the 360 room is "not recommended for people with epilepsy and/or photosensitivity"; tell staff (OFF).
- **No car:** the tour car park closed "from 1 June 2023" (OFF); "The Espai Barça works have removed private vehicle access" (SEC, Barcelona Urbana).
- **Ticket changes:** "Change your ticket directly at the ticket office" (fee), except Spotify Camp Nou Experience, Match Day Tour and others (OFF, Museum page).

**Bottom line on the official offer:** the ticket bundles a phone web guide reached by a QR card at the entrance, in up to 14 languages plus LSC and transcripts. **Its stop count, running time and narration are undocumented**, and it has to compete with loud immersive soundtracks in dim rooms. No official product publishes a walking order, a running time, or what to do at the viewpoint.

## 4. Common gaps and mistakes, and what our guide must do better

### 4.1 Evidence (paraphrased; exact quotes and URLs in Appendix A)
1. **Outdated stadium information is everywhere.** Live listings still promise the press box, tunnel, dugout and "walk onto the turf" (Headout e-3390); blogs updated in 2025–26 still describe the old dugout and visitors' changing room (Barcelona Lowdown) and say the audio costs extra (Barcelona Hacks). Reopening dates disagree: "until November 2024" (VoxPopGuide), "August 2026" (Tour Guy, artvisitguide), "before 2028" (Wanderlog), "end of 2027" (Crónica Global). The official car-park notice dates from 2023.
2. **Visitors expect the pitch.** It is the single biggest complaint (§2.3). Pitch access is sold only as the Match Day Tour, on match days. The bowl view is a separate guided product (§2.1).
3. **Legends and promotional claims are stated as fact.** "Europe's largest stadium" (Tour Guy, Audiala); Kubala "led directly to the construction of Camp Nou" (Musement 510207, Funbooker); "the most successful soccer academy in history" (Funbooker); Audiala's three capacities, a wrong architect and unsourced "secrets" (the roofline set by "winter solstice sun paths", acoustics "mathematically engineered").
4. **Politics are one-sided or unsourced.** "A symbol of Catalan identity and democratic resistance under Franco's dictatorship" with no sources (Funbooker); "the unofficial parliament of a banned language" and "municipal archives confirm" with no archive (Audiala). No competitor gives attribution or another view (USER_DECISIONS: "even-handed, attributed, no club partisanship").
5. **No directions in the museum.** The club publishes no route; there is only "An orientation map is in the entrance hall" and subdued lighting (OFF). Reviewers say "not very logical" and ask for floor arrows. Even the viewpoint's place in the route is described only on a 2023 page (last stop, through the old museum entrance).
6. **Nothing explains the works.** No product tells the visitor what they are looking at from the viewpoint (which stand, what is being built, the phases), although the club's own figures exist: phases 1A–1C, final capacity "nearly 105,000", return on 22 Nov 2025 against Athletic Club (SEC, Barcelona Urbana), completion "end of 2027" (SEC). Reviews: "three wooden planks".
7. **Delivery problems.** The guide competes with loud rooms ("so much noise", "far too loud"); a QR card at the door; WiFi "coverage is pretty poor" (Barcelona Lowdown, older).
8. **Length mismatch.** Official "1h"; visitors report 7 to 60 minutes; sellers claim up to "2-3 hours". Nobody states an honest track count or running time.
9. **Entrance and ticket confusion.** Access 15 (Arístides Maillol) vs Access 9 (Av. Joan XXIII), with Civitatis sending people to Joan XXIII; "little signage"; a reseller site titled "Official"; the Spotify Camp Nou Experience meets at Access 15 fifteen minutes early, and latecomers lose the stadium part (OFF).
10. **Match days are badly explained.** Conflicting rules circulate: "closed the full day" (Headout 3390), "up to 3 hours before kick-off", "Champions League museum until 15:00" (thebettervacation), panorama "until 6 pm" (Barcelona Hacks), versus the official list of early closings (12, 17, 22 Nov; 2, 8 Dec 2026).

### 4.2 Concrete list: what the Yo Tours guide must do (no prices)
1. **Track 0, before arrival: "What this ticket shows, and what it doesn't."** In plain words: the Museum ticket is the club museum and a viewpoint over the works. It does not include the pitch, the dressing rooms or a seat in the bowl. Those are other official products (name them, no prices). Say this on the booking page too.
2. **Give an honest, dated works briefing.** The stadium reopened to matches in Nov 2025 and is still being finished (roof, third tier). Give the phases and the official target capacity, attributed and dated. Never give a reopening date as fact. Add a line on what to check if the new museum has opened.
3. **Make the viewpoint a proper chapter.** Two or three tracks that tell you what you are looking at (which stand, what is new, the old benches on display, the screens), how long to stay, and the shade and heat warning. No competitor does this.
4. **Give a fixed, numbered walking order with directions at every step**, from the metro, through Access 15, the entrance hall and the orientation map, every room, the 360º room, the viewpoint, and the exit and store. Each track ends with "next, walk to...". Include the "Sat 15:00–17:00 quiet hours" tip.
5. **Make it noise-proof and offline.** Download in advance; no door QR, no signal needed. Tell listeners to pause in the 360 room and listen before and after it. Short tracks for loud rooms, longer stories for the quiet spots outdoors and at the viewpoint.
6. **Separate fact from legend and promotion.** Capacity, size and "largest" claims only from the club or another dated source. Kubala and the new stadium: a popular story, attributed. The pine tree, the Quini kidnapping and La Masia: facts with dates from the log.
7. **Politics: even-handed and attributed.** Franco era, Catalan identity, "Més que un club", the Real Madrid rivalry and Sunyol: say who claims what, give the documented events, and mark contested readings as contested. No club partisanship, no rival-bashing.
8. **Honest lengths:** 30 / 60 / 90 minutes with the real track count, stated up front. The 30-minute path still reaches the trophies, the Messi Zone, the 360 room and the viewpoint.
9. **Practical and current:** last entry 45 minutes before closing; event-day early closings; bag check; no car access; Access 15 vs Access 9; the photosensitivity warning; LSC and the transcripts in the official guide for deaf visitors (point to them, don't compete); wheelchair-friendly route (the Museum is flat; the stadium view is not).
10. **Languages EN, ES, FR, DE from day one** (USER_DECISIONS). The official guide covers 14, so our edge is quality, route and honesty, not language count.

## 5. What visitors most want to know, and the common confusions

### 5.1 What they want (from FAQs, reviews and guides)
- **Can I see the pitch / the stadium?** Not on the Museum ticket (OFF, Ajuntament; Headout warning "the panoramic stadium tour is not available"). It is the top complaint (§2.3).
- **How long does it take?** Official 1 h; real 15–60 min for most; fans longer (§4.1-8).
- **Which ticket?** Museum vs Flexible vs Total Experience vs Spotify Camp Nou Experience vs Match Day Tour (§2.1). "Buy online", the club says, and a 2026 reviewer found online cheaper (Wanderlog).
- **When to go:** quieter on weekday mornings and Nov–Feb; busiest 12:30–14:30 or 11:00–15:00 at weekends (SEC: thebettervacation; travel-buddies). Quiet Hours Sat 15:00–17:00 (OFF).
- **Getting there:** the club names no metro station (OFF). Secondary: L3 Palau Reial or Les Corts, L5 Collblanc or Badal, 10–15 min walk (SEC, Barcelona Urbana); an older guide maps Les Corts (L3) to gate 9 and Collblanc (L5) to gate 15 (SEC, Barcelona Lowdown).
