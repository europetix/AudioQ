## 0. Method and keys

- **Tools.** WebSearch and WebFetch only. One page at a time per site, no bursts. No curl, no browser, no CAPTCHA. All reads were done on 14 Nov 2026.
- **WebFetch caveat.** WebFetch returns a model summary, and verbatim excerpts are capped at about 125 characters. So every "verbatim" quote is **as relayed**. **Re-check the exact wording against the page before quoting on air.** The original language is marked: (EN), (CA), (ES).
- **Grades.**
  - **OFF**: official. FC Barcelona (fcbarcelona.com / .es / .cat and its regional mirrors, barcaacademy.fcbarcelona.com, espaibarca.fcbarcelona.com, the club's annual reports and statutes), the Ajuntament de Barcelona, the Generalitat. Also, for their own facts only: UEFA, FIFA, the Holy See, Limak (contractor) and Nikken Sekkei (architect). **OFF pages contain slips and the club's own superlatives**; both are flagged.
  - **SEC**: a named secondary source read directly: scholarly works, reputable press (La Vanguardia, El Periódico, ARA, El País, 3Cat/Esport3, betevé, Catalan News, El Nacional, Crònica Global, The Athletic, BBC, Guardian, Reuters/AFP/AP reprints) and named historians.
  - **SNIP**: a search-result snippet or title only.
  - **WIKI**: lead only, never sole support.
- **Verdicts.**
  - **OK**: safe to state as written.
  - **HEDGE**: true in substance, or sources conflict; use the safe wording.
  - **LEGEND**: tradition or tour lore; script only as "the story goes…".
  - **UNVERIFIED**: do not script.
  - **ERROR**: a claim found in circulation, or on an official page, that is wrong or contradicted.
  - **DATED**: a 2025–2026 fact that may change. Re-check before release.
- **Club superlatives** ("the most visited museum", "the best player in our history", "largest immersive room in Europe in a sports museum") are graded OFF for *what the club says*, and scripted **attributed** ("the club calls it…").
- **Politics.** Franco-era, Catalan-identity and rivalry material is attributed and even-handed: what happened, who says what, and where historians disagree.
- **Sources** are listed in §24 as [O…] (FC Barcelona), [P…] (other official bodies) and [S…] (secondary).
- **Blocked or failed this session** (none retried): listed in §24.
