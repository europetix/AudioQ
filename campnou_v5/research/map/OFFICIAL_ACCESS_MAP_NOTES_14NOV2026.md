Non-match-day access map: three public access points, each marked for walkers and wheelchair users:
- **Access 9**, at the corner where Carrer de Martí i Franquès meets Avinguda de Joan XXIII (north-west side).
- **Access 15**, at the south corner, where Carrer d'Arístides Maillol meets Travessera de les Corts.
- **Access 15A**, on Travessera de les Corts, a little east of 15.
The map does not say which access serves the Museum ticket. Between Access 9 and Access 15, inside the campus on the
Arístides Maillol side, it draws an octagonal building and long low buildings (not labelled). The stadium bowl is
drawn whole (no works shown).

Pedestrian map: metro stations around the campus — Les Corts (L3), Maria Cristina (L3), Palau Reial (L3), Zona
Universitària (L3, L9 Sud), Collblanc (L5, L9 Sud, L10), Badal (L5), Ernest Lluch (L5); tram stops Pius XII, Avinguda de
Xile and others; walking routes converge on the campus from Avinguda Diagonal, Av. Joan XXIII, Travessera de les Corts
and Collblanc.

Consequence: the guide's start cue can't name a gate until O1 (route research: the Oct 2026 page says Access 9, older
pages and the user's old guide say Access 15). 001 gives both as landmark-led options ("the gate the museum signs
direct you to; Access 9 on Avinguda de Joan XXIII or Access 15 on Arístides Maillol") — confirm on site.

## Barça Campus Map, phase 1C (official PDF, 13 Mar 2026; read in Chrome 14 Nov 2026)
Source: https://www.fcbarcelona.com/fcbarcelona/document/2026/03/13/e60ba067-28bc-4685-9bac-98821659b15d/campus-1C-ok-DEF.pdf
Images: official_campus_map_1C_mar2026.jpg (whole), official_campus_map_1C_mar2026_museum_corner.png (zoom).
(The map was drawn for the March 2026 members' vote: the blue "Col·legi electoral" tent is temporary. The
buildings and gates are the useful part.)
- **The Museum icon (trophy) sits on the long, ridged-roof building at the corner of Av. Joan XXIII and Av.
  d'Arístides Maillol, immediately beside the round Palau Blaugrana (2)** = the former Palau de Gel (ice rink).
  An information point and a café icon are next to it.
- **Campus Access 9 is right at that building, on Av. Joan XXIII.** Campus Accesses 11 and 12 are on Av.
  d'Arístides Maillol along the same building and the Palau Blaugrana. **Access 15 is much further along
  Arístides Maillol / Travessera de les Corts**, by Auditori 1899 (4) and the Seu Social-OAB (5), with ticket
  office, info point and café icons.
- So the map agrees with the Oct 2026 access page: **Access 9 is the museum's gate**. The old guide's "Arístides
  Maillol" street is the building's other side (Accesses 11/12), not Access 15. The museum's door itself is still
  O1 (which face of the building).
- Other campus landmarks: the Masia de Can Planes (3) is on the far side of the stadium, by Carrer d'Elisabeth
  Eidenbenz, NOT near the museum: never cue it as visible from the museum route. Sala París (7) and the Barça
  Store (shopping-bag icon) lie between the Palau Blaugrana and the stadium's main stand.
- The support FAQ ("Barça Immersive Tour") was read too: it only lists question titles (accessibility,
  complementary services, RoboKeeper); no route, exit or re-entry answers.
