Non-match-day access map: three public access points, each marked for walkers and wheelchair users:
- **Access 9**, at the corner where Carrer de Martí i Franquès meets Avinguda de Joan XXIII (north-west side).
- **Access 15**, at the south corner, where Carrer d'Arístides Maillol meets Travessera de les Corts.
- **Access 15A**, on Travessera de les Corts, a little east of 15.
The map does not say which access serves the Museum ticket. Between Access 9 and Access 15, inside the campus on the
Arístides Maillol side, it draws an octagonal building and long low buildings (not labelled). The stadium bowl is
drawn whole (no works shown).

Xile and others; walking routes converge on the campus from Avinguda Diagonal, Av. Joan XXIII, Travessera de les Corts
and Collblanc.
