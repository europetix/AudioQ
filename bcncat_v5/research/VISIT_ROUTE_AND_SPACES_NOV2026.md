# Barcelona Cathedral: visitor route, spaces and status (research as of 14 Nov 2026)

Prepared for: Yo Tours audio guide (bcncat_v5). Researcher: subagent, 14 Nov 2026.
Scope (USER_DECISIONS.md): everything on the cultural-visit ticket (facade and square, nave, side chapels, choir, crypt, cloister, rooftop by lift, chapter-house museum, anything else the ticket covers); lengths 30 / 60 / 90 min including walking.
Method: WebSearch + WebFetch only, one page at a time per site. No curl, no browser, no CAPTCHA. Wikipedia never alone (and in fact every Wikipedia page tried was blocked or failed, so nothing here rests on Wikipedia).

Grades:
- **OFF**: the cathedral's own site or file, fetched (catedralbcn.org, Capítol de la Catedral).
- **OFF-ARQ**: Archdiocese of Barcelona tourism page (turisme.esglesia.barcelona), fetched.
- **OFF-AJ / OFF-TUR**: Ajuntament de Barcelona page / Turisme de Barcelona (thisisbarcelona.com), fetched. Official-city, not the operator: can be stale.
- **SEC**: secondary page, fetched (type in brackets: encyclopaedia, press, vendor, reseller, blog).
- **SNIP**: search snippet only; a lead, not a fact.
- **INF**: my inference from the graded facts listed with it, or general architectural background I could not source this session. **Verify on site before scripting any INF placement.**

Unless noted every page was fetched 14 Nov 2026. The fetch tool returns short excerpts, so quotes are short.
**No prices or hours in scripts.** Where hours/prices appear below they are marked OPS ONLY.

Status: complete for desk research (14 Nov 2026). Raw graded notes are in Appendix A; the fetch log (including failed/blocked pages) is in Appendix B.

## 0. Key facts for the script (summary)

1. **What the ticket covers (OFF list, the cathedral's own "Espais visitables" page, 6 items, in this order):**
   | # | CA (official) | ES (official) | EN (official) |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | El temple i el claustre | El templo y el claustro | The Cathedral floor and cloister |
   | 2 | Els terrats | Las terrazas | The roof |
   | 3 | Cor amb el cadirat del Toisó d'Or | Coro con la sillería del Toisón de oro | The choir with the golden fleece choir stalls |
   | 4 | Sala Capitular | Sala Capitular | The Chapter Hall |
   | 5 | Memòria jueva a la Catedral | Memoria judía en la Catedral | Jewish memory in the Cathedral |
   | 6 | Capella del Sant Crist de Lepant | Capilla del Santo Cristo de Lepanto | The Saint Christ of Lepanto's Chapel |
   - The Ajuntament guide lists the same six for the full visit (OFF-AJ). Turisme de Barcelona's ticket page lists "Accés al cor", "Accés a la terrassa del terrat", "Accés a la Sala del Capítol", "Guia d'àudio virtual en diversos idiomes" (OFF-TUR).
   - **The crypt is not in the OFF list as a separate item.** It is part of "the temple" (the crypt lies under the presbytery and is open to the nave). INF: included, but closed during Mass at the high altar (SEC blog: no access to "the altar and the crypt" during Mass).
   - **Sant Sever church is NOT on the list.** OFF says only that it is "tocant a la Catedral" and managed by the Chapter; it is a separate church on Carrer de Sant Sever 11, reopened 10 Feb 2025 after restoration. No source says the cultural ticket includes it. **Keep it out of the walk; mention it (if at all) as "next door".**
   - The "Diocesan Museum" listed by one reseller (headout) is not an OFF item. Ignore.
2. **"Christ of Lepanto chapel" = "Chapel of the Blessed Sacrament" (Capella del Santíssim).** OFF (Revista de la Catedral núm. 1, Jul 2026): the 1407 room by Arnau Bargués "servia com a Sala Capitular"; "Al damunt del sepulcre de sant Oleguer es troba la imatge del Sant Crist de Lepant." OFF organ page: the Chapel of the Blessed Sacrament, also called "del Sant Crist de Lepant". **So Sant Oleguer's tomb and the Lepanto Christ are in the same chapel, the Santíssim.** There are **two "chapter houses"**: the old one (now the Santíssim/Lepanto chapel, inside the church) and the cloister one (now the **Sala Capitular museum**). Scripts must never mix them up.
   - **During Lent the Christ of Lepanto is moved** "at the Chapel of Saint Severus" (OFF). Say "usually".
   - Rules (OFF): the chapel is "dedicated to worship and prayer. Please do not enter or take pictures during the celebrations." **Weekday Masses are celebrated there on the hour during visiting hours** (OFF Masses page, OPS ONLY). The route must make this chapel skippable.
3. **Rooftop:** OFF: lift reached "though the chapel of the Innocent Saints, next to the door of San Ivo" (CA: "capella dels Sants Innocents, al costat de la Porta de Sant Iu"). The Sant Iu door is the side door onto Carrer dels Comtes (SEC encyclopaedia), with a bell tower above it (OFF). **As you face the high altar, Sant Iu and the lift are on the LEFT** (INF from OFF + SEC; two independent SEC blogs say the lift is "to the left of the main altar"). On top (OFF): both bell towers, the two Gothic lateral "cuculles", the cimbori crowned by the Holy Cross with the statue of Saint Helena, the cloister below, a city panorama. Terraces restored and reopened with "new stairs, new handrails and an updated lift" (SEC Catalan News, 10 Jul 2024; OFF "Obert per obres": lift modernised). The AR viewer "La Talaia de Barcelona" (45 points; device supplied) is "included in the price of accessing the roof" (SEC, Mar–Apr 2025). **Lift timing/slots/capacity: NOT published (O3).**
4. **Doors (OFF hours/Masses pages):** three public doors are listed: **Porta Principal (Pla de la Seu)**, **Porta de Santa Eulàlia (Carrer del Bisbe)** = "accés per a persones amb mobilitat reduïda", and **Porta de Sant Iu**. The cloister has two outer doors, **Pietat** and **Santa Eulàlia**, plus the **Romanesque white-marble interior door** to the church. **No OFF text says which door is the tourist entrance or exit.** The main door has "una escala amb 10 graons" (OFF-TUR). One SEC blog (2025) describes entering by the main door ("Directly in front of you, as you enter … the gothic choir stalls"); another says Sant Iu "usually remains closed". **Tourist entry/exit is the No. 1 on-site question (O1).**
5. **Works, Nov 2026 (OFF, calendar entry of 18 Nov 2026):** "obres de restauració de dues voltes de la zona del transsepte". **The transept bay is exactly where the lift (left) and the cloister door (right) are, and in front of the crypt stair.** Scaffolding there could re-route visitors (O2). Other OFF programme items (undated status): choir carvings and polychromy; cloister stone and a garden redesign; liturgical bell tower; Casa de l'Almoina roofs and façades on the square (contract awarded 22 Jan 2026).
6. **Choir:** closed to visitors during Mass (SEC GVAM 2019: the "Essentials" tour is for when "the choir is closed to visitors"). OFF: "At the entrance to the choir on the right is the wooden pulpit"; the trascoro (retrochoir) has "four scenes sculpted in relief of the life of Saint Eulalia", two each side of its door.
7. **Free / worship entry:** the old free-morning and free-evening model (Time Out 2017) is **not** on the current OFF hours page (modified 18 Sep 2025), which gives one visit window. A 2026 blog claiming free slots cites nothing. **Do not script anything about free entry.**
8. **The cathedral's existing audioguide** (GVAM, 2019): 6 languages (EN, CA, FR, DE, IT, ES per a reseller), subtitles, two tours, an "interactive map of the whole building" (SEC). Ours should add what it lacks: exact placement cues, the roof and cloister detail, and Mass-proof alternatives.

### 1.3 Rooftop lift
- Where: through the Sants Innocents chapel, next to the Sant Iu door (OFF, CA/ES/EN). Left side facing the altar (INF + SEC ×2).
- Included: yes, "Els terrats" is a visitable space on the cultural visit (OFF); "Accés a la terrassa del terrat" (OFF-TUR); resellers agree (SEC).
- Timing / capacity / waits / closing in rain or wind: **not published (O3).**
- On top: some walking and steps ("new stairs, new handrails", SEC 2024). INF: not step-free.

### 1.4 Choir, museum, crypt, Sant Sever, Jewish memory
- **Choir**: included (OFF). Closed during services (SEC GVAM).
- **Sala Capitular (museum)**: included (OFF); in the cloister gallery "next to the chapel dedicated to Saint Lucia" (OFF EN); "can be visited by wheelchair users" (OFF-TUR). No photos there (SEC blog). The fourth cloister side holds the "sales capitulars, antiga i moderna" (GEC): two rooms; one is the "New Chapter Room" with a painted baroque dome (SEC blog).
- **Crypt of Santa Eulàlia**: under the presbytery, visually open to the nave (SEC-enc). Reached by steps (INF; not step-free). Not accessible during Mass (SEC).
- **Sant Sever**: separate church, not on the ticket list (see §0.1).
- **Jewish memory**: in "the Old Archive of 1535" (OFF). **Its location in the complex is not published (O6).**

### 1.5 Rules that affect the route
- Santíssim/Lepanto chapel: no entry and no photos during celebrations (OFF).
- Museum: no photography (SEC blog, Jun 2025).
- Dress: shoulders and knees covered (SEC reseller); "Plunging necklines and transparent clothes are not allowed", hats off; unsuitably dressed visitors "may be turned away" (SEC blog). **Put a one-line dress reminder in the pre-visit track, not inside.**
- Behaviour during services: "mantingui el silenci i sigui respectuós" (OFF-TUR). INF: headphones only; no speaker playback.

### 1.6 Accessibility (for cueing only)
- Step-free entry: Porta de Santa Eulàlia, Carrer del Bisbe (OFF). Main door: 10 steps (OFF-TUR).
- Crypt: steps (INF). Roof: lift plus stairs on the terraces (SEC). Museum: wheelchair-accessible (OFF-TUR). Choir: unknown.
- "Solo algunas zonas son accesibles" (SEC Civitatis).

**Numbering.** The cathedral's own plan (`planta-catedral-barcelona.jpg`) could not be read (fetch failed). The only chapel numbering I could read is the **Enciclopèdia Catalana's** (*L'art gòtic a Catalunya*): **1–9 on the Gospel (left) side, 10–20 around the apse/ambulatory, 21–29 on the Epistle (right) side; chapel 28 = the former chapter house = Santíssim/Sant Oleguer.** INF: the numbering runs continuously (left side from the facade towards the apse, round the ambulatory, back down the right side), which puts 28–29 near the main door on the right. **Not the cathedral's numbering; do not print numbers in scripts until the official plan is read.**

## 5. Proposed routes (start: Pla de la Seu, facing the facade; end: the exit)

**Design rule.** The lift is in the LEFT transept bay; the cloister door is in the RIGHT transept bay; the crypt stair and the choir are between them. The cloister is the natural last part if the exit is via the cloister (Santa Eulàlia door, Carrer del Bisbe). So: **left aisle → lift and roof → choir → crypt → (ambulatory left to right) → cloister door → cloister → museum → exit.** No doubling back except the short lift up-and-down. The Lepanto chapel (right, near the main door) is either the first stop (Variant A) or the last (Variant B, if the exit is back through the church to the main door).

**Track budget.** 30 min ≈ 10–12 tracks; 60 min ≈ 20–24; 90 min ≈ 32–36 (user target). INF: the full ticket scope supports a real 90 min including a roof wait; the honest risk is the lift wait, not lack of material.
