## Story bank additions (new, from rows above)

1. **Fifty-one shields, a dozen knights** (G1–G12): trumpets up the square on 5 March 1519, Gideon tapestries hung round the stalls, a Requiem for the dead brothers; three days earlier a catafalque for Maximilian; in July the news that Charles is emperor.
2. **The boy king's shield** (G19): Louis II of Hungary, aged about twelve, among the arms over the stalls.
3. **Two brothers-in-law carry the saint** (G75, G127): "with our own hands", 1339; 1343 Pere takes Mallorca; 1349 Jaume dies at Llucmajor.
4. **A stone that says "in Barcelona"** (G72): the 9th-century plaque behind the tomb, the town's answer to the Mérida doubt.
5. **Hercules in a wooden box** (G25–G27): in 1603 locals said the founders' coffin was Hercules'; historians still argue who is inside.
6. **The countess with two living husbands** (G28–G31): Almodis, the hired fleet, the pope's ban, the stepson's knife.
7. **The tortoise and the swallows** (G124): justice as it should be and as it is, on the Ardiaca letterbox.
8. **Gravestones in the square** (G103–G105): after 1391, Hebrew tombstones built into the walls by the Sant Iu door; the Jewish quarter's voices survive in the cathedral's archive upstairs.
9. **A bishop and his dogs by the lift** (G41–G42): Escales's alabaster tomb, two greyhounds, eight mourners (if the chapel is confirmed).
10. **A mother's tomb** (G37–G39): Sança Ximenis de Cabrera's chapel; the tomb may be her mother's.
11. **The painter under the cloister** (G48): Bernat Martorell bought his grave here in 1435.
12. **The blessing from the apse cross** (G55) and **Helena on the lantern** (G54).
