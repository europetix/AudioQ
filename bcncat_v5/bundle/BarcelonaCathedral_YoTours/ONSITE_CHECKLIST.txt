BARCELONA CATHEDRAL V5: ON-SITE CHECKLIST (from v5/PLAN.md, 14 Nov 2026; 36 tracks)
==================================================================================
Item numbers O1–O12 keep the meaning given in research/VISIT_ROUTE_AND_SPACES_NOV2026.md §5; O13–O27 are new
(PLAN.md §5). Track numbers below are PLAN.md's.

One walk of the FULL route, in PLAN.md order (Variant A: in by the main door, out by the cloister's Santa Eulàlia
door to Carrer del Bisbe), before recording, with a phone camera and, if O5 has been done, a printout of the
official plan.
- Photograph every sign, plaque, chapel label, map board and arrow you are asked about. Save them to
  research/onsite/ as Oxx_<what>.jpg.
- Note times with a stopwatch: WALKING, QUEUING AND THE LIFT ONLY, not listening.
- Report each item as CONFIRMED / DIFFERENT (what you saw) / NOT FOUND.
- This is a working church. Never enter a chapel during a service, never photograph during a service, never step
  behind a rope or into a closed area, never lift a choir seat or touch the stalls. Keep your voice down. If Mass
  is on in the Lepanto chapel, note only what can be seen from its doorway, and come back to that item later.
- Never complete a CAPTCHA or create an account. Nothing about prices, hours, slots or tickets goes into the guide;
  note them only if they change the route.
- Never note directions as compass points. Use LEFT/RIGHT AS YOU FACE THE HIGH ALTAR (= as you stand on the square
  facing the facade), plus fixed features (doors by name, the choir, the choir screen, the crypt stair, the lift
  chapel, the cloister door, the pond, the fountain), so the lead can rewrite cues. Never use chapel numbers in your
  notes unless you are copying them from the official plan (O5), and say so.
- Do not leave and re-enter unless O1 says re-entry is allowed: walk the square first, then the inside.
- The transept-vault works (Oct–Dec 2026) may put up scaffolding: note it, but never write it into a cue.
