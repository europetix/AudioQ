WHY THIS EDITION
================
Built from scratch to the Yo Tours standard. This guide:
1. Is about Barcelona Cathedral, the cathedral of the Holy Cross and Saint Eulàlia (la Seu), in the Gothic Quarter.
   It is not the Sagrada Família, and the first track says so.
2. Walks one route, with directions at every step: from the Pla de la Seu and the facade, in at the main door, the
   Lepanto chapel, the choir screen and the baptistery, up the left aisle to the Sant Iu door, up to the roof by lift,
   the choir, the crypt, behind the high altar, through the cloister door into the cloister and the Chapter Hall, and
   out at the Santa Eulàlia door to Carrer del Bisbe.
3. Is Mass-proof: the cathedral is a working church. The Lepanto chapel is heard from its doorway, never inside during
   a service; the choir and the crypt have their own lines if they are closed (listen at the choir's entrance, or from
   the top of the crypt stair, where the tomb is in view).
4. Takes in the roof by the small lift (expect a wait; the lift tracks can be heard in the queue or at the top), the
   choir and the painted Golden Fleece shields above its stalls, the crypt and Santa Eulàlia's marble tomb, the
   cloister with its geese and the dancing egg on its fountain, and the Chapter Hall with Bartolomé Bermejo's Pietà.
5. Tells legends as legends, once each, with who tells them: the thirteen geese, the Lepanto Christ dodging a
   cannonball, the finger.
6. Corrects the popular errors: Saint Eulàlia's tomb is marble, not alabaster; 1448 is the date of the cloister, not of
   the cathedral; the front was built in 1887-1890 from a design drawn in 1408; Gaudí built nothing here.
7. Checks every fact against official and scholarly sources (the cathedral's own pages, the city history museum, the
   Gothic-art scholarship; Nov 2026), with independent audits of the facts and of the route and listening.
8. Works offline once the MP3s are on your phone. Headphones only: it is a church. No prices, hours or tickets.
9. Three lengths (one recording, three folders):
   Full tour   @@N_FULL@@ tracks, about 1 h 30
   Essential   @@N_ESS@@ tracks, about 1 h:      @@ESSENTIAL@@
   Highlights  @@N_HI@@ tracks, about 30 min:   @@HIGHLIGHTS@@
   The times include walking; add any wait for the roof lift. In the shorter versions a short spoken line at the end
   of a track tells you the next stop.
10. The Yo Tours sound: voice finishing, a soft room tone and a short chime before each track. MP3s are labelled for phones.

HOW TO MAKE THE AUDIO (on a Mac)
================================
English:          double-click generate_audio.command  (voice 1 = Ava)
                  option 1 = all three lengths (records once; folders _Essential and _Highlights)
                  option 2 = two voice samples (018 Europe's most exclusive club + 030 thirteen geese)
                  option 3 = Highlights only (quickest; option 1 later reuses those recordings)
Other languages:  double-click generate_audio_ES_FR_DE.command and pick the language, then the same options.
Needs internet, python3, ffmpeg (brew install ffmpeg) and an up-to-date edge-tts:
  python3 -m pip install --user --upgrade edge-tts
If it stops, run it again: finished tracks are skipped.
