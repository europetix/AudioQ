## 3. Wording that differs from PLAN, and why
- **012:** "after the siege of 1714" → "In 1714, … during the War of Succession": A4 names no siege. "imprisoned an earlier Honorata": the page says "this bell"; "earlier" is ours, consistent with S17 (today's bell is of 1865). The 1393 date is spoken as "before the end of the 1300s" and la Tomasa's 1758 is not spoken (three-date limit).
- **013:** gargoyles: Claperós's "many gargoyles" were for the **cloister** (S33), so "made many of them for the cloister". "the old medieval paving" → "the original floor tiles" (S35, O6 say "original", not "medieval"). The second lightning date is spoken as "sixty-four years later" (date limit).
- **014:** the two pinnacles are "tall Gothic pinnacles that flank the church" (O6 says only "laterals"). No gargoyle animal is named (O7; 013 has the city's list, attributed).
- **015:** **F17.5 reads O6 as "from the roof: … the Bishop's Palace …". O6 actually lists them as "a pocs metres de la Catedral".** The track therefore names them as the cathedral's neighbours "within a few streets" and says some may be hidden; after O7 the lead can turn the ticked ones into pointed looks ("the square tower with…"). Suggest correcting F17.5's wording for other writers. The bookend is unnamed ("the other great church … the one people so often mistake this one for") because lint bans "Sagrada Fam" outside 001; cut it if O7 doesn't see it.
- **016:** the Bonafè line gives no year ("half a century later"); "team effort" paraphrased, no quotation marks.
- **018:** **Lead's decision 5 applied: no king named.** Default sentence: "Among the arms are those of distant kings." Drop-in after O8 finds the shield (replace that sentence; 018 only; lint allows the name only here): "Among them is the shield of a boy king, Louis the Second of Hungary and Bohemia, then about twelve years old." The 1431 date is spoken as "two years after the founding". O5 is attributed as "a history of it on the cathedral's website" (it is Pere Molas's article there).
- **019:** "three days before, on 2 March" → "On the second of March, during the funeral rites for the emperor Maximilian … a catafalque stood here": GS1 calls 2 March "the second day of the exequies", so the rites were not one day. "fifteen saints and angels" → "fifteen full-length figures" (S9's words). The Annunciation is on the stair, not the pulpit (S9): line cut for length. The bishop's chair is stated, not pointed at (O8).

Not used (F18.24: no "never existed" in our voice); its title phrase is quoted, attributed. "A century ago" → "A century and more ago" (the sceptics were of the 19th and early 20th centuries).
