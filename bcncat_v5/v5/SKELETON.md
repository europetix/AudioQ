## Binding design decisions
1. **Not the Sagrada Família.** 001 says it in its first lines, warmly, with one sentence on what this is instead
   (the Gothic cathedral of Barcelona, Santa Creu i Santa Eulàlia).
2. **Route = Variant A** (main door in, cloister out to Carrer del Bisbe) with ALT-B (back through the main door) and
   ALT-C (Santa Eulàlia door in) held for O1. Facade and square first (outside); the roof from the left transept; then
   the choir, the crypt, the ambulatory/presbytery; then the cloister and the Chapter Hall; out.
3. **Mass-proof.** The Lepanto / Blessed Sacrament chapel is skippable: listened to from the doorway, with "if Mass is
   on, don't go in; you can hear this from the door". The choir and the crypt each carry a one-line fallback for Mass
   time ("if it's closed, listen from…"). No track asks people to walk into a chapel during a service.
4. **The roof** is a block of 3–5 tracks: one played in the lift queue (no view needed), then the terraces (only what
   O7 verifies is named; DATED/AR device not mentioned unless verified). 30-min version: roof dropped (ALT-30R).
5. **Legends told as legends** with who tells them: the 13 geese (tradition; geese recorded since the 16th century;
   nobody knows why geese), the Lepanto Christ's cannonball (first told 1762; the cathedral now says the carving may
   be older than the battle), the finger, the Saracen head (and today's criticism, evenly), the bell Honorata.
6. **Corrections spoken once, where the story is good:** marble sarcophagus (not alabaster; the alabaster is Sant
   Oleguer's effigy); 1448 = the cloister; facade 1887–1890 from a 1408 design, cimbori 1913 over the entrance;
   Gaudí built nothing here (he drew a losing facade entry); Barcelona's cathedral is not the Sagrada Família.
7. **One home per story;** no repeated payoffs; no closing one-line motto in more than a third of tracks; vary
   attributions (Park Güell lesson).
8. **Directions:** "as you face the high altar", fixed features (doors by name, the choir, the crypt stair, the
   transept, the lift chapel, the cloister door), no compass words, no distances; chapel numbers only if O5 reads the
   official plan. Works in the transept (Oct–Dec 2026) = DATED; cues must work with or without scaffolding (O2).
9. **Practical lines once, in 001:** headphones; respect worship (silence, no photos during services, covered
   shoulders if the cathedral states a dress rule — only if sourced); the lift may have a wait; offline. No prices,
   hours, ticket advice.
10. **Lengths:** Full ≈ 90 (honest arithmetic: ~125 wpm + walking + lift wait), Essential ≈ 60, Highlights ≈ 30.
    If Full can't be 90 honestly, say so; never pad.
