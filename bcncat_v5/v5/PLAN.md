### The route (binding order; placements to be confirmed on site)
**What the research settles** (ROUTE §0–§2, OFF unless marked): the public doors are the main door on the Pla de la Seu (ten steps up), the **Santa Eulàlia door** on Carrer del Bisbe (into the cloister; the step-free access) and the **Sant Iu door**; the cloister also has the **Pietat door** and, inside, the **white marble door** to the church. The **lift** leaves from the chapel of the Holy Innocents (Sants Innocents) **beside the Sant Iu door**; the Sant Iu door, its bell tower and the great organ in the gallery over it are on the **left** as you face the high altar (OFF + INF, two SEC blogs). The **white marble cloister door** is under the other bell tower, in the same bay on the **right** (INF). The **choir** stands in the middle of the nave in front of the crypt; the **choir screen** (trascor) faces the main door; the **crypt** lies under the presbytery, open to view from the nave. The **Lepanto chapel** = the chapel of the Blessed Sacrament (Santíssim) = the old chapter house, **with Sant Oleguer's tomb under the crucifix** (OFF, Revista 2026); INF: **on the right, near the main door** (encyclopaedia's chapel 28) [O4]. The **baptistery** is the first chapel on the **left** inside the main door (SEC) [O11]. The **Chapter Hall** museum is "under the cloister gallery, next to the chapel dedicated to Saint Lucia" (OFF).
**What it does not settle:** which door ticket holders use to enter and leave, and whether the flow is one way (**O1, the No. 1 question**); the transept-vault scaffolding (O2); the lift's queue (O3); the Lepanto chapel's view from the door (O4); **every individual chapel's position (O5: the official plan)**; Jewish memory (O6); the roof's sightlines and towers (O7); the choir's entrance, pulpit, cathedra and raised seats (O8); the cloister's corners, the pond and the fountain (O9); the crypt's opening (O10); the founders' coffins (O16: FACTS_GAPS G22 puts them on the sacristy wall, on the right of the high altar, so that is the default here); the ambulatory's chapel signs (O17). **Why this order:**
1. **The ticket's six spaces in one loop with no doubling back** except the lift's up-and-down (ROUTE §5 design rule): the lift is in the left transept bay, the cloister door in the right one, the choir and the crypt stair between them.
2. **The square first, the city's Roman corner first** (002): the bishops never moved, so the story starts where the town began, then the 1408/1887 front you are about to walk through (004).
3. **Inside the door, a sweep right → centre → left** (Lepanto chapel → choir screen → baptistery), all within a few steps of the main door [O4, O11], so the doorway-only chapel (decision 3) costs nothing if Mass is on.
4. **The choir screen at the door, not with the choir** (change from SKELETON): it faces you as you enter (ROUTE S5), it stays visible when the choir is closed, and its Renaissance sculptor is a different story from the Gothic stalls.
5. **The roof from the left aisle** (decision 2): the Sant Iu door and its organ are on the way to the lift; two queue tracks turn the wait into stories (013 needs no view).
6. **The choir after the roof** because the lift brings you back to the left transept bay beside it; the crypt next, at the foot of the choir (G85: its entrance is next to the choir); the ambulatory from the crypt stair round to the right side of the altar, the founders by the sacristy door (G22), and the cloister door, where the walk turns into the cloister.
7. **1936 at the cloister door, looking back** (027): everything the listener has just seen is the evidence; the spot is a fixed feature and needs no chapel position.
8. **The cloister as a loop ending at the Chapter Hall and the Santa Eulàlia door**: the fountain pavilion stands near the door from the church (G80), so it comes first; the museum is "next to the chapel dedicated to Saint Lucia" (OFF; G81 the same wing), so 032 → 034 is one short walk; the final cloister order follows O9 (§1 ALT-K9).
