Conventions: "as you face the high altar" is always "quand vous faites face au maître-autel" (never shortened to "face au", which could read as "opposite"). "If Mass is on, don't go in" becomes "Si une messe est en cours, n'entrez pas". "If the choir is closed, listen at its entrance" becomes "Si le chœur est fermé, écoutez à son entrée". "If the crypt is closed, stay at the top of its stair: the tomb is in view below" becomes "Si la crypte est fermée, restez en haut de son escalier: le tombeau est visible en contrebas". Centuries are in words ("le quatorzième siècle" for "the 1300s"). Decades are in digits ("les années 1880"). Every English year is present as digits. Tense is the passé composé or passé simple for the dead.

Typography (deliberate): the checker counts words by splitting on whitespace, and Python counts no-break spaces as whitespace too. So colons, question marks and guillemets are written without the usual French spaces ("compagnie: beaucoup", «texte»). A typographic pass can add narrow no-break spaces for the PDF if the word check is adjusted. The voice is unaffected.
