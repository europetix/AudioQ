## Ambiguities and choices
- 026 "His grandmother, Countess Ermessenda": "Su abuela" would be ambiguous after "la pareja", so ES says "La abuela del conde".
- 031 "The canon who paid for it": after the new sentence, "it" could mean the door (end of the 1300s) or the carving (about a century later). ES reads it as the carving ("lo pagó", masculine for el relieve), because he kneels in the relief. **Please confirm.** If he paid for the door, use "la pagó".
- 008 "an art historian" (gender not given): masculine generic "un historiador del arte".
- 028 "It treats both as open questions": it is really one either/or question. ES says "Deja ambas abiertas".
- 013 "Saint John's Eve, the city's midsummer night": rendered "la verbena de San Juan, la noche de pleno verano de la ciudad".
- 036 "the city's own": rendered "y es de la ciudad".
- 017 "the keen-eyed": rendered "los de vista aguda".
- 011 "rolled its eyes": rendered "giraba los ojos", meaning the moving eyes, not the gesture.
