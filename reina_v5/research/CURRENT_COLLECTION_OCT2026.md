# Museo Reina Sofía: current collection layout (research date 17 Oct 2026)

> **Correction, 17 Oct 2026 (fact audit):** the official easy-read plan (Oct 2026–Feb 2027) shows the **Nouvel roof terraces open**.
> "Cerrado por un tiempo" (closed for a while) refers to **Sabatini Floor 3**, not the terraces. Where this file says the
> terraces are closed, read it as Sabatini Floor 3. Track 056 (Nouvel terrace) stays; 055 keeps its fallback line.


Method: WebSearch and WebFetch only, one page at a time. Official sources first (museoreinasofia.es, recursos.museoreinasofia.es, cms.museoreinasofia.es).
Grades: **OFF** = read on an official museum page or PDF. **SEC2** = two reputable independent sources. **UNCONF** = could not confirm.
Caveat: the room web pages list only the first 12 works per room ("More artworks" is not expanded), and most room pages show no dates. A date appears below only where an official page gave one.

## 1. Collection presentations on view now

### 1A. Sabatini Floor 2: "Avant-garde Territories: City, Architecture and Magazines" (ES: "Territorios de vanguardia: ciudad, arquitectura y revistas")

- Page: https://museoreinasofia.es/en/collections/avant-garde-territories (ES: https://www.museoreinasofia.es/coleccion/territorios-vanguardia). Building and floor: Sabatini, Floor 2 (OFF).
- **Opening date:** the official page gives none. This floor is the avant-garde episode of the 2021 rehang "Communicating Vessels. Collection 1881-2021", presented on 27 Nov 2021. Sources: esMadrid https://w3.esmadrid.com/en/node/24447 (names "Avant-Garde Territories: City, Architecture and Magazines" on Sabatini Floor 2) and e-flux https://www.e-flux.com/announcements/433084/new-collection (27 Nov 2021, Floor 2 = late 19th c. to first quarter of 20th c.). RTVC/EFE reported on 14 Sep 2021 that about a dozen avant-garde rooms had been unveiled early: https://rtvc.es/gaceta-de-arte-encuentra-su-sitio-en-la-nueva-coleccion-permanente-del-museo-reina-sofia/ . **Grade: SEC2.** Officially, this floor is the last to be rehung (2028) (OFF, press releases above).
- Theme (OFF): modernity through three foundations: cities (Paris, Madrid, Barcelona, also New York and Latin America), architecture and urbanism, and magazines. It also covers artists' public positions in the 1930s.
- The presentation page lists only 8 rooms (201.01–204.01). The room pages' previous/next links chain on through 206.03. Full chain as read on the official room pages (OFF):

## 2. Guernica and surroundings (OFF)

- *Guernica* (Picasso, 1937, oil on canvas, 349.3 x 776.6 cm, DE00050) is in **Room 205.10**, Sabatini Floor 2.
- The **sketches and Dora Maar's photo report** are in 205.10 itself (e.g. *Man Falling*, DE00082, "Exhibited in Room 205.10"). Two horse studies, *Estudio para el caballo (II)* and *(IV)*, are in **205.09**.
- **1937 Spanish Pavilion material**: **Room 205.09** (immediately before Guernica). It holds the Calder *Mercury Fountain (Maquette)*, a 1:3 replica (DO00992); the Sert & Lacasa pavilion model (AS11702); Picasso *La femme au vase*; Miró *Aidez l'Espagne*; and Solana *Procesión de la muerte*.
- **Next room after Guernica: 205.11**, The Spanish Night (flamenco). Room **205.08** before 205.09 held Dumile Feni *African Guernica* until 22 Sep 2026; its current content is UNCONF.
- Richard Serra *Equal-Parallel: Guernica-Bengasi* (1986) and *Inversion*: **Room 102** (Floor 1). Sources: https://museoreinasofia.es/en/collections/artwork/equal-parallel-guernica-bengasi and https://www.museoreinasofia.es/en/collections/room-102 . The current plan lists "Collection: Richard Serra".

## 4. Visitor orientation

- **Entrances (OFF):** Sabatini, Calle Santa Isabel 52, and Nouvel, Ronda de Atocha 2. "It is recommended entering via the Nouvel Building … if you have already purchased your ticket online." Cloakroom and lockers are at both entrances. Sources: https://www.museoreinasofia.es/en/visit and map.pdf.
- **Lifts (OFF):** "two lift towers in the Sabatini Building, to the left and right of the main entrance, connecting Floors 1, 2, 3 and 4". A third, smaller internal lift serves Floors 0, 1 and 3. Two Nouvel lifts serve Floors 0–3. The medical point is "next to the Tower 2 lift" in the Sabatini Cloister. Information points are on Sabatini Floor 2 and at the inter-building connection on Floor 1 (meeting point). Source: https://www.museoreinasofia.es/en/visit/accessible-visits .
- **Glass towers (OFF):** "two towers-lifts made from glass and steel were erected to frame the entrance". Architects: Vázquez de Castro and Íñiguez de Onzoño, with Ian Ritchie advising (from autumn 1987). A third tower on Calle del Hospital is for services and artworks. Source: https://museoreinasofia.es/en/museo/architectural-heritage/hospital-museum .
- **Room-number signage:** **UNCONF.** No official page I found describes door signs. The leaflets cite only "Sala 205.10" for Guernica, and the Floor 4 map numbers spaces 1–21. The audio guide should use room titles and landmarks as well as numbers.
- **Usual route:** Floor 4 is officially "three linear routes", Rooms 1→21 (OFF). For Floor 2, only the web previous/next order 201.01→206.03 is available, not an official walking route (UNCONF).
