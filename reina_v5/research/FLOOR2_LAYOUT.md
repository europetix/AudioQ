# Sabatini Floor 2: physical layout research (17 Oct 2026)

Scope: the collection rooms 201.01 → 206.03 ("Avant-garde Territories", the Floor 2 episode of "Communicating Vessels", 2021). The goal is correct walking cues for the audio guide.
Method: WebSearch and WebFetch only, one fetch at a time. I also looked at the plan images already saved in `research/map/`: `f2.png` and `plano_full.png` are crops of the official easy-read plan, and `floor4_official_map.png` comes from the official Floor 4 PDF.
Grades: **OFF** = an official museum source. **SEC2** = reputable secondary sources. **UNCONF** = not confirmed, or my own inference.

## BOTTOM LINE

1. **An official Floor 2 room plan exists, but I could not read it.** WebFetch cannot decode images, and the IIIF copy was blocked. Someone needs to open it before any wing or left/right cue is scripted. See section 5.
2. **No text source I found says which wing holds 201, 202, 204, 205 or 206, or which way the numbers run.** Everything about wing assignment below is UNCONF.
3. **The web room numbers were renumbered after 2021**, and rooms 203, 207 and 208 have dropped out. Any number painted at a doorway may not match the website. Cue by landmark and title as well as number (see section 4).
4. Guernica's neighbours are certain: **205.09 (the 1937 Pavilion room) → 205.10 Guernica → 205.11 (The Spanish Night)** (OFF). Guernica is in the middle of the back wing, on the cloister side (OFF, plan).

## 2. Arrival on Floor 2 (lifts, stairs) and where the rooms start

These points come from the official easy-read visitor plan, valid Oct 2026–Feb 2027, NIPO 194-26-001-7, which I viewed in plano_full.png and f2.png. On that plan the **front (Calle Santa Isabel, glass towers) is at the bottom** and the back wing is at the top, towards Nouvel. Grade **OFF**. Sources: https://cms.museoreinasofia.es/sites/default/files/Visita/plano.pdf (also https://recursos.museoreinasofia.es/Visita/plano.pdf). EN version: https://recursos.museoreinasofia.es/Visita/map.pdf.

- **Shape:** a square ring around the cloister. The front wing is wider than the ring and sticks out left and right beyond the side wings, making a T-shaped front. There is a small bay on the outer side of the back wing, at its centre.
- **The two glass lift towers** stand outside the front wing. Each connects by a short passage into the **cloister gallery along the front wing** (shown as a pale corridor). The pale gallery also runs up the inner side of the left and right wings. **It is not drawn along the back wing**; there the Guernica block sits against the cloister.
- **Information point ("i"):** in the front cloister gallery, between the two lift passages and slightly right of centre. The accessible-visits page confirms an information point on Sabatini Floor 2 (OFF: https://www.museoreinasofia.es/en/visit/accessible-visits).
- **Staircases (pictograms):** (a) on the front, just left of the left lift tower, the same position as the main stair beside the left tower on Floor 4; (b) at the outer back-left corner; (c) at the outer back-right, beside a small annex that has a further lift; (d) on the outer side of the right wing. The plan does not label any of them "main staircase" (UNCONF which one visitors use).
- **Third lift:** in the back-right annex. The accessible-visits page says the internal lift serves only Floors 0, 1 and 3, so what this lift is remains UNCONF.
- **WCs with baby change:** at the far left and far right ends of the front wing. **"Education and mediation"** areas (blue) are at both front ends. There is a **refuge area and emergency exit** at the front-right inner corner, and **benches** in the right-wing cloister gallery and at the front right.
- **Where the rooms start (201.01):** UNCONF. The text sources give only the web order (201.01 first; 201.01's page has no "previous"). The official room plan in section 1 should show it.
- The museum recommends **entering through Nouvel** if you have an online ticket (OFF, collection pages). From Nouvel, visitors reach Sabatini through the Floor 1 connection and then take a lift or stairs (OFF: accessible-visits page, "connection between buildings").

## 6. Official Floor 2 room plan, viewed (17 Oct 2026, lead, in Chrome)
Source: https://recursos.museoreinasofia.es/styles/large_landscape/public/Colecci%C3%B3n/plano-colecciones-sabatini-2.png.webp (OFF).
The image is drawn with the front wing (glass lift towers, street side) on the RIGHT. Turned so the front is at the bottom:
- Front wing, between the two lift towers: 201.01–201.04 (two rows).
- Front wing beyond the right-hand tower: 202.01–202.03, with a stair; further out the front extension 203.01–203.02 (not in today's web chain).
- Right side wing, front → back: 204.01, 204.02, 204.03, stair at the back corner.
- Back wing, two rows along the cloister, from the right-hand corner: 205.01/205.02, 205.03 bis, 205.04/205.07, 205.05/205.06,
  205.08 (outer bay, centre), 205.09, 205.10 Guernica (black dot, cloister side, centre), 205.11/205.13, 205.12/205.14,
  205.15/205.17, 205.16/205.17, then the left-hand corner.
- Left side wing, back → front: 206.01, 206.02, 206.03, stair at the back corner. 206.03 ends at the front wing.
- Front wing beyond the left-hand tower: 208.01–208.05 and the extension 207.01–207.02 (not in today's web chain; may be closed).
So today's chain 201 → 202 → 204 → 205 → 206 is one loop round the cloister, anticlockwise when the front is at the bottom
