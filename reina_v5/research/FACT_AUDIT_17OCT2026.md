# Fact audit, Reina Sofía audio guide v5 (17 Oct 2026 edition)

Auditor: independent fact check. No track was edited.
Method: WebFetch on museoreinasofia.es, one page at a time (about 75 fetches). No page asked for human verification. Note that WebFetch returns a summary with short quotes, not a full transcript. Floor-plan facts come from the official Oct 2026–Feb 2027 plan (source/plano_oct2026_feb2027.pdf), read visually from research/map/plano_full.png.
Re-read after the coordinator's 17 Oct edits: 001, 003, 011, 014, 031, 034. The edited navigation sentences are covered in section B.

Verdicts: **OK** = matches an official page. **WRONG** = contradicted by an official page. **UNVERIFIED** = not found on any official page I read. **HEDGE** = broadly supported but stated more firmly or more precisely than the source allows, or the sources conflict.
URL shorthand: `A/` = https://www.museoreinasofia.es/en/collections/artwork/ , `R/` = https://www.museoreinasofia.es/en/collections/ (room pages).

## Key finding on the terraces (affects 001, 055, 056, 057)
research/CURRENT_COLLECTION_OCT2026.md §0/§1C says the Nouvel terraces are "Temporarily closed". **That is a misreading of the plan.** On the official plan for Oct 2026–Feb 2027, the Floor 3 legend has two separate keys. Light blue is "Terrazas: espacio para relajarse y ver esculturas públicas", and those are the Nouvel terraces. White hatching is "Cerrado por un tiempo", and that key covers only Sabatini Floor 3. The overview strip agrees: Nouvel "3 Terrazas", Sabatini "Cerrado 3". The services list also includes "Podrás hacer una pausa aquí: … Terrazas". So the guide is right to end on the terrace, and the "if the terrace is closed" fallbacks can stay. The research file should be corrected.
