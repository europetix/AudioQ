Sources: each work's own page on museoreinasofia.es ("Exhibited in Room …"), the museum's collection pages, the
Oct 2026 – Feb 2027 visitor plan (source/plano_oct2026_feb2027.pdf) and press releases (16 Feb 2026, 28 May 2026).

## Verdict: rebuild (not repairable)
- 49 single-work stops checked: **22 on display**, **20 not on display**, **7 not found** in the collection.
- Of the 22 on display, at least 6 are in a different room from the old guide (Guernica 205.10 not 206; Calder's Mercury
  Fountain is a 1:3 maquette in 205.09; Capa 205.16 and Renau 205.05, not "207"; Miró Portrait II 205.09; Serra Room 102).
- The old "Floor 1 · After the War" section (tracks 031–056) describes a display that no longer exists: the 1950s–70s
  collection is off view until Floor 3 reopens in 2027 (press release 16 Feb 2026). Tàpies, Saura, Millares, Bacon,
  Klein, Fontana, Bourgeois, López, Equipo Crónica, Arroyo, Genovés' Embrace: not on display.
- Floor 4 changed completely on 18 Feb 2026 ("Contemporary Art: 1975–Present", Rooms 1–21, three routes); the old
  Floor 4 tracks describe a hang that is gone.
- Many stops name a style, not a work ("Tàpies, matter painting", "Lucio Muñoz, worked wood") — unverifiable.
- Same template bug as the old Prado guide: header "TOUR No. 46 · V4.5 HUMANIZED".
