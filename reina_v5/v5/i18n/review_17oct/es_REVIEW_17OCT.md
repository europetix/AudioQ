# ES review — Reina Sofía, 57 tracks, 17 Oct 2026

Reviewer: independent editor. All 57 tracks were read sentence by sentence against the English, and every last paragraph was checked for navigation. The self-check still passes: `assemble.py --check … es` gives OK for 001–057 and exits 0. Every @title starts with «Sala NNN ·», or «Jardín · Extra ·» / «Terraza Nouvel ·» where the English uses those. The opening and closing titles have no room. All 14 Extras carry «· Extra ·».

## Sync change
- 055: the English changed from "LGTBIQ+" to "LGBTIQ+". In ES, «derechos LGTBIQ+» became «derechos LGBTIQ». 038 had «LGBTIQ+» and now also reads «LGBTIQ». The two tracks now match, follow the English letter order, which is also the usual Mexican and Latin American order, and have no "+" for the voice to misread.

## Other changes (track: before → after, why)
Navigation and room titles
- 026 P6 and 027 @where: «André Breton. El mago del surrealismo» → «André Breton. Mago del surrealismo». This is the official Spanish title, checked on coleccion/sala-205013.
- 027 P7 and 028 @where: «El tiempo universal de la cultura. Gaceta de Arte» → «La hora universal de la cultura. Gaceta de Arte». This is the official title, checked on coleccion/sala-205014.
- 020 P6: "Pase la sala 205.08 hasta la sala 205.09" → "Siga, pasando la sala 205.08, hasta la sala 205.09". The old wording could be heard as "pass through room 205.08 up to 205.09". The new wording keeps the English "walk on, past".
- 034 P4: "su Casa con palmera" → "La casa de la palmera". 011 uses the official title, and the two halves now agree.

Official titles
- 026 P6: "El gran masturbador" → "Rostro del Gran Masturbador". 027 @title changed the same way. This is the official Spanish title on sala-205013.
- 014 @where and P5: "Ballet triádico" → "Ballet Triádico", which is how the Spanish text of sala-20403 writes it.
- 045 P3: "una serie sobre la gravedad y el equilibrio" → "la serie Gravedad y equilibrio". The English names a series, and the official name is «Gravedad y equilibrio» (coleccion/obra/la-mesa).
- 015 @where: "Plan Macià" → "Pla Macià", the name of the work, as in the English @where. The body still says "Plan Macià", as the English body does.
- 004 P4: "grabados" → "aguafuertes". The English says etchings, and @where already said aguafuertes.

Quotations
- 048 P4: the reported speech became the museum's verbatim Spanish, checked on coleccion/obra/nothing-under-chair: «cuadros esperando». Y luego: «Incapaces por el momento de ser otra cosa, esperan». This matches how the English introduces it ("And then:").
- 043: «Te he arrebatado tu alma» is verbatim on coleccion/sala-9, which reads: …y él decía: "Te he arrebatado tu alma". The speaker is Yannis, Guibert's fictional version of Barceló, as Guibert describes the sitting. That matches the English ("As Hervé Guibert told it… his friend Miquel Barceló said"), so it is kept. «Pintar es borrar» is also verbatim on sala-9.
- 012: «monótono» is kept. The ES gives the press's words as reported speech, not as a quotation, so the English meaning applies. If this is ever quoted, the Spanish page's «falto de color» must be used.
- 002: «hacer sitio» is verbatim on coleccion/obra/toki-egin-homenaje-san-juan-de-la-cruz, so it is now confirmed.

Hedges and meaning
- 001 P7: "un proceso que termina en 2028" → "que debe concluir en 2028". The English says "due to finish".
- 004 P2: "Se reorganizará en 2028" → "Está previsto reorganizarla en 2028". The English says "due to be rehung".
- 057 P5: "se está renovando" → "se está reorganizando". The English says "is being rehung", and 001 uses the same word.
- 017 P4: "la Exposición de París" → "la Exposición Internacional de París". This is the Paris world's fair, as named in 021.
- 030 P1: "La ciudad estaba sitiada" → "Esta ciudad estaba sitiada". The English says "this city", meaning Madrid.
- 034 P5: "pasando por Guernica" → "pasando por el Guernica". The painting is meant, not the town.
- 050 P2: "hay algunos" → "hay algunos de esos artistas". The pronoun had nothing to refer to.
- 028 P6: "a una montaña de casa" → "de vuelta a una montaña de su tierra". The English says "back to a mountain at home".
- 049 P4: "y sin amabilidad" → "y sin complacencia". The English "not kindly" reads more naturally this way.

Unifying the two halves, plus naturalness and TTS

- 001 P5: "se la salta" → "así que no se visita".

- 048 P5: "de verdad escultóricas" → "verdaderamente escultóricas".
- 055 P5: "Detalle rico" → "Mucho detalle".
- 057 P7: "Recorrió el itinerario" → "Hizo el recorrido". In the Floor 4 tracks, "itinerario" means Route I, II or III.

Checked and left as they were: «Alhacena» is spelled with the h (035). The Schlemmer material in 014 is kept, as the lead asked.
