## Things in the English that looked wrong or need a look
- 012: The Spanish text on sala-20401 lists the French press's words as "frío, geométrico, intelectual, falto de color y… extranjero" ("lacking colour"). The English says "monotonous". I translated the English ("monótono").
- 014: According to WebFetch, the visible works list on sala-20403 shows only 4 works (Lipchitz, Léger, Gris, Ozenfant) and no Schlemmer. The Spanish text reportedly says the Triadic Ballet came before the Weimar school was founded. Please check that the Schlemmer works really hang in 204.03.
- 205.07: the Spanish page shows its title only in English (see above).
- 022: The official Spanish gloss "Ayudad a España" uses the vosotros form. Following rule 6, I said "un llamado a ayudar a España" instead.
- 021: "móvil" is used for Calder's mobile, because it is the standard art term and not the phone. "Coches" became "automóviles", and "hoja de bloc" became "hoja de cuaderno", to keep the vocabulary neutral.
- 001: The English says only "roof terrace of the Nouvel building", so I wrote "la terraza del edificio Nouvel" and did not add "planta 3".
