# DE notes — tracks 029–057 (Katja)

Files: reina_i18n/de/029.json … 057.json. `assemble.py --check … de` passes for all 29 tracks. Every track has fewer words than the English, between 1.6% and 13% shorter. Built with de_scripts/de_029_057.py in the scratchpad.

## Titles
- No official Spanish titles were needed for DE, so no museum pages were fetched.
- The museum has no German room titles, so room titles are translated freely and given in „…“. Room numbers are exact digits ("Saal 205.15", "Saal 1"). Floors are "Ebene 1/2/3/4".
- Established German titles used: Das Private ist politisch, Schule von Athen, Tod Mariens (Mantegna), Die zwei Fridas, Dem Freund, der mir das Leben nicht gerettet hat (Guibert).
- Titles kept in their original language: Semiotics of the Kitchen, Day's End, Hello Boys, Knives (glossed as "Messer"), The Wasteland, New Images from Spain, A Journey, Nothing Under a Chair / Nothing (glossed as "Nichts"), Un chien andalou, L'âge d'or, Le Cri paisible, Penetrable, Mediterránea, Pastorale, Serra de Busa, Las auras frías (glossed as "Kalte Auren").

## Choices to review
- **Route I/II/III.** Written as "Route eins/zwei/drei" so the TTS does not read "I" as a letter.
- **LGBTIQ+.** Written as "LGBTIQ-plus" (038) and "LGTBIQ-plus" (055), so there is no "+" symbol. The English spells it differently in the two tracks (038 LGBTIQ+, 055 LGTBIQ+), and the German keeps each spelling. Please make one consistent across languages.
- **MIT (045).** Spelled out as "Massachusetts Institute of Technology", because the TTS could read "MIT" as the German word "mit".
- **Extras.** In the text they are called "ein kurzer Extra-Titel", and the next main stop is "die nächste Hauptstation". "Nächster Titel" is never used.
