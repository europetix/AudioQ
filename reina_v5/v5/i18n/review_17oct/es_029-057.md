- 047 «cuerpos insubordinados»: verbatim from sala-13.
- 048 de la Cruz, "Paintings waiting…": reported speech without quotation marks, because the page was unverified.
- 051 «una pintura más pintada»: verbatim from sala-17. "artificer" became artífice, without quotation marks.
- 052 "a dictatorship that occurred in black and white": reported speech, because I could not get it verbatim.
- 054 «Lo afro está en el centro»: the room title and slogan, verified.
- 055 «El trabajo de igualdad en el Reina está todavía en construcción»: verbatim from sala-21. "dignity, intimacy and permanence" is reported speech.

## Things in the English worth a look
- 035: "Alacena" against the official "Alhacena".
- 035/034: the English Room 1 title, "Structures of Affect of the Spanish Transition", does not match the Spanish page («Estructuras afectivas de la Transición»).
