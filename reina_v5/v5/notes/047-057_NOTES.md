The AUDIT and PLAN say they are open (that legend refers to Sabatini Floor 3). The terrace web page says nothing about access. I followed PLAN: the 055 cue ends with "If the terrace is closed today, the last two stops work anywhere", and 056/057 can be heard anywhere. 056 does describe the works as being in front of you, though. Please confirm on site.
2. **Chillida "logo" (049 title).** The logo DRAWING (1986, ink) is in **Room 16**, not 15 (artwork page: "Exhibited in Room 16"). Room 15 has Chillida's *Reina Sofía*, 1986, a gold lost-wax piece 5.7 × 5.1 cm. The drawing's page says the symbol "was transferred to several gold medals… one presented to Queen Sofía at the CARS inauguration". The Room 15 page does not say this gold piece is one of those medals, so 049 only puts the two facts side by side. 050 covers the drawing. I kept the PLAN title.
3. **lint.py false positive:** `\beuro` matches "European". 051 now says "the Common Market" instead of "European Economic Community". Worth fixing the regex for other writers.
4. Soto's *Penetrable* is made to be walked through (official page). Whether visitors may enter now is not stated, so 056 says "Follow the staff's guidance on whether you can step inside today."
