# Notes, tracks 001–014 (17 Oct 2026)

All facts below were read with WebFetch on official museoreinasofia.es pages (or recursos.museoreinasofia.es PDFs), one page at a time. Full URL lists are in each track's @sources.

## 001 Welcome (ANCHOR)
- Read: architectural-heritage/construction-hospital-general, architectural-heritage/hospital-museum, architectural-heritage (index), /en/collection (rehang to 2028). Local: source/plano_oct2026_feb2027.pdf + research/map/plano_full.png.
- Not used: "the museum opened in 1990" (hospital-museum page). It clashes with the usual 1992 date, and the track doesn't need it.
- The construction page puts Hermosilla's dismissal in 1769 "with Charles II now on the throne", an error on the site. The track says only that work stopped when Charles III died.
- **Lead to decide: are the Nouvel terraces open?** research/CURRENT_COLLECTION says "Temporarily closed". The AUDIT and PLAN say open. On the official plan's Planta 3 legend, "Cerrado por un tiempo" is the white/hatched key, i.e. Sabatini Floor 3. The light-blue Terrazas key is separate. I read that as terraces OPEN and wrote 001 without a hedge. If the terraces close, change the route sentence in 001.
- Information desk: the official plan shows an "i" by the Sabatini entrance on Floor 1. Door-sign format for room numbers is still unconfirmed, so 001 says "the way the museum names them", not "the way the signs say".
