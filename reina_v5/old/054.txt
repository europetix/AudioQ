 From dictatorship to democracy
 Slow down for the last stretch of this floor, because it carries more political weight than anything else in
 the building except Guernica itself.

 Franco died in November 1975, and Spain began the fragile, dangerous passage that Spaniards simply
 call la Transición — the Transition — from dictatorship back to democracy. The museum has built a
 gallery around those years, opened in 2025 as part of the country's official marking of fifty years of
 freedom. It gathers the art made in the thick of that uncertainty — when nobody yet knew whether the
 country would reconcile or fall back into violence.

 At its heart is a single, deceptively simple painting that became the emblem of the whole era, and beside
 it, a steel sculpture that ties this museum's newest history back to its oldest wound. They're the right
 place to end this floor. The painting first — just ahead.
