TRACK 064 · FLOOR 4                                W-FOCUS                             164 WORDS · ~1 MIN

 Pedro Lemebel, protest and performance
    WHERE YOU ARE
    Most likely photographs, video or documents rather than a single object — the record of performances and
    actions, defiant and tender at once.
    WHAT YOU SEE
    Pedro Lemebel, the Chilean writer and artist who, with Francisco Casas, formed the collective Las Yeguas del
    Apocalipsis — "The Mares of the Apocalypse" — staging fearless queer performances in the dangerous last
    years of the Pinochet dictatorship and after. His presence here is part of the museum's deliberate opening
    toward Latin America and toward voices long pushed to the margins.
    LISTEN FOR
    This is what the contemporary floor is for — to widen the story of modern art beyond Europe, beyond the
    canvas, beyond the straight and the official. Lemebel made his own body and his words the work, insisting on
    dignity and desire under a regime that denied both. Set him against Guernica downstairs and you can see one
    continuous thread across the whole museum: art as a way of refusing to be silenced. The roof is waiting, just
    along here.
