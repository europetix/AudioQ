TRACK 044 · FLOOR 1                                W-FOCUS                              150 WORDS · ~1 MIN

 Lucio Muñoz, worked wood
    WHERE YOU ARE
    A large surface that isn't canvas at all but wood — gouged, carved, burned and painted, so the picture is dug
    into a physical relief.
    WHAT YOU SEE
    Lucio Muñoz, a Madrid abstractionist who made his characteristic work on tablex — hardboard — which he
    carved, gouged and scorched into rugged, cave-like reliefs before staining them in earthy colour. The result
    sits between painting and sculpture, like a weathered wall or a furrowed landscape.
    LISTEN FOR
    He belongs to the same matter-minded generation as Tàpies and Millares — the conviction that raw, worked
    material says more after a war than smooth illusion ever could. But Muñoz's surfaces feel geological rather
    than wounded: ridges, hollows and grottoes you could imagine light moving across. Stand to one side and
    watch the carved relief throw its own shadows. The cool, lyrical strain of abstraction gives way now to the
    return of the recognisable world, a short way on.
