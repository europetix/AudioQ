ALL LANGUAGES, 047 (fixed in the English on 5 Oct): "grooms and huntsmen" was a literal rendering of the French "grooms et chasseurs". Here "chasseur" means a hotel bellboy (Soutine's Le Groom / Le Chasseur, 1925), not a hunter. The English now says "hotel pageboys and bellboys". Use FR "ses grooms et ses chasseurs d'hôtel", ES "botones de hotel", DE "Hotelpagen und Liftboys". Never use hunters, veneurs, Jäger or cazadores.
