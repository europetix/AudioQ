## 2) Problems only

### 019
- **NOTFOUND (low)** — "One was the poet Stéphane Mallarmé." The evidence names Mallarmé but never calls him a poet. Closest: FACTS_A, Jeunes filles au piano: "Stéphane Mallarmé and Roger Marx (a young official of the Beaux-Arts administration) pushed for Impressionist works…" (OFF). Fix: "One was Stéphane Mallarmé."

### 021
- **NOTFOUND (low)** — "and above all against Velázquez, the great Spanish painter of the royal court." The gloss on Velázquez has no source. Closest: FACTS_A, Claude Renoir en clown: "measure himself against the old masters, Velázquez in particular" (OFF). Fix: "With it, he meant to measure himself against the old masters, and above all against Velázquez."

### 023 (cue note)
- **Cue ambiguity** — @where: "a seated bather drying her leg on a white cloth by the water". The collection also holds the 1914 *Baigneuse assise s'essuyant une jambe*, whose title means "Seated Bather Drying Her Leg". If that painting hangs nearby, visitors may stop at the wrong one. Evidence: FACTS_A, Baigneuse assise (1914, 51 × 41, OFF). Fix for @where: "a bather with brown hair pinned up, drying her leg on a white cloth by the water".

### 025
- **NOTFOUND** — "A plate, a few apples, the biscuits of the title, all set on a chest." The museum's description has no biscuits, so the listener is sent to look for something unsourced. Closest: FACTS_A: "a very balanced composition with just a plate and a few apples arranged on a chest" (OFF). Fix: "A plate and a few apples, all set on a chest."
- **NOTFOUND** — "The roundness of each apple is built out of colour rather than shadow." The words "rather than shadow" have no source. Closest: FACTS_A: "expressing volume through colour are fully developed here" (OFF). Fix: "The roundness of each apple is built out of colour."
- **NOTFOUND** — "Landscapes, mostly." This overstates the source, which says "notably", not "mostly". Closest: FACTS_A Cézanne article: Domenica "bought more after his death, notably landscapes" (OFF). Fix: "Landscapes above all."
- **WEAK (placement)** — "the portraits of the painter's wife and son that hang nearby." The hang is unconfirmed, and these works may be at the Grand Palais until Jan 2027. Closest: FACTS_A "Cézanne et nous… Portrait de Madame Cézanne travels to it, unconfirmed" and G5 "Which Cézannes are away right now is not confirmed". Fix: "among them portraits of the painter's wife and son."
- **NOTFOUND (cue)** — "Now find Madame Cézanne, the painter's wife, seated in a garden." *Madame Cézanne au jardin* is described only as full-length and outdoors. Nothing says she is seated, and 026's own @where says "full-length woman in a garden". Closest: FACTS_A: "A full-length portrait outdoors, which is unusual" (OFF). Fix: "Now find Madame Cézanne, the painter's wife, painted full length in a garden."

### 027
