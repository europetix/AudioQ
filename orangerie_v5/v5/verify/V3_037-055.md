## 2) Problems only

### 2a) Fact problems

**037**
- CONTRA — "The museum reads them as the painting's mixed feelings, the comfort of the room and the wish to be out in that light."
  - Why: The museum calls the interior *oppressive*, and the colours "reflect these contradictory emotions" (oppressive inside, liberating outside). "Comfort" reverses this, and it also clashes with the track's own "close and heavy".
  - Evidence: FACTS_B Femmes au canapé: "oppressive interior opening onto the liberating outside world … Reds and greens … 'reflect these contradictory emotions'" [OFF].
  - Fix: "The museum reads them as the painting's mixed feelings, the weight of the room and the wish to be out in that light."
- WEAK — "Matisse cared more about the divan, and he repainted it to get the angle exactly right."
  - Why: The repainting is OFF. "Cared more about the divan" than the women is the writer's inference.
  - Evidence: "The two women at right are 'more or less sketched'. The divan was repainted to get the best angle." [OFF]
  - Fix: "The divan got more attention. Matisse repainted it to get the angle exactly right."
- WEAK — "Another room seen from the inside, another small world Matisse made out of the place where he happened to be staying." (Le Boudoir)
  - Why: The claim that Le Boudoir shows his Nice hotel is WEAK (wikipedia), and FACTS_B lists it under Contested item 3.
  - Evidence: FACTS_B Le Boudoir: "Setting: a room in Matisse's hotel in Nice … [WEAK]"; Contested 3.
  - Fix: "Another room seen from the inside, another small world Matisse made out of an ordinary interior."
- NOTFOUND — @where: "Le Boudoir, a smaller interior, may hang nearby."
  - Why: No dimensions exist for Femmes au canapé, and Le Boudoir's 73 × 60 is WEAK. So "smaller" has no support.
  - Evidence: FACTS_B: Femmes au canapé "dimensions … not found"; Le Boudoir size "WEAK".
  - Fix: "Le Boudoir, another interior, may hang nearby."
