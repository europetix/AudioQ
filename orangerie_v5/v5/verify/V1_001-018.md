## 2) Problems only

### 008 — WEAK
- Quote: "At times, the guns of the front could be heard at Giverny."
- Why: the only sources are a BBC clip and americanacademy. G2 06-3 grades this "WEAK/SEC" and G1 26-4 grades it "WEAK–SEC2". It is already hedged, but the support stays below SEC2.
- Closest evidence: G2 06-3 / 12-4 "Sources say only that the guns could be heard" (https://clip.cafe/art-on-the-bbc-2020/the-guns-could-be-heard-in-giverny/).
- Fix: "It is said that at times the guns of the front could be heard at Giverny." Or cut the sentence.

### 010 — NOTFOUND
- Quote: "So these painted willows have real trees for neighbours, just beyond the stone."
- Why: no sheet says there are trees right outside the north wall. The evidence covers only the almost blind north façade on the garden side (ORG-HIST). This is a vivid detail with no source.
- Closest evidence: FACTS_C §1.3 "Glass south (Seine) façade, almost blind north façade" [ORG-HIST] OFF.
- Fix: "So these painted willows have the real garden for a neighbour, just beyond the stone."

### 012 — WEAK
- Quote: "The museum calls that entrance hall a transition from the outside world."
- Why: the museum's sentence is "A vestibule provides access to the two rooms and marks the transition from the outside world." G5 also uses "vestibule" for the passage between the two ovals. So it is not clear that the museum means the entrance hall.
- Closest evidence: FACTS_C §1.1 [WL-HIST] OFF.
- Fix: "The museum says a vestibule marks the transition from the outside world."

### 012 — NOTFOUND
- Quote: "Laid out flat, it would be longer than a city bus."
- Why: no source gives a bus comparison. Articulated city buses run to about 18 m, so the comparison may also be wrong.
- Closest evidence: FACTS_C §1.2 Les Deux Saules 200 × 1700 cm.
- Fix: cut sentence. Or: "Laid out flat, it would run for seventeen metres without a break."

### 013 — WEAK
- Quote: "Let your eyes drift around the room without looking for anything in particular. That's how it was meant to be seen."
- Why: it claims to know Monet's intent. The nearest support is the "endless whole" quote and the "unguided experience of the visitor", neither of which says this.
- Closest evidence: FACTS_C §1.1 "the unguided experience of the visitor through several entrances" and "the illusion of an endless whole" [WL-HIST] OFF.
- Fix: "Monet dreamed of an endless whole, and this is one way to find it."

### 013 — RULE (repeated anecdote)
- Quote: "He also kept the panels. The deed of gift was signed. In Paris, the architect was turning the old orangery into two oval rooms. And at Giverny, Monet went on retouching his canvases ... He died at Giverny in December 1926, at eighty-six ... So he never sat where you're sitting now."
- Why: 002 already tells the same chain: deed, Lefèvre's conversion, retouching "right to the end", death at eighty-six, and "He never saw them in place". 001 also says "Monet died before they were hung." The ending lands three times.
- Fix: in 013, cut "The deed of gift was signed. In Paris, the architect was turning the old orangery into two oval rooms." Or, in 002, cut "Monet, meanwhile, couldn't let go. Back at Giverny, he kept reworking the panels, right to the end." and leave that beat to 013.

### 004 / 006 / 007 — RULE (repeated looking device)
- Quotes:
  - 004: "Walk up close ... up close the reflected sky turns back into paint. Step back again, and it becomes water."
  - 006: "Walk right up to the canvas ... the pond falls apart into marks of colour. Now take a few steps back ... the water closes over again"
  - 007: "Then pick one small patch and go close ... break into quick strokes of colour. Step back, and the morning comes together again."
- Why: three of the four Room 1 tracks use the same close-up and step-back exercise.
- Fix: keep it in 006, where it is the track's main idea. In 007, replace it with "Then let your eye travel slowly from one end to the other, and watch the light in the water change as you go." In 004, trim to "Walk up close, and the reflected sky turns back into quick strokes of paint."

### 015 — CONTRA
- Quote: "The rooms down here are each given to one artist, and every stop in this guide stands on its own"
- Why: the official presentation has an opening hall of mixed "primitives", which the track itself describes. It also has two newer galleries, one of highlights and one of contemporary counterpoints. So not every room is given to one artist.
- Closest evidence: FACTS_A collection-level "There are monographic galleries, plus two newer galleries: one of highlights and one of contemporary counterpoints" (new-presentation-collection, OFF). G5 §1 "Collection hang".
- Fix: "Most of the rooms down here are given to one artist, and every stop in this guide stands on its own, so take them in whatever order the crowds allow."

### 017 — WEAK
