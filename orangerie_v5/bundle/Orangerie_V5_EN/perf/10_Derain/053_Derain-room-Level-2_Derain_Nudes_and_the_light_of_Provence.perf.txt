[curious] Derain was a modern painter who kept looking over his shoulder at the old masters. This corner of his room shows you how.

Find the Blond Model, Le Modèle blond. She sits with her legs under a cloth, her head resting in her right hand, and her body curves into an oval of dark contours. Derain drew on Boucher, Fragonard and Renoir's bathers for her. [amused] If you've been to the Renoir room, she may look like a relative.

[pause] Then the light changes. In 1932 Derain was painting in Provence. In The Road, La Route, the village of Eygalières sits above the road to Saint-Rémy, warm colour under an intensely blue sky. In Trees and Village, it's the same village, framed by plane trees, under a stormy sky. One place, two weathers.

[warmly] Old masters indoors, the light of Provence outdoors. This is the side of Derain that Paul Guillaume chose to collect, and it's where his collection takes its leave of you. A dealer who loved the new, and still wanted it to keep faith with the past.

When you've seen all you want down here, one more look waits upstairs. Follow the signs back up to the Water Lilies, step into either oval room, and listen to its track there. Short of time? Follow the exit signs instead. A last stop waits just outside, in the garden.
