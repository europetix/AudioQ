## Key reference: official collection counts (Orangerie "History of the collection" page)
"twenty-five paintings by Renoir, fifteen by Cézanne, one by Gauguin, one by Monet and one by Sisley … twelve works by Picasso,
ten by Matisse, five by Modigliani, seven by Marie Laurencin, nine by Henri Rousseau, thirty-one by Derain, ten by Utrillo,
twenty-two by Soutine, and one by Van Dongen." — https://www.musee-orangerie.fr/en/collection/les-arts-a-parishistory-of-the-collection (OFF)
Note: these sum to 150, while the museum also says "146 paintings" elsewhere, and third-party sites give Derain 28 and Laurencin 11.
Use the official per-artist figures and avoid giving a collection total that has to add up. **Derain (31) is the largest holding,
then Renoir (25), then Soutine (22).**

## Confirmed holdings, from the museum's own artwork pages (OFF)
- **Picasso (12):** L'Étreinte (1903, a large Blue Period pastel; Vollard provenance; bought by Guillaume in 1930) · Les Adolescents (1906, Gósol, rose-ochre, 157×117; bought by Guillaume in 1930) · Nu sur fond rouge (late 1906, "gestation phase of Cubism"; bought 1929) · Femme au peigne (1906, large gouache; bought from Picasso in 1929) · Grande baigneuse (1921–22, 182×101.5, "return to order"; bought by Guillaume after 1930) · Femme au tambourin (1925, "decorative Cubism"; bought 1927). The other six were not identified.
- **Matisse (10):** Les Trois Sœurs (1917, a SINGLE canvas, 92×73; Pellerin sale of 7 May 1926, then Guillaume) · Odalisque bleue ou L'Esclave blanche · Odalisque à la culotte rouge · Odalisque à la culotte grise · Femme au violon (1921–22) · Femme à la mandoline · Nu drapé étendu · Femmes au canapé ou Le Divan · Le Boudoir.
- **Modigliani (5):** Paul Guillaume, Novo Pilota (1915, oil on cardboard mounted on plywood, 105×75; Guillaume was "only twenty-three") · Antonia (1915) · Femme au ruban de velours (c. 1915) · Fille rousse (1915) · Le Jeune Apprenti (1918–19). **"La Jeune Fille brune" is NOT among the five.**
- **Soutine (22):** Le Petit Pâtissier (c. 1922–23; bought by Guillaume in the early 1930s) · Le Garçon d'étage (c. 1927, Châtel-Guyon hotel-staff series, red waistcoat) · **Bœuf et tête de veau (1925)**. The Orangerie's carcass is NOT titled "Le Bœuf écorché". "Bœuf écorché" is the Rembrandt (1655, Louvre) that it pays homage to.
- **Derain (31):** Arlequin et Pierrot (c. 1924, commissioned by Guillaume; Pierrot is a portrait of Paul Guillaume) · Arlequin à la guitare (1924) · La Nièce du peintre assise.
- **Laurencin (7):** Portrait de Mademoiselle Chanel (1923).

## Coverage (D), cross-track
- Van Dongen (1 work) is never mentioned. That is fine: it is optional.
- Derain's portraits of Paul Guillaume and Madame Paul Guillaume au grand chapeau, together with Modigliani's Novo Pilota, form a "dealer and wife" thread that the V5 tracks could use. Placement was not verified.
- Les Adolescents and Femme au tambourin (Picasso), Femme au violon (Matisse), the Chanel portrait (Laurencin) and Arlequin et Pierrot (Derain) are the strongest missing or under-used looks.

## Confirmed OK
- Picasso 12, Matisse 10, Modigliani 5, Soutine 22, Utrillo 10, Laurencin 7, Derain 31 (official counts).
- Grande baigneuse is at the Orangerie, dated 1921–22, return to order, Antiquity/Ingres/Renoir sources.
- Femme au peigne (1906) and L'Étreinte (1903) are at the Orangerie, though track 21 misdates L'Étreinte.
- Matisse's odalisques are Nice works from the 1920s. Femme au violon and Le Boudoir are in the collection.
- Paul Guillaume, Novo Pilota (1915, cardboard, inscription "Novo Pilota" = new pilot). Antonia (1915). Femme au ruban de velours. Le Jeune Apprenti (1918–19).
- Modigliani: born Livorno 1884, died 24 January 1920 of tubercular meningitis, aged 35. Jeanne Hébuterne died two days later, aged 21, carrying their second child.
- Soutine: born 1893 near Minsk, at La Ruche after 1913, died 9 August 1943 after a perforated ulcer, in hiding during the Occupation.
- Le Petit Pâtissier, c. 1922–23, at the Orangerie.
- Arlequin et Pierrot (Derain) is at the Orangerie.
- Utrillo is Valadon's son. Laurencin and Apollinaire were together.
