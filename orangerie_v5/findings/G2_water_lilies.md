Key consequence: **Morning (Matin) is on the SOUTH long wall, not the east wall.** The east end of Room 1 holds Green Reflections. Clouds and Morning face each other across the long axis. The museum's general line is still true: the rooms run east–west, with sunrise tones toward the east and sunset toward the west. Setting Sun is at the west end. The V4 narrative of a "four-point compass" with Morning facing east is wrong, and the route in tracks 04–09 inherits that error.

Panel count: the official per-artwork counts add up to 3+3+2+1 = 9 panels in Room 1 and 4+3+3+2 = 12 in Room 2, which is 21. The museum's own total is "8 compositions, 22 panels", so the counts don't reconcile. Several secondary sources give Matin as 4 panels, which would make 10 + 12 = 22. Total length is about 91 m (2 m high). The museum also says "nearly 100 m".

## Route summary (E) — recommended Room 1/2 order

Enter Room 1 from the west (Concorde side). **Setting Sun** is at the west end, by the entrance. **Morning** is on the south long wall (right as you face east). **Green Reflections** is at the east end, by the passage. **Clouds** is on the north long wall. Then go through the passage to Room 2. **Tree Reflections** is at the west end (inferred). **Le Matin aux saules** is on the north wall (left). **Les Deux Saules** is at the east end, facing you. **Le Matin clair aux saules** is on the south wall (right). Every V4 WHERE/closing cue in 04, 05, 06, 08 and 09 needs rewriting to fit this.

## Confirmed OK
- The eight official titles and their split between rooms (Room 1: Matin, Soleil couchant, Reflets verts, Nuages; Room 2: Les Deux Saules, Le Matin aux saules, Reflets d'arbres, Le Matin clair aux saules). OFF.
- All dimensions in the tracks: 200 x 600, 850, 1275 and 1700 cm. Height 2 m. OFF.
- Room 2 has 12 panels. Deux Saules has 4 panels, is the longest at 17 m, and hangs at the east end. OFF.
- Official dates 1914–1926; donation 1922; opening May 1927; Monet died 5 Dec 1926. OFF.
- Setting Sun is on the west; the general east–west, sunrise–sunset logic. OFF/SEC2.
- The Roger Marx 1909 quote "l'illusion d'un tout sans fin, d'une onde sans horizon et sans rivage" and "asile de méditation paisible au centre d'un aquarium fleuri" (translated correctly in 08). OFF/SEC2.
- The 1909 Durand-Ruel show of 48 water-lily canvases. SEC2.
- Cataracts diagnosed 1912; right-eye surgery (Coutela) in January 1923. SEC2.
- Jean Monet died February 1914, aged 46. Alice died May 1911. Clemenceau letter of 12 Nov 1918. Third studio 1915 with an overhead glass roof. Infinity-symbol plan with Camille Lefèvre. Skylights restored in the 2000–2006 renovation (Brochet).
- No Camille Doncieux claims appear in tracks 04–12, so there was nothing to check on her death date (5 Sept 1879).
