## Confirmed OK
- Paul Guillaume 1891–1934, died at 42 (peritonitis). He opened his gallery in Feb 1914 at 6 rue de Miromesnil and planned "the first French museum of modern art" (OFF).
- Jean Walter 1883–1957; Domenica 1898–1977; State purchases in 1959 and 1963 with usufruct; collection named for both husbands (OFF).
- 1918 Matisse–Picasso show at Galerie Paul Guillaume (OFF, Pompidou).
- Lerolle painting: 1897, 73×92, two Degas works on the wall, the Rouart marriages, never owned by the Lerolles (OFF).
- Le Rocher rouge c.1895, Bibémus; the Apollinaire 1910 quote is genuine (OFF).
- La Carriole 1908, 97×129, photo source, dogs altered, Max Weber retort (OFF). La Noce c.1905, about 163 cm tall.
- Snake Charmer is correctly located at Orsay in track 18. Rousseau: octroi clerk, retired 1893, never left France, 1908 Bateau-Lavoir banquet.
- Cézanne portraits of Madame Cézanne (two) and the still lifes *Pommes et biscuits*, *Fruits, serviette et boîte à lait* and *Vase paillé, sucrier et pommes* are all Orangerie works (OFF).
- Novo Pilota (Modigliani, 1915, 105×75) is covered in track 23.
