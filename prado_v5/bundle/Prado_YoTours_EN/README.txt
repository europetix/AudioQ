YO TOURS - MUSEO DEL PRADO, MADRID
Audio guide, edition of 10 Oct 2026 (81 tracks: 59 main stops + 22 short Extras)
English, Spanish, French, German

WHY THIS EDITION
================
Visitors told us the previous guide sent them to the wrong rooms and jumped around the
building. This edition was rebuilt from scratch:
1. Every painting's room was read on the work's own page on museodelprado.es (10 Oct 2026)
   and checked against the museum's 2026 floor plan. 17 works the old guide placed in the
   wrong room are now right; Zurbaran's Agnus Dei (on loan to the Louvre until January 2027)
   is no longer a stop.
2. The route follows the floor plan with no zig-zag: Floor 0 (Flemish and Italian masters),
   up to Floor 2 (Rembrandt, the Dauphin's Treasure), Floor 1 (Titian, El Greco, Velazquez,
   Murillo, Rubens, Goya), then Goya's story up and down at the far end, and the 19th century
   on the way back to the entrance hall.
3. Every track ends by naming the next room number. The number is shown at each doorway.
   Lost? Find the number on the nearest doorway and play the tracks for that room.
4. Silent rooms filled: 22 short optional Extras in rooms the route already walks through
   (hollow circles on the route map).
5. The Yo Tours sound: voice finishing, a soft room tone and a short chime before each track.
   MP3s are labelled for phones (title, album, track n/81, Yo Tours, cover picture).

WHAT YOU GET
============
81 tracks in walking order: 59 main stops (about 2 h 30) and 22 short Extras (about 1 min each,
titles start with "Extra"). Play the Extras if you have time; every main stop also tells you how
to reach the next main stop. One folder per section:
  00_Welcome/                                 1 track
  01_Floor0_Flemish_Italian_Medieval/        18 tracks (5 Extras)
  02_Floor2_Rembrandt_Dauphins_Treasure/      7 tracks (3 Extras)
  03_Floor1_Titian_El_Greco_Velazquez/       30 tracks (7 Extras)
  04_Floor1_Murillo_Rubens_Goya/             10 tracks (2 Extras)
  05_Goyas_Story_Floors_2_and_0/             10 tracks (4 Extras)
  06_Floor0_Nineteenth_Century/               4 tracks (1 Extra)
  07_Closing/                                 1 track
plus Prado_Audio_Guide_Route.pdf: a 7-page map made for phones. Page 1 shows the whole visit in five
chapters; each following page is a plan of one part of the route with every stop numbered. Share it with the MP3s.

Track files are named  <play-order>_<room>_<title>.mp3
  e.g.  049_Room-12_Velazquez_Las_Meninas.mp3

HOW TO MAKE THE AUDIO (on a Mac)
================================
English:            double-click generate_audio.command  (voice 1 = Ava; option 3 = main stops only)
Spanish/French/German: double-click generate_audio_ES_FR_DE.command and pick the language.
Needs internet, python3 and ffmpeg (brew install ffmpeg). If it stops, run it again:
finished tracks are skipped.
