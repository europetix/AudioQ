## New Extra tracks (v5/extras/)
- x01 Room 56 · Mor, Mary Tudor (226w). Peregrina pearl taken from the page's Spanish displayed-objects note ("descubierta por un esclavo"); Van Mander's account of Charles V sending Mor framed as "an early biography tells us". Key's Family Portrait left out (one story per Extra; word limit).
- x02 Room 55 · Sofonisba, Philip II + Sánchez Coello, Infantas + Pantoja copy of Elisabeth of Valois (227w). "Taught her to paint" from the Elisabeth page (Philip II page does not say it). Arnolfini link left out (page: "it has been suggested").
- x03 Room 55B · Baldung Grien, Ages of Woman and Death + Harmony (220w). Hook: listed as Dürer, kept in the Sala Reservada (1834/1857 inventories on the page).
- x04 Room 49 · Raphael and workshop, Spasimo + Correggio, Noli me tangere (225w). "Raphael and his workshop" as on the page; shipwreck legend framed as doubted by historians; Correggio's Charles I provenance kept to one line. Cue identical to core 010's.
- x05 Room 51C · Maderuelo + San Baudelio murals (212w). Elephant: exported 1926, Met 1926–57, Prado deposit since 1957. Maderuelo: transferred 1947, reassembled "as faithful to the original as possible" (no claim that the whole chapel is rebuilt; page doesn't say so).
- x06 Room 77 · Teniers, Leopold William's gallery (225w). Links Van Dyck's Isabel Clara Eugenia back to the girl in Room 55 (she governed the Netherlands, per the Sánchez Coello page).
- x07 Room 78 · Rubens, early Judgement of Paris (219w). Attribution story (Rubens → school of Rubens → Jordaens → Rubens) from the page's inventory list; points ahead to the late version in Room 29 (core 046) without repeating 046's anecdotes.
- x08 Room 79 · Rubens, Saturn (225w). Galileo hypothesis framed as the museum frames it (needs study); plants Goya's Saturn in Room 67 (core 053).
- x09 Room 41 · Titian, Self-portrait (217w). Did not say who knighted Titian (page silent; core 020 already says Charles V, 1533). Ties Leoni's medal to the Charles V bronze in Room 1 (core 019).
- x10 Room 43 · Titian, Salome + Christ carrying the Cross (226w). Veronese Magdalen not mentioned (no official page read).
- x11 Room 4 · Reni, Saint Sebastian (225w). "Here and in Paris, London, Puerto Rico and New Zealand" = Louvre, Dulwich, Ponce, Auckland.
