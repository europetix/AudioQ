# Extras x12–x22 · changes log (10 Oct 2026)

All facts re-read on the works' official museodelprado.es pages with WebFetch, one page at a time (OFF). Rooms, "play after" and cues follow PLAN_EXTRAS.md. Lint: all OK.

## New Extra tracks (v5/extras/)
- x12 · Room 6 · Guercino, Susannah and the Elders (204w). OFF: 1617 for Cardinal Ludovisi, Book of Daniel, Crouching Aphrodite pose, lilies, elder's hand "to warn us to keep still", Ludovisi until 1664 then Philip IV. PLAN said the elder "gestures for silence": page says he warns us to keep still, so that wording is used. Cue: Room 7 → Room 7A.
- x13 · Room 8 · Ribera, The Sense of Touch + Saint Bartholomew (228w). OFF: 1632, head of Apollo (1857 inventory), painting vs sculpture, light on forehead and eyelids, Gonnelli rejected on age. Bartholomew: apostle series, Counter-Reformation, knife = his martyrdom, fifty doubloons in the inventory after Charles II's death. Not used: "flaying" (not on the page), Norton Simon version. Cue: Room 8B.
- x14 · Room 10 · Velázquez, The Adoration of the Magi (215w). OFF: Seville 1619, largest early work, novitiate of San Luis (probably Jesuit), Serrera's family-portrait reading, Pacheco = white-bearded king, kneeling king/Saint Joseph near a supposed youthful self-portrait (hedged "may be"), daughter born 1619 (hedged), Botticelli precedent. Cue: Room 11.
- x15 · Room 26 · Reni, Hippomenes and Atalanta + Carracci, Venus, Adonis and Cupid + Orazio Gentileschi, The Finding of Moses (223w). OFF: Reni and Carracci both acquired for Philip IV in 1664 from the Serra family (hook), third apple hidden behind his back, Madrid/Naples debate with Pepper; Carracci = start of the Venus and Adonis story (link to core 037); Gentileschi painted in London at nearly seventy, delivered by his son, all figures female except Moses. Cue: Room 25.
- x16 · Room 36 · Goya, Self-Portrait 1815 (205w). OFF: threshold of old age, vulnerable and fragile yet serene, Venetian crossing strokes, gaze at the viewer not a mirror, signature incised perhaps with the brush handle. PLAN said "wet paint": page does not say so, dropped. Rooms 32–38 rehang hedge kept. Cue: Room 37 → Room 38.
- x17 · Room 39 · Mengs, Charles III (222w). OFF: 1767, the king's official image, replicas for foreign courts, Grimaldi's 1773 letter on the Empress of Russia's request, armour, three orders (Golden Fleece, French Holy Spirit, San Gennaro), baton, "severe but not haughty", queen's pendant invented from other likenesses. Not used: "prime version" (not on the page). Bridge to Tiepolo in one line, without repeating core 049. Cue: staircase landing → Room 23.
- x18 · Room 93 · Goya, The Pottery Vendor (213w). OFF: one of 20 cartoons commissioned by Mengs, Oct 1777, for the Asturias bedchamber at El Pardo; Goya's invoice; "all of them confront something they cannot have"; only the old woman content; letter: king and royal couple saw four paintings, almost certainly including this one (moved here from core 050). Cue: back through Rooms 92 and 91, past Room 90, to Room 85.
- x19 · Room 64 · Goya, General José de Palafox on Horseback (227w). REWRITTEN on the coordinator's decision (10 Oct 2026). It replaces the Room 74 Christina of Sweden Extra, because that display is temporary and ends 7 Feb 2027. Now played after 055, before x21. OFF (EN): 1814, Room 064, commissioned by Palafox himself, his 1808 defence of Zaragoza, theatrical pose brings out his vanity, inscription lower left. OFF (ES): born in Zaragoza; Captain General of Aragon 26 May 1808, declared war on Napoleon days later; never won a battle but refused to surrender Zaragoza; went to France after it fell; painted the same year as the 2nd and 3rd of May; campaign uniform, cavalry charge, without his decorations including Charles III's grand cross; Goya reported it finished Dec 1814 / Jan 1815; Palafox paid only in 1831, to Javier Goya. Not used: the EN page's bequest by his son, because the ES page and the provenance dates (1884 vs 1900) are inconsistent. Cue: Room 63 → Room 63B for the Fortuny Extra (x21). People skipping the Extras go on to Room 75.
- x20 · Room 66 · Goya, The Milkmaid of Bordeaux (212w). OFF: around 1827, light colours vs the Black Paintings, low viewpoint like riding a mule, milk jug at left, first owner Leocadia Zorrilla (link to core 053), then the Muguiro family, Prado 1945. Not used: the "ounce of gold" anecdote and the Muguiro portrait in the room (neither on the page). Cue: Room 65 → Room 64.
- x21 · Room 63B · Fortuny, The Painter's Children in the Japanese Room (188w). OFF: Villa Arata at Portici, María Luisa with a fan, Mariano aged three with a mask, Japanese features, gift meant for Federico de Madrazo, unfinished at his sudden death, legacy of Mariano Fortuny y Madrazo 1950 (the boy, grown up). Cue: Room 75.
- x22 · Room 61 · Federico de Madrazo, Countess of Vilches (204w). OFF: aged 32 in 1853, novels Berta and Ledia, sang at the Madrazo soirées, the fan and fingertips, Ingres training, "undoubtedly the most emblematic work" of the 19th-century collections, blue dress and red velvet wrap (displayed-objects list), legacy of the II Count of Vilches, 1944. Cue: Room 60 → Room 60A.
