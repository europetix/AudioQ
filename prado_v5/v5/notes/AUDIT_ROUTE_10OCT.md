# Route audit, Prado v5 (independent, 10 Oct 2026)

Scope: v5/tracks/001–059 (headers, bodies, final cue paragraph) checked against research/map/floor0–2.png, plano_ing_2026.pdf (pp. 3–5) and research/A/B/C_result.tsv + OFFICIAL_LOCATIONS_PRADO_10OCT2026.md. I edited no tracks. I did not use the web or a browser.

How the map was read: in the isometric plans, rooms in one row ("strip") follow each other along one diagonal. Rooms in neighbouring strips face each other across the other diagonal. "Adjacent" below means the two rooms share a wall segment. "Diagonal" means they meet only at a corner, so a visitor normally has to pass through a third room. The PNGs cannot show reliably whether a particular wall has a door in it. Those cases are listed under on-site checks.

## Verdict

**Rooms: pass.** Every @room, every @where and every "also in this room" line in the bodies matches the official TSV room. The guide does not mention Zurbarán's Agnus Dei ("A lamb", on loan), El Greco's Baptism (not on display) or Aesop. Las Meninas, the Majas, Danaë, Breda and the Buffoons are all in their correct rooms.

**Highlights: pass.** All 37 highlighted works on plan pp. 3–5 are stops (16 on Floor 0, 16 on Floor 1, 5 on Floor 2).

**Route shape: sound.** The order is Floor 0 north, then Floor 2 north, then Floor 1 from north to south, then Floor 2 south, then Floor 0 south, ending back at the entrance. There is no zig-zag of the kind the old guide had. The remaining backtracks are spurs the floor plan forces (76, 40–44, 25), apart from one small one at 58A that may be avoidable.

**Cues: need fixes before recording.** One floor change is under-specified (HIGH). Three cues name a next room that the map shows only diagonally across from the current one, and four cues give a direction with no path.

## Floor changes: what the map shows

- **014→015 (0→2).** Floor 0, around rotunda 51: stairs by Room 50/52C, stairs by 51C, and lifts by 51A/51B and by 52A. Floor 2 north wing: one stair (79/80 lobby, with toilets) and lifts by 77/76 and by 79B/82. Lifts are plausible all the way up. The stairs are plausible, but only one of the two Floor 0 staircases can continue to Floor 2. → HIGH above.
- **018→019 (2→1).** Floor 2: stairs in the 79/80 lobby and a lift next to 83. Floor 1: two stairs beside rotunda Room 1 (by 24/44 and by 6/24), and lifts by Room 2/3 and by Room 40 at the Goya entrance (labelled in the PDF). Plausible.
- **049→050 (1→2).** Floor 1: stairs in the strip between 39 and 23, and a lift between 39 and 37/38. Floor 2: a stair in 85 (at the 85/90 corner) and a lift by 86/87. The positions match, so this is plausible.
- **051→052 (2→0).** The same stair shaft runs down to the Murillo entrance on Floor 0. Two stair icons are drawn there (by 67, and in the corridor towards 71), with a lift by 71. Plausible. Room 67 is at the foot of the stairs, and 71 is up the corridor beyond the lift.

## @where lines

Every stop's @where gives the room number and a visual description. Where several works share a room, it says which is which (e.g. 005, 034, 036, 046), so a visitor could find each work. Weak spots:

- **019:** the work may not be in Room 1 (see the table).
- **050:** "next to Room 85". The stairwell lies between 85 and 90, so "past the stairs from Room 85" is clearer.
- **051:** "the room with the staircase". The stair may be in its own small stairwell; acceptable.
