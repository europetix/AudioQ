# ES notes: tracks 001–040 (Prado, 10 Oct 2026 edition)

Self-check: `assemble.py --check … es` prints OK for all of 001–040. Spanish runs 1–9 % shorter than the English. Every track has at least 3 pauses. There are no semicolons, parentheses, quotation marks, roman numerals or symbols. Regnal numbers are written in words.

Conventions:
- Room numbers are written "la sala 55 A" in speech and "Sala 55 A" in titles. Floors are "la planta 0 / 1 / 2".
- Room 49's "long hall" is rendered "la larga sala 49", to avoid confusion with "Galería Central".
- Signs marked Collection are rendered "los carteles de Colección".
- Bosch is "el Bosco" and Dürer is "Durero".
