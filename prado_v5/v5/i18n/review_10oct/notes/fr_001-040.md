# FR notes — Prado tracks 001–040 (Vivienne, vous)

Self-check: `assemble.py --check … fr` prints OK for all of 001–040. Each track is 0–6 % shorter than the English.
Also checked by script: delivery tags in the same paragraph and order, pauses ≥ English, at most 2 dashes, no ; ( ) & % × °, no roman numerals, no "St", room letters spaced ("salle 55 A"), average sentence length 9–13 words.
French typography note: a spaced colon or question mark (" : ", " ?") counts as a word in the checker, so I kept them few.

## Conventions
- Rooms: "salle 55 A", "la salle ronde 51", "salle 1". Floors: "niveau 0 / 1 / 2". "Shown at its doorway" → "indiqué à l'entrée de la salle". "Look for 55B at the doorway" → "Cherchez 55 B à l'entrée de la salle".
- Titles: "Salle 12 · Artiste — Titre", Extras "Salle 41 · Extra · …". 001 has no room. 027 keeps the English pattern "Salle 1 · Niveau 1 — … · …".
- Extras in speech: "un court Extra".
- Regnal numbers in words: Charles trois, Ferdinand sept, Philippe deux / quatre / cinq, Henri huit, Charles premier (England), François premier, Charles deux (Spain), Charles Quint.

## Things in the English that may be wrong (translated as written)
- 013 @where: "the Virgin and Child with two children and an older woman". La Perla shows the Christ Child and the infant Baptist, so "two children" may count the Child twice. Translated literally.
- 003 and 034: the English spells the queen's name two ways, "Isabella Farnese" and "Isabel Farnese". Harmless in French.
- 006: "That same year, an early biography tells us…" follows the sentence about July, so it means 1554. Kept just as ambiguous ("La même année").
- 008: "dates the work to 1507 years after the Virgin gave birth" → "l'an 1507 après l'enfantement de la Vierge" (same meaning).
