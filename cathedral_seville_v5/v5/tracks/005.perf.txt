[amused] The biggest bell in this tower has a nickname. She's called la Gorda, the Fat One.

Her proper name is Santa María la Mayor. Juan de Balabarca cast her in 1588, and she weighs over five tonnes. She is the largest of the twenty-four bells that the cathedral counts in the Giralda.

Some of her companions came from a famous workshop. Bartolomé Morel, the founder who cast the bronze figure that turns on top of this tower, also cast seven of the Giralda's bells.

Bells this heavy shake whatever holds them. Since 2021 la Gorda has hung on emergency shoring. The current works are rebuilding the frames of six bells, with dampers to soften the vibration, and are giving her a permanent support.

[quietly] Think of the timing. She was cast for a tower that had already stood over Seville for almost four hundred years.

Now head back down the ramps of the Giralda, keeping to the right. The next stop plays on the way down.
