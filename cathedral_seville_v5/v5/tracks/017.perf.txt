[lightly] Plenty of guides will tell you that Zurbarán hangs in the chalice sacristy. The Cathedral's own list of its paintings puts both of its Zurbaráns here, in the Sala del Pabellón, the exhibition room.

They share the walls with other painters of Seville's great century. There are two works by Juan de Valdés Leal, from the 1650s. And there is one by Francisco Pacheco. He is less famous today than the others, but he taught the young Diego Velázquez. Velázquez later married his daughter, so Pacheco became his father-in-law as well. Read the labels for their subjects.

[pause] Since early 2026 the room has also held a visitor. El Greco's Santa María Magdalena, Saint Mary Magdalene, has come from the parish church of San Eutropio in the town of Paradas. It stays here while that church is restored. It is a loan, so enjoy it while it lasts.

[warmly] Seville's painters, side by side, with a famous guest. A short detour, and a good one.

On to the Sacristía Mayor: the great domed room, with a dome carved in rings of figures.
