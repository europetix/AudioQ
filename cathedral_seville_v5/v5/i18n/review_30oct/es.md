## Quotations (rule 11)
- 006, "think we were mad" (tradition) and "so good that there is none its equal" (the 1401 decision as it has come down). Both were originally Spanish and neither is verified verbatim. FACTS §1.8 says no official page carries the "mad" line, the 1401 act is lost, and the Spanish catedraldesevilla.es pages were blocked in research. So both are reported speech with no quotation marks: "un canónigo propuso hacer una iglesia tan grande que quienes la vieran terminada los tomaran por locos", and "Habla de una iglesia tan buena que no haya otra igual". I fetched no pages.
- 002, Ibn Sahib al-Salat (Arabic): I translated the English rendering and kept it in quotation marks, introduced the same way: «como suspendido entre las estrellas».
- 011, The Nation (originally English): «si no hay falsificación en el caso», in quotation marks, introduced the same way ("decía").
- 014, Mélida ("as he put it"), 015 Faro a Colón director, 015 Hernando Colón's bequest, 018 Murillo to the sacristan (Ponz), 027 the Crónica's she-ass and Ortiz de Zúñiga's "lagarto deforme, o cocodrilo egipcio": the English has these without quotation marks, and the Spanish keeps them as reported speech with none. For Mélida I wrote "según él", not "en sus palabras", so it does not suggest his exact wording.

- 014 "if they had no share in the glory" became "si no compartieron la gloria", keeping the English "if".
- The [O1]-style source codes in the English @where were left out of the Spanish @where. They are internal references, not listener text.
