---

## 0. Method and keys

- **Tools.** WebSearch and WebFetch only, one page at a time on official sites. No curl, no browser, no CAPTCHA. All reads were done on 30 Oct 2026.
- **WebFetch caveat.** WebFetch returns a model summary, so "verbatim" quotes are as relayed. Re-check exact wording against the page before quoting on air.
- **Verdicts.**
  - **OK**: safe to state as written.
  - **HEDGE**: true in substance; use the safe wording (sources disagree, or a figure is approximate).
  - **LEGEND**: tradition or folklore; script only as "tradition says…".
  - **UNVERIFIED**: no adequate source found; do not script, or verify first.
- **Grades.**
  - **OFF**: Cabildo pages and PDFs on catedraldesevilla.es. OFF-UNESCO and OFF-Junta (the Junta de Andalucía archive) are marked separately.
  - **SEC**: a named secondary source read directly (academic journal, IAPH, a museum or foundation catalogue, a major press outlet, a specialist inventory).
  - **SNIP**: search snippet only.
  - **WIKI** appears only as a lead and is never the sole support.
- **Sources** are listed in §16 as [O1]… (official) and [S1]… (secondary).
- **Blocked or failed this session.** None of these was retried:
  - digibug.ugr.es: bot-protection page.
  - historia-hispanica.rah.es (RAH biography of Morel): JavaScript shell, no text.

## 17. Popular claims that are WRONG or unsupported (don't repeat)

1. **"The 1401 minutes say 'they'll think us mad'."** It is tradition. The minutes are lost, and the documented gist is "so good there is none its equal".
2. **"It's the largest church in the world"** or **"the third-largest church"**. Say instead: the largest Gothic building in Europe (UNESCO), and the cathedral covering the greatest area (Guinness).
3. **"The mosque was built 1172–98 by al-Mansur."** The prayer hall was begun in 1172 under Abu Ya'qub Yusuf; 1198 is the minaret's completion under al-Mansur. Even an official page conflates this [O2].
4. **"The Giralda has stairs" / "35 ramps".** The official congress says 34 ramps and 7 chambers.
5. **"A king rode his horse to the top."** Legend. The official page says only that the ramps were wide enough for a rider.
6. **"The Giraldillo on top is a copy."** The restored original went back up on 22 July 2005. Replicas stood in temporarily, and one is at ground level by the Puerta del Príncipe.
7. **"The Giraldillo was made by Juan Bautista Vázquez."** Unproven. Documents show founder Bartolomé Morel (contract 1566); design probably by Luis de Vargas.
8. **"Columbus's remains came in 1902."** The remains arrived January 1899; the monument was installed 1902.
9. **"Columbus and his son Diego are buried here"** (in WIKI and elsewhere). The son buried here is **Hernando**. His brother Diego's remains were used for the DNA comparison.
10. **"DNA proved Columbus was a Sephardic Jew / Spanish."** No peer-reviewed publication found as of 30 Oct 2026.
11. **"DNA proved his whole body is here."** Only part of a skeleton is in Seville, and the Santo Domingo remains have never been tested.
12. **"The front heralds look up because Castile and León supported him; the rear ones look down because Aragon and Navarre didn't."** Legend.
13. **"He hangs in the air because he swore never to lie in Spanish soil."** Legend. The monument was designed for Havana.
14. **"The crocodile is the stuffed original from Alfonso X's time."** Today's is a wooden copy. The chronicle lists a tusk, a giraffe and a zebra, not a crocodile.
15. **"Goya used prostitutes as models."** A rumour.
16. **"Zurbarán hangs in the Sacristía de los Cálices."** The official list places the Zurbaráns in the Sala del Pabellón.
17. **"The Altarpiece has over a thousand figures / tons of gold."** Official figures: 44 reliefs and more than 200 figures. The gold weight is unverified.
18. **"You can visit the Capilla Real / Biblioteca Colombina on the visit."** No: worship only for the Capilla Real, researchers only for the library.
19. **"Artemisia Gentileschi's Magdalene is on display."** Officially "en restauración".
20. **"Pedro de Campaña's Descent dates from c.1570."** The official date is 1547.
