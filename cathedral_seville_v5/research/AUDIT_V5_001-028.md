# AUDIT — Catedral de Sevilla + Giralda V5 (EN), tracks 001–028

Independent audit, 30 Oct 2026. The tracks were not edited. Inputs: `v5/tracks/001–028.perf.txt`, `v5/PLAN.md`, `v5/bridges/*.txt`, `build/static/lengths.json`, `v5/notes/*_NOTES.md`, `research/FACTS_OCT2026.md` (FACTS §), `research/VISIT_ROUTE_AND_SPACES_OCT2026.md` (ROUTE). `v5/lint.py` passes on all 28 tracks (it checks form, not facts or places).

**Pages re-read for this audit (WebFetch, one at a time, 30 Oct 2026; WebFetch returns a model summary, so quotes are as relayed):**
- GRECO (OFF), the El Greco loan page. It is undated and gives no end date. It says the painting is shown "next to … the María Magdalena by Artemisia Gentileschi".
- es.hispanopedia "Magdalena penitente (El Greco, Paradas)" (WIKI lead). It dates the painting 1580–85 (or 1586–90), says it was painted "with his workshop's help", and notes that some attribute it to a follower. It records no 2026 loan.
- Two searches found no news of the El Greco returning to Paradas, and no news confirming it is still in the cathedral.
- GIR (OFF): "a central prism … surrounded by four external walls", with the ramps between them. The page does not say whether the core is solid.
- TF (OFF): "34 ramps and 7 chambers"; "double-walled tubular structure". The "drawing book" is a hypothesis: the graffiti "would function as a 'drawing book'". The sketches were found in the 2017–2024 restoration, on the four façades.
- ARAMCO (SEC): "**The central core consisted of seven rooms**". Ibn Sahib al-Sala: "appearing to the traveler when he is several days from Seville as if it were suspended among the stars". The "full day's march" claim is Alfonso the Wise's. Yusuf was killed at Santarem; 'Ali al-Ghumari took over in 1188 and switched to brick; the balls went up on 10 Mar 1198; 34 ramps.
- PAINT (OFF):
  - Zurbarán ×2, Sala del Pabellón.
  - Artemisia: "En restauración".
  - No Goya and no Murillo *St Anthony* on the list, so the list is not complete.
  - No entry for the Capilla de San Pedro.
- biografiasyvidas (SEC, low): "Las pinturas del retablo de la capilla de San Pedro de la catedral de Sevilla (1635-1636)" are by **Zurbarán**.
- GLOBE 1875 (SEC), reprinting *The Nation* (New York):
  - "Thus the chapel in the Seville Cathedral regains, **if there is no counterfeiting in the case**, its complete altar-piece."
  - The cut-out piece was "a rough oval about 7ft high".
  - "some Spanish strangers"; one seller, a "catspaw".
  - Identified from "the photograph of the original furnished to all Spanish consuls".
  - Bought for 250 dollars.
  - The seller returned "by way of Havanna".
- REN (OFF):
  - Capilla Real grille: designed by van der Borcht, paid for by Charles III, installed 1771. Jerónimo Roldán's San Fernando on horseback receiving the keys stands on top.
  - Sala Capitular: Murillo's Immaculate "presiding over the whole group from the top" of the eight round canvases, in the vault.
  - Sacristía Mayor: Campaña's painting "in front of the central chapel". The San Fernando statue stands "by one of the head pillars".
- Prensa Latina, 18 Feb 2026 (SEC, citing the Cabildo's website):
  - The Capilla Real "reabrió hoy sus espacios" after "varios meses".
  - The urn is "de plata dorada y cristal que contiene el cuerpo de san Fernando", before the Virgin's altar.
  - The article does not list what was restored.
- A search found no peer-reviewed publication of the Granada Columbus DNA work; the newest items are 2024 news.
- GLASS (OFF):
  - The oldest glass is the seventeen windows by Enrique Alemán (1478–83) over the side chapels and the main west nave.
  - Menardo's *Los Evangelistas*, 1577, is "sobre la puerta principal"; Menardo made three west-front windows and died in 1578.
  - The Ascension window by Arnao de Flandes (1539–41) is listed at the "hastial del crucero norte sobre la puerta de la Concepción", **but the page's body text puts it on the opposite gable of the transept** (internal conflict).
  - The Justa y Rufina window is listed both in the "Capilla del Bautismo" and in the "chapel of San Antonio".
- AHPS (OFF-Junta):
  - Morel's contract is dated 27 Aug 1566, "**a tenerla acabada en ocho meses**".
  - The figure was placed on 13 Aug 1568, "dos años después de lo pactado".
  - "diseñada por el pintor Luis de Vargas"; J. B. Vázquez made the mould.
  - Morel "participó en la fundición de siete campanas".
  - Hernán Ruiz won a competition; his contract is dated 17 Dec 1557.
- OHC (SEC). This is an anonymous chapter from a Havana book, written about 1915 or later, not a modern study:
  - "ni ella, guardó nunca sus cenizas".
  - The remains lay in the wall niche until it was opened on 26 Sep 1898, then went to the sacristy, and sailed on 12 Dec 1898. They reached Cádiz on 17 Jan 1899 and Seville by 20 Jan 1899.
  - The monument was installed on 19 Mar 1898 and dismantled, with its base left in Havana.
  - Mélida on Aragon and Navarre: "**si** no tomaron parte en la gloria, vienen a tomarla en el duelo".

**Judgement on the Havana tomb story.** It rests on one document: an anonymous chapter of about 1915 in the Havana City Historian's repository. That chapter's own dated sequence supports it:
- The monument was installed in the crossing by 19 Mar 1898.
- The remains stayed in the wall niche until it was opened on 26 Sep 1898.
- They then went to the sacristy and sailed on 12 Dec 1898.

So the claim is credible, but it is still a single, older secondary source and is not in FACTS. Keep it, attributed, with the softened hook above. Do not make it stronger.
