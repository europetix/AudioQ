# Catedral de Sevilla + Giralda: official audio guide vs competitors (as of 30 Oct 2026)

Prepared for Yo Tours, as input to the planned "Yo Tours V5" audio guide.
Method: WebSearch and WebFetch only, one page at a time on official sites, no curl or browser, no CAPTCHA. Every source was read on **2026-10-30**.

**Source grades**
- **OFF**: the Cathedral's official site (catedraldesevilla.es).
- **SEC**: a named secondary page read directly. This covers the official guide's developer (GVAM), OTA product pages, app-store listings, publisher pages and first-hand forum posts.
- **SNIP**: a search snippet, or an aggregator or SEO page that summarises other reviews, often with truncated text or no dates. Use these as directional signals only.

Note: WebFetch returns a model-summarised version of each page, so verbatim quotes are as relayed by that tool.

---

## 0. Key findings (read first)

1. **The official guide is NOT included with the ticket.** It costs **€5 on a device** rented at the ticket office or **€4 as an app** on your own phone. A general ticket costs €13 online or €14 at the office, valid from 1 Jan 2026, and covers the Cathedral, the Giralda and the Salvador church. [OFF-1], [OFF-2]
2. **Who runs it.** The Cathedral (Cabildo) commissions it and **GVAM** develops it. GVAM relaunched it as a "smart guide" on **4 June 2024**, with:
   - more than 50 points of interest and 200 high-resolution photos;
   - a map, a searchable list, and a keypad for number look-up;
   - a 360° panorama from the Giralda with identified landmarks;
   - clickable hotspots on the High Altar (Retablo Mayor);
   - three routes: general, children's, and a **1-hour** highlights route. [SEC-1]
3. **Languages.** No official page we could read lists the current (2024) languages. The 2019 launch had **5 languages (ES/EN/FR/DE/IT)** plus a children's version in ES/EN. Subtitles and sign language were planned for 2020. [SEC-2] Resellers of app-based bundles also list these same 5 languages. [SEC-7], [SEC-14]
4. **Watch out for a look-alike.** catedralcms.turify.net calls itself "Official Audioguide of the Cathedral of Seville". Its own disclaimer says it is **not affiliated with the Cathedral**; it is run by Grupo Feel the City. [SEC-3]
5. **The recurring complaint is wayfinding, not content.** Reviewers of the official guide and the bundles that use it report:
   - the guide's numbers don't match the numbers posted in the cathedral, or aren't posted at all;
   - the numbers are hard to find on the app's map;
   - the order is hard to follow, or feels disorganised with items missing;
   - volume is lost, devices fail, there is no Wi-Fi to download the app, and the language is hard to select. [SEC-9], [SEC-8], [SNIP-1]
6. **No Rick Steves Cathedral audio tour was found.** The Rick Steves audio we found for Seville is the **Sevilla City Walk** (14 stops, about 66 minutes). It circles the Cathedral and Giralda **from outside** and tells you to visit the interior separately. [SEC-15] A 2014 Rick Steves forum post said there were no Spain audio tours, only the guidebook's self-guided text tour. [SEC-16]
7. **Coverage of other platforms:**
   - **Smartify:** no Seville Cathedral tour found. [SNIP-2]
   - **VoiceMap:** Seville walking tours only, with no tour of the cathedral interior. [SEC-17], [SEC-18]
   - **izi.TRAVEL:** only small single-language community tours. [SEC-19], [SEC-20]

## 2. Third-party audio tours

### 2.1 Rick Steves (free)
- **What exists:** the **Sevilla City Walk**, 14 stops, listed at about 66 minutes, though the narration says "allow at least 90 minutes".
  - Stop 3 is the Cathedral and Giralda and stop 4 is the Puerta del Perdón. The walk stays **outside** and recommends visiting the interior separately.
  - The narration is excerpted from the Rick Steves Spain guidebook and was produced by Cedar House Audio.
  - Source: a third-party mirror (VoiceWalks) that shows no publish date. Its transcript has errors such as "Heralda". [SEC-15]
- **Cathedral interior audio tour:** **none found.** The Rick Steves podcast feed listing shows only generic "no longer available" items and says the tours have moved to the Rick Steves Audio Europe app. [SEC-22]
- **The app:** free; version 4.2.0 was released the day before our read; its description does not mention Spain or Sevilla. [SEC-23]
- **Guidebook:** "Rick Steves Snapshot Sevilla, Granada & Andalucía" goes on sale 29 Sep 2026 with "self-guided walking tours" in **text**. [SEC-24]
- **Reviews:** none specific to a cathedral audio tour.

### 2.2 Smartify
- No Seville Cathedral tour found. A domain-restricted search of smartify.org returned only Murillo objects held by other museums. [SNIP-2]

### 2.3 izi.TRAVEL (free community tours)
- **"Inside Cathedral of Seville"** by WendyCrawfordWrites.com: **English only**, written as a narrative with no numbered stops.
  - Covers the Retablo Mayor, the Columbus tomb, the Capilla Real, the Puerta del Perdón and Patio de los Naranjos, and the museum in the main sacristy.
  - Does **not** cover the Treasury or the roof. The Giralda is mentioned only as a landmark.
  - Shows no rating and no date. [SEC-19]
- **"Catedral y Giralda"** by "Aurora": **Spanish only**, a **single audio track**.
  - Covers the Giralda and the Patio as remains of the mosque, plus a general mention of "otros tesoros".
  - Does not cover Columbus, the Treasury or the roof. Shows no rating and no date. [SEC-20]

### 2.4 VoiceMap
- Seville tours are **city walks**, for example "Gateway to the Americas" by Ben Barclay: $14.99, 120 minutes, 2.5 miles, 60 locations, with 112 ratings shown but no score.
  - The listing does not say whether it enters the Cathedral. [SEC-17]
- No VoiceMap tour of the cathedral interior was found. [SEC-18]

### 2.5 TouringBee, "Seville Cathedral and Giralda self-guided audio app tour"
Sold via Musement, Freetour and Magpie.
- **Price:** from **$6 or €5**. Some listings show $11 for the same tour.
- **Ticket:** **not included**.
- **Languages:** EN, ES, FR, DE, IT, PL.
- **Content and length:** 21 audio commentaries. Listed at **1 h 15 min**; another listing says about 2 hours.
- **Coverage:** the Giralda climb and Columbus's burial place. No mention of the Treasury, sacristies, Patio or roof.
- **Access:** 1 year of access, with offline download.
- **Reviews:** 0 on Musement and 0 on Freetour. [SEC-5], [SEC-6], [SEC-25]
- **Quality flags:**
  - The Musement copy says the Giralda is where you see works by Goya and Murillo; those works are in the Cathedral itself, not the tower. [SEC-5]
  - The Magpie listing says "entry tickets" are included and also tells you to buy your ticket in advance. [SEC-25]

## 4. What each one does well

- **Official (GVAM):**
  - The deepest content: 50+ points, 200 photos and the High Altar hotspots.
  - Three route lengths, including a 1-hour route and a children's route.
  - A 360° panorama from the Giralda.
  - A cheap rental device for visitors without a phone.
  - Covers El Salvador too.
  - Sold at the point of sale and online together with the ticket. [SEC-1], [OFF-2]
- **Rick Steves:** a free, personable, story-driven voice, and the **outside context** of the Cathedral, Giralda and Barrio Santa Cruz that interior guides skip. [SEC-15]
- **izi.TRAVEL (Wendy Crawford):** joins up the exterior and the interior, from the Puerta del Perdón and Patio to the nave, Retablo, Columbus and Capilla Real. [SEC-19]
- **TouringBee:** cheap, 6 languages, offline, a year of access, and a tight 21-track scope. [SEC-6], [SEC-25]
- **MNPQ AR bundle:** the widest language set (8, including ZH and KO); 3D reconstructions; an AR layer during the Giralda climb. [SEC-14]
- **OTA bundles:** a single purchase that covers ticket and audio. [SEC-11], [SEC-12]
- **Navibration:** location-triggered audio that works offline, plus links to the Alcázar and the city. [SEC-4]

## 5. The bar to beat: checklist for Yo Tours V5

**Must match**
- [ ] Price at or below **€4** for the audio alone, or clearly better value when bundled; bundles today cost €25–$45. [OFF-2], [SEC-11]
- [ ] **50+ interior points** with photos, including the High Altar in detail (zoomable hotspots on the panels). [SEC-1]
- [ ] Three route lengths, including a **1-hour highlights route**, and a separate **children's track**. [SEC-1]
- [ ] Giralda **panorama with named landmarks** from the top. [SEC-1]
- [ ] At least the **5 core languages (ES/EN/FR/DE/IT)**; aim for **8 or more**, adding ZH, KO and PT to match the AR bundle. [SEC-2], [SEC-14]
- [ ] Works **fully offline** once downloaded, so there is no dependence on venue Wi-Fi. [SEC-9]
- [ ] Coverage of **El Salvador**, which is free with the ticket. [OFF-1], [OFF-2]
- [ ] Route sequence that follows the official one-way flow, which **starts at the Giralda**. [OFF-2]

**Must beat: fix the documented pain points**
- [ ] **Wayfinding that does not depend on the official numbers.** Reviewers say the numbers don't match, aren't posted, or are hard to find on the map. Use photo-anchored "look for…" cues, one-tap "I'm here" selection on a floor plan, and a fallback list. [SEC-8], [SEC-9], [SNIP-1]
- [ ] **A strictly linear default route** with a visible "next stop" direction ("walk 20 m towards…"), plus a free-roam mode. [SEC-8], [SEC-9]
- [ ] **Language chosen at first launch**, and switchable at any time. [SEC-9]
- [ ] **A pre-visit check**: download test, battery/storage check, headphone prompt, and a reminder of the dress code and ID. [SEC-9], [SEC-26]
- [ ] **Live closure handling**: Masses in the Capilla Real, the Antigua chapel and the silver altar; 4–8 Dec restrictions; capacity limits. Skip or reorder stops and say why. [OFF-2]
- [ ] **Accuracy QA**: no misplaced artworks of the kind in TouringBee's Giralda/Goya copy, and no stops that "don't match the chapels". [SEC-5], [SEC-27]
- [ ] **A dated "content verified on" stamp** in the app; none of the competitors shows one.

**Gaps none of them cover well (Yo Tours' opportunity)**
- [ ] **Treasury, sacristies and chapter house:** no source confirms coverage in any guide except a passing mention of the main sacristy in one izi tour. [SEC-19]
- [ ] **Patio de los Naranjos and Puerta del Perdón as the Almohad mosque story**, linked to the Giralda's minaret origins. Only the free izi and Rick Steves walks touch on this. [SEC-15], [SEC-19], [SEC-20]
- [ ] **A roof-tour companion**: the official roof visit is guided only, in EN/ES/FR, and reportedly ends inside the Cathedral. A pre- or post-roof module or a "continue your visit" track would fill this. [OFF-4], [SEC-21]
- [ ] **Exterior plus interior in one product** (Rick Steves outside, official guide inside): no single guide does both. [SEC-15], [SEC-1]
- [ ] **A stated accessibility offer** (transcripts, subtitles, sign language, step-free route): planned for the official guide in 2020, but its delivery is not confirmed anywhere. [SEC-2]
- [ ] **Hand-off for the free Sunday visit (16:30–18:00)** and the time-slot logic: no guide plans around it. [OFF-2]
- [ ] **Transparent claims**: a look-alike "official" reseller page confuses buyers. Yo Tours should state clearly that it is independent and say what it does better. [SEC-3]
