WHY THIS EDITION
================
Built from scratch to the Yo Tours standard. The official guide is sold separately, and visitors complain that its numbers
don't match the cathedral's; ticket-and-audio bundles are poorly rated. This guide:
1. Names every place by its Spanish name as on the signs and gives one big landmark to look for (no numbers to hunt for).
2. Follows the Cathedral's own order: the Giralda first (34 ramps, the bells, the mosque story on the way down), then one
   loop through the cathedral with no doubling back: the Capilla Mayor and its altarpiece, the Coro, Murillo's Saint
   Anthony, the west end, the Capilla de la Antigua, Columbus's tomb, the sacristies and chapter house (Goya, Zurbarán,
   Campaña, the Custodia, Murillo's Immaculate), the Capilla Real seen from its grille, the Patio de los Naranjos, and out by
   the Puerta del Perdón.
3. Checks every fact on the Cathedral's own pages and reliable sources (30 Oct 2026); legends are told as legends, and the
   Columbus question is told exactly as far as the evidence goes. Works and restorations are single, dated sentences.
4. Three lengths (one recording, three folders):
   Full tour   @@N_FULL@@ tracks, just over 1 h 30 with the Giralda
   Essential   @@N_ESS@@ tracks, about 1 h 15:  @@ESSENTIAL@@
   Highlights  @@N_HI@@ tracks, about 45 min:   @@HIGHLIGHTS@@
   In the shorter versions a short spoken line at the end of a track tells you the next stop.
5. The Yo Tours sound: voice finishing, a soft room tone and a short chime before each track. MP3s are labelled for phones.

HOW TO MAKE THE AUDIO (on a Mac)
================================
English:          double-click generate_audio.command  (voice 1 = Ava)
                  option 1 = all three lengths (records once; folders _Essential and _Highlights)
                  option 2 = two voice samples (008 the Retablo Mayor + 014 Columbus's tomb)
                  option 3 = Highlights only (quickest; option 1 later reuses those recordings)
Other languages:  double-click generate_audio_ES_FR_DE.command and pick the language, then the same options.
Needs internet, python3, ffmpeg (brew install ffmpeg) and an up-to-date edge-tts:
  python3 -m pip install --user --upgrade edge-tts
If it stops, run it again: finished tracks are skipped.
