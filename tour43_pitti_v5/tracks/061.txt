 Romantic Age & Courtwear — Room 4
    WHERE YOU ARE
    In Room 4, Romantic-era dress and court wear, with wedding and day gowns of the crinoline age.
    WHAT YOU SEE
    The nineteenth-century return to structure, the bell-shaped crinoline skirt, and the elaborate day, wedding and
    court dress of the Victorian decades.
    LISTEN FOR
    How the silhouette swung back from classical simplicity to the vast crinoline, and what that said about the
    century.

 Room four is the swing back. The light classical line of the Empire didn't last. Through the eighteen
 thirties, forties and fifties the waist dropped back to its natural place and tightened, and the skirt began
 to grow, and grow.

 By the eighteen fifties it had become the crinoline, a vast bell-shaped skirt held out by a cage of steel
 hoops, sometimes wide enough to fill a doorway again, as in the eighteenth century. Look at the day
 dresses, the wedding gowns, the court wear gathered here. The fabrics are rich, the construction
 elaborate, the message one of prosperity and propriety, the values of the rising nineteenth-century
 middle class as much as of the court.

 This is the world of the Romantic novel made visible: sentiment, modesty, and an enormous amount of
 cloth.

 When you've finished, continue to room five and the Belle Epoque. Play the next track when you arrive.
