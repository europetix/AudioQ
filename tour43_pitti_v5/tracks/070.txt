 Imperial & Royal Apartments — Entrance &
 Orientation
    WHERE YOU ARE
    At the entrance to the Imperial and Royal Apartments, the south wing of the first floor, reached from the
    Palatine Gallery. Check in at the Sala di Bona.
    WHAT YOU SEE
    Fourteen ceremonial rooms on the piano nobile, lived in by the Medici, the Habsburg-Lorraine and the House
    of Savoy. Reopened January 2025; timed entry, reservation compulsory.
    LISTEN FOR
    Why the apartment needs a separate booking, the three dynasties layered in its rooms, and the official route
    through the fourteen rooms.

 These rooms reopened on the twenty-first of January twenty twenty-five, after five years closed and
 nine months of restoration. Reservation is compulsory and entry is by timed slot; if you're here without
 one, that's why you'd be turned away. Book a slot before you visit.

 What you're about to walk are fourteen ceremonial rooms that, over the centuries, served three different
 ruling families. The oldest layer is Medici: in the late seventeenth century these were the everyday
 apartment of Grand Prince Ferdinando de' Medici, the brilliant, short-lived son of Cosimo the Third.
 The second layer is Habsburg-Lorraine, who from seventeen thirty-seven redecorated throughout,
 adding the white-and-gold stucco ceilings and, later, the silk wall-hangings. The third is Savoy: when
 Florence became capital of a united Italy in eighteen sixty-five, King Victor Emmanuel the Second
 moved in, and the family used the rooms until nineteen nineteen, when they handed the palace to the
 Italian state.

 The tour is a single enfilade, and the official route runs in order: you check in at the Sala di Bona, then
 pass through the Antechamber and the Hall of Niches, the Green Room and the Throne Room, the Blue
 Room and the Chapel, the Parrot Room and the Queen's rooms, the two little Cabinets, and finally the
 King's rooms and the Red Room. As you go, watch for the layering: a Medici ceiling over a Lorraine
 wall-silk over a Savoy bed.

 Six tracks here, one per cluster of rooms. Check in at the Sala di Bona, then begin in the Antechamber
 and the Hall of Niches. Play the next track.
