 Sala di Marte — The Room of War
    WHERE YOU ARE
    In the Sala di Marte, the third Planet Room on your route. The ceiling shows Mars in the chaos of battle.
    WHAT YOU SEE
    A grand reception room with a Pietro da Cortona ceiling fresco of Mars — the god of war — surrounded by
    allegorical figures of war, battle, and the hardening of the prince. Walls dominated by Rubens and Titian,
    with two of the largest paintings in the Palatine on the long walls.
    LISTEN FOR
    What the ceiling is doing in the Education of the Prince programme, the two enormous Rubens canvases here,
    and a Titian portrait that's worth crossing the room for.

 You've moved one room deeper into the Grand Duke's reception suite. The ceiling here is by Pietro da
 Cortona again, painted in the late sixteen forties. Mars at the centre, sword raised, surrounded by the
 chaos of war — figures tumbling, weapons clashing, the smoke of battle.

 In the Education of the Prince programme that runs through all five Planet Rooms, Mars is the stage of
 hardening. The young prince has been tempted by Venus, instructed by Apollo, and now must learn the
 brutal arts of rule. Cortona doesn't make war glorious here. He makes it violent and confused. The
 lesson the prince is supposed to take is not that war is beautiful but that he will sometimes have to fight,
 and that fighting costs.

 The walls of this room carry some of the largest and most ambitious paintings in the Palatine. Two of
 them are by Peter Paul Rubens — the Flemish master who worked as court painter and diplomat across
 half of seventeenth-century Europe. Rubens spent eight years in Italy in his twenties and never stopped
 responding to Italian painting after he went home. The two Rubens canvases here belong to different
 decades of his life and show different sides of him.

 Three works in this room get their own track. Start with the Four Philosophers, a small intimate group
 portrait of four men seated around a table — far quieter than the rest of the room. It hangs at eye level.
 Find it and play the next track.
