 The Italian Designer Decade — Room 11
    WHERE YOU ARE
    In Room 11, the decades when Italian designers conquered world fashion, Versace, Armani, Prada, Gucci.
    WHAT YOU SEE
    The made-in-Italy labels that defined global fashion from the 1980s onward.
    LISTEN FOR
    How a handful of Italian names came to rule world fashion.

 Room eleven belongs to the labels that made made-in-Italy the most powerful phrase in fashion.

 From the nineteen-eighties on, a handful of Italian designers, most of them based in Milan, came to
 define how the world dressed. Giorgio Armani softened the suit into something fluid and powerful, the
 uniform of a new kind of professional. Gianni Versace did the opposite, loud, glamorous, gold and
 colour and rock-star excess. Prada, an old Milanese leather house, reinvented itself around a cool,
 intellectual restraint. Gucci swung from heritage to scandalous glamour and back. Together they turned
 Italian fashion from the upstart of the Sala Bianca days into a global empire.

 Look at the pieces here as a kind of family argument: four very different ideas of what Italian luxury
 should be, all of them right.

 arrive.
