# Route and location audit: Tour 43 Palazzo Pitti, V5.1 English (tracks 001–061), 7 Oct 2026

Independent, read-only audit. Inputs: `v5/tracks/001–061.perf.txt`; `research/OFFICIAL_LOCATIONS_7OCT2026.md`; the Ministry of Culture (culturaitalia "museiditalia") records supplied with the brief; `findings/G6_ROUTE.md` (4 Oct, partly superseded); the official route given in the brief. The only file written is this one.

**Evidence classes**
- **OFFICIAL**: the room appears on the museum's own uffizi.it page (artwork Location field, room page, notice, or video page text), or in a Ministry record.
- **SECONDARY**: the claim rests on G6 T2 sources, on a uffizi.it page the author cites that is not in the 7 Oct evidence file, or on reputable non-official sources.
- **UNSUPPORTED**: no evidence places the work in that room.
- **CONTRADICTED**: the evidence places the work somewhere else.

## Verdict

The Palatine Gallery block (002–032) is now clean on location:
- Every work the audio places in a Palatine room has OFFICIAL support for that room.
- Every cue names the next track's room using the Italian name on the door, in the official order.
- The Iliad-closure detour is handled coherently by the cues in 010, 020 and 023.
- Leo X, the Four Philosophers, Titian's Young Englishman and the Galileo portrait are all gone from the spoken text.

Residual risk sits in three places:
- The **Gallery of Modern Art**. The Rotonda di Palmieri is placed against the Ministry record. The Napoleon head is placed in a room nobody publishes, and the next track asserts that placement as fact.
- A few **unsupported room claims** in the Modern Art, Icons and Royal Apartments tracks.
- Some **"you've just seen" back-references** to La Gravida, now that she has moved to the Sala di Prometeo.

## B. Problems, ranked by how likely each is to strand a visitor

**1. La Rotonda di Palmieri is placed and sequenced against the Ministry record (038, 039; also 040).**

Exact sentences:
- 038: "One more of Fattori's best-loved pictures is in this gallery, though the museum doesn't say exactly where it hangs. Keep an eye out for a tiny panel of women under an awning by the sea."
- 039 @where: "the museum's site does not publish its room, and the Ministry of Culture catalogue lists Room 18. It is tiny … so look closely along the walls of the Macchiaioli rooms."
- 039 cue: "When you're ready, follow the room numbers on. Room seventeen begins the last stretch of the loop."

Why it strands a visitor:
- The only official placement is MiC **Room 18**. The track sits before the Room-17 intro (040), and the audio sends the visitor to hunt for a 12 × 35 cm panel in Rooms 10–16.
- A visitor either wastes time searching rooms where it is not, or plays 039 in front of nothing.
- 040 then sends them on from Room 17 without mentioning the Rotonda, so they walk through Room 18 unprompted.
- The bundle file name `039_Macchiaioli-rooms_…` repeats the error.

Fix (preferred): move the track so it plays after the Room-17 intro.
- Swap 039 and 040.
- New 040 cue: "In Room eighteen, look for Fattori's tiny Rotonda di Palmieri, a strip of women under an awning by the sea, and play its track there."
- @room "Room 18". @where: "In Room 18 (Ministry of Culture catalogue); if it is not on view, walk on."
- Move the Room-19 "La cugina Argia" paragraph and the exit-to-Fashion cue into the Rotonda track, or keep them in the Room-17 track and add "after the Rotonda".
- In 038, replace "though the museum doesn't say exactly where it hangs" with "a few rooms on, in Room eighteen".

Minimal fix (no renumbering):
- 038: "…it hangs a little further on, in Room eighteen. The next track is its story; save it until you're there."
- 039 cue: "You've passed the start of the last stretch in Room seventeen; if you haven't played its track yet, play it now. Look for Fattori's cousin Argia next door, in Room nineteen."

**2. The colossal head of Napoleon is placed in a room nobody publishes, and 036 states that placement as fact (034, 035, 036).**

Exact sentences:
- 034: "And in these first rooms there's also a colossal marble head of Napoleon himself… Stand in front of it to hear it."
- 035 @room: "Room 2". Bundle file: `035_Room-2_Canova_and_workshop_Colossal_Head_of_Napoleon`.
- 036: "the same man who was given Napoleon's marble head in the room you've just left."

Why it strands a visitor:
- The Ministry gives no room; the 7 Oct read found no museum page.
- The only official Napoleon bust in Room 2 is the **Sèvres porcelain bust**. A visitor will very likely play 035 ("This isn't life-size. It's colossal… marble") in front of the wrong object.
- 036 then asserts the head was in the previous room, even when the visitor was told it may not be on view.

Fixes:
- 034: "There may also be a colossal marble head of Napoleon in these rooms. It's not the small white porcelain bust from Sèvres in Room two; the head is far bigger than life. If you find it, play its track there."
- 035 @room: "First rooms (unpublished)", so the file name no longer says Room-2.
- 036: replace "in the room you've just left" with "the same man who was given that marble head of Napoleon".
- Separate content point: 035 credits "Antonio Canova and his workshop, about 1810". The Ministry record calls it "an old copy after Canova", so the @what and the line "The museum gives it to Canova and his workshop" should be checked.

**3. Unsupported room claims in the Modern Art spoken text (034, 037).**

Exact sentences:
- 034: "One great canvas in these first rooms is by Pietro Benvenuti… the Saxons swearing an oath to Napoleon."
- 034: "In these first rooms hangs Giuseppe Bezzuoli's portrait of Napoleon's sister, Elisa Bonaparte Baciocchi."
- 035 then builds on it: "the woman you may have just seen in Bezzuoli's portrait".
- 037: "Look too for Giovanni Dupré's sculpture of Abel, dying."

None of these three works has a published room. Room 3 has the Baciocchi *centrepiece*, which a visitor may mistake for the portrait.

Fix: hedge each one ("the gallery owns…", "if it is on view…"), or cut it. In 035, change "the woman you may have just seen in Bezzuoli's portrait" to "Napoleon's sister Elisa".

The same applies to the room numbers in the @what/@where fields, which are not spoken but feed the PDF and route sheet:
- 038: Banti and Boldini in Room 10; Zandomeneghi and Pissarro in Room 11. Official Room 11 is Sernesi's Fig thieves.
- 037: portraits in Room 7.

Drop those room numbers, or mark them "unpublished".

**4. Two lines still point visitors to the Iliad Room, which is closed until 25 Oct 2026 (005, 006, 008).**

The normal order is the owner's decision, and 010's detour line is good. The issue is two lines that point to the Iliad from earlier rooms:
- 008: "One of them hangs in the Iliad Room, a little further along this route."
- 005: "If she isn't on these walls, she has gone back to her old place in the Sala dell'Iliade, so keep her track for that room."

Until 25 Oct, the visitor cannot reach the Iliad. They hold the La Gravida track for a room they will be turned away from, and 010 then says "Skip the Iliad tracks", so 006 is silently lost.

Fixes:
- 008: "…one of them normally hangs in the Iliad Room, next on the route."
- 005: "…she may be back in the Sala dell'Iliade. Keep her track for that room, if it's open."
- 011: add one line, "If Raphael's La Gravida is on these walls, play her track, number six, in front of her", because nothing in the Iliad tracks reminds the visitor to play 006.

**5. The cue from Prometeo to Ulisse skips four rooms without saying so (005 fallback, 006).**

- 006: "If you're in the Sala di Prometeo, walk on to the room marked Sala di Ulisse."
- 005 has the same wording.

The official route runs Prometeo → Colonne → Giustizia → Flora → Putti → Ulisse. Told to "walk on to" Ulisse, a visitor who meets four other door signs may think they have gone the wrong way, or start playing tracks by door name and find none.

Fix: "walk on through a run of smaller rooms (the Corridor of the Columns, Justice, Flora and the Putti) to the room marked Sala di Ulisse."

**6. "Just seen" back-references to La Gravida are now false (018, 019).**

- 018: "around the same time as La Gravida, which you've just seen."
- 019: "the young Raphael of the small Florentine pictures you've just met, La Gravida and the Granduca."

In the normal order she was seen in Prometeo eleven rooms earlier. Under the Iliad closure, if she was not in Prometeo, the visitor never saw her. Visitors may look around Saturn for her, and "described a work not in the room" is exactly the complaint.

Fix: 018 "…as La Gravida, which you may have met earlier in the Sala di Prometeo"; 019 "…La Gravida, earlier on the route, and the Granduca here".

**7. Royal Apartments: the start and end rooms are unsupported (044).**

- 044 @what: "…a staff-led group visit … that starts in the Green Room."
- 044 spoken: "In the Green Room, where you begin…" and "and finally the King's Antechamber, the old entrance to the private apartment."

No official room order is published. The risk is low because staff lead the group and the audio is paused, but the riddle and the "watch for" list depend on the order.

Fix: "In the Green Room, look up…" (drop "where you begin"). Replace "finally the King's Antechamber…" with "the King's rooms and the Bona Room", or make it order-neutral.
