## 032 · NEW · Raphael, Portraits of Agnolo and Maddalena Doni (Room 24)
- New track. Placement: the official artwork page gives "Saturn Room, Palatine Gallery, Pitti Palace". The 2026 Saturn-closure notice moved "the Doni couple portrait" temporarily to the Room of Apollo, which confirms they are at Pitti. The older Saturn online-exhibition text, which says they moved to the Uffizi in 2018, is superseded.
- All facts are from https://www.uffizi.it/en/artworks/portraits-doni-raffaello. Painted c.1504–07, oil on basswood, 63.5 × 45 cm. Agnolo Doni (1474–1539) was a rich fabric merchant. Maddalena Strozzi (1489–1540) was a noblewoman; they married on 31 Jan 1504, so she was about 14 and he about 30. The panels were painted en pendant as a hinged diptych. The Flood is on Agnolo's back and Deucalion and Pyrrha on Maddalena's. The Mona Lisa links suggest Raphael studied it in Florence, with clear shape and colour instead of sfumato. Her unicorn pendant carries a ruby, emerald, sapphire and pearl. Leopold II bought the pair from the heirs in 1826.
- Hedge: whether the backs are visible today is unknown → "If they're on view, find them."
- The card notes that the museum credits a second painter, the Master of Serumido, alongside Raphael. This is not in the audio.
- Not used, because it could not be confirmed on an official page: Agnolo also commissioned Michelangelo's Doni Tondo (uffizi slugs 404).
