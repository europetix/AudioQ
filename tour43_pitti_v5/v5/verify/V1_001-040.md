## Problems

### 1. HIGH — 036 Sala di Marte: ceiling described wrongly
- **Quote:** "Mars sits at the centre, sword raised. Around him, bodies tumble, weapons clash, and smoke rolls across the sky. … it is not a victory parade. … Cortona doesn't make war glorious. He makes it violent and confused. The lesson for the prince was a hard one. One day he would have to fight, and fighting costs." The track also says "The walls carry the same argument."
- **Issue:** WRONG. This is carried over from old 011, and G1 did not check it. The official page says:
  - a naval battle "rages along the perimeter, watched over by Mars who bestows strength to the young man by lighting him with his star";
  - Hercules "makes a trophy with the enemy spoils";
  - "a parade of prisoners … advances towards the female figures of Victory, Plenty and Peace";
  - the Medici arms are crowned with Ferdinando II's name.
  - So the ceiling literally includes a victory parade. The official dates are 1643–44 and 1647, so "mid-1640s" is fine.
- **Fix:** Replace the paragraph with the official reading (Mars lighting the prince with his star over a sea battle, then the trophy and the prisoners led toward Victory, Plenty and Peace), and rewrite the bridge to the Rubens ("same argument") to match.
- **Source:** https://www.uffizi.it/en/artworks/mars-room

### 2. MED — 040 Sala di Apollo: Ferri's completion date, and the card's "twenty years"
- **Quotes:**
  - Spoken: "It was his most gifted pupil, Ciro Ferri, who came and completed the cycle, in the sixteen-sixties."
  - @listen: "a ceiling finished by two hands twenty years apart".
- **Issue:** WRONG. The official page says Cortona painted the main fresco in 1647 and left that year, and Ferri finished the room "between 1659-1661". That is roughly 12–14 years later, not twenty, and mostly not "the 1660s". The date comes from old 008 and was not caught by G1.
- **Fix:** Say "who came back and finished it around sixteen-sixty", and change @listen to "about a dozen years apart".
- **Source:** https://www.uffizi.it/en/artworks/apollo-room

### 3. MED — 031 Madonna del Baldacchino: "first try at painting truly big"
- **Quote:** "The Baldacchino was among the last things he worked on in Florence, and his first try at painting truly big."
- **Issue:** WRONG. This is old 024's "first time he painted truly large", which the findings did not catch. Raphael had already painted altarpieces almost three metres tall, for example the Mond Crucifixion (c.1502–03, 283 × 167 cm).
- **Fix:** Say "…and his most ambitious Florentine picture", or drop the clause.
- **Source:** https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/paintings/raphael-the-mond-crucifixion

### 4. LOW–MED — 036 / 037 / 038: Rubens placement stated as fact (G1 hedge slipped)

### 6. LOW — 033 Sala di Giove (calibration body): the moons are stated as certain
- **Quote:** "Around Jupiter float four female figures. … They're the four moons of Jupiter."
- **Issue:** The official page says they are "probably … a tribute by the iconographer to the Medicea Sidera". Everything else in 033 is CONFIRMED on the same page: the canopy hooks, the Argonauts' ship, Hercules' club, Victory engraving the M, and 1642–44.
- **Fix:** Say "They're almost certainly the four moons of Jupiter."
- **Source:** https://www.uffizi.it/en/artworks/jupiter-room

### 7. LOW — 022 / 026 / 036 vs 033: where the Medici guest's walk ended
- **Quotes:**
  - 022: "They started at Venus and ended next door, in Saturn."
  - 026: "guests began at Venus and ended here."
  - 036: "He began in the Room of Venus and ended in the Room of Saturn."
  - 033 (heard right after Saturn): "an important visitor was led through one grand room after another … until he arrived here. This was the throne room."
- **Issue:** Listeners hear two different end points. Officially both are true in different senses: Saturn is the last of the series and the first private room, while public audiences were held in Jupiter.
- **Fix:** In 026 and 036, say "the sequence ended at Saturn, the first of the Grand Duke's private rooms, just beyond the throne room". 033 is calibration and should not be changed.
- **Sources:** https://www.uffizi.it/en/online-exhibitions/saturn-room-in-pitti-palace ; https://www.uffizi.it/en/artworks/jupiter-room
