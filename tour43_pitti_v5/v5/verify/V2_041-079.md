## Problems

### P1 · 041 (Titian, Magdalene) · WRONG
- **Quote:** "And down at the bottom left, she holds a jar, the ointment jar from the anointing story. Look at it closely. Titian has signed his name on it."
- **Issue:** The official page puts the jar ("vase") at the bottom left, with "TITIANUS" on the rim of its opening. It does not say she holds it; her hands are at her breast, holding her hair.
- **Fix:** "And down at the bottom left stands a jar, the ointment jar from the anointing story; Titian signed his name on its rim."
- **Source:** https://www.uffizi.it/en/artworks/saint-mary-magdalene ("on the rim of the opening on the vase, bottom left")

### P2 · 065 (Palatine Chapel) · UNSUPPORTED superlative
- **Quote:** "Some of its pieces were made for the most ambitious Medici project of all, the altar of the Chapel of the Princes at San Lorenzo, which was never finished."
- **Issue:** The official page says only that the 1785 altar (Santi Pacini) reused "different parts of the unfinished altar from the Chapel of the Princes in San Lorenzo". Nothing supports "the most ambitious Medici project of all", and it is arguable.
- **Fix:** "Some of its pieces were made for one of the grandest Medici projects, the altar of the Chapel of the Princes at San Lorenzo, which was never finished."
- **Source:** https://www.uffizi.it/en/artworks/cappella-palatina

### P3 · 069 (Grotta di Madama) · WRONG detail + NEEDS HEDGE; card and speech disagree
- **Quote 1:** "a neat stone frame with a gable on top, and a marble door in the middle." The @what card also says "with a marble door".
- **Quote 2:** "According to the museum, Madama was Maria Maddalena of Austria, wife of Cosimo the Second, whose coat of arms is on the wooden door."
- **Issue:**
  - The official page describes "a small door with jambs and a cornice in white marble", so the door*way* is marble. The door itself is wooden, which is why the speech goes on to call it "the wooden door". As written, the listener hears a marble door and then a wooden one.
  - The museum also hedges the name: "from which name Madama **presumably** derives". It describes the grotto as having been "closed with a wooden door" with the Habsburg arms, in the past tense, so it does not confirm that the door is there today.
- **Fix:** Use "…and a small doorway framed in white marble", and "According to the museum, the name probably comes from Maria Maddalena of Austria, wife of Cosimo the Second, whose Habsburg arms were on the grotto's wooden door". Change the card to "white-marble doorway".
- **Source:** https://www.uffizi.it/en/artworks/madama-grotto-grotta-di-madama

### P4 · 052 (Ussi, Expulsion of the Duke of Athens) · inexact
- **Quote:** "On the left, calm and composed, are Archbishop Acciaioli and the noblemen who plotted against him."
- **Issue:** The official text says the figures on the left are "the figures behind the plot: Archbishop Acciaioli and representatives of the aristocracy and of the people". The plot was not only the nobles'.
- **Fix:** "On the left, calm and composed, are the plotters: Archbishop Acciaioli, with representatives of the nobles and of the people."
- **Source:** https://www.uffizi.it/en/artworks/expulsion-of-the-duke-of-athens

### P5 · 078 (Ways out) · RULES grey zone (ticketing in speech)
- **Quote:** "It's a separate garden, not covered by a Boboli ticket."
- **Issue:** This is a ticket-coverage statement in the spoken text. G5 C put "on the same ticket" in the Add-on #25 ticketing territory.
- It is also open to misreading: since 3 Mar 2026 there has been a combined Boboli+Bardini ticket, so some listeners' Boboli ticket *does* cover Bardini.
- **Fix:** "It's a separate garden, with its own entrance." Leave ticket details to the card.
- **Source:** G5 B/C 091; https://www.uffizi.it/en/notices/combined-ticket-for-boboli-gardens-and-bardini-gardens

### P6 · 066 (Boboli Stop 1) · audit slip (low), G5 B/C 079
- **Quote:** "The garden is in the middle of a long restoration programme called Boboli Twenty-Thirty."
- **Issue:** G5 asked for the time-stamped "mid-way" to be dropped because it will date. "In the middle of" brings back the same reading, even though the change log says it was cut.
- **Fix:** "The garden is going through a long restoration programme called Boboli Twenty-Thirty."
- **Source:** G5 B 079 and C 079

### P7 · 059 cue and 060 @where · route wording (low)
- **Quote (059):** "A short flight of stairs takes you into the Museum of Fashion and Costume, on this same floor."
- **Quote (060 card):** "…along the corridor to the right and a short flight of stairs."
- **Issue:** G6 §1.4 (feelflorence, T2) describes this as "a stair of two flights of 18 steps (stair-lift available)", which is not a short flight. Leaving out the direction is correct, because it is unverified.
- **Fix:** "A staircase of two short flights takes you into the Museum of Fashion and Costume, on this same floor." Add the stair-lift to the 060 card.
- **Source:** G6 §1.4; https://www.feelflorence.it/en/node/12234

### P8 · 079 (Closing, location-neutral) · route wording (low)
- **Quote:** "A short walk west, in the Brancacci Chapel, are Masaccio's frescoes…"
- **Issue:** The track is meant to work at any of the four exits. "West" is right from Piazza Pitti (G5 E 092), but not from Porta Romana (the Carmine is north) or the Forte di Belvedere (north-west, downhill, about 1.5 km).
- **Fix:** "A short walk away, here in the Oltrarno, the Brancacci Chapel holds Masaccio's frescoes…"
- **Source:** G6 N92 / §4.4 (location-neutral requirement); G5 E 092
