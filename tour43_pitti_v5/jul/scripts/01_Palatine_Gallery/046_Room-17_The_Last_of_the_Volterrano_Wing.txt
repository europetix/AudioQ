You've reached the last of the small rooms of the Volterrano Wing. The ceiling shows Flora, the Roman goddess of flowers and spring, surrounded by garlanding attendants and putti, a decorative early-nineteenth-century fresco for a room the grand duchesses used for informal afternoons. These last rooms of the wing are easy to walk past, but each is named for the scene on its ceiling, and together they round out the Grand Duchess's private quarter of the gallery. If they're open as you pass, glance up. And that's the Palatine Gallery, all twenty-eight rooms of it. From here the route goes up: leave the Volterrano Wing and take the staircase to the second floor and the Gallery of Modern Art. Play the next track when you reach the gallery entrance.
