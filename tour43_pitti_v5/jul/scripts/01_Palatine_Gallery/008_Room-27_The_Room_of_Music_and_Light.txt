You're now in the room of poetry, music, and civilisation, Apollo's room. In the programme of the Planet Rooms, this stage of the prince's education comes after Venus's temptations and before Mars's hardening. Apollo is the instructor: rationality, learning, the arts. The ceiling was begun by Pietro da Cortona but left unfinished when he left Florence in sixteen forty-seven. His most talented pupil, Ciro Ferri, came down from Rome and completed the cycle in the sixteen sixties. If you look carefully you can sometimes see the seam, Ferri's figures are a touch more brittle than Cortona's, the colours slightly cooler, but the programme reads as a single work. The most important permanent resident of this room is one of Titian's greatest devotional paintings: his Mary Magdalene, of about fifteen thirty-one.
