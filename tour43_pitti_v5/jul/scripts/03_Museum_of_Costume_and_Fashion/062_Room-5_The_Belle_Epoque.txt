Room five is the Belle Epoque, the beautiful era, those few glittering decades before the First World War when European high society dressed with a confidence it would never quite recover. The silhouette now is the S-bend, the corset pushing the bust forward and the hips back into a long, sinuous curve, the body shaped into the line of an Art Nouveau ornament. This room ties dress to portraiture: the painters Giovanni Boldini and Vittorio Corcos made their names painting exactly these women in exactly these gowns, all swept brushwork and shimmering silk. Look for the gown associated with Franca Florio, the Sicilian noblewoman who was one of the great beauties and hostesses of the age, a woman Boldini painted and the era half-worshipped.
