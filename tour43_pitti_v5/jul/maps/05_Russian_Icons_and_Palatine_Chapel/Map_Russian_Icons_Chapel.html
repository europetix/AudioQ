Cappella Palatina

the Palatine Chapel
The summer apartment's central hall, consecrated as a court chapel by Pietro Leopoldo in 1766. Walls and ceiling frescoed by Luigi Ademollo in illusionistic Neoclassical style. Orthodox icons next door, Catholic chapel here — two devotional traditions under one roof.
