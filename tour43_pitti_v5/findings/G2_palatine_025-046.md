## HEADLINE ISSUES (read first)

1. **Sala dell'Iliade (Room 23) is CLOSED from 30 Jun to 25 Oct 2026 (Tier 1).** Tracks 025–029 cannot be used today (4 Oct). The guide's route from Saturno (24) through the Iliade to Educazione di Giove (21) is also blocked. Official notice (modified 29 Jun 2026): https://www.uffizi.it/avvisi/palazzo-pitti-tempranea-chiusura-della-sala-delliliade (EN: https://www.uffizi.it/en/notices/palazzo-pitti-tempranea-chiusura-della-sala-delliliade). Official detour: "Sala della Stufa, Sala dell'Educazione di Giove, Sala di Ulisse, Sala di Prometeo; da qui accederanno alla Sala di Giove e quindi alla Sala di Saturno, … Marte, Apollo, Venere e Sala delle Nicchie".
2. **Quartiere del Volterrano (tracks 039–045): last official word is CLOSED, with no end date.** The notice "Galleria Palatina. Lavori di restauro e riallestimento" (metadata modified 31 Mar 2026) says the Quartiere "rimangono chiusi al pubblico per consentire … un ampio programma di restauri, finalizzato al riallestimento delle sale": https://www.uffizi.it/avvisi/galleria-palatina-lavori-di-restauro-e-riallestimento. The page is still live, but it is **no longer listed** on the current /avvisi index (checked 4 Oct 2026). I found no reopening announcement, so its October status is UNVERIFIED and should be treated as probably closed. The works programme was announced 29 Jul 2025 as running "until spring 2026": https://www.uffizi.it/en/news/palazzo-pitti-via-ai-grandi-lavori
3. **The Volterrano-wing tracks are largely fabricated or wrong:**
   - **Berenice:** the ceiling is by Bezzuoli and shows Titus leaving Berenice. It is not by Volterrano and does not show the Egyptian queen's lock of hair.
   - **Allegorie:** the allegories are not Faith, Hope, Charity, Prudence and Justice.
   - **Ercole:** the room holds Benvenuti's Hercules paintings and one Sèvres vase from 1784. There is no Doccia porcelain series.
   - **Arca:** the Ark procession is on the walls. Ademollo did not do "this whole stretch" of ceilings.
   - **Flora (Room 17):** this room is NOT in the Volterrano wing.
   - **"Sala dell'Iride":** this room does not exist in the Palatine sequence.
4. **Placement conflicts:**
   - Raphael's **La Gravida**: its official artwork page now gives "Saturn room", not the Iliade.
   - Caravaggio's **Sleeping Cupid**: still not officially pinned (no uffizi.it page found).
   - The **Knight of Malta** (Fra Antonio Martelli) moved to the **Iliad Room** in Oct 2023 (Tier 1). It is uncovered by the guide.
5. **Out-of-scope flag for the lead:**
   - The official Saturn-closure notice (modified 5 Jun 2026) says that during that closure "il doppio ritratto dei coniugi Doni" was moved to the **Sala di Apollo**. That implies the Doni portraits are back at Pitti in 2026, which contradicts the BRIEF's rule that they are at the Uffizi.
   - Source: https://www.uffizi.it/avvisi/galleria-palatina-lavori-di-manutenzione
   - Please have the Saturn/Apollo reviewer re-check this rather than applying the rule blindly.
