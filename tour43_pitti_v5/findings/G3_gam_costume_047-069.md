### Headline findings
1. **Costume museum (059–069): the route from room 6 on is out of date.** The guide's room sequence (Fortuny/Jazz Age → Schiaparelli → Sala Bianca/Schubert → Space Age → black & white/Valentino/Gaultier → Versace/Armani/Prada/Gucci → Tosi/Tirelli room 13) matches the Dec 2023 / Jul 2024 installation. Since **16 Jul 2025** the 20th-century section has been a **new 9-room display** (S13): Charleston 1920s (Chini triptych, *Turandot* 1926 dress) → interwar ×2 (Schiaparelli, Vionnet, Casorati) → post-war (young Yves Saint Laurent, Ingrid Bergman's Gattinoni) → 1960s–70s/Space Age ×3 (Courrèges, Laug, Cardin) → Roberto Capucci → Enrico Coveri (1980s). The press (Tier 2) calls it "the first chapter" of an **annual** rotation. Only the 18th/19th-century rooms and the Medici garments are said to stay unchanged. Since 18 Jun 2026 the **Sarmi exhibition** has also occupied "the ballroom and adjacent rooms" (S14). Tracks 067, 068, 069 describe displays the official 2025 list does not include. 065 is mostly obsolete. 063 and 064 only partly match. No official room numbers were found for any Costume room.
2. **The Costume museum is OPEN** (no closure notice, S16; temporary exhibition running to 31 Dec 2026, S14). The **Medici funeral garments are "permanently on display"** (S12, S13). Tier 1.
3. **De Chirico (056) is wrong.** The GAM's De Chirico (S8) is *Composizione metafisica*, **1950–60**, a late replica of *Canto d'amore* (1914, MoMA), showing a head of Apollo and a red rubber glove. It came with the **Ambron gift of 1964**. It is not an early "Metaphysical Piazza" with arcades. Its room and whether it is on display are UNVERIFIED.
4. **Canova's Napoleon (049): the room is right (Room 2), but the details are wrong.** The bust is **colossal**, not life-sized. Uffizi gives it to "Canova and his workshop, c. 1810" and says it is "traditionally believed" to be his. It came as a **gift in 1845 from Louis Bonaparte to Leopold II**. The life sittings were with the **First Consul (1802)**, when Napoleon was 33, not with the newly crowned Emperor aged 36.
5. **Fattori dates (051, 054) are wrong.** The *Rotonda di Palmieri* dates from **1866**, so it is not "in the 1880s". Fattori was about **40–41** then, not 38. He moved to Florence in **1846, aged about 21**, not "in his teens".
6. **Eleonora's dress (058):** "the same dress as the painted dress" repeats a myth that has been disproven (Hall). The garments were **not first exhumed in the late 20th century**: Eleonora's tomb was opened in 1857, and the dress was shown only after restoration more than a century later.
7. **GAM rooms:** we found no GAM room closure notice for 4 Oct 2026. The "Sala della Musica" closure notice refers to the **Palatine** and has been lifted (it reopened 20 Jun 2026). The Salone del Fiorino maintenance (S11) was scheduled to end by spring 2026. The official room themes (S1) differ from the guide's banding: there are Macchiaioli rooms at 6 and 10–13, and history paintings at 5, 14–15 and **Ussi in Room 3**.
8. **Loans as of Oct 2026 (S9):** Morandi's *Paesaggio di Grizzana* (Sala 30) is in **Shanghai until 28 Oct 2026**. The other loans listed (Zandomeneghi ×4 in Sala 11, Puccinelli in Sala 8, Lega in Sala 12, Signorini and Banti in Sala 25, Gioli works) were due back between June and September 2026.
