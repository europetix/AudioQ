# G5 — Boboli Gardens stops 1–13 + closing (tracks 079–092)

Reviewer: G5. Audit date Sunday 4 Oct 2026. Read-only; this file is the only output.
Sources were fetched with WebFetch and WebSearch. "Tier 1" means a uffizi.it or ministry (cultura.gov.it) page. "Tier 2" means at least two independent sources that agree.

**Key official sources used (fetched 4 Oct 2026)**
- [U-NOTICE] https://www.uffizi.it/en/notices/restoration-work-currently-in-progress-at-the-boboli-gardens. Last modified **20 Aug 2026**. It lists these areas with "access prohibited; however, most … remain visible": Princes Garden; **Fountain of Neptune**; **Amphitheatre stands**; Chestnut Meadow (restricted); **Gates to the Lower Botanical Garden and the Limonaia**; part of the Upper Labyrinth alongside the **Cypress Lane** (the Fountain of the Tiny Birds stays accessible); **Inner area of the Island Basin**. It also lists the "Access ramp to the Kaffeehaus from the Forte Belvedere entrance (available)".
- [U-BOBOLI] https://www.uffizi.it/en/boboli-garden. Entrances: "Access to the Boboli Gardens (and possible exit) is possible not only from the Pitti Palace and the Annalena entrance in Via Romana, but also from the gates of Forte di Belvedere and Porta Romana." Also: "The Porcelain Museum remains closed for refurbishment works until further notice." The Italian page https://www.uffizi.it/giardino-boboli says the garden "si estende per **oltre 30 ettari**".
- [U-2030] https://uffizi.it/en/news/boboli2030 (1 Aug 2022). Gives "**more than 33 hectares**" and says "After years of being closed to the public … [the Kaffeehaus] will reopen in October" 2022. The masterplan PDF https://ap.uffizi.it/1689160603-presentazione-kaffeehaus-e-masterplan-boboli-2030.pdf (Oct 2021) also says "OLTRE 33 ETTARI". The €50m figure is from finestresullarte (en, Aug 2022) and gonews 1 Aug 2022.
- [U-HIST] https://uffizi.it/en/boboli-garden/history. Begun 1549 under Eleonora; Tribolo, then Ammannati and Buontalenti; Grotta di Madama 1553–55; Buontalenti grotto 1583–93; expansion southward under Cosimo II and Ferdinando II; the Viottolone leads to "a large **elliptical** basin with a central islet".
- [U-GROTTA] https://uffizi.it/en/artworks/grotta-del-buontalenti. Michelangelo's Slaves were placed in the corners in 1585 and "transferred to the Accademia Gallery" **in 1908**, then "replaced with concrete replicas". Located "in the far north of the Boboli Gardens alongside the entrance to the Vasari Corridor".
- [U-MADAMA] https://www.uffizi.it/en/artworks/madama-grotto-grotta-di-madama. Built 1553–55; "directed by **Davide Fortini, the son-in-law of Tribolo**". Situated in "a small garden … alongside the wall of the San Giorgio Convent".
- [U-MORG] https://www.uffizi.it/en/artworks/morgante-sopra-una-tartaruga. The statue at the Bacchus exit is a "Modern copy" in **plaster** after Cioli's original of **1564–1568**. The page also says Morgante was "sold by his parents"; came "from the countryside near Bologna"; received a farm near Arezzo; and the pose "ironically replicat[es] the courtly gesture of Marcus Aurelius".
- Lead-verified (cited, not redone): https://www.uffizi.it/en/artworks/boboli-amphitheatre.
