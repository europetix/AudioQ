## A. PLACEMENT

Official room sequence after the January 2025 reopening, from U-NEWS (Italian and English) and repeated in the Finestre sull'Arte write-up of the press release. Tour **starts in the Green Room** (U-HIST: "The tour of the Apartments starts from the Green Room"):
1 Sala Verde · 2 Sala del Trono · 3 Salotto Celeste · 4 Cappella · 5 Sala dei Pappagalli · 6 Salotto della Regina · 7 Camera della Regina · 8 Gabinetto Ovale · 9 Gabinetto Rotondo · 10 Camera del Re · 11 Studio (Stanza) del Re · 12 Salotto Rosso · 13 Anticamera del Re (Anticamera degli Aiutanti). The press note says "14 rooms" but names only 13. The museum page (U-RA) also lists "White Hall" and "Bona Room" among the apartment's rooms. Uffizi publishes **no room numbers** for the apartments, and none were found anywhere.

Placement summary: the order of the 12 named rooms from the Green Room to the Red Salon is right. The guide adds two rooms at the start that are not on the tour (Anticamera, Sala delle Nicchie), so every room label is 2 too high. It also leaves out the real final room, the Anticamera del Re. The "ROOMS 1-2 … 10-14" labels are not official numbers: Uffizi uses names only. Recommendation: drop the numbers from the R27–R31 headers or renumber them as Green Room = 1.
