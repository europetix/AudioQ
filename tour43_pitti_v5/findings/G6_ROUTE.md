# G6 — ROUTE AUDIT: Tour #43 Palazzo Pitti + Boboli (V4.5), checked 4 Oct 2026

Scope: the physical visitor route in 2026 compared with the guide's 92-track running order and every spoken cue.
Sections A–D of the brief are out of scope for this task. Section E (route cues) and F (could not verify) are covered here in full.
Read-only audit. Only this file was written.

---

## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

The complaints are justified. The **Palatine Gallery is sequenced almost exactly backwards** compared with the official one-way route.
- **What the guide does:** it sends visitors from the Castagnoli Room "straight ahead" to the Planet Rooms, starting at **Venus**. It then runs Venus→Apollo→Mars→Jupiter→Saturn→Iliad, zig-zags through rooms 21→22→19→14→20, and returns to Castagnoli for a Volterrano Wing sequence in the order 8→4→5→11→6→7→9→17.
- **The official route:** it runs by room number 1→28. That is antechamber → Statues → Castagnoli → **Volterrano Wing first** (4–11) → Music Room/Poccetti → Prometheus (14) → … → Ulysses (19) → Napoleon's Bath (20) → Education of Jupiter (21) → Stove (22) → Iliad (23) → **Saturn → Jupiter → Mars → Apollo → Venus (28)** → exit through the Sala delle Nicchie.
- **The guide contradicts itself:** its own WHERE cards say Saturn is "the first Planet Room you enter" and Venus "the last", but the audio plays Venus first.
- **Royal Apartments:** they are wrongly framed. They are now a **staff-accompanied 30-minute group visit** that leaves every half hour from a meeting point in the **first-floor entrance atrium of the Palatine Gallery**. The guide instead sends visitors there *after* the second floor, and to the "Sala di Bona".
- **Boboli:** the first spoken direction is wrong. It says "walk to your **right**" for the Bacchino; the Bacchino and Grotta Grande are to the **left/east**. Grotta di Madama is placed *below* the Grotta Grande when it lies above/beyond it. The Limonaia is presented as being "near the southern edge", when it is north of the Isolotto toward the palace, so the walk would double back about half the garden.
- **Two live 2026 conditions break the audio:**
  - The **Sala dell'Iliade is closed 30 Jun – 25 Oct 2026**, with an official detour that turns the Planet Rooms into Jupiter → Saturn (dead end) → Mars → Apollo → Venus.
  - The **Porcelain Museum is closed**, but the audio promises it ("which we'll come to later").

- Tier: T1 from room 14 to the exit; T2 for rooms 1–13.
- The exact internal order of rooms 4–11 is inferred from the numbering. That is **U** at room level.
- Whether staff physically block counter-flow is **U**. I found no 2025–26 visitor report: the session's web-search budget ran out before TripAdvisor or YouTube sources could be searched.

**What is live today (4 Oct 2026, T1): the Iliad detour.** The Iliad is closed **30 Jun – 25 Oct 2026**.
- Visitors reach the Moro atrium from the Stove, then turn back: Stove → Education of Jupiter → Ulysses → Prometheus.
- They then go to **Jupiter → Saturn**. Saturn is a dead end while the Iliad is shut.
- Then Mars → Apollo → Venus → Nicchie → exit.

Saturn is therefore **open**: it was closed only "until Tuesday 16 June 2026", and the 29 June notice routes visitors through it. Track 020's warning that Saturn is closed is now stale.

### 1.7 Boboli gates and layout

**Entrances and exits open in 2026 (T1).**
- Palazzo Pitti (central portal), **Annalena** (Via Romana 37/A), **Forte di Belvedere** gates, and **Porta Romana**.
- Sources: uffizi.it/giardino-boboli; uffizi.it/en/boboli-garden; feelflorence (four entrances/exits).
- A separate "Bacchino exit" onto Piazza Pitti is **not listed** officially. Wikipedia mentions an exit onto Piazza Pitti by the Bacchino. Status: **U**. The guide should not call it a "gate" visitors can use.
- **Bardini:** there is no internal link inside Boboli. A combined Boboli+Bardini ticket exists (T1). Bardini is not "on the same ticket" as Pitti/Boboli, as track 091 says.

**Relative positions (T2 unless marked).**
- **Bacchino → Grotta Grande** (Buontalenti): both are low, along the east boundary wall on the Vasari Corridor side, next to Piazza Pitti. Wikipedia: "poco più avanti, sempre lungo il bordo … la Grotta del Buontalenti".
- **Grotta di Madama:** in the **Giardino di Madama, above or beyond the Grotta Grande**, reached by small secondary paths.
  - APGI: "Grotta di Buontalenti: below the Giardino di Madama". https://www.apgi.it/wp-content/plugins/custom_garden/ajax/get_pdf_client.php?id=1219
  - Wikipedia: "più a sinistra la Grotticina della Madama".
  - theflorentine.net, 1 Jun 2020: "nearby, but you will have to take the small secondary paths". https://www.theflorentine.net/2020/06/01/boboli-meaningful-walk/
  - The guide has this the other way round.
- **Central axis, going up:** courtyard → Artichoke Fountain → amphitheatre/obelisk → Neptune basin → **Abundance** at the top of the axis. The **Giardino del Cavaliere** (Porcelain Museum) is above it, "offset to the south". (Wikipedia; Touring)
- **Kaffeehaus:** "descending the hill toward the north-east, at about the height of the Abundance statue". It is **east** of the axis. Track 084's "up and to the left" from Neptune is consistent with that. (Wikipedia; APGI "north of the Vasca del Forcone")
- **Viottolone:** the head is the raised **Prato dell'Uccellare**, west of the upper axis. It runs SW downhill to the **Isolotto**, and the **Porta Romana** exit is right there. (Wikipedia; Touring; feelflorence: "Porta Romana entrance … easy to reach la Vasca dell'Isola")
- **Limonaia:** on the **west (Via Romana) side, reached along the Viale della Meridiana**, "midway between the Palace and the end of the garden".
  - Sources: it.wikipedia Limonaia di Boboli; APGI.
  - Touring places it in the "upper" part instead.
  - Exact spot: **U**. However, all sources agree **it is NOT at the southern edge.** From the Isolotto it is a walk back north toward the palace.
- **Annalena gate:** on Via Romana, between the Meridiana/Limonaia area and the southern end. It is not "further north than Porta Romana" in the sense of being "a southern exit".
- **Porcelain Museum:** "remains **closed** for refurbishment works until further notice". **T1**: https://uffizi.it/en/boboli-garden
- **Elevation:** the guide quotes about 60 m of rise on the main axis (not re-verified). Practical point: going Isolotto → Limonaia → Pitti means climbing back up most of the garden's length. Isolotto → Porta Romana exit is flat.

**Natural walking loop from the palace end (recommended):** Courtyard/Artichoke → **left** to Bacchino → Grotta Grande → up the side paths to Grotta di Madama → west and up to the amphitheatre → Neptune → Kaffeehaus (east terrace, view) → Giardino del Cavaliere (top) → Abundance (top of the axis, view down) → Prato dell'Uccellare / Viottolone head → down the Viottolone → Isolotto → **then either** exit at Porta Romana **or** walk north along the Via Romana side to the Limonaia → exit at Annalena or back through the Meridiana side to Pitti.

An alternative for the Kaffeehaus, Cavaliere and Abundance stretch is Neptune → Abundance → Cavaliere → Kaffeehaus → back west. Both work. The guide's Kaffeehaus → Cavaliere → Abundance → Viottolone is acceptable and is kept; only the cues need fixing.

- **⚠** = live 2026 condition.

- **N01 (cur 001) Piazza Pitti.** ✎ Replace "You'll start one floor up … From there you go up again to the second floor … Then you work your way back down: the Imperial and Royal Apartments on the first floor" with the real shape: Palatine (one-way) + the RA slot on the first floor → second floor → ground floor → Boboli. ## 4. Genuine alternatives: how the guide should handle them

1. **Iliad closed (to 25 Oct 2026; live today).**
   - The official detour turns back at the Moro atrium: Stove → Education → Ulysses → Prometheus → **Jupiter → Saturn (dead end) → Mars → Apollo → Venus** → Nicchie.
   - The guide should not be re-cut for a 4-month closure. Instead:
     1. Add one navigation-only line to N21 (Stove): "if the Iliad is closed, follow the signs back to the Sala di Giove".
     2. Make each Planet-Room intro self-contained, with no "you've just come from X".
     3. Put a room-indexed track list in the PDF.
   - During the detour the Iliad tracks (N22–N26) are unplayable. Whether their works are re-hung elsewhere is not stated in the notice (U).
2. **Royal Apartments slot.** The visit is staff-led, 30 min, every half hour from the Palatine entrance atrium (T1). The guide must not assume it comes last upstairs.
   - Place it right after the Palatine (N47–N52).
   - Say in N01 and N47: "if your slot is earlier, play this section first; staff can then lead you on into the Palatine" (T1: "accompagnati dalle unità di personale").
   - If the slot falls mid-Palatine, visitors should pause and return to the atrium. The Palatine tracks must therefore be resumable by room number.
3. **Ground floor first or last.** The Icons and Chapel open off the courtyard. Either works. Keep them last (on the way to Boboli), self-contained.
   - Fix N11 (042) so the Ark Room text doesn't assume the chapel has been seen.
4. **Boboli first** (combined ticket, or the palace queue). Make Section G self-contained.
   - N79: hedge the Costume Museum reference.
   - N92: neutral ending that doesn't depend on order ("half the painters you met today").
5. **Boboli exits.**
   - Porta Romana sits by the Isolotto (flat).
   - Annalena is on Via Romana, in the west.
   - Forte di Belvedere is at the top, from the Prato dei Castagni.
   - Pitti is the main gate.
   - The guide should end with a "ways out" track that is valid at any of these, not a forced return to Piazza Pitti.
6. **Saturn / Jupiter swaps.** Earlier in 2026 one or the other was shut; for example Saturn until 16 Jun 2026, with its Raphaels moved to Mars, Apollo and Venus (T1). Same remedy: room-indexed, self-contained tracks, and closure lines only where navigation needs them (Add-on #25).
