## Palatine Gallery (106 artwork pages)
Gallery of Statues: Asclepius, Athena Giustiniani, Athlete, Bust of Antoninus Pius, Bust with head of Doryphoros,
Knidian Aphrodite, Mercury with petasos, Lucius Verus.
Castagnoli Room: Dacian prisoner; Statue in armour. (Table of the Muses: uffizi.it video page only, no location field.)
Allegories Room: Volterrano Father Arlotto's Trick; Mehus Genius of Sculpture; Sustermans Senators swearing allegiance
to Ferdinando II; Zocchi Young Michelangelo; Giovanni da San Giovanni Venus combing Cupid's hair.
Fine Arts Room: Cristofano Allori Adoration of the Magi. Hercules Room: the room (Benvenuti 1817–29); Sèvres vase.
Aurora Room: Ligozzi Adoration of the Magi. Berenice Room: Riminaldi Martyrdom of St Cecilia; Furini Painting and
Poetry; Mehus St Zenobius. Room of Fame: van Aelst still lifes (two).
Poccetti Gallery: Furini Hylas and the Nymphs. Corridor of the Columns: van Aelst three dead birds.
**Prometheus Room:** Filippo Lippi Madonna and Child with scenes from the life of St Anne (Bartolini Tondo);
Botticelli Portrait of a lady; Ribera St Francis; Cosimo Rosselli Adoration of the Christ Child; **Raphael La Gravida**.
Flora Room: Helmbreker Rustic scene; circle of Volterrano The cook. Putti Room: van Aelst still life with ram's head.
**Ulysses Room:** Raphael Madonna dell'Impannata (Madonna and Child with Saints Elizabeth, Magdalene, young John);
Raphael Elisabetta Gonzaga; Raphael Guidubaldo da Montefeltro; Filippino Lippi Story of Lucretia; Cigoli Ecce Homo;
Riminaldi Amor Vincit Omnia; Vasari Temptation of St Jerome; Carlo Dolci Madonna; Dolci St John on Patmos.
**Education of Jupiter Room:** Cristofano Allori Judith with the Head of Holofernes; Carlo Dolci Martyrdom of St Andrew;
Filippo Napoletano Snail seller. (Caravaggio Sleeping Cupid: no artwork page.)
Stove Room: room page (Giovanni da San Giovanni 1627, Pietro da Cortona 1637–).
**Iliad Room** (closed to 25 Oct): Andrea del Sarto Passerini Assumption; Bartolini Charity; Artemisia St Mary Magdalen;
Fra Bartolomeo Holy Conversation; Veronese Baptism; Barocci Visitation; Bassano Christ in the House of Martha; copy after
Correggio; Caravaggio Knight of Malta (video). (Artemisia Judith and her Maidservant: no artwork page.)
**Saturn Room:** Raphael Madonna della Seggiola; Madonna del Granduca; Madonna del Baldacchino; Doni portraits;
Young Man with an Apple; Ezekiel's Vision; Cardinal Bibbiena; Julius II (workshop); Perugino Lamentation; Carlo Dolci
Sleeping Infant St John; Guercino St Sebastian.
**Jupiter Room:** Raphael La Velata; Andrea del Sarto St John the Baptist; Verrocchio St Jerome; Sebastiano del Piombo
Martyrdom of St Agatha; Consani Victory.
**Mars Room:** Bronzino Luca Martini; Foggini Baptism of Christ (bronze). (Rubens Consequences of War: uffizi.it video
page only, no location field.)
**Apollo Room:** Titian Penitent Magdalene; Andrea del Sarto Luco Deposition; Andrea del Sarto Medici Holy Family;
Ligozzi baby Leopoldo.
**Venus Room:** Canova Venus Italica; Titian The Concert; Titian Pietro Aretino; Titian La Bella; Salvator Rosa two
harbour scenes; Cigoli Calling of Peter and Andrew; Manetti Ruggiero and Alcina; Bilivert (attr.) Apollo flaying Marsyas.
No location on page: Raphael Inghirami ("Phaedra"), Dosso St John the Baptist, Luca Giordano works, Bassano Parable of
the Sower, Bandinelli bust of Cosimo I, the room pages of Apollo, Jupiter, Mars, Venus, Stove.
**Not a Pitti work any more:** Raphael Leo X with two cardinals: returned to the "Gallerie degli Uffizi" in Dec 2020 for a
Pitti exhibition to Jan 2022; no Palatine artwork page; do not place it in Saturn.

## Palatine Chapel (8 pages)
Ademollo decoration 1791–92 (Entry into Jerusalem, Crucifixion on Calvary); altar with Last Supper (1604–06) and
Adoration of the Magi in pietre dure; Chigi Crucifix (Rues and Raggi 1670–72).

## Ammannati Courtyard (4)
Weary Hercules; Hercules and Antaeus; Hercules and Cerberus; Ammannati's Portrait of a female mule (La Muletta).

## Boboli (91; route-relevant)
Bacchino = "Morgante on a tortoise", location **"Bacchus exit"** (1564–68). Buontalenti Grotto façade: Apollo and Ceres;
inside: Helen and Theseus; Giambologna Bathing Venus (Grotta Grande). Madama Grotto 1553–55. Artichoke Fountain
1639–42. Amphitheatre c.1551. Kaffeehaus. Knight's Building and Rampart 1527–30. Lemon House (Limonaia) 1777–78/1816.
Garden of Ganymede, Grotto of Adam and Eve (1817), Jupiter seated, Peasant and his Barrel (1554–57).
