## Headline findings (read first)

1. **The Planet Rooms run in the wrong direction for today's one-way route.** The official route enters the Planet Rooms at Saturn and finishes Saturn → Jupiter → Mars → Apollo → Venus → Sala delle Nicchie → exit. Until 25 Oct 2026, with the Iliad closed, it goes Prometheus → **Jupiter → Saturn → Mars → Apollo → Venus** → Niches → exit. The guide plays Castagnoli → **Venus** (005) → Apollo → Mars → Jupiter → Saturn → Iliad (025), which is the opposite direction. Its own text contradicts this: in 002 ("start with the Castagnoli Room and the Room of the Iliad … ending at Venus"), and in the PDF cards for 005, 008, 011, 016 and 020, which say "last / fourth / third / second / first Planet Room on your route". It looks as if the play order was reversed in V4.5 and the cues were never rewritten. Three tracks also refer back to works the listener hasn't reached yet: 010 ("the other Titian in the Mars room"), 018 ("Passerini Assumption you saw in the Iliad Room") and 024 ("the Gravida … you've already met").
2. **Sala di Saturno is OPEN again.** The official notice closed it only "until Tuesday 16 June 2026", and the later Iliad notice (route from 30 Jun 2026) sends visitors through Saturn. Track 020's closure warning is now outdated. The Saturn Raphaels are back on their official artwork pages as "Saturn Room". **The Sala dell'Iliade is now CLOSED, 30 Jun – 25 Oct 2026**, which breaks the cue at the end of 024 and the references in 018, 023 and 024.
3. **Placement errors:** Titian's *Aretino* is in the **Venus Room**, not Apollo (Tier 1). The Sustermans *Galileo* that the track describes (c.1635–36, "near the end of his life") is at the **Uffizi, room E7** (Tier 1). Nothing places any Galileo portrait in the Sala di Giove.
4. **Factual errors that can't be defended:**
   - Canova's Venus was not commissioned by Napoleon, and the Medici Venus was taken from the Uffizi, not the Pitti (005, 006).
   - Rubens's *Consequences of War* was not a gift to Ferdinando II. Sustermans commissioned it, and Grand Prince Ferdinando bought it in 1691 (013).
   - The Titian *Magdalene* was not painted for Federico Gonzaga (009).
   - The Four Philosophers are identified in the wrong positions, and Philip Rubens died in Antwerp, not Italy (012).
   - The two cardinals in *Leo X* are swapped (022).
   - The del Sarto model was not "Giuliano Bugiardini", a painter 11 years older than del Sarto (018).
   - The Concert's "feathered cap" belongs to the wrong figure, and the player does not look out at us (007).
   - The English Gentleman wears no hat (013 cue, 014 card).
   - The Velata veil logic contradicts the official page (017).
   - A stray leftover sentence in 023 calls the Saturn room "the throne room".
5. **Conflict with the BRIEF:** the official uffizi.it artwork page for the **Doni portraits** now gives "Pitti Palace, Palatine Gallery, **Saturn Room**". The Feb 2026 Saturn-closure notice also listed "the double portrait of the Doni couple" as temporarily "in the Room of Apollo". The brief's assumption ("at the UFFIZI since 2018") appears outdated: they went to Uffizi room 41 in June 2018 but are now back at Pitti. I have not proposed them as a gap (house rule). I flag it so the lead can decide.
