Four men sit around a table in this painting. When Rubens painted it, two of them were already dead.

It looks like a meeting of scholars. It's really a family picture. Rubens painted it in Antwerp around sixteen eleven, a few years after he came home from Italy. Every man in it was tied to the others by friendship, by teaching, or by blood.

Start at the far left. The man standing a little apart is Rubens himself. He wasn't a scholar, and he paints himself as a witness rather than a member of the circle.
