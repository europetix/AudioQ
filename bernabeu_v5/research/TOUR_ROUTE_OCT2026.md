# Tour Bernabéu: Classic route as of October 2026 (research for Yo Tours audio guide v5)

Research date: 23 Oct 2026. Method: WebSearch and WebFetch, one page at a time. No page asked for human verification. One site (whattodoinmadrid.com) blocked fetching and was skipped.

**Grades**
- **OFF**: official Real Madrid source (realmadrid.com or the club brochure).
- **OFF\***: official non-club authority (CRTM / Comunidad de Madrid for Metro; esMadrid is the city tourism board and is graded as a reputable secondary source, not OFF).
- **SEC2**: two independent reputable or recent sources.
- **WEAK**: one blog, review or listing.
- **NONE**: nothing found.

**Main warning.** Many OTA and tourism pages still describe the pre-renovation tour (Presidential Box, players' tunnel, "14 European Cups", "Best Club in History room", 10-language audio guide). These include Tiqets, thebettervacation, visitmadrid.es and TUR4all. Do not script from them. Current official product pages list only these zones: Museum, Panoramic view, Photos, Dressing rooms, Benches, (Press room, subject to availability), Madridista Premium Zone, Store.

## 1. Route table (Classic tour, best current evidence)

**Note on order.** The official brochure (July 2026) numbers the zones 1 to 7: 1 Museo, 2 Panorámica, 3 Recuerdos inolvidables (photos), 4 Vestuarios, 5 Banquillos, 6 Zona Madridista Premium, 7 Tienda. It does not say the numbers are the walking order. It does say the tour ends at the shop ("Completa el Tour visitando la tienda"). Independent 2025–2026 listings and reviews say the visit **starts at the top tier with the panoramic view and then descends into the museum**. Stops 2 and 3 below follow the visitor evidence. ## 2. Match days, event days and the pitch

**Official rules**
- **Hours:** Mon–Sat 09:00–19:00; Sundays and holidays 09:30–18:30. Closed 25 Dec and 1 Jan (OFF). The listed times are entry times. esMadrid gives the last exit as 20:30 (Mon–Sat) and 20:00 (Sun/holidays).
- **Match day:** The tour runs until **5 h 30 min before kick-off**. Dressing rooms and benches are **closed on match day and the day before** (OFF). From 12:00 on the day before a match, only the museum and panoramic view are open (esMadrid).
- Match dates are usually confirmed 2–3 weeks ahead (OFF, flexible page). The route and hours "podrá variar" because of events. Changes are posted on the web, at the taquilla, and at the tour door (OFF).

**Closures announced on the official pages (weekday check places them in 2026–27)**
- Dressing rooms and benches closed: Thu 5 Nov, Mon 9 Nov and Tue 10 Nov 2026.
- Reduced hours 09:00–15:30: 21 Nov (flexible page only), Tue 24 Nov 2026 and Tue 19 Jan 2027.
- GuruWalk lists Sun 18 Oct 2026 as 09:30–15:30.
- Realmadrid Games does not operate on match days (GuruWalk, WEAK).

**Pitch / hypogeum**
- Officially, the turf "will remain underground in its maintenance area and will only be installed on the surface for matches". Other equipment may be on the pitch during visits (OFF).
- Script for a **bare or covered floor by default**, never "look at the perfect grass".

## 3. Official audio guide or app

**What the club offers today**
- **None found.** No current official product page (Classic, Flexible, RM Games, Guiada, Schools, Individual FAQ) mentions an audio guide, app, or the languages of self-guided content. Grade: OFF (by absence).
- The only official human commentary is the **Guiada** (live guide, max 20 people). Third-party listings say these guides speak English or Spanish.

**History**
- In April 2017, Real Madrid, Microsoft and GVAM launched an interactive audio-guide app. It ran in 6 languages (EN, ES, DE, FR, RU, Mandarin), offered gamified challenges, and had three tour configurations. This was the pre-renovation route.
- Late-2018 prices were €18 standard and €23 with audio.
- Whether this product still exists is **unverified**. Treat it as discontinued unless seen on site.
- Sources: https://www.gvam.es/en/wp-json/wp/v2/posts/2529 ; https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g187514-i126-k12119355-Tour_of_Santiago_Bernabeu_Stadium_Guided_or_self_guided-Madrid.html

**Third-party audio on sale (competitors)**
- **Headout / madrid-ticket.com** sells an "audio guide" format: phone audio or a radio device. It describes a one-way route "from the panoramic stands into the museum" and says "the last floor has no elevator".
- **WeGoTrip** has an English-only app tour, €33.15. Its content dates from the construction era and the listing shows as "Unavailable".
- A Tripadvisor reviewer (Aug 2026) mentions "adding an audio guide for €6 per person". The seller is unclear (WEAK).
- Sources: https://www.madrid-ticket.com/santiago-bernabeu/audio-guide/ ; https://wegotrip.com/madrid-d14/santiago-bernabu-stadium-tour-discover-the-legends-of-real-madrid-p4622

**What visitors say is missing (our opportunity)**
- **No commentary.** "No guides or audio guides… incomprehensible" (Tripadvisor, May 2025). "Could have been better to have had a docent" (Sep 2026).
- **Wayfinding.** "Poorly marked corridors" (Aug 2025). A Nov 2025 visitor relied on staff for directions.
- **Pacing.** Late slots get rushed: staff were "rushing us to finish" at 19:30 on a 18:30 slot (Sep 2026). People in long queues at photo stations and touchscreens (Oct 2026, crowding reviews).
- **Too much text** in the museum (2018 audio-guide review). Curation beats completeness.
- **Upsell fatigue.** Repeated photo stations and AI-photo pricing (€30) (2026).
- **Content gaps.**
  - No explanation of *why* the pitch is bare (the hypogeum).
  - Nothing on what visitors are seeing in the roof and screen.
  - No closure-aware alternatives when the dressing rooms or benches are shut.

## 4. Duration, accessibility, photography and signage

**Duration**
- No official figure.
- Civitatis, GuruWalk and miradormadrid say "45 min" on average.
- GYG lists 1.5 h. 2026 reviewers report about **2 h and ~4.5 km walked**; routeandstay reports up to 3 h.
- Plan the audio around **75–110 min** of content plus optional extras. Grade: SEC2 that real visits are well over 45 min.

**Accessibility**
- The official FAQ says: "The Bernabéu Tour route includes **numerous staircases**". The Accessibility FAQ section is empty (OFF).
- GYG: reduced-mobility visitors can access the **museum and panoramic view only**; the benches and dressing rooms are "not currently accessible".
- Civitatis: "No es accesible en silla de ruedas".
- Headout: last floor has no lift.
- TUR4all (undated, probably pre-renovation): lifts and stairs, an alternative accessible entrance with staff escort, and manual wheelchairs available.
- Grade: SEC2 that the route is **not fully step-free**. Lift locations: NONE.

**Photography**
- Personal photos are allowed in the museum and throughout (miradormadrid; reviews).
- No tripods or professional equipment (one commercial guide, WEAK).
- No official photo rules were found. Bag rules: NONE. Security uses metal detectors.

**Signage and numbering**
- The brochure numbers the zones **1–7** (OFF).
- Whether physical **numbered signs** exist on site is **unknown** (NONE). miradormadrid says visitors "follow explanatory signage", and one review complains of poor marking.
- Do not reference sign numbers in the script until verified.

## 5. Metro station works and the exit point

**Station works** (OFF\*)
- The project is a €66 m full rebuild (Comunidad de Madrid): the station roughly triples to 12,400 m², with 12 lifts, 24 escalators and a large open lobby.
- Works started Feb 2024 and are due to finish **Q1 2027**. They were about 45% complete in June 2026.

**Service suspension**
- L10 has been **suspended Nuevos Ministerios–Cuzco from 28 Mar 2026 to the end of 2026**. In summer (4 Jul to end of Aug 2026) the cut was extended to Plaza de Castilla.
- Replacement bus **S10** (free for Metro riders) runs every 2–3 min on weekdays 06:00–21:00. Its stops nearest the stadium are on **Castellana at Plaza de Lima**.

**New station entrances** (El Debate, May 2025; WEAK/press)
- New main access "at the height of the stadium's Torre B".
- The Azca access is reinstalled in front of Torre Europa.
- Plaza de Lima accesses were closed during the works.

**Tour exit**
- The tour ends in the official store. The flagship store is on **Calle Padre Damián, on the east side** (OFF).
- The nearest transport from there has not been checked. Cercanías / Metro L6/L8 at Nuevos Ministerios is to the south, and Metro L9 is reinforced as an alternative.
- Verify on site which street visitors exit onto and the walking time to S10 / Nuevos Ministerios.

## 6. Open questions only an on-site visit can settle

1. **Entrance door.** Is the tour door "Puerta 84", inside "Torre B", or both? What is written on the signage? Is there a separate queue for online vs box-office tickets? Where is the Castellana 140 taquilla relative to the door?
2. **First vertical move.** Lift, escalator or stairs from the entrance to the top tier? How many flights? Is there a lift alternative for mobility-impaired visitors, and where?
3. **True order.** Panoramic first and then the museum (visitor evidence), or museum first (brochure numbering)? Does the order change on match days or the day before?
4. **Panoramic stop.** Exactly which tier and stand (third tier per the brochure; which side faces which way)? Where are the maqueta and the 1947-to-today audiovisual show?
5. **Museum layout.** How many floors, and what are the current room names? Where exactly are the 15 European Cups, the Femenino space, any Ballon d'Or display, the Bernabéu Infinito arch, and the RM Games entrance? Is there a one-way path?
6. **Bernabéu Infinito.** Can non-members buy it on site, at what price, how long does it last, and in which languages?
7. **Photo stations.** How many, where, and can visitors bypass them?
8. **Dressing rooms.** Home only, or also away? What is inside (lockers, names, shower area)? What replaces this stop when it is closed?
9. **Pitch level.** What is the path from the dressing room to the benches (tunnel or corridor)? Can visitors walk onto the pitch surface? What is on the surface on non-match days?
10. **Press room.** How often is it open, and where does it fall in the route?
11. **Madridista Premium Zone.** Is it just a desk, or does it have content?
12. **Exit.** Which street does the store exit onto (Padre Damián assumed)? Is there any exit that skips the store? How long is the walk to the S10 stop at Plaza de Lima and to Nuevos Ministerios?
13. **Signage.** Are zones physically numbered 1–7 on site? What are the exact sign texts and languages? Are there QR codes or official audio points?
14. **Official audio guide.** Is anything sold at the taquilla (the 2017 GVAM app or a €6 add-on)? Languages, device, and quality?
15. **Rules.** Bag size limits and lockers, tripods/selfie sticks, flash, strollers.
16. **Timing.** Typical time per zone, and when staff start sweeping visitors out before closing.
17. **Metro.** Status of Santiago Bernabéu station if it reopens early in 2027. Locations of the new entrances relative to Torre B.
