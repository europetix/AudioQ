**Purpose:** decide what can be carried into the Yo Tours rebuild.

## Grading key

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **OK** | Confirmed by official realmadrid.com, or by two independent reputable sources. |
| **WRONG** | Contradicted by sources. The correct fact and its source are given. |
| **UNVERIFIED** | Could not be confirmed. This covers invented-sounding quotes, insider colour and single-source numbers. Do not narrate as fact. |
| **DATED** | True now, but will go stale: the coach, the captain, the "last two seasons", records, transport works. Narrate with a date, or keep it out of the audio. |

Source IDs such as [S12] point to the source list at the end. Where "(title)" follows a source, the item was corroborated by a search-result headline, not a full fetch.

## Route reality check (official Tour Bernabéu, October 2026)

The official zones are listed on realmadrid.com [S1][S2] and in the July 2026 tour brochure [S3]:

1. Museum
2. Panoramic view of the stadium (brochure: from the upper tier)
3. Photo spots ("Exclusive photos" with trophy/player, paid)
4. Changing rooms (including the First Team dressing room)
5. Benches (dugouts, "right by the pitch")
6. Madridista premium zone (brochure)
7. Official store

Other official notes:

- **Press room:** the Classic and all other packs say "temporary press room visit subject to availability" [S2].
- **Match days:** locker rooms and benches are not accessible on match days or the day before. Extra closure dates are also published. The tour runs until 5 h 30 min before kickoff [S2].
- **Not on the tour:** the **Palco / presidential box** and the **players' tunnel** are not named for any public pack [S1][S2][S3].
- **Pitchside:** "pitchside" exists only as the benches/dugout stop. No pitch-edge walk is advertised.
- **Not covered by the old guide:** the press room, the photo spots, the premium zone and the RM Games add-on.

## Summary

### Most serious problems

1. **Route.** 4 of 20 tracks (001's promise, 016 Palco, 018 tunnel, and partly 019 pitchside) describe places the public Classic tour does not visit. Nothing covers the press room, the photo spot or the premium zone. Tracks 014–015 invent a "middle tier" stop.
2. **Hard factual errors** a fan will catch:
   - Ramos as 2014 captain (it was Casillas).
   - "Nobody else won it as player and three times as coach" (Ancelotti and Guardiola both did).
   - Zidane's 1998 Ballon d'Or shown as a Real Madrid trophy.
   - The pig's head on Figo's "first" return.
   - "Hala Madrid y nada más" since the 1950s (it dates from 2014).
   - "Six managers" from 2002 to 2014 (there were 12).
   - Messi "after retiring".
   - "Without missing a season" (2020-21 was played at Valdebebas).
   - Concert fines of €2.5 m (actually about €0.8 m, paid by promoters).
   - The shuttle "SE10" (actually S10).
   - Benzema in 15 minutes (actually 17); Joselu in 90 seconds (actually about 3 minutes).
   - The roof-weight "correction", which is itself wrong.
   - Ramos's 2014 goal placed on the Bernabéu pitch (it was in Lisbon).
   - Barinaga described as Argentine.
3. **Invented or unsourced quotes.** These include the 1944 "temple" epigraph and the Messi, Puyol, Ferguson, Casillas and Roberto Carlos quotes. Track 018 is built almost entirely on them, so it cannot be published.
4. **Insider colour presented as fact:** dressing-room citrus smell, curved lockers, screens per locker, ice baths, staff numbers, Palco deal-making, and the tunnel's dimensions and lighting. All UNVERIFIED. Some of the Palco material is reputationally risky.
5. **Dated material:**
   - Coach (Mourinho, with Arbeloa missing from the story).
   - Captain (Valverde).
   - "Two trophyless seasons", which overstates 2024-25.
   - The PSG and Barcelona doubles.
   - "No Ballon d'Or since 2022", which expires on 26 October 2026.
   - The metro closure.
   - The record attendance.
   - "Nearly two years, no concerts".

### Keep (with fixes)

- **Structure and tone:** short zone intros plus single-focus tracks, the honest "doors may be shut" framing, the express route idea, and the willingness to name uncomfortable facts (the 32-year gap, the concert saga, the 2024 boycott).
- **Track 002 (facade):** Acerinox, anti-glare finish, gmp/L35/Ribas & Ribas, old bowl retained. Drop the area, slat count and napkin.
- **Track 003 (Bernabéu):** fix 1911 and Barinaga's nationality, and drop the "temple" quote and the bonds anecdote.
- **Tracks 005–008 (trophy story):** fix the "nothing at all" claim, the manager counts and the 2014 captain.
- **Track 009:** reframe around Real Madrid's own 12 Ballons d'Or. Remove Zidane 1998 as a vault item, fix the player-coach claim and the Figo timeline, and update after 26 October.
- **Track 013 capacity paragraph:** accurate and distinctive. Remove the Messi quote.
- **Track 014 LED ring:** good, once "deep" becomes "high" and the roof-weight correction is rewritten (about 12,000 t of roof steel, more than the Eiffel Tower).
- **Track 015 hypogeum and concerts:** strong story. Fix the fines, the court ruling wording and the dates.
- **Track 020 practical tips:** fix the gate side, the S10 shuttle and the metro wording.

### Drop

- **Track 016 (Palco):** not on the tour; mostly unverifiable or risky. A short "the presidential box above you" aside at the panoramic stop could cover Pérez facts.
- **Track 018 (Tunnel):** not on the tour; built on unsourced quotes.
- **Sensory and insider claims in 017:** citrus, curved lockers, screens, staff counts. Rewrite only from a site recce.
- **Unsourced statistics:** 200,000 objects, 500 m fans, 4,000 peñas, 3 m shirts, 45° rake, 40 m drop, pitch costs and dye.

### Add (gaps the old guide missed)

- Press room (subject to availability)
- Trophy/player photo spot
- Madridista premium zone
- Arbeloa's January–June 2026 spell
- The UEFA Super Cup and Intercontinental Cup won in 2024-25
- The NFL game (November 2025) as an example of the multi-use floor
- The new "Bernabéu" metro station, due in 2027

### Verdict

**Rebuild, reusing selected material.** The voice and several narratives are reusable, but the guide cannot be republished with light fixes:

- 4 of 20 track locations are off-route.
- About 20 claims are outright wrong.
- One full track depends on invented quotes.
- Much of the 2025–26 layer is already dated.

Rebuild the guide on the official zone order (Museum, Panoramic, Photos, Dressing rooms, Benches, Press room if open, Store) and do a site recce before scripting tracks 012–019. Put every date-sensitive line (coach, captain, Ballon d'Or, recent seasons, transport) in a separately versioned "current season" track that can be re-recorded without touching the rest.
