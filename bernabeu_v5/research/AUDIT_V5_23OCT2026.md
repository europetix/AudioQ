# Independent audit of the Bernabéu V5 English guide (tracks 001–028), 23 Oct 2026

Checker: independent of the writers. No track was edited.
Method: I read all 28 `.perf.txt` files (headers and bodies), PLAN.md, the four research files and the three writers' notes. I checked facts with WebSearch and WebFetch, one page at a time. No page asked for human verification, and Wikipedia was not used as a source.
Verdicts:
- **OK**: confirmed (OFF = realmadrid.com / UEFA / FIFA, or SEC2 = two independent reputable sources).
- **WRONG**: false, or contradicted elsewhere in the guide.
- **UNVERIFIED**: only one source, or no source I could reach.
- **HEDGE**: true in substance, but the wording claims more than the sources support, or it will go out of date soon.

### Case B: match day or the day before, 001–021, 022, then 027–028

- **Dead end at 019 (main track).** Its cue, "The next main stop is the first team's dressing room", is wrong on a closed day. The two Extras are presented as optional. A listener who skips them walks to a closed door with no instruction.
  - The only "play 22" cue is in **021, which is itself an optional Extra**. 020 ends "after the museum, the next main stop is the dressing room", with no fallback either.
  - 001 promises that "when you reach the end of the museum, the guide will tell you what to play instead". That promise holds only if the listener plays 021.
  - **Must fix.** Put the closed-day line in 019, and in 020 as well.
- 021 → 022 → 027 → 028 works, and the spoken numbers are right.
- **028 recap assumes stops not heard in case B.** "A bench that few coaches keep for long" refers to 024. "A room where fifteen minutes can turn a night around" is partly covered by 022. This is a minor listening point, not a dead end.
- **Match day plus museum first (B and C together) contradicts itself.**
  - At 022 the first condition, "closed → play twenty-seven", fires first. The listener is sent to the store and never sees the panoramic view.
  - If they take the second branch instead, they go 004 → 008. 008 then says "go on to the dressing room … play number twenty-three", which is closed.
  - This combination is plausible: TOUR_ROUTE §2 says that from 12:00 the day before a match only the museum and the panoramic view are open.
  - **Must fix.** Reorder 022's last paragraph and add a closed-day branch to 008.

### Case C: museum first, 001–003, 009–022, then 004–008, then 023–028

- 001 and 003 send the listener to "number nine" correctly. 001 says "The guide will bring you **back up** to the view afterwards", but in case C the listener has not been up yet. Minor; say "take you up".
- **019, 020 and 021 send a case-C listener to the dressing room.**
  - 021's cue covers only the closed day ("If the dressing room and benches are closed today, play number twenty-two instead").
  - On an open day, a case-C listener who follows the spoken cue goes straight to the dressing room and misses 022, so they never get the "play number four" instruction.
  - **Must fix (021, and 019/020).**
- **022 contradicts itself in case C.**
  - "You've seen this stadium from its highest seats" is false for a case-C listener at that point, because they go up afterwards.
  - "head **back** up to the panoramic view" is also off: they have not been there yet.
  - **Must fix.**
- **007 contradicts the case-C route.** Its cue, "the next main stop is the museum, down from this top tier", is wrong for someone who has already seen the museum. Only the optional Extra 008 carries the case-C fallback. **Must fix:** add the fallback to 007.
- 008 → "play number twenty-three" is correct. 023 → 028 is unchanged and works.
- Content order in case C:
  - 005 repeats the 1957 final from 011 almost word for word (Di Stéfano penalty, Gento second, 2–0 Fiorentina).
  - 004's "think back to the story outside" now refers to something heard roughly 50 minutes earlier. Acceptable.
