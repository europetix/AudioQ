# Tour Bernabéu: official and competing audio guides, October 2026 (competitor research for Yo Tours audio guide v5)

Research date: 23 Oct 2026. Method: WebSearch and WebFetch, one page at a time. No page asked for human verification. One page was blocked because a permission request timed out, and it was not retried: the Google Play listing (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.realmadrid.android).

This file adds to TOUR_ROUTE_OCT2026.md §3 and does not repeat it.

**Grades**
- **OFF**: realmadrid.com, the club brochure, or a club app-store listing.
- **SEC2**: two independent sources, or one primary vendor source plus one independent source.
- **WEAK**: a single blog, listing or review. "WEAK (vendor primary)" means the claim comes only from the company that built the product.
- **NONE**: nothing found.

## 0. Headline finding (corrects §3 of TOUR_ROUTE_OCT2026.md)

**An official digital guide on loan devices has been running since 5 June 2026.** No realmadrid.com page mentions it.

- **Who built it.** GVAM, the Madrid firm that made the 2017 Bernabéu audio guide, published "GVAM develops the new digital guide for the Tour Bernabéu" on 9 Jun 2026.
- **What the vendor says:**
  - "Since last June 5, the initial version can be enjoyed on smart loan devices that can be reserved with the ticket or directly at the start of the Tour."
  - It is "an application specifically designed for the new Bernabéu Stadium" and follows the club's brand identity.
  - It offers "more than fifty multimedia experiences available in twelve languages". The vendor wording is "will be able to access", so not all of this may be live yet.
  - Content is grouped into tours by interest, time available or visitor profile. Planned profiles: families, groups, international visitors, corporate, VIP.
  - Formats: storytelling, historic images, immersive pieces, games and interactive content.
  - Named spaces: dressing rooms, trophy room and "technical areas".
- **Roadmap features (not yet live).** These arrive "little by little": interactive maps, points of interest, synchronised audiovisual playback, augmented reality and personalised tours.
- **Phase 2 (future).** VR of historic moments and of the stadium's construction, an AI assistant for navigation and recommendations, and GStats analytics for the club.
- **Not stated by GVAM:** the price, whether visitors can use their own phone, offline use, accessibility features (sign language or audio description), headphones, and a product name.
- **Corroboration from a visitor.** Tripadvisor reviewer "barbara" (5 Aug 2026, translated from Italian) wrote: "We purchased the tickets on the Real Madrid website, €45 adult €37 children, without a guided tour." She then added "an audio guide for €6 per person" and reported a total visit of 1.5 to 2 h. This suggests the device is a **€6 add-on in the official checkout or at the start of the tour**.
- **Grades:**
  - Existence and launch date: **SEC2** (vendor primary source plus one visitor review).
  - 12 languages and 50+ experiences: **WEAK (vendor primary)**.
  - €6 price: **WEAK** (one review).
  - Presence on realmadrid.com: **OFF by absence**. It does not appear on any product page, FAQ, the July 2026 brochure, the Dec 2023 brochure or the Groups page.
- Sources:
  - https://www.gvam.es/en/gvam-develops-the-new-digital-guide-for-the-tour-bernabeu/ and https://www.gvam.es/en/wp-json/wp/v2/posts/13892 (published 2026-06-09, modified 2026-06-10)
  - https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g187514-d796251-Reviews-Santiago_Bernabeu_Stadium-Madrid.html

**What this means for Yo Tours**
- The club does sell an audio-capable guide, but on **rented devices** and with **no public marketing**.
- Visitors who book through OTAs, or who never see the add-on, are unlikely to know it exists.
- The live-feature set is only an "initial version". Maps, AR and personalised routes are still being added.
- **To do on site:** check the device, the languages actually live, the price, and whether you can use your own phone or headphones.
