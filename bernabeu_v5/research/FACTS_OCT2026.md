# Bernabéu audio guide: fact sheet (checked 23 Oct 2026)

**Grades:** **OFF** = official source (realmadrid.com; UEFA where noted as OFF-UEFA). **SEC2** = two independent reputable non-official sources agree. **WEAK** = one source only, or a supplier/trade source, or sources that conflict. **NONE** = not verified, or the sources contradict each other.
**Method:** WebSearch and WebFetch only, one page at a time. No Wikipedia-only facts. Wikipedia was used only to find leads.
**Before using any fact marked "changes":** check it again on the day of recording.

## DO NOT USE: false, outdated or unverifiable claims

1. Real Madrid won the 2025 or 2026 Champions League. **False.** PSG won both.
2. Real Madrid won the 2024-25 or 2025-26 Liga. **False.** Barcelona won both. The count is still 36.

4. "Nine FIFA Club World Cups". **Misleading.** The club's 9 merges Intercontinental Cups, Club World Cups and the 2024 Intercontinental Cup.
5. "Real Madrid players have won 18 Ballon d'Or awards" (or "11 players"). That count includes awards won at other clubs. Use **12 awards / 8 players while at the club**.
