# DE notes: Bernabéu tracks 001–028 (Katja, Sie-Form)

All 28 JSON files written. `assemble.py --check … de` passes for every track. Each body is shorter than the English, from 1% (014: 256/261) to 11% (002: 298/328).

## Quotations
- 019 AFP quote ("Real Madrid does not go where it is not respected."): the original is Spanish and no German original exists. I translated the English faithfully, in quotation marks, introduced the same way: „Real Madrid geht nicht dorthin, wo man es nicht respektiert.“
- 016 „minuto noventa y Ramos“: the Spanish original is kept in quotation marks, as in the English, then glossed "Minute neunzig und Ramos".
- 012 „yeah, yeah, yeah“ and „She Loves You“: the English lyric and song title are kept.
- 009 „Real“: a single word in quotation marks, as in the English. It is not a quotation.
- 009 and 027 "in the club's own words…" / "the club calls it…": kept as reported speech with no quotation marks. No German official wording was used.
- No German original quotation appears anywhere in the tour, so nothing needed verifying.

## Adaptations (facts unchanged)
- 010 "Old Big Ears" became "Henkelpott", the German fans' nickname for the same trophy. It is also named for the handles.
- 002 "Ribas & Ribas" became "Ribas und Ribas" so the TTS does not have to read the & symbol.
- 020 "CD Tacón" became "Club Deportivo Tacón", the full name, to avoid an abbreviation.
- 005 World Cup and European Championship are written out ("Weltmeisterschaft", "Europameisterschaft"). WM and EM are not used.
- 028: "Line 10" became "Linie zehn" and "Metro lines 6 and 8" became "Metrolinien sechs und acht". The bus line name S10 is kept as the English has it.
- Proper names and acronyms the English uses are kept: UEFA, FIFA, NFL, AFP, gmp, L35, EuroLeague.
- 008 "lost five goals to one" became "verlor … mit eins zu fünf", which is the German convention of giving the losing side's score first.
- 018 "just before the hour" became "kurz vor der sechzigsten Minute".
- Spanish terms are kept with the English glosses: miedo escénico, hipogeo, socios, La Séptima, La Décima, Galácticos, Ye-yé ("das Ye-yé-Madrid"), Los Blancos, Saeta Rubia, Copas del Rey.
- Regnal number: "Alfons der Dreizehnte".
- "Laws of the Game" became "die Fußballregeln", the standard German term.
- Extras are introduced as „ein kurzer Extra-Titel“, per the glossary.
