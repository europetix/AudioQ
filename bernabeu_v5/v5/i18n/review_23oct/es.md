## Adaptations (facts unchanged)
- English glosses of Spanish terms are dropped where Spanish makes them redundant: Nuevo Chamartín/New Chamartín (003), Saeta Rubia/Blond Arrow (011), La Séptima/the Seventh (013), Copas del Rey/King's Cup (021), socios (009), Hasta la próxima/until next time (028).
- 010 "Old Big Ears" is rendered as "la Orejona", the usual Spanish nickname.
- 017 "first hat-trick of Champions League titles" is rendered as "la primera vez que alguien ganaba tres Champions League seguidas" ("triplete" means league, cup and Europe in Spanish).
- 004 "third tier" is rendered as "tercera grada", and in @where as "el tercer nivel según el folleto".
- 003/008 decades with digits in the English (1940s, 1920s, 1980s) are rendered as "la década de 1940/1920/1980". Decades in words in the English (fifties, sixties, eighties, nineties) are kept as words.
- "As of autumn 2026" is rendered as "En otoño de 2026" throughout.
- Scores are in words. "One all" is rendered as "Empate a uno". "Six goals to five" is rendered as "seis a cinco".
- Day-of-month digits are kept where the English uses digits (27 de octubre, 14 de diciembre, 21 de abril). Words are used where the English uses words.
- Long sentences, including the "if… play number…" directions in 007/008, are split for the ear. Every condition and number is kept.
