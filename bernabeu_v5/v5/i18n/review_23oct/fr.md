## Quotations
- 019, AFP quote ("Real Madrid does not go where it is not respected"): the original was Spanish, from a club source to AFP. Translated faithfully, in guillemets, introduced the same way as in the English: « Le Real Madrid ne va pas là où on ne le respecte pas. » An AFP-based French version exists, "Et le Real Madrid n'est pas là où il n'est pas respecté" (https://www.brut.media/fr/articles/le-real-madrid-convaincu-que-vinicius-ne-sera-pas-ballon-dor-boycotte-la-ceremonie). It is not used: it appears in only one outlet, the wording looks garbled, and the quote is not a French original. Swap it in if you prefer the press wording.
- 016, "minuto noventa y Ramos": kept in Spanish as the English does, with the French gloss "la minute quatre-vingt-dix-et-Ramos".
- 009 (the club "owned by its members"), 011 (Di Stéfano called "the best player of all time"), 014 (France Football's "most beautiful goal"), 015 (Pérez's promise) and 008 (Valdano's essay title) have no quotation marks in the English. All are given as reported speech.

## Wording choices (please check)
- 002, "Ribas & Ribas": written "Ribas et Ribas" because the TTS rule bans "&".
- 002 and 027, sums of money in words: "un milliard quatre cents millions d'euros" and "un peu plus d'un milliard deux cents millions d'euros".
- 010, "Old Big Ears": rendered with the usual French nickname "la Coupe aux grandes oreilles". The English name is not spoken.
- 017, "Kyiv": written "Kiev" so the French TTS reads it naturally.
- 020, "CD Tacón": written in full as "le Club Deportivo Tacón" (no abbreviations). "3.1 seconds" becomes "trois secondes et un dixième", and "1 July" becomes "le premier juillet 2020".
- 021, "1931 to 1932": written "la saison 1931-1932".
- 028, Line 10 and lines 6 and 8: numbers written in words. "S10" is kept as printed on the bus.
- 007, 008 and 020, the condition "or, if it's closed today": "it" is spelled out as "si le vestiaire est fermé aujourd'hui" so it cannot be read as the museum. The meaning is unchanged.
- 019, "if you came into the museum before the view": written "si votre visite a commencé par le musée, avant la vue". 022 uses "Si vous n'avez pas encore vu la tribune supérieure". Both avoid gendered past participles ("entré/entrée"). Throughout, "Quand vous le souhaitez" replaces "quand vous êtes prêt(e)".
- 023, title "The home dressing room": rendered "Le vestiaire du Real Madrid".
