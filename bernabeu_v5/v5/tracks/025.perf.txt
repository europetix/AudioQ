[conspiratorial] If you ever watch a Champions League night here, keep an eye on the clock. In this stadium, the last few minutes have a habit of changing everything.

Go back to that night against Paris Saint-Germain, in March 2022. At half-time, Madrid were two goals behind over the two legs. Just after the hour, Karim Benzema pounced on a mistake by the Paris goalkeeper. [hmm]

Two months later, Manchester City came here for the second leg of the semi-final. When the ninetieth minute came, Madrid were two goals down over the tie. Rodrygo scored in the ninetieth minute, and again a minute later. In extra time, Benzema scored a penalty. Six goals to five, over the two legs.

And in May 2024 it was Bayern Munich. They led here in the eighty-eighth minute. Then Manuel Neuer couldn't hold a shot from Vinícius Júnior, and the substitute Joselu got there first. In added time, Joselu scored again. Madrid went on to win the cup at Wembley.

All of this happened while the stadium around them was still being rebuilt.

[pause] Not every great night here comes late. In March 2026, Federico Valverde scored three times against Manchester City before half-time. As of autumn 2026, he's the club captain.

[quietly] And here's the honest part. That same season ended without a single trophy. Comebacks make the legend. They don't guarantee the cup.

There's a short Extra on the press room, number twenty-six, if it's open today. The next main stop is the store, where the tour ends.
