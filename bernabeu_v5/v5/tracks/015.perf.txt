[curious] In the summer of 2000, a man won the presidency of Real Madrid with a promise about one footballer.

The man was Florentino Pérez, an engineer and businessman. The footballer was Luís Figo, the star of Barcelona, Madrid's great rivals. Pérez said that if he was elected, Figo would come. [pause] He was elected in July, beating the sitting president, Lorenzo Sanz. And Figo came.

Then it became a habit, with another of the most famous players on earth arriving every summer. Zinedine Zidane from Juventus. Ronaldo, the Brazilian, from Inter. David Beckham from Manchester United. The nickname for the team was the Galácticos, the galactic ones, and it stuck.

The idea was simple: the best players in the world, in the most famous shirt in the world. And for a while, it won. Cup number nine, the Zidane volley you just heard about, belongs to these years.

[wry] Critics said that a collection of stars is not the same thing as a team. In 2006 Pérez resigned, by his own decision. Three years later he was back, and the signings started again, this time with Cristiano Ronaldo.

He has been president ever since. The stadium around you was rebuilt under him. Then, in June 2026, the socios had their first contested presidential election in twenty years, a real choice between Pérez and the businessman Enrique Riquelme. Pérez won about two thirds of the vote. As of autumn 2026, he is serving his eighth term.

Love him or not, much of what you see on this tour, from the signings to the steel outside, carries his mark.

Head back to the row of cups now, for cup number ten, the one won in Lisbon.
