[wry] The European Cups get the spotlight in this museum. But the trophies that fill a club's ordinary seasons are the league and the cup.

As of autumn 2026, Real Madrid counts thirty-six Spanish league titles and twenty Copas del Rey, the King's Cup. The first cup came in 1905. The first league title came in the season of 1931 to 1932, and the team went through it unbeaten. It did so under a plainer name. The year before, when Spain became a republic, the crown had come off the badge, and the club was simply Madrid Football Club again.

Add nine world club titles, in the club's own count, which joins the old Intercontinental Cup to FIFA's club competitions.

[pause] Then there's the odd one out. On the eleventh of December 2000, at a gala in Rome, FIFA named Real Madrid the best club of the 20th century, with more than forty-two percent of the vote. Manchester United came next, with under ten. Florentino Pérez and Alfredo Di Stéfano collected the trophy together. No other piece in this museum covers a whole century.

Leaving the museum, the route passes a photo stop, where buying the picture is up to you, and then leads to the dressing room. If it's closed today, or you haven't been up to the top tier yet, play number twenty-two.
