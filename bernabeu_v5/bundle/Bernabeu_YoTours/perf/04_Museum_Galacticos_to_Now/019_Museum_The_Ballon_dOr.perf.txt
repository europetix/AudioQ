[curious] Only one footballer has ever been given a Super Ballon d'Or. It was a one-off award, a kind of best of the best, and he played for this club.

It was Alfredo Di Stéfano, in 1989. The French magazine France Football, which created the Ballon d'Or, gave it to him, on top of the two ordinary ones he had already won here.

The Ballon d'Or is football's best-known prize for a single player, and the trophy is exactly what the name says, a golden ball. Counting up to and including the 2025 award, players have won it twelve times while wearing this club's shirt, eight different men in all.

Di Stéfano, twice. The Frenchman Raymond Kopa. Luís Figo, in his first season here. Ronaldo, the Brazilian. The Italian defender Fabio Cannavaro. Cristiano Ronaldo, four times. Luka Modrić. And Karim Benzema, the most recent of the twelve.

[wry] Now, you may see or hear a bigger number. The club's own list counts eleven players and eighteen awards. That's because it also includes golden balls its players won at other clubs, like Zidane's, which he won at Juventus, and it adds Di Stéfano's special award. Both numbers are honest, they just count different things.

And one more story, because it shows the club's pride from the other side. In 2024, Vinícius Júnior was widely expected to win. [pause] Hours before the ceremony in Paris, Real Madrid announced that its whole delegation would stay away. The club saw the result as a snub to its player. A source at the club gave the news agency AFP a short explanation: "Real Madrid does not go where it is not respected." The award went to Rodri of Manchester City. Vinícius finished second.

Whatever you make of that answer, it tells you how this club sees itself.
