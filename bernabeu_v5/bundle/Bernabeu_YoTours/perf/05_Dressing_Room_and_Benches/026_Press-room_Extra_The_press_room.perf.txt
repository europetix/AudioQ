[lightly] The night before a big European game here, the person answering questions in this building is often not from Real Madrid at all.

Here's why. Under UEFA's rules for the Champions League, each club holds a press conference on the day before a match, with its head coach and at least one player. The visiting team normally has to hold its press conference in the stadium where the game will be played. The home club is allowed to hold its own at its training ground instead. So when the great European sides come to the Bernabéu, their coaches usually face the cameras in this stadium first.

The club opens this room to visitors only some days, as a temporary visit, subject to availability. If you're in it now, you're seeing the other side of a familiar picture. On television, you only see the person answering. In here, you can see where the questions come from.

[wry] A match lasts ninety minutes. The questions start the day before, and they go on long after the final whistle.

When you're ready, carry on to the store, where the tour ends.
